Introduction

For our Communication & Image division, we are looking for a project manager whose main mission will be to manage partnerships related to the Perpetual Planet Initiative.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and strengthen relations with Rolex's various partners related to environmental conservation.
  • Maintain close collaboration with the Communication & Image Division teams on communication regarding the said partners and their activities

Profile

  • University training, ideally in the areas of Biology, Communication and/or Media
  • Minimum 2-3 years experience in managing international partnerships, ideally in the fields of environment, biodiversity, animals, science, exploration
  • Being comfortable with international and varied interlocutors: personalities, researchers, NGO leaders in the field
  • Understanding international issues related to the protection of the environment and endangered species
  • Level of English C1, minimum

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The Department of Psychiatry, with nearly 1,300 employees, is responsible for a three-fold mission in the area of psychological disorders, training and research. Quality teaching is also provided by the Department for students and also for doctors who are being trained for the title of specialist by raising awareness of the psychological, biological and social dimensions. The department also supports quality training in psychotherapy. The Geriatric Psychiatry Service offers psychiatric, psychosocial and psychotherapeutic treatment to the elderly population of the Canton of Geneva as part of a public service mission. The service contributes to the prevention of mental illness and the psychosocial consequences of psychiatric disorders in collaboration with various partners.

Description of post

As part of your activity, the accompaniment of people of age with inpatient mental health problems is at the heart of your practice. You develop occupational therapy approaches around human occupations and adapted to the status of the patient. You play a key role in the promotion of autonomy, rehabilitation and psycho-affective accompaniment of AVS patients with psychiatric disorders associated with cognitive disorders or not. Your role includes the conduct of occupational therapy treatment plans, both individually and as a group, as well as the seizure of the services performed. The management and updating of patient records are also part of your responsibilities, ensuring that you document the interventions performed and the relevant clinical elements. Your responsibilities include providing information in a synthetic and structured manner, as well as participating in the regular evaluation of therapeutic objectives at interdisciplinary meetings. As such, you are actively contributing to the co-construction of therapeutic care, while maintaining a critical reflection on your professional practices. You guarantee the quality and continuity of care in connection with other health professionals and professionals. Collaboration with an interprofessional team is a central element of the position, in order to ensure consistent support and focus on the specific needs of each patient. At the same time, active participation in the department's projects is expected, as is the contribution to the sharing of practices within the HUG occupational therapist sector.

Qualifications

Having a diploma in occupational therapist HES or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Swiss Red Cross, you can benefit from an institutional experience. Mental health knowledge and the Geneva network are additional assets. For the benefit of experience with AVS patients, you are taking an innovative approach and actively participating in the evolution of professional practices. On a daily basis, you demonstrate an excellent organizational capacity to cope with simultaneous demands. Your sense of priority, stress management and flexibility are essential assets to effectively meet the requirements of the position.

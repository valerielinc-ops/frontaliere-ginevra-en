As an M Club Intern, your main responsibilities will include

  • Providing a warm and personalized welcome to M Club guests.
  • Ensuring high-quality service and guest satisfaction throughout their visit.
  • Assisting with the setup and preparation of the M Club in accordance with hotel standards.
  • Supporting the team with food and beverage service.
  • Communicating effectively with the kitchen regarding allergies, special requests, and guest needs.
  • Maintaining cleanliness and organization within work areas.
  • Following all company safety procedures and policies.

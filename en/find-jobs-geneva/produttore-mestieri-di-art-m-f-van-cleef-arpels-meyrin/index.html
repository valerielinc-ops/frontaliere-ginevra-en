Reference code: JR136215

Who are we?

A High Jewelry Home with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

You have technical training (design/microtechnical) and experience confirmed in 3D design and development of technical or art products.

You master 3D design software. You have an excellent understanding of mechanical assembly logics and retrodesign.

Recognized for your rigour and your concern for excellence, you are committed to delivering a job of irreproachable quality within the deadlines set.

Proposition force, agile and with a constructive spirit in the face of technical challenges, you combine aesthetic sensitivity, autonomy and taste for teamwork.

What do we expect from you?

Attached to the Technical Office Manager, you guarantee the technical feasibility and aesthetic conformity of creations Métiers d'Art throughout their development phase.

As part of your duties, you

  • Guarantee the technical feasibility of the parts, from the prototyping phase to their launch, integrating into the construction of the volumes the aesthetic codes transmitted by the studio;
  • Make the backdesign from 3D scan files and design the full technical prototype folders
  • Bring innovative approaches by being the strength of proposals, faced with the technical and aesthetic constraints encountered;
  • Validate, in close collaboration with the Project Manager and the Technical Bureau Manager, the 3D drawings and files of the partners, from the development phase to production;
  • Collaborate with Industrial Methods in the definition of tooling needs to ensure efficient passage in the Workshops

More than a job... We're recruiting for a career!

Integrating Van Cleef & Arpels is first and foremost a team where the development of employees is at the heart of our ambition. From your integration, you will be immersed in the world of the House thanks to trainings on our history, know-how and culture.

The recruitment process

Apply directly online. If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for a first interview. You then meet the Technical Office Manager to discuss your technical skills and motivations.

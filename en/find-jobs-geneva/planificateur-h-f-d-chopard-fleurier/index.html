Full description

Your Mission Propose production plans tailored to your needs by taking into account the main constraints of production in meeting quality and safety management standards.

  • Your major responsibilities Analyze RPM data and make any corrections. Establish manufacturing orders and ensure the smooth execution of these orders. Consolidate field information and ensure the distribution of the planning. Identify planning constraints and propose solutions. Monitor the adequacy of data and planning policy.

Role overview

Within the digestive and bronchial endoscopy unit, you ensure the management of outpatients and inpatients receiving diagnostic and therapeutic examinations in a specialized technical environment.

You welcome, inform and accompany patients throughout their care journey, from the preparation of the examination to post-intervention surveillance.

You ensure the nursing care of patients in the pre-, per- and post-examination phases, ensuring their safety, comfort and quality of care.

Company and context

  • You coordinate the flow of patients on the technical platform and in the monitoring room by demonstrating autonomy, organization, responsiveness and an excellent sense of priority.
  • You ensure rigorous clinical monitoring and appropriate transmissions to promote continuity and security of care.
  • You participate in the guards or pickets to ensure the care of endoscopic emergencies 24 hours a day.
  • This function offers you the opportunity to evolve within a specialized, dynamic and innovative technical plateau, with the contact of multidisciplinary teams expert in gastroenterology and pneumonology.

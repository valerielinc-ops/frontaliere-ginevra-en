Full description

You assist several health professionals, including doctors and team leaders for the management and administrative organisation of the service, in the clinical, academic and research fields, ensuring the interface between the various professional fields. You organize appointments, sessions and seminars by managing the electronic agenda of these health frameworks. You prepare the files, prepare the minutes of the meeting, and coordinate the follow-up of decisions made. You work in coordination with the assistant of the physician-chef and you replace him or her as necessary. While responding to the various requests, you carry out the administrative activities related to your function, such as the management of doctors' schedules (VRH), the administrative management of private or faculty funds, the organization of conferences and symposia of the medical profession, the administrative support to the projects of the managers of the service, or the monitoring of the budget allocated to the service and research. You are pro-active in carrying out the logistical tasks arising from the life of a secretariat such as orders, applications for appointment, reimbursement or training. Within the team, you develop a close relationship with your fellow secretaries, ensuring continuity of service during absences. And more generally, your sense of service makes you an agile and diplomatic person.

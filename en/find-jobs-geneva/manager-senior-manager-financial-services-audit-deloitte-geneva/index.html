Full description

Location: Geneva Work Pattern Percentage: 60-100% Help our clients successfully manage global challenges Our purpose as audit and assurance professionals is building trust and confidence in business and protecting the public interest. Audit quality has never been more critical for stakeholders, people and society. Together with your future colleagues, you will conduct audit engagements with very diverse national and international clients. You will be able to work independently while benefiting from the expert advice of our experienced staff and enjoying exceptional learning. You will contribute and actively participate in the development of our services, learn a lot about people and businesses and get a lot of responsibility early in your career. How you can make an impact You will be:

  • involved in providing auditing and assurance services to our portfolio of international and local banking clients
  • responsible for the execution of the financial and regulatory audits, leading audit teams during the fieldwork at clients and managing and coaching junior team members
  • working on complex financial areas of the clients' balance sheets, income statements, including goodwill, loan loss provisioning on credit portfolio, financial investments portfolio incl. complex and non-standard investments and taxes
  • working on regulatory areas such as anti-money laundering, internal organisation, client and market conduct, risk management, solvency, consolidated supervision, etc.
  • supporting and ensuring the continued training and development of the audit team Who we are looking for You:
  • are a Swiss Certified Public Accountant (Swiss CPA) or a Federal Audit Oversight Authority (FAOA) registered auditor with equivalent foreign qualification, or you have started your curriculum to become one
  • have +4 years' experience in auditing, ideally at one of the Big 4, and have working knowledge of Swiss accounting principles and FINMA regulatory requirements for banks
  • communicate confidently in French and English. Any further languages would be an advantage
  • work independently and are motivated by new challenges. You are driven by an entrepreneurial spirit and like taking on responsibility
  • enjoy working as part of a team. Building and cultivating customer relationships comes easily to you Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team at our Geneva office. We will help you grow both personally and professionally: through our trainings, mentoring programmes and on-the-job learning, but also by connecting with your colleagues across all business lines.

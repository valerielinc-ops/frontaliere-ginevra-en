Full description

Introduction

For our Information Systems Division, we are looking for a Business Analyst MES (M/F) whose main mission will be to support the functional aspects of project implementation and the maintenance of production processes.

Responsibilities

  • Collect, analyse and clarify the needs expressed by users and production professions
  • Translate business needs into adapted functional solutions
  • Provide interface between business users, Product Owner and technical teams
  • Contribute to the evolution, maintenance and smooth operation of production applications
  • Support technical teams to ensure a common understanding of needs
  • Participate in functional tests, user recipes and deployment of developments
  • Ensure functional maintenance of production applications
  • Contribute to level 2 and 3 incident resolution
  • Participate in support, development and implementation of ESM solutions

Profile

  • University training in management computing or equivalent experience
  • Experience as Business Analyst or similar function; industrial experience is desired
  • Ability to collect, analyse and formalize business needs and then translate them into clear and workable functional requirements by technical teams
  • Experience in implementing, evolving or supporting an MES system; knowledge of DELMIA Apriso is an important asset
  • Mastery of functional tests, user recipe and application support
  • Knowledge or experience of an Agile environment
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis and organizational capabilities; rigor, methodology and direction of priorities
  • Autonomy, solution orientation and initiative
  • Team spirit, very good communication skills and ability to collaborate with multidisciplinary teams across multiple sites

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

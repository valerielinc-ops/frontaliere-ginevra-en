Full description

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) is a national and international reference institution. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . This course is offered by the Radiation Protection Unit of the Medical Directorate of the HUG. The task of the group is to ensure the safety of employees of the HUG and the public present at the institution's premises in relation to exposure to ionizing radiation. Several HUG sectors use radioactive materials or X-rays within the institution for various activities.

Description of post

You are part of the team that currently consists of a radiation protection engineer and a radiation protection technician. The purpose of this course is to carry out job studies in order to evaluate occupational exposure to ionizing radiation from different positions in order to contribute to the optimisation of radiation protection. You collect and analyze the data necessary for the radiological characterization of workplaces. You evaluate the predicted doses associated with the activities studied. You identify factors influencing workers' exposure. You propose technical and organizational optimization measures. You participate in the classification of staff according to their activity. You write and present the results of the post studies to stakeholders. All these tasks involve collaboration with radiation user services.

Qualifications

You are currently in Master 1 or Master 2 in the field of radiation protection or related discipline. You are looking for a compulsory and agreed internship as part of your university curriculum to develop your skills in a medical environment. You have a solid knowledge of radiation protection, including risk assessment of ionizing radiation, analysis of exposure conditions and proposals for appropriate preventive measures. You know the basic principles of radiation protection and the regulatory requirements for the medical sector. You are able to work independently and to write clear and structured reports. You have good relationship skills that allow you to interact effectively with a variety of interlocutors such as doctors, caregivers, laboratory technicians, etc. You have mastered French both orally and in writing.

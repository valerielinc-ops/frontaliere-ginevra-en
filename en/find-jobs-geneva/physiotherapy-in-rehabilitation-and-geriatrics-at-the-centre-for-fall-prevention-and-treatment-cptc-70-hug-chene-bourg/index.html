Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. The Rehabilitation and Geriatric Department is looking for a physiotherapist interested in outpatient care for the day hospital. The mission of the Centre for the Prevention and Treatment of Falls (CPTC) focuses on the prevention of falls through individual groups and sessions, in interprofessional operation. It is located on the estate of Belle-Idea, in a building close to the Three Oak Hospital.

Description of post

Are you interested in rehabilitation, therapeutic education, interprofessional work, group care and standardized assessments? As a proposaler, do you want to contribute to the projects of a large institution such as the HUGs and recognize yourself in our institutional values? As a physiotherapist, you are involved in the development, application and evaluation of treatments in accordance with physiotherapeutic assessment, diagnosis and medical prescription. You manage patients, individually or in groups, according to the objectives set for interprofessionality and patient. You document daily the files (treatments performed and clinical information). You also record the services provided. You attend and participate in interdisciplinary seminars and case discussions.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree in Physiotherapy or a degree in Physiotherapy recognized and registered by the Swiss Red Cross. Knowledge in the functional assessment tools of patients, training or experiences related to group animation are a plus. For non-Francophone applications, the level of French C1 is required. A perfect mastery of common computer tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word,...) is necessary. Mastering HUG applications is a plus. If you are flexible, that you have a sense of organization that allows you to face multiple simultaneous requests while keeping smiling, that you know how to work independently while enjoying the collaboration as a multidisciplinary team and that you want to invest in your missions with conviction and dynamism, do not hesitate to apply!

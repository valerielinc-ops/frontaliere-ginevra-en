Full description

Introduction

For our Production Division, we are looking for an Operations Technician (M/F) whose main mission will be to coordinate and supervise the various activities of the production workshop - machining and finishing. Combined with the entire production process, it is able to integrate a multidisciplinary team of specialists and collaborate with support services. He is a collegiate participant in the management and facilitation of the flow performance on the quality, cost and timeliness axes. In addition, it is the guarantor of employee training processes.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure the proper functioning and performance of the means of production, while respecting the objectives of the sector,
  • Support daily the workshop's trades in case of technical difficulty and quality,
  • Be the technical sponsor of the area, analyse the risks, communicate them to the workshop and follow them,
  • Managing tools and consumables,
  • Define and maintain indicators,
  • Piloting and running the various technical sessions,
  • Ensuring the reliability of the workshop's basic data and compliance with procedures,
  • Analyze production data and ensure the reliability of our information system entries,
  • Check compliance of new tools and processes,
  • Participate in the qualification of new products, continuous improvement actions and optimisation of operating procedures.

Profile

  • Diploma of ES Technician or diploma deemed equivalent,
  • First experience in the industrial field,
  • Knowledge of problem solving quality tools (8D, Pareto, etc...),
  • Ability to analyse important data,
  • Spoken team spirit, listening and autonomous,
  • Excellent written and oral communication,
  • Watchmaking experience and concept of project conduct: an asset,
  • SAP skills: an asset.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

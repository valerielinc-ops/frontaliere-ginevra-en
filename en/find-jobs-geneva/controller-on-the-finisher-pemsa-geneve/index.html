Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team to position Governor on the Finisher

Your assignment

  • Ensure the precise and optimal adjustment of the finishing machines in order to ensure a uniform laying of the materials and in accordance with the technical requirements.
  • Regularly check the parameters of the equipment to adapt the machine to the construction site conditions and project specifications.
  • Strictly collaborate with the leaders and operational teams to ensure an efficient performance of asphalting.
  • Quickly identify and resolve any technical malfunctions when using the finishing machine.
  • Participate actively in preventive and corrective maintenance of the finishing machines to ensure its performance and durability.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have at least 2 years of professional experience in Switzerland in the regulation of finishing machines, demonstrating your mastery of technical requirements and local standards.
  • You have a valid driver's license, which is essential to move to the yards throughout Switzerland.
  • You are an expert regulator of finishing machines, able to intervene accurately on different configurations of machines and types of materials.
  • The M1KBMF driver's license would be an advantage.

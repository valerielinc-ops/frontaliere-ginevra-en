Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With close to 1,200 collaborators, the Department of Acute Medicine (DMA) includes services such as anaesthesiology, clinical pharmacology and toxicology, intensive care and emergencies. It responds to a quadruple mission through its services that bring their expertise in frequent interaction with all university hospitals in Geneva, thanks to a cross-sectional plateau, with the aim of ensuring the best possible care for patients. The ICU supports adult patients who present or are in immediate danger of developing vital organ failure as part of a pathology or its complications, whether medical or surgical, and whose life risk is generally involved. He also advises and intervenes when necessary in situations of resuscitation in other services of the HUG and participates in urgent hospital interventions.

Description of post

Your main mission is to accommodate the patient's families on weekends and holidays at the ICU. You accompany families with their relatives while ensuring the telephone standard during their presence. You also perform some secretarial work, sort and classify patient files for scanning.

Qualifications

You are a student in nursing, physiotherapy or medicine. You have the ability to manage the unexpected and stress situations while maintaining a good organization. While having ease in contacts, you can show empathy in difficult situations. You have a good ability to adapt and are a discreet, courteous, rigorous and precise person in the tasks entrusted to you. English is an asset.

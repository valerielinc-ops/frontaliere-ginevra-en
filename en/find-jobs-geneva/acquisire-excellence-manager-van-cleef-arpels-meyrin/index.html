Who are we?

A High Jewelry Maison with a strong heritage which promotes exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to hire talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

  • You have proven experience in digital transformation and purchasing excellence, acquired in an international and matrix environment.
  • You have solid knowledge of Source to Contract and Procure to Pay processes.
  • You are comfortable with digital purchasing tools and have concrete experience using AI to optimize processes, automate recurring activities and enrich analyses.
  • Your interpersonal and communication skills allow you to act as a true partner, to unite and engage various stakeholders around transformation projects.
  • You demonstrate initiative, responsibility, autonomy and a strong results orientation.
  • You master the analysis and exploitation of purchasing data as well as the construction of dashboards, particularly in Excel, Power BI and SAP
  • You have a perfect command of English.

If so, don’t hesitate to apply!

What do we expect from you?

Reporting to the Indirect Purchasing and Quality Director of the House, you will lead initiatives aimed at strengthening the performance and efficiency of the purchasing function through process excellence, support for digital transformation, data analysis, and performance management.

You are involved in all purchasing processes, from Source-to-Contract (S2C), from identification of needs to contracting, to Procure-to-Pay (P2P), from purchase request to payment. You work in close collaboration with the Purchasing teams as well as the Finance, Legal and IT functions, the P2P teams and the Richemont Group, in an international and matrix environment.

As part of your duties, your responsibilities are as follows

  • Operational Excellence: You manage the Excellence roadmap, implement purchasing and quality standards, optimize processes and lead continuous improvement projects for the team across all Indirect Purchasing categories.
  • Transformation: You identify opportunities for simplification, standardization, automation and optimization of purchasing and quality processes to deliver maximum value.
  • Digitalization: You promote the adoption and optimization of purchasing technologies, and identify AI opportunities for the team.
  • Compliance Operational Excellence & Risk Management: You ensure the application of the House Purchasing Policy, manage audits and monitor the associated action plans.
  • Transmission: You develop and deploy toolkits and training programs aligned with the Group's standards to support the improvement of team skills.
  • Analysis & Reporting: You keep purchasing data up to date, design and communicate activity control tables and ensure regular reporting of the team's KPIs (from S2P to P2P).
  • Community animation: You actively contribute to the animation of the Procurement Excellence community at the level of the Company and the Group.

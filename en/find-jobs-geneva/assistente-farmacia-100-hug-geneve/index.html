Full description

Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The aim of the pharmacy, which is linked to the Medical Directorate and the quality of the HUGs, is to provide reliable and responsive medicines, from pharmacy to health care units, by anticipating market risks, to manufacture tailor-made drugs in accordance with quality standards to improve safety of use, to offer expertise to health professionals and professionals and patients, to optimize drug management from prescription to administration and to be a reference and competence centre in training, teaching and research, integrating innovative methods.

Description of post

As a pharmacy assistant or assistant, you manage daily stocks of HUG care units. Your tasks are resupply and inventory review of care units, with or without automated cabinets, in consultation with healthcare teams and pharmaceutical logistics teams. You have to carry loads between 5 and 10 kg. You participate in tasks and projects of the pharmaceutical logistics unit. The post requires mobility throughout the canton of Geneva. The person recruited will be required to work on various sites, including the main site of Cluse-Roseraie and on the outside sites: Loëx, Joli-Mont, Belle-Idée, Trois-Chêne and Bellerive. You are recognized for your ability to work as a team, your relationship with caregivers, and your common sense of communication. You have the sense of organization and you know how to work independently in a multidisciplinary team. You bring great rigour to your work and you know how to manage priorities. You manage stress situations easily. You are comfortable with computer tools and have perfect command of French.

Qualifications

You hold the federal certificate of ability to assist in pharmacy. You have experience in inventory management and in tracking and processing orders. An experience in the field of institutional care (hospital, EMS, GSASA training...) would be an asset.

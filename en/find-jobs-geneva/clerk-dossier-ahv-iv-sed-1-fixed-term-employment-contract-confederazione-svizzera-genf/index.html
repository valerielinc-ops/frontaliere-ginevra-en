Are you looking for a varied job in the field of social security? With this position, you have the opportunity to participate in the mission of the ZAS by processing the data of the first pillar of persons insured abroad – We look forward to your application!

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20993
  • Place of work: Av. Edmond Vaucher 18, 1203 Genève

You can make this contribution

  • Identify insured persons on the basis of various criteria through consultation of electronic registers
  • Record paper correspondence and e-mails in the electronic document management system (GED)
  • Processing European forms from Swiss Equalisation Funds and liaison bodies (foreign social security funds)
  • collect data and make the necessary changes in the central register of insured persons and, where appropriate, create AHV numbers
  • forwarding requests which do not concern the CAS to the competent authorities
  • Process correspondence and e-mails mainly in French, Italian and German, but also in Spanish and Portuguese.

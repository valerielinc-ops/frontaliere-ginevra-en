Introduction For our Information Systems division, we are looking for a System Engineer (H/F) whose main mission will be to administer the systems on a daily basis, and to implement the monitoring and automation tools to ensure the stability and availability of the systems under management.   • Information security awareness
  • Very good customer orientation with sense of service
  • Methodical and rigorous, with good stress resistance
  • Proposition force, autonomous and likes to work as a team
  • Curious, passionate and strongly committed to developing your skills Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

Application Support & Adoption Specialist — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Pictet Asset Services provides custody, fund services and trading solutions for external asset managers, fund managers and institutional clients. We manage every aspect of the asset servicing value chain and our range of bespoke solutions include value-adding analytics, trading strategy support and digital front-end interfaces designed to provide an unrivalled customer experience.

You will join our Technology team for a six-month assignment, helping clients and internal users make effective use of our business applications. Working with product, business and technical teams, you will combine functional support, user training and continuous improvement.

Your role

  • Provide functional support, investigate user issues, assess their business impact and document them in Jira.
  • Coordinate incident follow-up with application owners and technical teams, keeping users informed through to resolution.
  • Assist users with access requests through established approval processes.
  • Deliver application training, maintain user documentation and support release communications and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
  • Analyse recurring issues and user feedback to recommend improvements to applications, support processes and monitoring.
  • Follow up on enhancement requests with Product Managers, who retain responsibility for prioritisation.
  • Maintain documentation and handover records to ensure service continuity.

Your profile

  • Experience in functional application support or incident coordination.
  • Ability to understand business applications, investigate issues methodically and explain functionality clearly.
  • Familiarity with Jira or a comparable ticketing tool.
  • Analytical skills and a practical approach to improving processes.
  • Organisational skills, clear communication and a service-oriented mindset.
  • French at C2 level and English at B2 level, both written and spoken.
  • Experience in user training, UAT or financial services would be an advantage.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland
  • Start date : asap

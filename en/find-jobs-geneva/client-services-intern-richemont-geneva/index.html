Who are we?

A High Jewelry Home with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allow our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

Start of internship : January 2027

Duration: 6 months - full time

Location: Geneva – Switzerland

Do you match the profile?

Do you have experience in international trade/marketing and an excellent sense of organization?

Do you like teamwork, are you curious and proactive?

Do you have project management skills and sensitivity to the retail world?

Do you have a real interest in the luxury sector and customer service?

Do you speak fluent English?

What do we want from you?

Within the Customer Service Department of the House, you will accompany the Customer Service Manager and the 3 Project Managers in their daily missions.

From content creation to needs collection to user data analysis, you will coordinate the various improvements with the Customer Service teams of the world regions (US, Asia, Europe, Japan, Middle East) and with the support of the project manager.

You will strengthen the customer experience, through the improvement of digital solutions in collaboration with the different teams in-house (customer, commercial, digital) and external (digital agency).

You will guarantee the planning and facilitation of meetings with the internal teams, the newsletter sent quarterly to promote the Customer Service team within the House. Finally, you will be in charge of promoting projects to the international community Service Client Van Cleef & Arpels.

In order to facilitate your integration, we will offer you a training course in the history and values of the House within the School of Joailliers Arts.

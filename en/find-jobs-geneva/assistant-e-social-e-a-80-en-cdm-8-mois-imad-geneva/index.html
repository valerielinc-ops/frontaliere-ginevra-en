Full description

The social worker works in the homes of IMAD clients and tenants of buildings with supervision for the elderly (hereinafter IEPA) mainly of AVS age and requiring social assistance and/or administrative support. He/she participates in a public service mission aimed at guaranteeing people access to the canton's usual social services. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Identify, upon request from home support teams or IEPAs, the nature of the client's or tenant's needs
  • Regulate the flow of requests and its activity with its hierarchy
  • Evaluate the social situation and the primary and secondary networks of the client/tenant
  • Develop a social intervention project within juridico-legal limits in collaboration with the client/tenant
  • Carry out actions resulting from the social intervention project
  • Document the actions carried out in the file of the client/tenant concerned
  • Direct the person and/or their caregivers to services adapted to their needs
  • Participate in conferences related to the situations monitored
  • Collaborate and coordinate with other professionals in the institution depending on the situation
  • Work in collaboration with the socio-health network
  • Bachelor in social work HETS or training deemed equivalent
  • Excellent knowledge of the Geneva socio-health network
  • Ability to work independently as well as in a multidisciplinary team AND mobility within the Canton
  • Analytical and negotiation skills
  • Very good writing skills
  • Very good ability to adapt to varied audiences
  • Experience in health issues related to aging desired
  • Comfort in relationships with older people
  • Administrative rigor and organizational qualities
  • Good command of standard IT tools.
  • Application deadline: 27.09.2026
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file: an up-to-date curriculum vitae, a cover letter, a copy of your diplomas and recognition of diplomas, your work certificates.

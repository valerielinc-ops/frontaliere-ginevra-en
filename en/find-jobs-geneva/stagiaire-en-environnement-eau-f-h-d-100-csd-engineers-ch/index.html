More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company.   • Your profile
  • EPF student in environmental science and technology or training deemed equivalent. Graduates cannot be detained.
  • Good knowledge of GIS tools (ArcGis)

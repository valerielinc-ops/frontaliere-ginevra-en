Your role

Participating in design thanks to BIM You will design, develop and update BIM digital models dedicated to electrical systems (strong current and weak current), ensuring the quality, reliability and consistency of integrated data. Integrate and structure technical data Enrich BIM models with technical information, electrical schematics and equipment characteristics in order to produce a complete and usable model from all stakeholders involved in the project. Locate and resolve technical conflicts You will analyze interfaces with other disciplines (plants, structure, hydraulics, architecture) in order to identify any interference and propose optimized solutions before the execution phase. Coordinating trade BIM As a real technical representative, you participate in BIM coordination meetings and cooperate closely with design teams, project managers and external partners to ensure the proper progress of projects. Draft technical documentation Starting with digital models, you will realize the executive drawings, patterns, material distancing and the necessary computations for monitoring and construction of the works, ensuring precision and reliability. Ensure project compliance Make sure the molded systems comply with Swiss standards, regulatory requirements and quality standards defined for each project And now we're talking about you? A specialized and recognized training You have a BIM certification and training in electricity, electrical engineering or an equivalent technical field. This dual competence allows you to master both the challenges of digital modeling and the technical constraints of electrical installations. A solid experience in the Swiss context You have matured at least 2 years of experience in Switzerland in BIM modeling applied to electrical projects. This experience allows you to master local practices, customer requirements and regulations in force. Perfect mastery of BIM instruments Use daily software such as Revit, AutoCAD and major BIM collaborative platforms. You are able to produce reliable models, structured and adapted to the needs of the different subjects involved in the project. Good knowledge of standards and standards You know the Swiss standards for electrical installations as well as BIM methodologies and standards. Ensure the application to ensure the quality, compliance and consistency of projects. A rigorous and quality-oriented profile Metodic, precise and organized, pay particular attention to details and reliability of data. You can manage multiple projects at the same time, respecting the deadlines and goals set. A true collaborative spirit Appreciate teamwork and communicate effectively with engineers, designers, project managers and external partners. Its sense of coordination and its ability to propose solutions contribute to the collective success of projects

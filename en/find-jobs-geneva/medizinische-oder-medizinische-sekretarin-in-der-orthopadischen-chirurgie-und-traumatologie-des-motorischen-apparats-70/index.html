Full description

Within the department's secretariat, it is your mission to organize and independently carry out the day-to-day work related to the work of the assistant doctors and clinic chiefs.

Thanks to your relational ease, you welcome, inform and direct patients and patients attending the orthopaedic surgery and trauma department of the motor device and manage the phone calls you receive.

You organize the consultations by managing the electronic physician agenda and preparing patient files. After the consultations, you ensure that medical reports and operational reports are struck independently and quickly. You are also involved in supporting the processing of service exit letters. If necessary, you schedule additional medical examinations. You are also responsible for the management and follow-up of insurance applications (DRM).

While responding to the various requests, you carry out administrative activities related to your function, such as patient records monitoring, mail and e-mail management, independently.

You are pro-active in carrying out logistical tasks arising from the life of a secretariat such as receiving, sorting, distributing and/or tracking paper mail and orders, scanning and archiving various documents.

Within the team, you develop a close relationship with your fellow medical secretaries, and ensure continuity of service during absences. More generally, your sense of service makes you an agile person ready to put your versatility at the service of the institution to practice in different services as needed.

Full description

Particularly recognized for its rapid diagnostic approach and for the quality of care, the internal medicine department has a central role within our establishment. Its mission is to receive patients without a diagnosis, often referred by the emergency department. In order to complete our service we are recruiting a: Hospital Assistant in Medicine 2 - CDM 6 months at 70% (ref: 1949) In a cutting-edge establishment you occupy a varied position within a multidisciplinary team including nurses, nursing assistants, doctors. You will have to ensure the reception of patients with multi-faceted pathologies, as part of comprehensive care in the acute phase. As such you are in charge of;

  • Welcome the patient and his family by making them actors in the care
  • Collect administrative data
  • Take care of the patient as a whole in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team and meet their basic needs (hygiene, pain, nutrition, comfort, etc.)
  • Ensure the supply of equipment, check their operation and ensure maintenance, apply cleaning and disinfection procedures
  • Participate in the proper functioning of the service, complete the computerized care file
  • Update its professional practices based on service or institutional care procedures We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:
  • Ideally a minimum of 2 years of experience in a hospital structure (compulsory)
  • Nursing Assistant diploma
  • Autonomous, caring, versatility, capacity for adaptation, organization and sense of priorities are essential qualities
  • Sense of contact and service in the context of a private establishment
  • Team spirit We offer you: Type of contract: CDM 6 months Date of entry into office: As soon as possible Rate: 70%

Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a Data Analyst whose main mission will be to transform data into reliable, accessible and intuitive indicators, in order to provide teams with concrete levers to effectively manage their performance on a daily basis.

Responsibilities

  • Transforming operational data into reliable indicators and decision support tools
  • Design and deploy reporting and visualization solutions adapted to industrial challenges
  • Ensuring data quality, consistency and traceability
  • Standardize reporting and disseminate data culture
  • Consolidate and analyse ERP, PLM, MES and industrial equipment data
  • Design, test and deploy dashboards
  • Produce actionable analyses and document management solutions and rules
  • Collaborate with trade and technical teams

Profile

  • Diploma of HES Engineer, EPF or equivalent university training, data oriented, information systems, decision-making computing or industrial engineering
  • Tableau, SQL and BI environments mastery; data preparation and analysis with Dataiku, Tableau Prep, Python, KNIME or equivalent
  • Knowledge of PLM data models, Windchill being an asset
  • Knowledge of statistics, predictive modelling and machine learning
  • Ability to translate business needs into indicators and reporting
  • Relationship, active listening, collaboration with trade and technical partners
  • Autonomy, rigor, organization and management of priorities
  • Team spirit, flexibility, curiosity, proactivity and continuous improvement orientation

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

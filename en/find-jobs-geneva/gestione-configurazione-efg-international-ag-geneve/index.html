General Info · Department: IT & Digital · Work time Percentage: 100% · Location: Geneva

Our Company

Our Purpose and Mission

Job Description

We are looking for an IT Configuration Manager to maintain reliable information about IT assets and services. You will work with IT colleagues and other stakeholders to improve data quality, provide information for reporting and audits, and contribute to effective IT service management.

Configuration management is the primary focus of the role. You will also provide backup for change management and contribute to incident resolution and root cause analysis when needed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate and complete configuration records throughout the lifecycle of IT assets, working with IT colleagues to document changes.
  • Review configuration data, resolve inconsistencies and identify opportunities to improve data quality.
  • Develop and maintain configuration management documentation and standards, and help IT colleagues understand and apply them.
  • Provide configuration information and reports to support internal and external audits and compliance requirements.
  • Collaborate with IT service management colleagues to ensure configuration information supports change, incident and problem management.
  • Provide backup for change management and contribute to incident resolution and root cause analysis when needed.
  • Track configuration data quality and prepare reports to inform stakeholders and support improvements.

Requirements and Qualifications

  • Education: No minimum or preferred formal educational qualification is specified.
  • Professional experience: At least six years’ experience in IT change and configuration management. Experience in banking or financial services is preferred.
  • Technical and functional knowledge: Strong understanding of IT service management practices, including ITIL; experience managing configuration data, improving data quality and producing reports; and the ability to coordinate with technical stakeholders. ITIL certification is a strong asset.
  • Languages: Fluent spoken and written French and English.

Our Values · Accountability: Taking ownership for tasks and challenges, as well as seeking continuous improvement · Hands-on: Being proactive to rapidly deliver high-quality results · Passionate: Being committed and striving for excellence · Solution-driven: Focusing on client outcomes and treating clients fairly with a risk-aware mindset · Partnership-oriented: Promoting collaboration and teamwork. Working together with an entrepreneurial spirit.

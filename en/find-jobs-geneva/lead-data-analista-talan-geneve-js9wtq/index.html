Full description

You will intervene throughout the life cycle of the data, including:

  • Analyze and document data from various existing sources and systems.
  • Design and evolve data models: conceptual, logical, physical and dimensional.
  • Participate in migration and data integration projects .
  • Design and implement data bases .
  • Conduct exploratory analyses: profiling, volumetrics, completeness, anomalies, consistency.
  • Write functional specifications of data streams: source/target mappings, transformation rules and boundary cases.
  • Collaborate with Data/Engineering teams on flow design and implementation.
  • Produce analyses, indicators and dashboards to meet business needs.
  • Contribute to data quality, governance, documentation and lineage.
  • Maintain and enrich the data catalogue.
  • Support users and help formalize needs.
  • 10 to 15 years of minimum experience in Data / BI / Data Analysis.
  • Expertise in Data Modelling .
  • Concrete experience in data migration .
  • Experience in designing or setting up Data platforms.
  • Excellent advanced SQL mastery: complex queries, multi-source joins, aggregations.
  • Controlling a BI / Data Visualization tool: Power BI or Qlik.
  • Good understanding of ETL/Data Warehouse architectures.
  • Knowledge of MDM concepts and data repositories .
  • Ability to draft functional specifications and formalize transformation rules.
  • Good understanding of Data Quality issues.
  • Excellent oral communication in French .
  • Ability to be autonomous and strength to propose.

Assessed competencies

  • Experiences with solutions from Data Virtualization, Data Catalog / Data Lineage, Data Quality.
  • Good statistical knowledge.
  • Experience in testing engineering.
  • Experience in training and support of users.

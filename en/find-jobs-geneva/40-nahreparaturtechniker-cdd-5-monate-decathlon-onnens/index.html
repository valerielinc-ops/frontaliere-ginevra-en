Mission At Decathlon, sport is at the heart of our daily lives, and every member of the team can invest and grow. Here, team spirit is essential: it is by playing together that we achieve our goals together. Join a company where it is good to live, and integrate a motivated and united team. Come and express your initiative, take responsibility and help us make sport accessible to all. Your playground: The Federal Workshop is responsible for repairing and repairing Decathlon customers' products for all Decathlon subsidiaries in Switzerland (e-commerce site, warehouse and 40th store). Created in 2022, the Federal Workshop consists of a team of 20 people working daily to develop the country's circular hub strategy. We are looking for a 40% specialist in sewing repairs. Your Missions:

  • Make sewing repairs on our textile products from our sports customers (clothes, accessories, etc.): hole recovery, zip replacement, adjustments, etc.
  • Support embroidery repairs: stitch recovery, imperfection correction, pattern restoration; and support orders related to embroidery.
  • Be in reinforcement of flocking controls if necessary.
  • Ensure careful and accurate work, guaranteeing the satisfaction of our clients.
  • Manage priorities and meet repair times.
  • Keep your workspace clean and organized. Profile Your Profile:
  • You are recognized for your manual skill and thoroughness.
  • You have a confirmed experience in sewing and ideally embroidery.
  • You are autonomous and able to work on a variety of tasks.
  • Your sense of detail is blameless.
  • You are rigorous and organized.
  • You are passionate about the sport, the company's DNA!
  • You speak fluently French and ideally English (German is a plus!) What we offer for your CDD: Even for a fixed period, you enjoy the benefits of the Decathlon family. (Small accuracy: the amounts below are valid for 100% and will logically be proratized according to your activity rate!).
  • Salary and bonuses: You are offered a fixed salary of between CHF 4!600 and CHF 4!750 per month depending on your years of sewing experience. To this is added a monthly bonus (up to 10% of your gross salary) directly linked to the store's collective perfs from the 4th month of contract.
  • Passion Sport: We support your practice! Benefit from 25% discount on your Decathlon purchases after 1 month of seniority.

Full description

Mission context

As part of the evolution of a Data platform within a banking environment, we are looking for a Data Engineer experienced with Databricks and Microsoft Fabric .

The mission mainly consists of extending the functional coverage of an existing Data platform by integrating new datasets according to already defined patterns and standards.

As the architecture and framework are already in place, the objective is not to redefine the solution, but to deploy new data flows while adhering to the existing models, quality rules and CI/CD processes .

Main responsibilities

  • Integrate new datasets into the existing Data platform.
  • Configure the new flows according to established patterns and standards.
  • Develop and adapt processing with PySpark and SQL .
  • Correctly define the modeling of the new data: dimensions, facts, granularity, business keys, SCD1/SCD2, snapshots.
  • Configure the datasets using a declarative approach, notably via TOML .
  • Validate the data according to the existing quality rules and contracts.
  • Analyze and correct anomalies encountered during the integration of new sources.
  • Deploy the changes through Azure DevOps CI/CD pipelines .
  • Diagnose and resolve failures related to quality gates and the various promotion stages.
  • Understand the source banking data from a functional perspective to ensure its proper integration into the platform.

Data Engineering

  • Strong experience with Databricks
  • Proficiency in Delta Lake
  • Good knowledge of Unity Catalog
  • Knowledge of Change Data Feed
  • Very good proficiency in PySpark
  • Very good proficiency in SQL

Data Modeling

Ability to determine for each new dataset

  • dimension vs fact;
  • SCD Type 1 vs SCD Type 2;
  • snapshot logic ;
  • granularity ;
  • natural business keys ;
  • transformation and validation rules.

CI/CD & Dev Ops

  • Good command of Azure DevOps
  • Experience with existing CI/CD pipelines
  • Understanding of gate mechanisms and branch-based promotion
  • Ability to diagnose deployment errors
  • Exposure to Databricks Asset Bundles and/or Terraform is an advantage.

Banking knowledge

Experience in the banking sector, ideally in Private Banking / Wealth Management, is essential.

Good understanding of data such as

  • positions;
  • transactions;
  • Fund for Management (AuM);
  • Net New Fund (NNA);
  • - The "Gals" centre;
  • client hierarchies.

The ability to understand data from a Core Banking System from a functional perspective is particularly important.

Preferred skills

  • Experience with TOML or declarative/config-driven data frameworks
  • Experience with Auto CDC patterns
  • SCD1 / SCD2
  • Franz Kafka
  • Spark Declaration Pipelines
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Fabric
  • Experience with dimensional modeling
  • Knowledge of the Kimball methodology
  • Terraform

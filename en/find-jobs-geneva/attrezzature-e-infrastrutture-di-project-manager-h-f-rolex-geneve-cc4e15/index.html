Introduction For our Industrialization Logistics department, we are looking for a Project Manager (H/F) whose main tasks will be the industrialization of equipment and logistics infrastructure for the Production and Commercial divisions. Responsibilities

  • Analyze and frame projects: collect and consolidate input data, formulate needs and define objectives accurately
  • Propose innovative solutions: develop concepts tailored to the objectives, integrating strategic orientations and advanced technologies focused on sustainable development
  • Draft specifications: formalize technical and functional requirements in clear and structured reference documents
  • Lead the coordination of the project team: lead regular meetings, synchronize the actions of the team members and ensure that the work progresses well
  • Managing planning and budgets: developing planning, monitoring, budgeting and ensuring compliance throughout the project
  • Ensuring communication and reporting: regularly inform stakeholders, line managers and relevant teams of the progress of projects
  • Foster relationships with industry partners: organize and coordinate meetings with partners to foster collaboration and achieve common goals. Profile
  • Diploma of engineer HES, EPF or equivalent in logistics, mechanics or microtechnics
  • Confirmed experience in project management
  • Experience in providing automated industrial equipment (carrying, vision, special machine...)
  • Experience in supply chain and/or industrial logistics, an asset
  • Environmental training or sustainable development, a more
  • Strong organizational capacity and autonomy
  • Relationship, listening, synthesis and effective communication
  • Dynamism, flexibility and teamwork
  • Knowledge of English and/or German, an asset Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

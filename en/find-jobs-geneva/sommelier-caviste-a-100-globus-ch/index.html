Full description

It is with great passion and love of detail that our employees make GLOBUS the first address of the Swiss Luxury and Premium. They are people like you who make the difference and who, with us, give meaning to "the place we love to be". That's what you're doing.

  • As a host/sitting, you ensure that our customers always feel welcome and in good hands.
  • Thanks to your excellent service, your passion for wine and personalized advice, you guarantee unique shopping experiences.
  • You support the visual merchandising (including promotions) and the organization of tastings, which awaken the inspiration of our customers as well as the desire to buy our exclusive products.
  • You ensure strict compliance with internal and legal hygiene guidelines and requirements (HACCP).
  • Thanks to your positive aura and your passion for our wines and spirits, you make a decisive contribution to the company's success.

WHAT DISTINCE YOU

  • You are a service oriented person and passionate about quality food products.
  • You like to take on the role of host and live it fully, your approach is resolutely focused on our customers.
  • You have successfully completed a professional apprenticeship in retail or gastronomy and you already have professional experience in the field of wines and spirits.
  • You have an irreproachable know-how and a neat appearance.
  • You have excellent knowledge of the French and English languages; any other language constituting a major asset to serve our international clientele.

BENEFITS FOR YOU With more than 1,700 other retail enthusiasts, you are an integral part of our fascinating high-end and luxury world. You are recognized and valued, you work with an exciting and motivated team and we give you our confidence so that you can enjoy free time and make a difference with your know-how. If you're an expert in your profession and you're putting your heart into it, many doors will open to develop your career. We will gladly support you in your internal and external trainings.

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • an exceptional working environment with a horizontal hierarchy in the city centre.
  • attractive social benefits .
  • flexible work models and at least 5 weeks of vacation (PS: You can enjoy more free time thanks to the option "unpaid leave" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on Globus universe products for you and your family.
  • And much more...

Your contact We look forward to receiving your application online. It's very simple. Instead of a cover letter, we look forward to your answers to our three questions - please use the link provided in the announcement to this effect.

We are at your disposal.

Sustainability Manager - 6 months contract — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Pictet Wealth Management combines more than 200 years of Swiss banking heritage with global investment expertise. The partner-owned financial services group provides a comprehensive offering for wealthy individuals and families, including discretionary and advisory investment solutions and family office services.

We are seeking a Sustainability Manager, joining the Sustainability Team which supports the advancement of Pictet’s sustainability strategy across the private bank. You will report directly to the Sustainability Lead and work day to day with PWM’s investment platform and sustainability colleagues across the Group.

Your role

  • Support the delivery of PWM’s Sustainability Ambition 2030 by translating strategic priorities into concrete initiatives and business outcomes.
  • Coordinate the development, enhancement and roll-out of responsible investing solutions across locations, in line with evolving regulatory requirements.
  • Support the continued strengthening of PWM’s responsible investing framework, methodologies, policies and governance.
  • Help shape and develop PWM’s impact investing mandate, including the underlying data, reporting capabilities and operational groundwork required to support this investment solution effectively.
  • Lead and monitor key sustainability-related initiatives across products, ESG data, reporting, active ownership topics and tool/process improvements.
  • Act as a key interface across the Investment Platform and with functions including Legal, Compliance, Risk, Technology, PAM and Group Sustainability.
  • Support the promotion and positioning of PWM’s responsible investing offering to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Act as a subject-matter expert on responsible investing topics, providing guidance, training and support to bankers, investment specialists and, where relevant, clients.
  • Prepare high-quality internal and external materials, including management updates, presentations, training content, client materials and business development support.

Your profile

  • Strong understanding of investments and financial services; wealth management and/or asset management experience preferred.
  • Strong interest in sustainability and responsible investing; prior experience in sustainable finance, ESG or stewardship is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of ESG data, sustainability metrics, reporting and their practical use in investment processes and product management.
  • Strong execution and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities independently.
  • Hands-on, structured and solution-oriented, with the ability to move effectively between strategy and delivery.
  • Excellent communication, writing and presentation skills in French and English
  • German and/or Spanish would be a plus.

Starting date : 1 February 2027

We will not accept any CVs via agencies

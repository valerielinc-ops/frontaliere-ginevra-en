Full description

Introduction

For our Communication & Image division, we are seeking an English Content Writer and Editor (M/F) . The primary mission of this role is to create, adapt and optimize high-quality English-language content in line with the brand’s editorial guidelines. You will ensure linguistic excellence – including grammar, spelling, style, tone and readability – across all communication materials.

Responsabilités

  • Create, adapt and refine high-quality English-language content in line with the brand’s editorial guidelines
  • Ensure linguistic quality, including grammar, spelling, style, tone and readability
  • Write and edit press releases, brochures, exhibition content, digital-platform content and advertising campaigns
  • Coordinate text-production processes involving freelance writers, agencies and internal reviewers
  • Conduct collaborative reviews and cross-checks with relevant stakeholders and ensure that approval feedback is duly incorporated
  • Collaborate with internal teams, including Sponsorship and Graphic Design, as well as external partners, including agencies
  • Manage production timelines and oversee several editorial projects simultaneously
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of content processes and strategy.

Profil

  • Native English speaker preferred; exceptional command of written English required. A very good command of written and spoken French would be beneficial in this role
  • Master’s degree in English, communications, journalism or a related field
  • Proven experience in writing, editing or communications for a high-profile international brand (5–10 years)
  • Good knowledge of culture, environmental conservation and sport, together with an affinity for the world of watchmaking, would be an asset
  • Creativity, curiosity and adaptability, with the ability to produce engaging copy tailored to different audiences and markets
  • Excellent writing discipline, attention to detail and critical thinking, ensuring accurate, reliable and error-free content
  • Ability to work independently, strong organizational skills and a collaborative mindset to manage deadlines, priorities and team feedback
  • Excellent communication and active-listening skills, with the ability to understand the needs of multiple stakeholders
  • Intellectual curiosity, creativity, cultural awareness and a commitment to continuous learning
  • Reliability, autonomy, resilience under pressure and the ability to receive feedback constructively.

Conclusion

We offer a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and a comprehensive benefits package.

Please submit a complete application, including your CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas.

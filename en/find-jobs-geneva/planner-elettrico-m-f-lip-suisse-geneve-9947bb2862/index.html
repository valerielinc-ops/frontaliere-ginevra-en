Description of post Your LIP SUISSE SA agency is looking for one of its customers, a recognized company in the field of electrical installations based in Geneva, we are looking for an electrician planner in a fixed position to be filled as early as September.

Your missions

Project preparation

  • Review building plans to be built or converted
  • Discuss plans with the authorized person, explain possible solutions, advise her in the choice of systems and devices
  • Define the characteristics of the installations and their exact design according to safety rules

Project planning

  • Establish distribution plans for different systems, e.g. lighting, telephone, security and alarm devices
  • Determine pipe and cable length, location and number of sockets and switches
  • Complete detailed drawings of electrical installation assembly, wiring and connection with design software
  • See catalogues or websites of equipment suppliers
  • Write offers with description of services
  • Check invoices and statements

Project supervision

  • Monitor and monitor progress and installation of facilities during the various stages
  • Ensure that the project is carried out in accordance with existing plans, specifications and rules
  • Participate in sessions with representatives of different trades and coordinate work
  • Test and commission facilities
  • Educate users on the operation of installations
  • Prepare final documents with all important information, such as instructions for use and safety instructions
  • Discuss plans with the authorized person, explain possible solutions, advise her in the choice of systems and devices
  • Define the characteristics of the installations and their exact design according to safety rules

Your profile

  • Electrician Planner CFC or equivalent training.
  • Confirmed experience of at least 2 years in a similar position in Switzerland.
  • Good knowledge of Swiss electrical standards (NIBT).
  • Mastery of technical drawing software (AutoCAD, Revit or equivalent).
  • Team spirit, rigour and organizational sense.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.

Introduction

Within the CERN Engineering department (EN), the Cooling and Ventilation ( EN-CV ) group is responsible for the design, the building, the operation, and the maintenance of the cooling and ventilation plants for large energy physics accelerators, experiments, and technical facilities.

The EN-CV group pursues sustainable cooling and ventilation systems enhancing energy saving and recovery.

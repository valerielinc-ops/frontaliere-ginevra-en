Full description

Are you looking for an opportunity to contribute to the Future Circular Collider (FCC), one of the most ambitious scientific projects ever envisioned? Help develop the coating technology that will equip its vacuum chambers with a Non-Evaporable Getter thin film. Working alongside experienced engineers, technicians and physicists, you will contribute to the design, fabrication and coating. You will be involved at every stage of the process. We will provide all the necessary training and support, making this an excellent opportunity to develop specialist skills in a unique international environment.

Your responsibilities

  • Design & construction of mechanical parts for customised physical vapor deposition systems and associated tooling.
  • Assembly of coating setups operating in Ultra High Vacuum.
  • Validation of the coating technology and process (in real scale prototypes up to 12 meter long beam pipes).
  • Validation of the vacuum performance of the coatings produced (in Ultra High Vacuum systems).

Your profile

  • Experience in machining metal parts in lathe and milling machines. CNC would be an advantage.
  • Familiarity in using CAD software to design mechanical parts.
  • Practical experience in assembling mechanical equipment.
  • Experience in vacuum technology would be an advantage.

Skills

  • Working in teams.
  • Curious and proactive.
  • Knowledge of vacuum technology would be an advantage.
  • Knowledge on welding would be an advantage.
  • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the basics of the other language.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • By the application deadline, you have a maximum of two years of professional experience since graduation in Mechanical Engineering (or a related field) and your highest educational qualification is a general secondary education diploma .
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
  • Applicants with a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD degree are not eligible.

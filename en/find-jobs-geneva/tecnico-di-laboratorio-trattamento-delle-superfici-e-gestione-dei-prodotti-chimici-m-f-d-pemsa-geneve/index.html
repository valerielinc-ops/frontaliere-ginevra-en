Your assignment

  • Check the chemical composition of the bathrooms and perform the necessary analyses in accordance with quality standards and customer specifications
  • Identify, analyze, monitor and make the necessary corrections to the treatment bathrooms
  • Ensure monitoring of production samples and associated controls
  • Perform and monitor internal or external tests and analyses
  • Create, maintain and update the technical data sheets of the bathrooms and laboratory analysis methods
  • Ensure monitoring of laboratory equipment and their calibration in collaboration with suppliers
  • Provide technical support for production in case of process issues
  • Actively participate in the control and improvement of chemical processes and respect for the quality system
  • Manage chemicals: regulations, security, stock levels, supplies and physical and computer movements
  • Ensure monitoring of certificates of analysis and conformity
  • Coordinate special waste management and maintain relations with competent suppliers and authorities
  • Oversee the operation and monitoring of industrial water treatment plants
  • As part of the PERCOS function, participate in the application of safety and health protection standards, audits, accident analysis and employee awareness

And now we're talking about you?

  • CFC diploma of chemical technician, training in the treatment of surfaces or title considered equivalent.
  • Experience gained in the fields of surface treatment, industrial chemistry or chemical processes.
  • Good mastery of monitoring, analysis and correction of chemical baths
  • Knowledge of industrial laboratory methods and procedures
  • Experience in sample monitoring, testing, analysis and production controls
  • Good knowledge of the quality requirements applicable to industrial processes; NADCAP experience is a real advantage
  • Knowledge of chemical management: storage, safety, regulations, traceability and supply
  • Experience or good knowledge in special waste management
  • Knowledge of industrial water treatment plants and principles
  • Explained sensitivity to issues related to occupational safety and health protection
  • Experience or knowledge of the PERCOS system is an advantage
  • Good mastery of common IT tools and an ERP system
  • Great mastery of French, both written and spoken
  • Good knowledge of technical and professional English (ideally level C1)
  • Autonomous, rigorous and organized personality, able to manage multiple activities simultaneously
  • Good analysis and ability to intervene effectively in support of production
  • Sense of responsibility, commitment and perseverance
  • Good relational skills and ease in relations with internal teams, suppliers and external interlocutors
  • Team spirit, flexibility and ability to adapt

Your assignment

  • Welding work, clinging, installation of heating systems

  • Connection of heat pumps and geothermal probes
  • TIG welding on steel and stainless steel in position
  • Laying of heated floors
  • Functioning of the system, execution of the necessary adjustments (pressure, temperature, run...)

And now we're talking about you?

  • CFC Diploma of installation of heating systems or equivalent training
  • Minimum 2 year experience in Switzerland
  • Iron and stainless steel pipe laying mastery.
  • Ability to read and interpret assembly drawings.

Full description

To allow family caregivers a period of rest, the ARUs offer AVS-age clients short stays and provide services in continuity with those provided at home. The care and support of clients adapts to their physical and mental health. The ASSC works in an interdisciplinarity on the Villereuse site (1207 GE), respecting the mission and objectives of the institution and may be required to intervene on an ad hoc basis also on the site of Mervelet (Petit-Saconnex) or Les Jumelles (Thônex). As the UATR is a 24-hour service throughout the year, working hours are in 3x8 hours. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Provide basic care and medical procedures, delegated by the referent nurse, in a comprehensive client approach
  • Promote the restoration of the client's health, strengthening or maintaining his or her autonomy to enable him or her to restore, continue or adapt his or her lifestyle so that he or she can stay or return home
  • Evaluate and re-evaluate practical aid situations and take the lead
  • Accomplish practical assistance in accordance with the customer's lifestyle and environment
  • Promote and collaborate with the community by identifying resources and providing information and support.
  • Federal certificate of ASSC capacity and authorisation of practice in the Canton of Geneva
  • Meaning of communication and relationship with others, listening quality
  • Ability to assess situations and act responsibly
  • Resistance to stress and physical work
  • Ability to work in autonomy and interprofessionality within a multidisciplinary team
  • Essential mobility (on foot / bike / TPG / car).

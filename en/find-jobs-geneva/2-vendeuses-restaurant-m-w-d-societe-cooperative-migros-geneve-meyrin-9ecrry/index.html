Full description

You like customer contact and days that don't look alike? Join our restaurant in Meyrin and become the face of our welcome. Between cashing, preparing products and highlighting buffets, you play a key role in the customer experience. Every smile counts, every gesture makes a difference and every service is an opportunity to make our know-how shine.

Luogo di lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Genève, Restaurant Migros Meyrin, Avenue de Feuillasse 24, 1217 Meyrin

Important remarks

The use of women aims for simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You're doing the cash flow with rigour and fluidity. You accompany clients when they purchase and bring quick solutions when a situation requires your intervention.
  • You welcome every client with attention and good humor. You listen to her needs, advise her on our products and create a pleasant moment that makes you want to come back to our restaurant.
  • You prepare salty and sweet products according to the defined standards. You ensure quality, freshness and presentation in order to offer a gourmet experience to every customer.
  • You set up the buffets and value the products throughout the day. You quickly spot the missing ones and act without delay to keep an attractive and complete offer.
  • You take part in the maintenance of the restaurant and equipment. You apply hygiene and safety rules seriously to ensure a clean and welcoming environment.
  • You work closely with the team and share useful information. You also bring your ideas to improve service, expand sales and satisfy customers more.

Required

  • 1 year experience in a similar function: in restoration.
  • You make every exchange a positive experience. You listen, you advise with accuracy and you adapt your approach according to expectations. You easily create a lasting relationship of trust.
  • You handle several tasks in parallel without losing quality. You plan your work, keep an overview and respect priorities even during the most intense periods.
  • You move easily from mission to mission. You react quickly to unforeseen events, find simple solutions and keep your efficiency throughout the service.
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

Reference code: JR134751 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

YOU ARE

Passionate and enthusiastic young talent curious to participate in the day-to-day of Regional Marketing projects across all our products categories: Jewellery, Watches, Accessories.

YOUR ROLE

Based in Geneva, you will work for the European region, in close contact with the 7 European markets (France, United Kingdom, Benelux & Nordics, Northern Europe, Switzerland, South-East Europe, Iberia).

Under the supervision of the Regional Product Marketing team, you will be a key support for the development of the Marketing plan of the region.

You will be in charge of coordinating various projects in contact with the local Marketing teams, assessing the business performance, nourishing the product knowledge and monitoring the competition environment.

KEY RESPONSABILITIES

Performance monitoring for HQ, Regional & Markets’ teams

  • Performance monitoring dashboards (Excel / Looker): optimization of existing tools and creation of new ones
  • Monthly / weekly dashboards updates
  • Insights & learnings delivery for official meetings
  • Ad-hoc analysis according to needs or projects (Business Plan, Annual & Semestrial Review, etc.)
  • undefined

Product tools orchestration for the Marketing community within the region

  • Product News monthly monitoring
  • Product News videos planning & coordination from agency brief to final diffusion
  • Competition intelligence: sharing competition news
  • Launch planning updates
  • Launch Newsletters updates for key launches

Support in the organization of community animation & internal communication

  • Support in the animation of the European product community through the organization of Marketing seminars (approx. 2/category/year)
  • Product training rendezvous for Marketing and Learning & Development teams
  • Novelties workshops
  • Internal communication platform feed ownership

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

  • 1st experience of minimum 6 months in Marketing or Data Analysis
  • Fluent in English & French – both written & spoken
  • Confident in the use of PowerPoint & Excel, Looker & Analysis are a plus
  • Organizational, time management & analytical skills
  • Curious, proactive and able to work with tight deadlines
  • Previous experience in luxury industry is a plus

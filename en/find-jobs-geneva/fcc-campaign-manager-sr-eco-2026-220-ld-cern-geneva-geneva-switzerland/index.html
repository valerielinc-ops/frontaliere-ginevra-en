Full description

Introduction CERN is preparing the Future Circular Collider (FCC) project for potential approval by the CERN Council in 2028/29. The importance and the size of the FCC project require a communication campaign from a local to an international scale, engaging CERN’s neighbouring population, the wider public, the broader scientific community, and decision makers. CERN is therefore seeking an experienced senior professional to lead the FCC communication campaign, implement a communication and engagement strategy, and coordinate the creation of content and its delivery to the various stakeholders. The role will comprise strategic thinking, strong leadership as well as excellent delivery skills. Functions

  • Be responsible for implementing a comprehensive communication and engagement strategy.
  • Lead campaign planning and execution, manage messaging across channels, and ensure clear, consistent, and impactful communications for diverse audiences.
  • Coordinate content and product development, working closely with the FCC Project Office and technical teams and, interfaces with external stakeholders, as well as with writers, multimedia producers and the media and digital team.
  • Develop key messages, narratives and content for internal and external audiences.
  • Manage relationships with senior project leaders and diverse stakeholders.
  • Anticipate and manage reputational risks and issues.
  • Support local and public events related to the FCC project.
  • Monitor, evaluate, and report on campaign performance.
  • Manage potential contracts with external companies, lead and support communication staff and consultants as required.

Master's degree or PhD or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Communication or Marketing or a related field.

Experience

  • Proven senior-level leadership experience in large-scale communications or public engagement campaigns.
  • Experience working in complex, high-profile projects or multi-stakeholder and scientific environments.
  • Demonstrated experience in managing projects effectively and delivering results under pressure.
  • Experience across digital, media, and stakeholder communications.

Technical competencies

  • Strategic communications planning, including the implementation of a vision.
  • Project management.
  • Science communications.
  • Stakeholder relationship management and community engagement.
  • Participatory processes and community dialogue techniques.

Behavioural competencies

  • Demonstrating Accountability: making appropriate use of the Organization's resources.
  • Creating Vision and Strategic Partnerships: demonstrating respect, diplomacy and tact with external partners.
  • Communicating Effectively: delivering presentations in a structured and clear way; adjusting style and content to the audience; responding calmly and confidently to questions.
  • Achieving Results: delivering high quality work on time and fulfilling expectations.
  • Working in Teams: understanding when teamwork is required to achieve the best results; including others accordingly and sharing information.

Language skills

Excellent spoken and written English with a good working knowledge of French.

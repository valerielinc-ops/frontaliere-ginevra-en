Head of mandate, works manager maintenance railway 60-100% CFF - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of mandate, works manager, maintenance railway Geneva, 01.12.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Are you passionate about railway infrastructure and want to join a dynamic team at the heart of rail maintenance projects? Would you be interested in the role of conductor coordinating the technical needs of railway maintenance sites, safety requirements, operational measures and the conduct of work? This post is for you! What you can move.

  • Take responsibility for mandates and ensure implementation/execution of multidisciplinary maintenance mandates involving small multidisciplinary missions.
  • Provide guidance and coordination to local technical teams to ensure the quality of interventions.
  • Collaborate with internal and external partners for complex projects.
  • Ensure the conduct of work and financial monitoring of mandates.

What you bring.

  • Significant experience in the railway field, ideally on railway projects.
  • Knowledge of existing standards and regulations and preferably confirmed safety management training TEN 20100.
  • Good team spirit and ability to work in the field, in collaboration with local teams.
  • Good project management and technical problem solving skills.
  • Driver's licence category B.
  • You master French (level C1).

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the G level and is eligible for a Level 2 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .

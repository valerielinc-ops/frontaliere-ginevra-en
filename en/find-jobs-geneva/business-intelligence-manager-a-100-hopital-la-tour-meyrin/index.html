Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Founded in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, La Tour Hospital places continuous improvement, medical excellence and the support of its staff at the heart of its priorities. The institution employs more than 1,200 employees, representing more than 60 professions, and collaborates with more than 550 registered doctors.

We recruit one

100% Business Intelligence Manager (ref: 2029)

Do you want to combine BI expertise, proximity to trades and strategic impact in a changing environment?

Created six years ago, the Business Intelligence function of the La Tour Hospital now supports more than 100 users using more than 100 dashboards, powered by a dozen source systems.

The function is entering a new phase of its development: migration of the data platform, strengthening of external data, predictive analyses and creation of a portal for doctors.

Attached to the CFO, you are the institution's preferred interlocutor on BI topics. You ensure the continuity of the unit while structuring its evolution and strengthening the place of data in decision-making processes.

Your role

At the interface between strategy, technology and trades, you pilot a high impact BI activity and turn the data into concrete levers of decision support.

IB piloting and development

  • Collect the needs of the trades and develop new Power BI dashboards related to the Hospital's strategy.
  • Develop data sources with the DSIO and contribute to the platform's migration.
  • Contribute to the development of predictive analyses and the physician portal.
  • Strengthen the structure by recruiting an assistant and a trainee.

Analysis and decision support

  • Conduct ad hoc and strategic analyses, including on billing, patient flows and staffing efficiency.
  • Present the analyses to the directorates and contribute to the management of the institution.
  • Automation of statistical processing and production, including OFS and REKOLE.
  • Intervention in various fields: finance, billing, human resources, stationary and outpatient care, pharmacy, medical coding, procurement, quality and medico-technical.

Governance and data quality

  • Manage access rights and data security: groups, roles and filters by perimeter.
  • Ensure the maintenance of dashboards, quality checks and necessary corrections.
  • Assist users in the interpretation of data and respond to daily requests for services.
  • Set up a ticketing system to structure and prioritize requests.

Why join this project?

  • Take responsibility for a BI function already firmly established in the institution.
  • Pilot a new transformation phase of the data platform.
  • Work in direct contact with Directorate-General, Management Control, DSIO and service managers.
  • Contribute to decisions that have a concrete impact on medical, administrative and operational activities.
  • Develop a team and structure the BI activity on a sustainable basis.
  • Join a private, independent and human-centred institution.

Your profile

  • Master's or Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Management Computer Science, Statistics, Economics or equivalent.
  • Minimum five years experience in business intelligence or data analysis. Experience in the health sector is an asset.
  • Mastery of SQL and a dashboarding tool such as Power BI, Qlik, Looker or Tableau.
  • Mastery of R or Python and good mastery of Excel.
  • Ability to understand business issues and to engage with non-expert interlocutors.
  • Rigor, autonomy, sense of priorities and discretion.
  • Excellent mastery of French.

Want to grow the IB with us?

Join La Tour Hospital and help transform the data into useful, reliable and valuable decisions for the entire institution.

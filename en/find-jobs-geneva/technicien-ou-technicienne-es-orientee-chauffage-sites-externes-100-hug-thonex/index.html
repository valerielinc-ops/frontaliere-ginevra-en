Description of post

The scope of your activities includes three hospital sites as well as several consultation centres throughout Geneva. You independently perform all preventive and corrective maintenance tasks as well as installation work requested by your hierarchy. You monitor and monitor construction sites and works by ensuring compliance with applicable time limits, standards and directives. You regularly return your interventions to your hierarchy and report any malfunctions, damage or technical anomalies. You are responsible for updating the technical documentation and for maintaining and cleaning the tools, premises and equipment available to you. As required, you also supervise maintenance and troubleshooting related to your business line. As a contact person, you coordinate, control and accompany mandated external companies. You are involved in the management of the stock and in the preparation of material requests. Curious or curious and motivated or motivated by the evolution of the techniques and technologies in your field, you also appreciate sharing your know-how and collaborating with your colleagues. Sensitive to environmental issues and energy savings, you integrate these concerns into all your activities. You work on a regular schedule and participate, according to a planned tournus, in the picket service of your area. In case of emergency you are also willing or willing to intervene outside the usual hours, including night, weekends and holidays.

Qualifications

You have a technical or technical degree ES in the field concerned or a title deemed equivalent, supplemented by a professional experience of at least 5 years in maintenance and troubleshooting of CVS installations. You have strong knowledge of the various hydraulic couplings and circuits as well as in the field of regulation. You have control over Johnson Controls and Sauter Building Control (user level) control systems. You have very good knowledge of industrial thermal installations, including overheated water and steam, and are aware or aware of the risks associated with their operation. You are the holder of the ASIT certificate of boiler conductor or operator or are willing or willing to take this training in order to obtain certification within two years. General knowledge of building trades (carpentry, locks, etc.) is an asset to provide technical picket services in autonomy. You are domiciled or domiciled less than 35 minutes from your place of work and must have a driving licence category B. An advantage would be a connection authorisation (NIBT) allowing intervention on electrically connected equipment. You master the usual office tools, including Microsoft Office, as well as the use of GMAO software. Equipped or equipped with an excellent sense of service, you appreciate teamwork and show openness to change, allowing you to integrate quickly and easily into your professional environment. You have perfect command of French, both oral and written, and you express yourself in a clear and concise way. In case of necessity, you may be prompted or required to intervene on all HUG sites to ensure specific missions. Thanks to your calm, discreet and laid-back personality, you easily establish a relationship of trust with your interlocutors. You have a strong spirit of collaboration and are sensitive to the constraints of the hospital environment and to the patient's presence.

Full description

As a Junior Financial Analyst, you will join the Financial Planning and Control team in the FAP Department and contribute to the Organisation's financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and reporting activities. Working closely with experienced financial analysts and internal stakeholders, you will support the preparation and analysis of financial information, help ensure reliable budget data, and contribute to the continuous improvement of financial processes and tools.

Your responsibilities

  • Support the preparation and monitoring of annual budgets, forecasts and medium-term financial plans.
  • Contribute to the preparation of official financial documents for the Organisation's governing bodies.
  • Contribute to finance-related projects and initiatives.

Your profile

  • Initial experience in financial planning, budgeting, controlling, reporting or financial analysis.
  • Experience in using financial, ERP, or administrative software packages.

Skills

  • Good working knowledge of Excel and other MS Office tools.
  • Familiarity with Power BI would be an asset.
  • Good analytical and numerical skills.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • Accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Curious and proactive approach.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • By the application deadline, you have a maximum of two years of professional experience since graduation in Finance, Business Administration (or a related field) and your highest educational qualification is either a Bachelor's or Master's degree .
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
  • Applicants without University degree are not eligible.
  • Applicants with a PhD are not eligible.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.

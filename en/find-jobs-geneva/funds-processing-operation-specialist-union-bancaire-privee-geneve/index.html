Full description

  • Mission
  • Ensure the accurate and timely booking of fund transactions across Mutual Funds, Hedge Funds, and Private Equity Funds, while maintaining a strong focus on client satisfaction and service quality.
  • Proactively follow up on ongoing trades, resolve operational issues efficiently, and investigate and clear breaks identified through daily cash and stock reconciliation reports.
  • Act as a reliable point of contact for clients and internal stakeholders by providing timely updates, clear communication, and effective solutions.
  • Anticipate potential issues, minimize operational risk, and ensure that all client needs are addressed accurately and promptly.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of operational processes by identifying inefficiencies, proposing innovative solutions, and bringing forward new ideas to enhance service quality, efficiency, and client experience.
  • Encourage a proactive and collaborative approach to improving existing workflows and implementing best practices.
  • Main responsibilities
  • Manage and accurately book fund transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, and switch orders, across Mutual Funds, Hedge Funds, and Private Equity Funds.
  • Manage the booking of Equalization Factor and crystallization operations, ensuring accurate calculations and timely processing.
  • Monitor and follow up on pending trades with custodians and fund administrators, particularly for alternative funds, to ensure transactions are completed and booked within the required deadlines.
  • Perform daily cash and stock reconciliations based on reconciliation reports, investigate discrepancies, and resolve outstanding breaks promptly.
  • Identify potential operational risks, escalate issues in a timely manner, and coordinate with internal teams, custodians, fund administrators, and other stakeholders to ensure effective resolution.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of operational processes by identifying inefficiencies and proposing new ideas to enhance accuracy, efficiency, and client service.
  • Your Profile
  • Languages

French and English: fluent (written and spoken).

  • Education and Technical Skills:

Bachelor’s degree in Economics (or equivalent). Proficient in Microsoft Office tools (Excel, Word, Outlook, VBA a plus). Familiarity with fund lifecycle workflows is an asset

Personal Skills

  • Strong sense of ownership, with the ability to follow tasks through to completion.
  • Clear and professional communicator, able to interact effectively with custodians, fund administrators, and internal teams.
  • Flexible and adaptable, with the ability to adjust to changing priorities, manage multiple tasks, and respond effectively to operational requirements.
  • A strong team player, with a collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively with colleagues and internal and external stakeholders.
  • Sound understanding of operational risks, with disciplined controls and timely escalation of issues.

Full description

Mission context As part of the maintenance and evolution of its collaborative platforms, our client is looking for a Senior SharePoint Consultant / Microsoft 365. You will intervene on SharePoint On-Premises and Microsoft 365 environments, with a central role in administration, Level 3 support, complex incident resolution and accompanying the progressive transformation to Microsoft cloud solutions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Administer and maintain SharePoint On-Premises and SharePoint Online environments in operational conditions.
  • Provide L3 support and handle complex incidents.
  • Ensure the availability, stability and performance of collaborative platforms.
  • Administer the main Microsoft 365 associated services.
  • Automate administration and operating tasks with PowerShell.
  • Participate in identity and access management with Microsoft Entra ID, including authentication, authorization, Conditional Access, App Registrations and Microsoft Graph API.
  • Contribute to Microsoft 365 security, compliance and governance issues.
  • Participate in ITSM processes: Incident, Problem and Change Management .
  • Identify recurring incidents and implement corrective actions and automation.
  • Participate in SharePoint On-Premises migration projects to SharePoint Online .
  • Produce and maintain technical documentation and ensure knowledge transfer.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, security, trades and projects teams in an Agile/Scrum environment.

Essential skills

  • 7 years minimum experience on SharePoint and Microsoft 365 environments.
  • Expertise in administration and maintenance of SharePoint On-Premises .
  • Very good control of SharePoint Online and Microsoft 365.
  • Experience confirmed in technical support L3 .
  • Excellent mastery of PowerShell for administration and automation.
  • Good Mastery of Microsoft Entra ID / Azure AD:
  • authentication and authorization;
  • Conditional Access;
  • Apps Registrations;
  • Microsoft Graph API.
  • Good understanding of Microsoft SQL Server , Active Directory and network concepts.
  • Controlling Microsoft 365 security, compliance and governance issues.
  • Knowledge of ITSM processes, including Incident, Problem and Change Management.
  • Experience in an Agile/Scrum environment.

Assessed competencies

  • Migration SharePoint On-Premises to SharePoint Online .
  • Migration tools ShareGate and SharePoint Migration Tool (SPMT) .
  • Power Platform: Power Apps, Power Automation and Power BI.
  • SharePoint development with SPFx, TypeScript and React .
  • Monitoring and reporting tools such as Prometheus, SPDockit or AdminDroid .
  • Microsoft Certifications related to SharePoint / Microsoft 365 .

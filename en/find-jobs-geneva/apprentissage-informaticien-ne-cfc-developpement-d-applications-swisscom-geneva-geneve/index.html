# Learning: Informaticien*ne CFC Development of applications

He tuo futuro inizia who

In the development of applications, you realize software solutions for products, processes or services and you are responsible for implementing functional solutions according to customer requirements. You're ready,

  • You have a close exchange with your clients to inform them at an early stage of the evolution of the demands of their ICT solution or the system context.

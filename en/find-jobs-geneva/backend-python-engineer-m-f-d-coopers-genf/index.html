Role overview

For our client in Geneva, we are looking for a Backend Python Engineer to join an international scientific environment and contribute to the development and modernisation of large-scale information platforms.

In this role, you will work on backend services, data pipelines and search technologies that support scientific discovery and open access for researchers worldwide.

As part of the Tools and Services team, you will help evolve platforms that manage scientific publications, research data and digital archives.

  • Hands-on experience with Python frameworks such as Django, Flask and FastAPI
  • Solid experience designing and developing REST APIs

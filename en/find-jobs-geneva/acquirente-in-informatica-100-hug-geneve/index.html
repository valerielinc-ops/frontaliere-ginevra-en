Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. Acting on behalf of the University Hospitals of the cantons of Vaud and Geneva, the Biomedical Procurement and Engineering Centre (CAIB) ensures the purchase of goods and services of all kinds to ensure the optimum functioning of hospitals under the best conditions of the market, in accordance with the rules and laws governing our institutions. In this context, it coordinates all purchases and plans the acquisition and renewal of heavy equipment. The CAIB has partnered with various regional hospitals and is located in Suisse Romande.

Description of post

As a buyer or buyer, you centralize the various needs emanating from the hospital network and conduct the appropriate procurement processes in accordance with public procurement rules. You are the guarantor or guarantor of the development and deployment of the procurement strategy of the IT category from the hospital network. You are responsible for the management and control of the supplier panel of your category. In this capacity you conduct tenders, collaborating with your internal customers and intervene from the definition of need, until the contract with the supplier. You participate in the facilitation of the CAIB-affiliated hospital network by sharing your purchasing expertise and knowledge of market trends.

Qualifications

You have a Master's degree in HES or equivalent, preferably with a recognized Master's in Purchase. You have 5 minimum years experience confirmed in purchases with a first confirmed experience in computer purchases. You have a perfect command of the French language (read, spoken and written) and a very good knowledge of English (level C1). Also required are excellent mastery and knowledge of computer tools.

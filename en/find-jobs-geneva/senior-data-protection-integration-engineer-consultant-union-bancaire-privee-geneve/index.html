Full description

Mission The role focuses on protecting the Bank’s personal and sensitive data. It involves designing tokenization, anonymization, and data protection solutions. These solutions ensure data availability, integrity, security, and compliance.

Main responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain integrations around RegData Protection Suite.
  • Develop interfaces enabling applications to use tokenized data.
  • Design and maintain REST API, SQL Server, and IBM MQ Series proxies.
  • Develop integration and data-transformation components and backend services using Java and Quarkus.
  • Contribute to the definition of technical architectures and integration standards.
  • Analyze consuming applications’ requirements and ensure data-flow quality.
  • Ensure the security, traceability, performance, and resilience of data exchanges.
  • Automate deployments, configurations, and operations using PowerShell, Ansible, and Azure DevOps.
  • Implement unit, integration, and regression testing.
  • Troubleshoot incidents and solve complex issues in distributed environments.
  • Collaborate with architecture, security, infrastructure, database, middleware, and development teams.
  • Produce technical documentation and support knowledge sharing.

Your Profile

  • Senior IT professional with more than 10 years of experience in development, systems integration, or middleware, including 7 years in the banking sector.
  • Strong command of Java, Quarkus, REST APIs, SQL Server, IBM MQ, PowerShell, Ansible, and Azure DevOps.
  • Autonomous, pragmatic, rigorous, and able to manage assignments end to end in a critical environment.
  • Demonstrate analytical thinking, strong communication skills
  • High level of awareness of security, confidentiality, and compliance.
  • Hands-on, integration- and development-oriented.
  • Fluent in French and English.

Others Swiss Residence

Core Competencies : Adherence to the company’s values: Dedication, Conviction, Agility and Responsibility - Compliance with regulations and internal directives

Your Purpose

As an Employee Engagement Intern, you will play a vital role in the Employee Engagement Team in supporting MSC Cruises' Employee Engagement strategy and Internal Communications initiatives. Your Impact

  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to execute relevant and impactful Employee Engagement and Internal Communications projects.
  • Promote internal communications best practices globally, working with both onboard and ashore colleagues to strengthen engagement.

  • Support in organizing events for employees.

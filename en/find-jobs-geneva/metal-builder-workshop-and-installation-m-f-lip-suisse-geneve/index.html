Full description

Description of post Your LIP SWISSE SA agency is looking for an autonomous metal manufacturer for one of its customers. You intervene both in the workshop and on the construction sites for the manufacture, assembly and installation of metal works.

Your responsibilities

  • Manufacture and assembly of metal construction elements in workshop.
  • Reading and interpreting technical plans.
  • Flow, drilling, welding and mounting of the various structures.
  • Place on site guardrails, stairs, doors, windows, metal structures and other structures.
  • Adjustment and finishing work.
  • Compliance with existing quality and safety standards.
  • Collaboration with construction teams and the technical office.

Your profile

  • Metal manufacturer CFC or equivalent training.
  • Experience confirmed in Switzerland in metal construction.
  • Mastery of workshop and installation work.
  • Good reading of technical plans.
  • Welding knowledge (steel, stainless steel and aluminium, an asset).
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of responsibility.
  • Driving licence required.

Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated service provider for the elderly and has about 4000 employees. Ambulatory and stationary offers merge perfectly. While Senevita sites are synonymous with autonomous shelter and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading private service provider of help and home care throughout Switzerland. To strengthen our care team at the Senevita Casa Geneva site, we are looking for one/one:

60-80% Community Health and Care Assistant

Your tasks

  • You relieve and support our customers in their daily lives and in the domestic economy
  • You provide basic and medical care according to the individual needs of our clients
  • You accompany our clients to the doctor, for shopping, walks

Your profile

  • You have an ASSC training course
  • Several years of professional experience in long-term care or home care are an advantage.
  • You are stress resistant, flexible and used to working independently
  • You live in the Geneva region
  • You have a driver's license
  • Knowledge of German is an asset

Our offer

  • A varied activity according to your availability
  • The possibility of working in a very autonomous way and of exchanging with an experienced team
  • The extensive continuing training programme of the Senevita Academy, which can be followed free of charge
  • Various discounts (gasoline, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)

We look forward to receiving your application online. Do you have any questions? Do not hesitate to contact us. Catherine Béguin Head of Care Chemin du Château-Bloch 1219 Le Lignon Tel. +41 22 340 40 95

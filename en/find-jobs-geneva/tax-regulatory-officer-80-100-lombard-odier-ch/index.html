Tax Regulatory Officer (80-100%)

Within the legal department, and in particular the Tax Regulatory team in charge of QI, FATCA and CRS, you will be responsible for carrying out cross-checks at the Group level in the areas of QI, FATCA and CRS. You will have to work with our foreign offices.

YOUR ROLE

  • Through different controls, ensure the quality and consistency of customer data in the Bank's systems in relation to regulatory taxation;
  • Collaborate with the Customer Life Management and Technology & Operations teams in the preparation, review and production of FATCA and CRS regulatory reporting;
  • Provide support for FATCA and CRS customer notifications;
  • Support and participate in the annual information gathering exercise from Relationship Managers;
  • Prepare files for communication with AFC related to FATCA consolidated applications
  • Maintain regular monitoring with our foreign offices to ensure consistency of controls within the Group;
  • Wants to develop new knowledge so that, in the long run, it can support the Front Office in relation to IQ, FATCA and CRS issues.

YOUR PROFILE

  • 3-5 years of professional experience in a regulatory tax environment with a specialization in FATCA and CRS subjects, ideally acquired in a private bank or consulting firm accompanying financial institutions;
  • University degree (or equivalent) in banking, finance, or similar field;
  • Excellent capacity for analysis, coordination and monitoring;
  • Ability to perform multiple tasks at a time, to work independently and to adapt to priorities;
  • Great rigor, sense of detail and strong organizational skills;
  • Dynamic and positive with a very good ability to work as a team, interest and ease of contact;
  • Excellent mastery of French and English; any other language is an asset;
  • Home in Switzerland or willingness to relocate to Switzerland.

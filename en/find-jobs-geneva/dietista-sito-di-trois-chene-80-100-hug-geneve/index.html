Full description

You ensure the nutritional care of patients hospitalized in geriatric acute care and geriatric rehabilitation, for the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics, within the Hospital of the Three Oaks. As a dietitian or dietitian, your mission is to enable patients to maintain, restore or improve their nutritional status, functional abilities and quality of life in accordance with ethical and ethical principles.

  • You apply Evidence Based Practice (EBP) and perform your care in accordance with the nutritional care process.
  • You are responsible for a rigorous and regular documentation of patient records.
  • You actively participate in interdisciplinary seminars on rehabilitation and acute care and collaborate closely with the entire interprofessional team. You are contributing to the continuous improvement of professional practices and the quality of care, especially in the field of protein-energy malnutrition in the geriatric context, and are a force of proposal. You ensure, if necessary, the replacement of your colleagues in the other care units of the Three Oak Hospital. You participate in the training of students.

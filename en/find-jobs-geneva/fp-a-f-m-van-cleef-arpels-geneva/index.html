At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself. At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to hire talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence. Based in Geneva ARE YOU A GOOD MATCH? You have a strong and relevant financial background. You act as a business partner with excellent communication skills. You have a team player attitude, and you like to work with different kinds of teams. Proactive and very agile, you like to propose in a challenging and innovative environment. You are structured, rigorous, problem-solving oriented and have good analytical skills. You have good IT knowledge (Excel and PowerPoint, BI). You speak French & English fluently WHAT ARE OUR EXPECTATIONS? The Senior Operations Controller is a strategic finance partner supporting the Operations departments. He/she drives financial planning, reporting, and performance analysis across operations, while supporting transversal topics such as costing, margin analysis, CAPEX and digital transformation. He/She will also have the mission of establishing, developing and leading the new Customer Service Controlling activity. He/She will report to the Operations Performance Controlling Senior Manager. In this function, you will: Business Partner Operations

  • Act as the financial business partner to Operations departments (Stones, Purchasing, Innovation, Logistics, etc.)
  • Produce and analyze consolidated reporting for Operations departments
  • Ensure reliability and ownership of financial data
  • Deliver insightful analysis on performance, trends, and risks Operations & Métiers Reporting
  • Consolidate all financial information related to Operations & Métiers department
  • Ensure data quality, participate in data collection process improvement
  • Participate in the elaboration of a "control tower" view of the Operations & Métiers department Controlling leadership for Customer Service
  • Establish, develop, and lead the controlling function for the Customer Service division, which is currently non-existent in close collaboration with our Retail Customer Service department
  • Define and implement comprehensive financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and reporting processes specifically tailored for Customer Service.
  • Provide in-depth financial analysis of Customer Service performance, identifying trends, risks, and opportunities to support strategic decision-making.
  • Define and monitor KPIs for Customer Service, ensuring the achievement of financial objectives. B&O and Purchases Controlling
  • Establish, develop and lead the controlling of the O&M Real Estate cost by ensuring a full understanding of our costs, making sure benchmarks and best practices are clear for stakeholders
  • Be the single-entry point of contact for Indirect Purchases, both for VCA and Group Controlling Digital & Reporting Transformation
  • Drive reporting improvements and automation initiatives
  • Participate in deployment of reporting tools (e.g., Looker, dashboards)
  • Anticipate business needs and propose innovative solutions MORE THAN A JOB...We recruit for a career!

Who is Sonar?

Sonar enables organizations to ship agentic code with confidence. Code creation is abundant, but confidence is scarce. Our core platform, SonarQube, verifies over 750 billion lines of code every day, delivering agentic verification and governance for all code. Trusted by more than 7 million developers and 75% of the Fortune 100, including teams at Nvidia, ServiceNow, Booking.com , Goldman Sachs, AstraZeneca, and Ford Motor Company, Sonar enables organizations to reduce outages, improve security, and lower the cost and risk of agentic software development.

Founded in Geneva and now headquartered in Austin, Texas, Sonar has a global team working across offices and hubs, including Silicon Valley, London, Bochum, Barcelona, Singapore, Tokyo, and the Washington, D.C. area. At Sonar, we’re enabling enterprises to build agentic software factories that can move quickly while maintaining the control, consistency and evidence required to ship with confidence.

Position description

Identity is a critical dependency for how Sonar works. Our people need reliable access to the right systems, while the business needs confidence that access is appropriate, traceable, and removed when it is no longer required.

As a Staff Identity and Access Management (IAM) Engineer in our Systems Engineering squad, you will design, build, and operate the identity services that connect our workforce, enterprise applications, and infrastructure. You will combine deep technical expertise with hands-on engineering to make identity secure, reliable, and straightforward to use.

You will shape the technical direction of workforce IAM, turn complex access requirements into maintainable engineering solutions, and lead initiatives from design through production operation. Your scope includes employee and external-worker identity lifecycles, authentication and federation, access governance, privileged access, and the non-human identities supporting our internal services and automation.

This is a technical leadership role without direct people-management responsibilities. You will work across IT Operations, Information Security, Business Technology, People Operations, and engineering teams to establish clear ownership, manage dependencies, and deliver improvements that remain effective as Sonar grows.

What you will do

  • Identity architecture and technical direction: Assess our identity landscape, identify material risks and technical debt, and develop a prioritized IAM engineering roadmap with squad leadership and Information Security..

• Identity lifecycle engineering:. Connect authoritative workforce data to identity platforms and downstream applications, with reliable identity matching, reconciliation, expiry, and exception handling. End to end verification of deprovisioning outcomes • Authentication and federation: Engineer and operate SSO, MFA, and application provisioning integrations using SAML, OpenID Connect, OAuth, and SCIM. Advance phishing-resistant authentication and secure enrollment, recovery, and session-management practices. Manage federation certificates and other expiring dependencies before they disrupt access. • Access governance: Own practical role- and attribute-based access models, approval workflows, access reviews, and time-bound exceptions. Make entitlements understandable and auditable, with named owners and appropriate separation of duties. Introduce next-gen automation to improve speed and accuracy of oversight. • Automation and integration: Develop production-quality code, API integrations, and infrastructure-as-code to reduce manual administration. Build in testing, peer review, least-privileged execution, safe retries, reconciliation, and actionable failure reporting. Use AI-assisted engineering where useful, with human review and controls appropriate to the sensitivity of identity changes. • Non-human identity: Partner with service owners to improve the inventory, ownership, permissions, credential rotation, and retirement of service accounts, application identities, and automation credentials. • Reliability and operational ownership: Participate in the squad’s incident and escalation arrangements, diagnose complex failures, and deliver durable corrective actions. • Security and auditability: Partner with Information Security and control owners to implement agreed controls, provide reliable evidence, and close findings through engineering improvements. Connect identity telemetry to security monitoring while protecting sensitive identity data. • Technical leadership and enablement: Review designs and code, mentor engineers, and help application owners adopt supported identity patterns. Equip operational teams with documentation and tooling so routine requests do not depend on individual experts.

Experience and qualifications

  • Substantial hands-on experience engineering and operating enterprise IAM services, with demonstrated impact: leading ambiguous, cross-team initiatives, making sound architectural trade-offs, and improving the effectiveness of other engineers.

• Deep experience with a modern workforce identity platform such as JumpCloud, Okta, or Microsoft Entra ID. JumpCloud experience is valuable; equivalent platform expertise and the ability to learn are equally relevant. • Strong understanding of authentication, authorization, federation, directories, and provisioning, including SAML 2.0, OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, SCIM, and WebAuthn/FIDO2. • Proven delivery of automated identity lifecycle processes, including workforce-data integration, account correlation, access changes, and verified deprovisioning across multiple applications. • Experience with cloud IAM and federated access, particularly AWS IAM and IAM Identity Center, including permission boundaries, temporary credentials, and least-privilege access patterns. • Strong programming and automation skills in Python, PowerShell, Go, TypeScript, or a comparable language, together with REST APIs, Git-based delivery, automated testing, and infrastructure-as-code. • Practical experience with access governance, privileged access, secrets management, and producing evidence that demonstrates controls operate effectively. • Experience operating business-critical services: observability, incident response, root-cause analysis, safe change management, and recovery testing.

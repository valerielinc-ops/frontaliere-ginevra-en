Role overview

The Mobile Social Emergencies Unit (UMUS), composed of nurses and social workers, intervenes in situations mainly related to violence and precariousness in coordination with the Geneva social health network.

The team works in pairs and operates from 17:00 to 08:00 on weekdays and 24 hours on weekends and public holidays.

Evaluate the nature of the request from a professional in the Geneva social and health network and respond as soon as possible or direct the situation towards a more suitable service

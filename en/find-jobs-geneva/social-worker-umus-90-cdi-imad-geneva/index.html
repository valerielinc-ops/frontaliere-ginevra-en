Full description

The mobile social emergency unit (UMUS), made up of nurses and social workers, intervenes in situations mainly linked to violence and precariousness in coordination with the Geneva socio-health network. The team works in pairs and operates from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. on weekdays and 24 hours a day, weekends and public holidays.

  • Evaluate the nature of the request from a professional in the Geneva socio-health network and respond as quickly as possible or direct the situation to a more suitable service
  • Intervene and carry out jointly with the nurse, a socio-health assessment by bringing their social expertise into this analysis and developing short-term intervention projects, while ensuring the juridico-legal limits in interventions
  • Propose to the client and those around them ways to reduce the intensity of the crisis or emergency felt; offer them listening and support, aiming to immediately restore their balance and that of those around them
  • Carry out a written assessment of each intervention by accurately reporting the evaluation, the services offered as well as the chosen direction
  • Evaluate the circuit and the relevance of the management of each intervention
  • Participate in conferences and team supervisions, outside of intervention hours
  • Intervene actively in sessions with partners.
  • Bachelor in social work or title deemed equivalent
  • Ability to manage crisis situations
  • Comfort in mediation and aptitude for appropriate positioning when dealing with various interlocutors
  • Very good knowledge of the Geneva socio-health network
  • Experience in the areas of exclusion, precariousness, care of minors or mental health would be an asset.
  • Application deadline: 10/18/2026
  • Entry into office: as soon as possible
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an up-to-date curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.

Full description

Recruitment

  • Pilot recruitment from end to end. Clarify needs with service managers, define selection criteria, draft and disseminate announcements, pre-selection applications, conduct interviews and accompany the commitment decision. Follow-up of applications and co-ordinate the transfer of relays to HR teams responsible for taking up office.
  • Review and harmonize the recruitment process. Analyze practices and timelines with HR teams and services, clarify validation roles and steps. Adapt the steps in SmartRecruiters and support the teams in their implementation.
  • Design and develop trade tests. Identify with field teams the competencies to be assessed, create job placements representative of positions and develop common assessment grids. Test these tools on pilot recruitments and adjust them from feedback.
  • Advise service managers and monitor the quality of recruitment. Support them in the conduct of interviews and the evaluation of applications, ensure fairness of processing and confidentiality of data, and monitor the deadlines and returns of candidates and teams.
  • Train and support managers in the use of the recruitment tool, setting and updating the career page of the PPE website.
  • Contribute to the attractiveness of PPE as an employer and to the development of recruitment policy.

HR Council

  • Provide expertise and support to Service Branches, executives and collaborators on all human resources issues.
  • Ensure quality and monitoring of HR processes.
  • Ensure that managerial processes comply with company legislation and regulations.
  • Establish a close functional link with the human resources manager working with him/her to enable the exchange of information necessary for the proper management of administrative recruitment actions.
  • Participate in various HR projects and HR process development (to be a proposal strength).
  • Conduct exit interviews and draft work certificates.

Qualifications

  • Be eligible for a Federal Human Resources Patent or equivalent training.
  • Confirmed experience of at least 5 years in an HR position, with strong HR recruitment and/or advice, including end-to-end recruitment and executive advice.
  • Have already contributed to the revision of an HR process and know how to develop practices with interlocutors from different trades.
  • Mastering structured interviews and translating the requirements of a position into observable evaluation criteria. Experience in designing job tests or situations is an asset.
  • Good knowledge of administrative personnel management.
  • Knowledge of Labour Law (LTR), Law B 5 05 (LPAC), Code of Obligations and Wage Mechanisms of the State of Geneva; an in-depth knowledge of the framework of the Geneva public sector personnel is an asset.
  • Mastering common office tools and ease with recruitment tools, ideally SmartRecruitersTM (recruitment software).
  • Be able to manage multiple recruitments in parallel, conduct exchanges with teams and make reasoned recommendations.
  • Exercise rigor, discernment and editorial convenience.
  • Be customer-oriented, with understanding and organizational sense.
  • Have good communication and listening skills.
  • Be pragmatic, flexible and have a sense of priority.
  • Demonstrate dynamism in understanding what is expected and in carrying out the tasks.
  • Have a strong interest in the mission of integrating PPE with people with disabilities or with difficulties of integration.

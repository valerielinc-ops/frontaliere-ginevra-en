Full description

Get in charge! Manages with passion and innovation one of our Dairy Products spaces. Give our customers a unique experience in our Vibert store: quality, freshness and proximity. Your curiosity and your sense of detail will make a difference. Join us to meet this challenge!

Luogo di lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Genève, MM Vibert, Avenue Vibert 29, 1227 Carouge

Important remarks

The use of women aims at simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You receive and check the arrivals to ensure the quality and freshness of the products.
  • You ensure that products are placed and rotated for optimal presentation.
  • You analyze the performance of the rays and propose strategies for improvement.
  • You manage orders, inventory and inventory to maintain a smooth supply.
  • You ensure cleanliness and comply with HACCP standards and safety standards.
  • You maintain an excellent knowledge of the products, including their origin, their instructions for use and their peculiarities.

Requirements

  • 3 years minimum experience: in the retail sector
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail trade or equivalent title.
  • Rigor, dynamism, versatility, autonomy and strong desire to work as a team.
  • Excellent mastery and interpretation of the numbers to improve the performance of the rays.
  • Expertise of the field, strong proposal and excellent customer orientation.
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and free of charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Djaina Julliard Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we take an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for the continuation of the selection process. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Personal interview video or telephone: In the case of a large number of candidates, personal video or telephone interviews are optional. This allows us to get to know more candidates in a short time in order to invite the people concerned for the next round. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100%

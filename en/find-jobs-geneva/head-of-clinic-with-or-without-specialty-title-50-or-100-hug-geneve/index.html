Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1'300 employees, the Department of Psychiatry is responsible for a three-fold mission in the field of psychological treatment, training and research. Thus, the department's mission is to effectively detect and treat increasingly frequent and polymorphic pathologies with respect for patients and their families. The Liaison and Crisis Response Psychiatry Service is responsible for the evaluation, guidance and monitoring of interdisciplinary treatment of psychiatric disorders in patients who are hospitalized or monitored during outpatient consultations at different HUG sites. The service also has a mandate to welcome, care for and provide guidance to patients addressing HUG emergencies. The Service offers a stimulating and diverse clinical environment, at the heart of the hospital, fostering interactions with many medical and surgical specialty services. It also offers a quality trainer framework, with structured internal training including clinical presentations and other interactive teaching formats. The development of our employees in their mission is an important value for us with a personalized career path and several opportunities for evolution within the Institution.

Description of post

You are part of an intra-hospital care unit in the Crisis Response Beds (CIL) of the Liaison and Crisis Response Psychiatry Service. The mission of this unit is to provide crisis hospital care for patients with suicidal ideas or behaviours. The ICLs offer a brief, specialized and evidence-based intervention (mentalization-based therapies, good clinical practice for borderline personality disorder). As head or head of clinic, you are actively involved in the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic follow-up of hospitalized patients and patients, while coordinating their care with network partners. You play a key role in the support of internal doctors, the management of patient flows and interprofessional dynamics, notably through the conduct of medical-infirm interviews. Regular clinical support is also provided through supervision by an assistant doctor.

Qualifications

You have a federal doctor's degree or training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO). If you are in receipt of a speciality title, you are in possession of the cantonal right of practice or are able to obtain it before you take office. For non-French speakers, the level of French C1 on the spoken and written test is a requirement. The FMH title in psychiatry and psychotherapy and/or experience in the field of psychiatry and in particular in emergency and crisis psychiatry are assets.

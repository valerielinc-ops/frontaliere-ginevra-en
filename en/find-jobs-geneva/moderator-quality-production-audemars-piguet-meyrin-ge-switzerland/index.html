Full description

Your Mission

Integrated into the Quality Manufacturing team of the Meyrin site and directly attached to the Quality Production Manager, your role will be to ensure product compliance and contribute to the continuous improvement of production processes in a demanding watchmaking environment.

Your responsibilities

  • Identify, document and address non-compliance
  • Open and track non-compliance sheets
  • Participate in root cause analyses and in the implementation of corrective and preventive actions (Ishikawa, QRQC, 8D, etc.)
  • Monitoring and analysing quality indicators (starts, updates, compliance rates)
  • Train and sensitize employees on quality requirements
  • Conduct post audits and participate in process validations
  • Ensure the application of quality standards in workshops
  • Contribute to continuous improvement projects to reduce non-compliance and non-quality costs
  • Technical CFC, quality or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience confirmed from 3 to 5 years minimum in the watch industry, ideally in the field of dressing
  • Good knowledge of high-end requirements, aesthetic criteria and surface treatments, decoration and assembly processes
  • Mastery of plan reading, GPS ratings and conventional means of control
  • Mastery of quality tools (8D, AMDEC, SPC, QRQC, 5M) and ease with computer tools, including an ERP type M3
  • Ability to analyze quality issues, identify root causes and manage improvement actions
  • Rigor, sense of observation, spirit of analysis and ability to work in collaboration with production teams
  • Reactivity, pragmatism and orientation results

What we offer

  • Insurance coverage - disease (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities as well as subscriptions encouraging soft mobility (e.g. SBB)
  • A business restaurant
  • An LPP with favourable rates

Description of post

You are integrated or integrated into the biomedical service and equipment of the HUG, within the Sector of Biomedical Engineering (SIB), and are supervised or supervised by excellent and excellent professionals. This internship is part of the major project to build the new children's hospital to be delivered in 2035. You will assist the biomedical engineer in charge of this project, in collaboration with technical experts, users and suppliers. You contribute to the technological monitoring process aimed at identifying new and/or innovative technical solutions, their benefits and related constraints. The work and main subject of the course concerns the updating of a complete hospital equipment plan according to latest plans and numbering of premises, the updating of technical reservations and constraints of implantation of materials (according to the standards and technological watch), as well as the analysis of the expected related loads (related to the installation of the equipment their connectivity and maintenance). In addition, an analysis and updating of the current HUG paediatric equipment stock by units and categories (valorization, histogram, maintenance) will allow for a projection of investment and transfer costs according to the end-of-project scenarios and identification of issues. You are involved in the preparation of a preliminary bypass for the radiological imaging project (local maps and plans; CT MRI imaging RX, anaesthesia, flow). You also have the opportunity to get involved concretely in several projects at different levels of advancement or coordination.

You know how to work as a team and know how to manage projects in the organisation of technical platforms and in the field of medical devices (and related regulations). In addition to your spirit of collaboration, you are autonomous and discreet or discreet and ensure a fluid flow of information.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. Composed of eight medical services, its mission is to provide diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The primary mission of the Laboratory Medicine Service is to take care of liquid samples of all kinds, such as blood, urine, cerebrospinal fluid and wound smears, and to submit them to numerous and varied analyses that offer quality results as soon as possible. The results guide medical decisions and therapeutic choices. They are obtained using the latest robotic, semi-robotized or manual technologies and the most efficient methods to date. The service also has research and teaching missions.

Description of post

The position concerns a specialized hemostase activity, but this sector is part of the laboratory of Hematology and Hemostase and within this framework an activity in Hematology is also possible according to the constraints of the laboratory. As part of your activities, you receive biological samples of adult and paediatric haemostasis, and register requests for examinations according to the procedures in place. You are using the routine and specialized automatic hemostase Sysmex CN-6000 and CN-6500, GeneXpert Cepheid, BioFlash Ruwag, Chrono-Log and Apact 4004. You technically and biologically validate the routine and specialized hemostase results. You participate in the courses and meetings of the service. You participate in quality assurance at the laboratory: passage of IQCs and IQCs, testing for method verification files. You perform weekend guards and pickets, holidays and nights, depending on the constraints of the laboratory.

Qualifications

You are in possession of the Biomedical Analysis Technician (BAT) degree or an equivalent qualification. You can justify very good knowledge of haematology and haemostasis. Professional experience in the fields of haematology (cytology, automata) and haemostasis (routine and specialized) are assets. You feel comfortable with computer tools. You have good stress resistance, you have organization and you know how to manage your priorities. Your rigour, sense of responsibility and autonomy allow you to perform the tasks entrusted to you in an optimal and efficient manner. Your relationship ease allows you to work easily as a team and integrate.

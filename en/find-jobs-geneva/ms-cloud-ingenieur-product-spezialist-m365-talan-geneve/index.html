Full description

In our consulting and engineering teams, you support a client in its Cloud migration. This is a hybrid role between technology and Product Management. Your mission: to define directions, organize governance and secure the transition to M365 tools, integrating regulatory constraints. Missions:

  • Governance M365: Design, implement and automate governance M365. Set schedule and organize access to applications (Planner, Viva, Teams).
  • Security & Risks: Framework data leak protection (DLP), internal collaboration and conditional access. Participate in legal assessments to validate compliance of data storage in the Cloud.
  • Technical migration: Supervise the migration of mailboxes to Exchange Online, revision of mail traffic, archiving, retention and backup.
  • Collaborative Space: Deploy SharePoint Online and OneDrive for teams.
  • Product Management: Transmit the vision, bring together technical and business teams, and ensure communication with external stakeholders. We are looking for a profile capable of popularizing the technique, questioning the existing one and conducting planning. Mandatory skills:
  • Experience: 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role (MS Cloud Engineer, Consultant M365, Architect).
  • Technique: Modern Workplace Environment Management (Teams, Viva, Planner, Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive).
  • Product Management: Practice in roadmap management, planning, team coordination and external communication.
  • Leadership: Ability to unite interlocutors and lead change.
  • Risks: Knowledge of Compliance and Data Leak Protection (DLP).
  • English and French current.

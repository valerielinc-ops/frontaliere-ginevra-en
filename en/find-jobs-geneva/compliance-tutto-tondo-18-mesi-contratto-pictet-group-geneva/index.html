Full description

Your team

The Corporate Functions division brings together the Group's non-banking activities and has over 900 employees. The core functions are carried out by specialists who are fully familiar with all business lines and are divided into various departments: Communication, Compliance, Finance, Human Resources, Information Security, Internal Audit, Legal, Corporate Office, Risks and Taxation.

Would you like to discover the different professions of the Bank's Compliance Advisory & Controls teams?

We offer you a rewarding experience. You will be accompanied by professionals who will share their know-how and skills, and you will have the opportunity to develop your knowledge and understand the different regulations related to compliance, by making two rotations of nine months each within the Advisory & Controls teams below:

  • Investigation & Embargo
  • Control Testing (AML, Investor Protection, Market Integrity & Crossborder)

Your role

You will support the employees of these services, in particular in the following areas:

  • Control testing of account openings, including for increased risk clients and politically exposed persons (PIPs), as well as review during the relationship and during changes in circumstances.
  • Research and investigation of existing or prospective clients using different compliance databases.
  • Preparation of summary documents and presentation of conclusions to the various relevant committees of the Bank.
  • Support for files identified by the first level of control in order to define the measures to be taken.
  • Follow-up of clarification processes initiated with the Front Office in particular.
  • Participation in the sending of communications/requests addressed to the Office of Money Laundering Communication (MROS) (drafting, collection of documentation, entry in its GoAML application, etc.).
  • Participation in regulatory oversight activities (anti-money laundering/AML and market abuse).

Your profile

  • Master in Economics or Law.
  • Professional experience: an asset.
  • Excellent spirit of analysis and synthesis.
  • Master's degree in English and French. Any other language: a plus.
  • Home in Switzerland required.

Your spirit of initiative and excellent sense of contact will allow you to take advantage of this professional immersion and actively seize the learning opportunities that will offer you. Dynamic and flexible, you have an open, attentive and listening personality, as well as a keen sense of ethics and discretion.

Position to be filled on 1 March 2027

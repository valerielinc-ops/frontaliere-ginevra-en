About us

Number 1 in French-speaking Switzerland Founded in 1972 in Sion (VS), Sun Store is Switzerland's first pharmacy chain and has been part of the Galenica network since 2009. Its large assortment includes medicines as well as beauty, health and wellness products. Most pharmacies Sun Store is located in French-speaking Switzerland and in the south-western part of Switzerland, in popular places. At Sun Store, you are part of a company that has the dynamism and innovation in its DNA. Our pharmacies are continually developing according to the latest customer needs. Our new store design, which offers our customers an even more attractive experience, is one example.

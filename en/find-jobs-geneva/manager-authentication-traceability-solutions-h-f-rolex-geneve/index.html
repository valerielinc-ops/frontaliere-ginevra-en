Introduction

For our Information Systems division, we are looking for a Proximity Manager (H/F) from the Authentication and Traceability team whose main mission will be to lead the technical team (internal/external) on authentication and traceability solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Lead internal and external technical teams in the implementation of projects by ensuring the application of DevSecOps good development practices
  • Working with SI and Trade partners to achieve business objectives
  • Ensure the architecture of the solutions by respecting the requirements of safety, availability and performance
  • Contribute to integration with all information systems solutions (MES, ERP, PLM, dedicated applicatives, ...)
  • Pilot strategic projects from end to end, from design to deployment.

Profile

  • University training in computer science, HES engineer or equivalent
  • Successful experience over 5 years in a similar position, including piloting and team development and project management
  • Good knowledge of DevSecOps practices and application integration (REST, SOAP...)
  • Mastery of Waterfall, Agile, SAFe project methodologies...
  • Understanding of business issues and strength of proposal
  • Master's of English and French in oral and writing
  • With a good ability to work as a team, you are rigorous and have strong analytical and communication capabilities.

Conclusion

We offer a stimulating environment, attractive working conditions, flexibility schemes and top social benefits.

Full description

Your Mission

The aim of the course is to contribute to the development of the Hospice General's artificial intelligence ecosystem, ensuring that each solution brings concrete added value to our beneficiaries and our collaborators. Your mission will be to challenge different technological solutions, including agentic approaches, and test them on specific and measurable cases of use. Within the Information Systems and Digital Directorate, you will work with the Product Data Intelligence team to design, test and evaluate these solutions in their social context. Your main activities

  • You will be actively involved in the construction of the AI ecosystem and the realization of first cases of high added value use.
  • You will contribute to the implementation of the elements of the AI ecosystem (models, orchestration tools, integration with existing systems, ...).
  • You will design and test solutions via usage cases (instant translation, appointment making, management of meeting records, ...).
  • You will compare and challenge available technological solutions and make reasoned recommendations.
  • You will be able to propose to make AI a lever of value for our beneficiaries and collaborators.

Methods used

  • IA agentics: designing agents capable of orchestrating tools, automating tasks.
  • Using AI platforms: Hugging Face, VLLM, Ollama, LM Studio.
  • Development with Python for integration and orchestration.
  • Deployment on prime or on private cloud to ensure data confidentiality.
  • Language models (LLM) based mainly on open source solutions (Llama, Mistral, Apertus, etc.).
  • Integration of the MCP (Model Context Protocol) protocol for secure interactions.
  • Using web frameworks (FastaPI, Flask) to deploy applications.
  • Containerization with Docker for deployment of AI solutions.
  • Principles of responsible and ethical AI in development.

Your profile During Master level training in a computer, data science, artificial intelligence or related field (HES, university or EPFL)

Skills sought

  • Interest in AI: agent design, tool orchestration, MCP protocol.
  • Strong knowledge of Python programming.
  • Familiarity with AI tools and frameworks (Claude, Hugging Face, OpenAI, etc.).
  • Understanding language models and their practical integration.
  • Experience with REST APIs and databases.
  • Containerization (Docker) and deployment of applications.
  • Understanding data security and confidentiality issues.
  • Excellent ability to collaborate and communicate.
  • Proposition strength and search for innovative solutions.
  • Sensitivity to the ethical issues of AI.
  • Ability to popularize complex technical concepts.

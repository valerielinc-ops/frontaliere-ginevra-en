Associate Security Technology Engineer — Richemont

Full description

Reference code: JR131014

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

The primary role of the Associate Security Technology Engineer is to support Richemont’s Group Operational Technology/Internet of Things (OT/IoT) security strategy through the deployment, operation, and continuous improvement of OT/IoT security technologies across manufacturing and industrial environments. It operates at the intersection of Group Security, Business Owners, and Maison manufacturing teams, ensuring that OT/IoT security capabilities are aligned with operational constraints, risk profiles, and Group security standards, reinforcing accountability within the First Line of Defense (Group Platform & Group Technology).

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Deploy and implement OT/IOT security monitoring technologies across manufacturing and operational environments in line with Group Security standards.
  • Act as an operational Group OT security interface for Manufacturing entities and selected Maisons and Group Platforms & Group Technology
  • Execute the Group OT/IOT security strategy by translating defined requirements into operable and scalable security technology solutions.
  • Ensure OT/IOT security monitoring technologies are operational, reliable, and delivering expected services within industrial availability and safety constraints.
  • Operate and manage OT/IOT security technologies within assigned scope, ensuring compliance with Group policies and applicable industry regulations.
  • Contribute to Group‑level security initiatives and projects on the OT/IOT technology track, working with cross‑functional stakeholders across regions and Maisons and Group functions
  • Maintain and update documentation related to OT/IOT security technologies, including deployment, configuration, and operational procedures.
  • Provide technical guidance and enablement to internal stakeholders and team members on the use of OT/IOT security technologies in manufacturing environments.
  • Contribute & Supports OT SOC development.
  • Supports CSIRT/FUSION Center, if required, in case of OT/IT incidents.
  • Manage and/or contribute to projects, also outside of the OT/IOT scope, ran by Group Security Technology.
  • Contribute to the general support within Group Security Technology.

REQUIREMENTS / SKILLS

  • Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 3 years of professional experience in Information Technology / Computer Science or equivalent experience.
  • Proven experience in OT/IOT security/systems, evidenced by relevant industry certifications is an advantage.
  • Hands-on experience in technical engineering and operational experience.
  • Experience in cross-teams projects.
  • Experience in a multinational, global environment.
  • Proficiency in Windows and Unix/Linux OS environment.
  • Knowledge of Python, or any other programming language is an asset.
  • Knowledge of OT/IoT most common protocols and standards
  • Knowledge of Cloud and Cloud Security.
  • Exposure to industrial environments (manufacturing, production systems, etc.)
  • Experience working with plant engineers, automation teams, or industrial vendors
  • Excellent proficiency in English & French. Fluency in additional languages is a strong asset.
  • Collaborative and open mindset, good listening skills, and strong feedback culture.
  • Highly curious, proactive, autonomous, and agile.
  • Outstanding personal and professional integrity and work ethics.
  • Exceptional analytical mindset, keen attention to detail, and an advocate for cybersecurity.

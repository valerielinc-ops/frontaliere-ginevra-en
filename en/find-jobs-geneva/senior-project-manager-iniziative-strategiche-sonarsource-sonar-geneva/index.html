Full description

Who is Sonar?

Sonar enables organizations to ship agentic code with confidence. Code creation is abundant, but confidence is scarce. Our core platform, SonarQube, verifies over 750 billion lines of code every day, delivering agentic verification and governance for all code. Trusted by more than 7 million developers and 75% of the Fortune 100, including teams at Nvidia, ServiceNow, Booking.com , Goldman Sachs, AstraZeneca, and Ford Motor Company, Sonar enables organizations to reduce outages, improve security, and lower the cost and risk of agentic software development.

Founded in Geneva and now headquartered in Austin, Texas, Sonar has a global team working across offices and hubs, including Silicon Valley, London, Bochum, Barcelona, Singapore, Tokyo, and the Washington, D.C. area. At Sonar, we’re enabling enterprises to build agentic software factories that can move quickly while maintaining the control, consistency and evidence required to ship with confidence.

Position description

Sonar is becoming an AI-first company. Strategy Enablement turns Sonar's strategic ideas into real, lasting results: company-critical initiatives, business transformations, acquisition integrations and new market expansion. As Sr. Project Manager, Strategic Initiatives, you will lead some of these initiatives from fuzzy intent to measurable outcome with pace and focus, as a trusted thought partner to leadership. Everything we do ultimately serves our internal and external customers, and you will keep them at the center of the solutions you design. You will reinvent both how projects are run and what they deliver: you use AI agents every day in your own work, and you design solutions AI-first, ambitious yet practical. Your first focus will be the AI transformation of our Go-To-Market organization, with our Business Technology team (including SALT, its AI team) and the Data & Insights team. Over time, you will lead initiatives across the company, in domains you may not know yet.

What you will do • Frame ambiguous strategic intents into clear problems, objectives and business cases, starting from the impact on our customers and the business. • Bring the customer voice into every initiative: use customer feedback and data to shape solutions, and make sure internal changes improve how customers buy, use and renew with Sonar. • Ground decisions in data: establish baselines, size opportunities, define success metrics up front and measure impact after go-live. • Lead complex, cross-functional initiatives end to end, driving pace and keeping the focus on customer and business outcomes. • Re-plan quickly when priorities or context change, and recommend stopping work that no longer pays off. • Redesign processes AI-first, from the outcome back: decide what AI agents do, where humans and AI work together and where human judgment stays essential, with guardrails proportional to business risk. • Build working prototypes (skills, agents, lightweight apps, automations) to test ideas early with users, then partner with Business Technology to industrialize what works. • Use AI agents systematically in your own business analysis and project management work, and share reusable skills and workflows to raise the AI bar around you. • Bridge leadership and operational teams: engage stakeholders early, manage competing priorities and keep sponsors and teams aligned. • Stay agile and pragmatic: run initiatives with simple, effective tools and communication rather than heavy process and admin, pick the approach that drives the behaviors and actions needed, re-plan quickly when context changes, and recommend stopping work that no longer pays off. • Master business analysis and project management foundations, and apply them pragmatically: just enough process, simple tools and clear communication, never process for its own sake. • Own change management as part of delivery (communication, training, champions, reinforcement) until new ways of working stick, with particular care for the human side of AI-driven change.

Experience and qualifications

The ideal candidate will

• Have 10+ years of experience leading complex, cross-functional, international transformation or strategic initiatives, ideally combining strategy or transformation consulting with in-house experience in a fast-growing tech or SaaS company. • Bring a proven track record of measurable outcomes (revenue, cost, efficiency, adoption, customer experience), not just projects delivered. • Have a strong customer mindset: see internal work as a way to serve customers, and use that lens to set priorities. • Use AI agents and assistants (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) hands-on every day, with concrete examples of skills, agents or workflows they have built. • Have designed or delivered AI-enabled processes, with a pragmatic view of AI's value, limits and risks. • Be strongly data-driven: analyze business data themselves (spreadsheets, SQL or BI tools, AI-assisted analysis) to build business cases and track results. • Demonstrate strong change management, stakeholder management and communication skills, having led people through significant change and influenced executives and operational teams alike. • Master business analysis and project management foundations, and apply them flexibly rather than by the book. • Thrive in ambiguity: decide with partial information and adapt the plan without losing sight of the goal. • Show strong curiosity and learn new domains quickly. Go-To-Market experience is a plus. • Be fluent in English, written and spoken. French is a plus.

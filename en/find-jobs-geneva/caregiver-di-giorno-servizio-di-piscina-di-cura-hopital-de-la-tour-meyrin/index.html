Day Caregiver / Care Pool Service (ref: 2003)

Your mission

In a state-of-the-art facility you hold a varied position on a multidisciplinary team of nurses, health care workers, doctors.

You will need to accommodate patients with multiple pathologies as part of a comprehensive acute phase of care.

As such you are in charge of;

  • Welcoming the patient and his/her family by making them involved in care
  • Collect administrative data
  • Care for the child as a whole in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team and meet its basic needs (hygiene, pain, food, comfort, ...)
  • Ensure the supply of equipment, verify their operation and ensure the management of maintenance, apply cleaning and disinfection procedures
  • Participate in the proper functioning of the service, make transmissions on the computerized care file
  • Updating of professional practices in relation to service or institutional care procedures

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • 1 to 2 years minimum experience with a hospital structure
  • Diploma of Caregiver
  • Self-governing, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and focus are essential qualities

Full description

The Hospital of La Tour brings together the best of two worlds. Private medicine in an environment that stimulates the development of competence. Independent practice coupled with the institutional support of physicians in research and quality. At La Tour Hospital, we aim to bring together the best talents around our patients, develop a team that is inspired by what is best in the world and be a place of motivation for healthcare professionals who have the ambition to provide better care every day. The Hôpital de la Tour is a reference institution in the Canton of Geneva, known for its quality of care and modern working environment. Our paediatric service offers children an integrated and comprehensive care, among various disciples (pediatricians, pediatric emergency workers, neonatologists, surgeons and pediatric orthopaedists). Obtaining ISFM accreditation as a post-graduate training centre in paediatric surgery — category C is in progress. We recruit one Internal physician (Assistant physician) in paediatric surgery (ref: 1971) Your mission: In a state-of-the-art facility, you are employed in a multi-disciplinary team of nurses, caregivers, pediatric doctors, pediatric emergency workers, neonatologists, surgeons and pediatric orthopaedists. As part of your function, you are in charge of;

  • Pre- and post-operative management of paediatric patients (0–18 years)
  • Patient care in paediatric emergenciesActive participation in surgery in the operating room
  • Clinical monitoring of inpatients and outpatient consultation
  • Preparation of operational reports and exit letters
  • Participation in symposia and medical-surgical meetings
  • Collaboration with paediatric, anaesthesiology and nursing teams
  • Involvement in the continuing training activities of the service We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:
  • Holder of the Federal Medical Diploma recognized by the Federal Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO).
  • Interest in paediatric surgery or general surgery
  • An initial experience in surgery or paediatrics is an asset
  • Having a perfect oral and written knowledge of French and a knowledge of English is an asset.
  • Relationship with children and their families
  • Autonomy, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and sense of priority are essential qualities.
  • Positive attitude within a team and being able to generate a climate of trust. We offer you:
  • Structured post-graduate training, supervised by experienced pediatric surgeons.
  • Current ISFM Certification Application – Category C
  • Modern hospital environment, equipped and well organized.
  • Individual coaching and active mentoring.
  • Terms and conditions of employment that meet the SSMG/FMH standards for assistants (internal).
  • Access to e-learning platforms and paediatric surgery congresses.

Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

We would like to talk to you about the opportunity to join our Maison for an internship as Data Scientist Intern (6 months). As Data Scientist Intern, contribute to the ARIA machine learning project by revamping and enhancing an existing model for short and long-term daily sales prediction. The objective is to update the current outdated model, adapt it to existing data sources, and extend its capabilities to include very short-term (maximum 3 days) predictions for traffic and transactions. Ultimately, this project aims to create an intelligent assistant to help organize boutique operations and provide worldwide forecasts to inform strategic decision-making.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

  • Revamping the existing machine learning model within the ARIA project
  • Adapting the chosen model to integrate with current and existing data sources
  • Extending the model's predictive scope to include traffic and transaction forecasts

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE & PROFILE

  • Engineering School or Master Degree with a specialization in applied mathematics or computer science
  • Analytical mindset and structuring skills
  • Fluency in English is required
  • French is a plus

  • To support the delivery of the CPS strategic roadmap, we are recruiting a B2C Payment Operations & Project Manager – Group Customer Payment Services This role is for someone who is a self-starter, understands new technologies and is passionate about creating best in class customer experiences, is committed to operational excellence, has excellent communication skills and is able to document effectively, enjoys exploring data for insights, can build the bridge between the business and the technology teams, who has excellent project management skills.
  • Specifically, the role is to: Payments Operations & Compliance: Act as a Subject Matter Expert for B2C payment activations, ensuring strict adherence to Group operational procedures and evolving regulatory requirements (e.g., 3DS, PSD2, GDPR, PCI, Scheme rules).
  • Establish and continuously optimize customer payment operational processes and procedures, collaborating closely with key stakeholders across Retail, Digital, Anti-Fraud, Security, Technology, and Finance.
  • Serve as an escalation point for critical operational matters and facilitate the seamless integration of newly acquired businesses into Richemont's B2C payment framework.
  • Coordinate the contractual review and contract signature in collaboration with internal and external stakeholders Develop and maintain a centralized repository for contract data Operational Excellence & Process Improvement: Proactively identify and address bottlenecks and inefficiencies within payment operations and global contracts.
  • Design and implement streamlined workflows and best practices to enhance efficiency and significantly reduce manual effort (e.g., MIDs review, KYC reviews, reconciliation escalations).
  • Analyze existing payment flows, identify inefficiencies in configuration and payment acceptance, propose solutions to reduce transaction fees, and optimize payment methods setup across all markets.
  • Develop and maintain a standardized support model for B2C payment operational queries and oversee payment platforms user access and payment accounts management (Adye, Paypal, myAccess, etc).
  • Coordinate change management activities related to payment terminal fleet upgrades and regulatory changes impacting established processes.

Job descriptionCommercial strategy & developmentDetermine, develop and deploy an innovative and ambitious business strategy, consistent with the hotel's positioning, its revenue objectives and its development strategy.Identify and conquer new markets (corporation, leisure, groups, MICE, travel agencies, high-end individual customers, etc.) in order to diversify customer segments and optimize the hotel occupancy rate. Actively develop business prospecting by identifying new customers and potential accounts, establishing targeted contacts, organizing business meetings and ensuring regular monitoring of opportunities. Develop and retain a portfolio of existing and prospective customers, including through personalized business actions, customer visits, tariff negotiations and the establishment of trade agreements.Create, develop and maintain sustainable partnerships with travel agencies, businesses, tour operators, event organizers and key players in the hotel sector.Identify new revenue opportunities and propose commercial actions to increase the turnover of accommodation and catering. Ensure the overall promotion of the hotel, its accommodation and catering offer, in collaboration with the departments concerned. Work closely with the reception, reservation, catering and accommodation teams to ensure consistency of the customer experience and the optimization of commercial performance. Work closely with Accor's luxury sales teams to monitor relations between the hotel and partner agencies, to increase its visibility in strategic markets and to identify new areas of development.Participate in trade fairs, business events, workshops and representation operations, with the possibility to travel to the relevant markets.Follow and analyse commercial performance indicators (turnover rate, occupancy rate, ADR, RevPAR, segment production and distribution channel) and implement corrective action plans to achieve the objectives. Ensure ongoing competitive and commercial monitoring: analyse the local Geneva market, identify trends in the sector, monitor changes in demand and anticipate development opportunities.Coordination of the booking teamCoordinate and supervise the daily activities of the booking team in order to guarantee the quality of service, the fluidity of operations and the achievement of commercial objectives. Accompany, mentor and motivate the employees of the booking team in their daily missions, ensuring that the hotel's procedures and quality standards are respected.Ensure the proper management of individual, group and corporate booking requests, ensuring rigorous follow-up of customer files and requests. Optimize the conversion of applications to confirmed bookings through a proactive business approach, quality of trade, and relevance of tariff proposals. Develop additional sales (upselling and cross-selling) by valuing the different categories of rooms, additional services and catering offers.Ensure good communication between the booking, reception, revenue management and other hotel departments. Monitor the team's performance through specific indicators: volume of requests, conversion rates, revenue generated, average basket and additional sales.Participate in the continuous improvement of business procedures, tools and practices to improve productivity and customer satisfaction.To ensure the proper use of booking tools and PMS, including OPERA CLOUD, as well as the reliability of tariff information and availability.

Your Purpose

Join MSC Cruises for an internship supporting the design, understanding, and continuous improvement of the end-to-end Customer Journey. The role combines customer journey mapping, service standards, guest insights, and project coordination across pre-cruise, embarkation, onboard, disembarkation, post-cruise, and Customer Care touchpoints.

You will work closely with the Customer Journey department and cross-functional stakeholders to identify improvement opportunities, document journeys and standards, support ongoing initiatives, and translate guest feedback into structured recommendations and follow-up actions.

Your Impact

  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to ensure consistency across the guest journey.
  • Maintain action trackers and monitor the progress of Customer Journey improvement initiatives.
  • Support cross-functional projects aimed at improving the guest experience.
  • Prepare presentations, summaries, and documentation for Customer Journey initiatives.
  • Contribute to benchmarking activities, including guest feedback, mystery shopping, and competitive reviews.
  • Support guest experience analyses to identify pain points and improvement opportunities.
  • Assist in the development, review, and maintenance of Customer Journey standards.
  • Support the mapping and review of end-to-end guest journeys across all travel touchpoints.

Your Journey so far

  • Degree, or currently enrolled, in Business, Hospitality, Customer Experience, Marketing, Data Analytics, or a related field.
  • Strong customer-centric and analytical mindset, with excellent attention to detail and structured problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to organise information clearly and translate feedback into concise insights, journey documentation, and actionable outputs.
  • Good project coordination, communication, and stakeholder collaboration skills.
  • Advanced Microsoft Office skills, particularly PowerPoint and Excel.
  • Fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage.
  • Basic understanding of customer journey mapping, service design, guest satisfaction metrics, and NPS.
  • Interest in hospitality, luxury travel, guest experience, service improvement, and cross-functional transformation.
  • Familiarity with survey analysis, open-comment analysis, benchmarking, or service standards.
  • Previous internship or project experience in customer experience, hospitality, consulting, research, or project management is an advantage.

Your Essentials

  • Hands-on experience across journey mapping, service standards, guest insights, and Customer Journey improvement projects.
  • Exposure to the full guest lifecycle and collaboration with a broad range of corporate, operational, and onboard stakeholders.
  • Opportunity to contribute to tangible improvements in a global, dynamic, and guest-focused environment.

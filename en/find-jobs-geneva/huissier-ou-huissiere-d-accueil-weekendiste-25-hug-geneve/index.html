Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1'400 collaborators and collaborators, the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics (DRG) is a medical centre focused on the elderly, located in 5 sites: Trois-Chêne, Loëx, Bellerive, Beau-Séjour and Joli-Mont. It takes care of acute situations, thanks in particular to the geriatric emergencies and the intermediate care of the Hospital of the Three Oaks, acute units including some centered on geriatric syndromes, geriatric, musculoskeletal, cardiac, neurological and oncological rehabilitation units, and long stays units. It also includes a specialized palliative care service, a community geriatric unit, a liaison geriatric unit and the Memory Centre.

Description of post

The Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics is looking for its future bailiff or its future bailiff. In this position, you are responsible for providing the patient, the public and staff with a courteous, personalized and competent reception, in face-to-face situations and on the telephone. Your dynamism, your sense of service and hospitality, and your respect for confidentiality are already recognized and emphasized. You inform, orient, or even accompany any person attending receptions and you collaborate with other services involved in the reception chain (admissions, care units, consultation centres, etc.). You are able to work autonomously while being self-help and collaborating. You complete all the procedures for admission of outpatients and inpatients, ensuring that the institutional procedures are followed. To do this, you rely on your very good sense of organization, adherence to procedures and your management of priorities and stress. You contribute to the supervision and security of the facility. You work mainly on weekends, holidays and evenings (until 8:30 pm).

Qualifications

You are in the course of higher education and have a very good command of the French language (another language is an asset). You're comfortable in hospitals and geriatrics. You are perfectly comfortable with computer tools. Dynamic and autonomous, you like to work as a team and have a strong relational sense.

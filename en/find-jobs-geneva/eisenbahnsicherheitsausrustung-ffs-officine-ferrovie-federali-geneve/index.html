What you can move.

  • Build, maintain and maintain various safety facilities such as switches, needle motors, barrier facilities and ground and height signals.
  • Independent execution of delicate and complex mechanical work on outdoor safety installations.
  • Remove disturbances to mechanical devices.
  • After in-house training, provide picket service

What you bring.

  • CFCs in a mechanical industry (automotive mechanic, polymerician or equivalent profession, and with electrical bases).
  • Driver's licence category B mandatory.
  • Place of residence in Geneva or in the vicinity (accessible in no more than 30 minutes at any required time).
  • Strong sense of responsibility and teamwork, high sensitivity to security issues. Proactive and skilful, you are very flexible and stress-resistant.
  • Knowledge of technical computer tools.

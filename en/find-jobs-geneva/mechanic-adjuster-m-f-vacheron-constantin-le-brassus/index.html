Full description

Reference code: JR135721

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

Ensure and guarantee the production of parts in terms of quality, quantity and time on milling equipment for which it is responsible. Proceed with the commissioning of a machining centre.

Machine parts by removing material until the shape, dimensions and surface aesthetics are obtained.

Your role in the House

  • Making prototypes

On unit parts or preseries in close collaboration with technical offices and methods.

Participate in testing, test technical feasibility and propose optimisation solutions for future industrialization.

Drafting of improvement results following testing

  • Getting started

Perform the settings of CNC machines (using a standardization/optimization approach). Check and validate the setting part before starting the series Remount corrections or proposal for programme improvement to the programmer

  • Ensure machining and production monitoring

Monitor the development of machining (controls during production) Change tools and make necessary corrections Soften the pieces

Clean rooms according to work and shift instructions

  • Ensure preventive maintenance of CNC machines

Implement and enforce maintenance plans Perform first-level maintenance

  • Perform self-control

Perform self-checking according to work and shift instructions Locate defects, determine the measures to be taken and apply them at each manufacturing stage Detect, solve or trace quality and/or technical problems Control with three-dimensional control tools.

The qualities to excel

You have a Micromechanic CFC, a Polymechanic CFC or a title deemed equivalent.

You are comfortable reading technical and industrial (ideally in the field of watchmaking) and also on digital controls (ideally FANUC).

You justify a proven experience on 3 axis machining machines of the Willemin Macodel type.

Are you methodical, efficient, careful, quick execution and endowed with great dexterity of a very good vision?

Vacheron Constantin celebrates over 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

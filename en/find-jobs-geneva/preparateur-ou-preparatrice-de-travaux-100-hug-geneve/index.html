Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. This department, consisting of nine medical services, is responsible for providing diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. Genetic medicine is both a clinical service, with consultations, and a laboratory service. He is primarily involved in genetic and therapeutic advice to patients following genetic analysis, including modern methods of high-throughput cytogenetics and sequencing. The services offered mainly provide diagnosis, prognosis and prediction of hereditary diseases. The service also has research and teaching missions. The Molecular and Genomic Diagnostic Laboratory is an accredited laboratory that conducts analyses using conventional and modern molecular methods in the search for hereditary anomalies through the exploration of genes and genomes, including classical or high-throughput sequencing of the DNA of the xome and the whole genome. The Cytogenomics Laboratory (conventional, haematological and molecular cytogenetics) is an accredited laboratory that performs analyses using QF-PCR, conventional karyotype, in situ fluorescence hybridization, DNA and array chip molecular karyotype (SNP or CGH) and optical mapping (OGM) methods in the field of constitutional and onco-haematological diseases.

Description of post

In genetic laboratories, you ensure that you take care of requests for analyses and samples received. You prepare the files in accordance with current requirements and ensure effective communication between the sampling centre and the various laboratories, in strict compliance with established procedures and quality standards. You are actively involved in maintaining the laboratory's quality system and accreditation. As such, you contribute to the management of non-compliances related to pre-analytical steps, monitor them and participate in the implementation of corrective and preventive actions. You are also responsible for managing the inventory of reagents, consumables and laboratory equipment. You ensure their availability by monitoring stock levels, placing orders, receiving goods and their traceability. As part of the proper operation of laboratory activities, you contribute to operational maintenance by ensuring that workstations are properly supplied with consumables. You also provide ad hoc support to equipment managers during maintenance and control operations. You also have the responsibility of archiving files, blades and other materials that require systematic classification and preservation, in collaboration with technical teams, in order to ensure efficient organization and compliance of laboratory processes. Finally, you assist laboratory managers in various administrative tasks, including document management, archiving and monitoring of quality documents. This function description is not exhaustive. Missions and responsibilities may evolve according to laboratory needs, service organization and operational priorities.

Qualifications

You have a Federal Certificate of Capacity or an equivalent degree and you have experience in a similar position in a laboratory. You are able to work in the context of a quality system based on ISO 15189 and work with laboratory analysis tracking software and common office automation software. Your rigour, sense of responsibility and autonomy allow you to perform the tasks that are dedicated to you in an optimal and efficient way. You show an interest in medical genetics and have great versatility and professional experience. Your relational ease and your sense of collegiality allow you to work as a team and integrate easily.

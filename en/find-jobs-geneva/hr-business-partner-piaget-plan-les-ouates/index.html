Reference code: JR128463 Surrounded by his family and guided by his strong entrepreneurial mindset, Georges-Edouard Piaget decided, in 1874, to create a watchmaking workshop in La Côte-aux-Fées, a small village in the middle of the Swiss mountains of the Jura. Since that day, Piaget has pushed the limits to “do what has never been done before” and crafts sophisticated and flamboyant watches and jewellery creations. Within the walls of our Ateliers de l’Extraordinaire, our master artisans continue to harness rare skills that have been preserved and perfected from generation to generation. Today, Piaget’s exceptional craftsmanship is celebrated worldwide, supported by a strong global presence and a dedicated network of client support and retail colleagues who bring the spirit of our Maison to life every day. HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT? Reporting directly to the Head of People, you will play a pivotal role in driving the People agenda and serving as key HR Business Partner for multiple departments within our Headquarters’ population. You will be the primary point of contact for managers and employees on impactful Human Resources topics, providing comprehensive support and strategic guidance. Your role will be instrumental in ensuring Piaget's continued success by implementing the HR strategy and initiatives across the departments in scope, closely aligning with and providing feedback to the Centers of Expertise (Talent and Development, People Experience, Reward, Talent Acquisition) and fostering strong relationships across the organization. You will be role-modelling and nurturing Piaget’s culture, both within our Maison as well as more broadly – within Richemont Group and beyond. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Strategic HR Partnership & Advisory: Act as a key HR Business Partner, supporting the Head of People in driving the People agenda and providing comprehensive HR support to departments in scope. Serve as the primary contact for managers and employees on major Human Resources topics, offering guidance on employee relations, engagement, and other HR matters. Talent Acquisition & Integration: Identify recruitment needs, closely work with the Talent Acquisition Partner and ensure a seamless integration experience for new joiners. Performance Management: Oversee the performance review process, advising and training managers, and ensuring appropriate follow-up actions for people development. Organize and coordinate Performance Reviews with relevant departments and challenge organizational structures to optimize talent utilisation. Talent & Succession Planning: Drive talent management initiatives, including succession planning, talent reviews, and career development programs across the organization. Reward & Compensation Expertise: As an HR Business Partner, you will drive the Compensation review for your population and build compensation packages for new joiners. Change Management & Transformation: Support leaders through significant transformation initiatives, ensuring smooth transitions and people-centric approaches. Compliance & Risk Management: Ensure all HR practices comply with diverse local Labor laws and regulations, mitigating risks effectively. Leadership Development & Coaching: Coach leaders on effective people management, performance optimization, and talent development strategies. Project Management: Lead and contribute to transversal HR projects, ensuring successful execution and stakeholder collaboration. Culture Ambassador: Immerse personally within the Maison’s culture, support populations in scope with understanding our culture anchors and behaviours, role modelling culture internally and externally. HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US? We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced HR professional to join our team. If you possess the following, we encourage you to apply: Experienced HR Leader: Minimum 7 years in HR Business Partnering, holding a University degree (HR, Psychology) or Brevet Federal. Strategic & Business-Driven: A strategic thinker who aligns HR initiatives with business goals, delivering data-driven, impactful solutions. Swiss Labor Law Expert: In-depth knowledge of Swiss Labor Law, with CLA watchmaking experience highly valued. Change & Process Master: Proven ability to manage complex HR processes (performance, career, salary review, recruitment) and drive organizational change. Exceptional Communicator & Influencer: Builds strong relationships across all levels, from Executive Committee to employees, providing client-centric solutions. Proactive & Autonomous: Highly organized, self-motivated, and dedicated to driving results independently in a dynamic setting. Collaborative & Resilient: A resilient team player, excelling in cross-functional collaboration and navigating complex environments. Innovative & Agile: Embraces new ideas, challenges the status status quo, and brings an entrepreneurial spirit to HR. Project Management Pro: Expert in end-to-end project coordination, managing multiple tasks and diverse stakeholders effectively. Tech Savvy: Mastery of Excel, SAP, and PowerPoint. Bilingual: Fluent in French and English. STILL WONDERING WHY YOU SHOULD APPLY? A Welcoming Team: Join our friendly, open-minded, and talented team, where your skills will be valued and your contributions will make a real difference. Dynamic and Engaging Work: Experience daily activities that are interesting, challenging, and diverse, keeping you engaged and motivated. Investment in Your Growth: We are passionate about developing our people, and you will have access to various training and development opportunities to enhance your skills and advance your career. The Piaget culture of encouraging Extra-Care, fostering Mastery and bringing out the Extraordinary in our people, means that we will always go further, doing “what has never been done before”.

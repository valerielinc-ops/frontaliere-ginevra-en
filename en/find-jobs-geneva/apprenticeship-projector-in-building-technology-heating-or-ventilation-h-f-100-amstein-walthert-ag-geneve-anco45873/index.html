Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with approximately 1100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we deal with all aspects of building technology and infrastructure throughout their life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and durable. Your missions:

  • What if your apprenticeship was used to build the Geneva of tomorrow? You want a real job that makes sense? You want to help build more sustainable buildings in Geneva? Join our engineering office and train for 4 years in a trade with high added value, at the heart of energy and environmental issues. Here, you don't just make plans: you participate in designing the buildings we live in tomorrow, you are integrated into a team that allows you to develop desired skills What you're going to learn and do according to the curriculum Design useful and sustainable facilities for a heating curriculum
  • Understand how heating systems ensure comfort in buildings
  • Participate in the design of sustainable heating systems
  • Perform simple calculations (power, energy requirements)
  • Discover and compare different heating systems
  • Understanding the current issues: energy savings, renewable energy, environmental impact Design useful and sustainable facilities for a ventilation curriculum
  • Understand how air circulates in buildings (comfort, health, energy)
  • Participate in the design of efficient ventilation systems
  • Dimensioning real installations (air flow, networks)
  • Integrate concrete issues: energy saving, noise, fire safety Common to the curriculum:
  • Use professional software (AutoCAD, Revit)
  • Drawing and developing technical plans
  • Visualize 3D installations in concrete projects in Geneva
  • Understanding how a project moves from idea to realization
  • Go on site and see the projects come to life
  • Understand how systems are installed in real life
  • Participate in monitoring work and testing facilities
  • Exchange with the various actors of the project Your skills and qualifications: Your profile
  • Compulsory education completed (good math level recommended)

  • Easy with digital tools

  • Team spirit
  • Motivation to learn and invest in demanding training
  • Ability to work as a team and communicate What is expected above all Motivation Curiosity The desire to build something useful and to carry out your work well Finally, why this profession has value

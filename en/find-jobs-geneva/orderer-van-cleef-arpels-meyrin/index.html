Full description

Reference code: JR134300

Who are we?

A High Jewelry House with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

You justify significant experience in scheduling or production management, ideally in a precision industrial sector such as watchmaking, fine mechanics or luxury mechanics. You master ERP tools for the monitoring of manufacturing orders and inventory management. You're comfortable with MRP logic. A good knowledge of outsourcing flows and continuous improvement processes would be a valuable asset.

You are rigorous, organized and autonomous, able to manage multiple priorities simultaneously in a demanding production environment. You know how to anticipate blockages before they impact production and communicate clearly and effectively with various interlocutors.

What do we expect from you?

Attached to the Head Planning and Ordering, you ensure the scheduling, launching and monitoring of manufacturing orders in the worlds Movement, Watch and Mechanical Art. You coordinate internal and external flows to ensure that components are available, priorities are respected and deadlines are met.

As part of your missions

  • You analyze the needs of the MRP, create and launch manufacturing orders by setting priorities, and ensure the reliability of the statutes and deadlines in the ERP.
  • You work with project leaders to plan and prioritize manufacturing orders at each key step, anticipating risks that may impact timelines.
  • You control the flow of components and subcontracting, perform the associated operations in the ERP and contribute to the reliability of the inventory.
  • You actively manage the performance of your perimeter by participating in production monitoring rituals, by measuring key indicators such as the rate of service and the availability of manufacturing orders, and by proposing continuous improvement actions to optimize processes and make information shared with all stakeholders reliable.
  • You document business practices, ensure team formation according to the versatility matrix and contribute to business continuity.

More than a job... we recruit for a career

Integrating Van Cleef & Arpels is above all a team where the development of employees is at the heart of our ambition.

From your integration, you will be immersed in the world of the House thanks to trainings on our history, know-how and culture.

The recruitment process

Apply directly online.

If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for an interview.

You meet the Planning & Ordering Manager, then the Logistics Director, Planning and Purchasing, as well as the Human Resources Manager.

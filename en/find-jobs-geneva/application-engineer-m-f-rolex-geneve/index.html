Introduction

For our Information Systems Division, we are looking for an Application Engineer (M/F) whose main mission will be to contribute as a GMAO/EAM functional to strategic projects in the field of logistics, especially GMAO (computer-assisted maintenance).

Responsibilities

  • Write functional specifications based on user needs expressions
  • Monitor and validate developments and settings
  • Train key users and support them during the deployment phases of the new EAM solution for Rolex
  • Maintaining the solution and supporting users
  • Support for future development projects
  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of IT solutions to meet the company's needs in the above-mentioned field
  • In the framework of large-scale strategic development projects, in partnership with business teams in the GMAO/EAM field, the Logistics Functional will contribute to the definition of maintenance IT flows and will accompany the implementation of the appropriate tool.

Profile

  • Academic training desired; significant professional experience in the field of GMAO can compensate
  • Good knowledge of GMAO logistics flows and their integration into company processes
  • Business Analysis Capacity, Business Process Writing and Description
  • Knowledge of a solution such as SAP PM/EAM, Hexagon or IBM Maximo is an asset
  • Excellent relational qualities, autonomy, team spirit and analytical ability.
  • Open to travel throughout the group's production sites (4 locations in Switzerland).

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

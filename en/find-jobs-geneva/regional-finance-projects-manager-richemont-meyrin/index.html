CONTEXT

The Regional Finance Project Manager is a pivotal role within Richemont Europe's Finance team, reporting to the Regional Senior FP&A Manager. This position is responsible for leading strategic finance projects, driving the digitalization and automation of financial processes, and fostering community engagement across Richemont Europe. The role demands a blend of project leadership, technical expertise in data product development, and strong stakeholder management. This is a fixed-term contract starting from December 1st, 2026 to November 30th, 2027. SCOPE: Richemont Europe, including RLGE Swiss Branch Legal Entity

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

1- Strategic Finance Project leadership (60%)

  • Lead and coordinate critical finance projects across the European region, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives and Group initiatives
  • Drive special projects and initiatives, providing consistent updates and insights to diverse stakeholders
  • Facilitate seamless communication channels with internal and external partners
  • Direct Finance Operations projects to enhance efficiency and effectiveness

2- Lead Finance automation (25%)

  • Champion the strategic transition of financial reporting and analytics, specifically leading the end-to-end migration from Power BI to Looker
  • Develop robust Looker semantic models to deliver scalable, reliable, and governed financial dashboards, ensuring successful adoption and decommissioning of legacy systems
  • Translate complex financial business needs into clear data product requirements, ensuring comprehensive documentation, knowledge transfer, and continuous enhancement of automated solutionsEnsure continuous enhancement of Finance automation post migration
  • Optimize financial processes through standardization and automation to improve data product efficiency

3- Community Engagement & Strategic Support (15%)

  • Foster an effective and engaged European finance community through proactive communication and animation
  • Support the Regional Senior FP&A Manager in developing a Global FP&A Community of Experts (COE), aligning with Group Finance Process & Data strategies

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • Proven Expertise: 5 years of experience in analytics/BI engineering, with substantial experience within a Finance context
  • Business Acumen: Demonstrated ability to translate complex financial business needs into technical data product requirements, semantic definitions, validation criteria, and adoption strategies
  • Project & Stakeholder Management: Proven track record of successfully managing complex projects, time-sensitive transformation initiatives and effectively collaborating with diverse stakeholders across Finance, IT, and regional teams within a matrix organization
  • Mindset: Results-oriented, structured, reliable, with a strong emphasis on data quality and clear communication
  • Language : Fluent in English, French is an advantage

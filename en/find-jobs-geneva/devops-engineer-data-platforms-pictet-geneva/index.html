DevOps Engineer - Data Platforms — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Pictet Tech designs, builds, and operates the technology that underpins the Group’s core activities, from portfolio management and trading to banking services. Within Data Platforms , our ambition is to build a modern, resilient, and scalable data foundation across the Group. We are transforming our technology landscape by combining cloud and on-premise strengths, modernizing how data is stored, processed, integrated, and distributed, and raising engineering standards across all domains. As a member of the Analytics team, part of the Data Platform unit within the Platform Engineering department, you will help design, build and operate reliable data platforms and pipelines, turning complex challenges into secure, reliable, and high-performing data capabilities that power advanced analytics, AI solutions, and innovative audit use cases, and enable teams to deliver faster and with greater confidence.

Your role

  • Design, build and maintain reliable data ingestion and transformation pipelines, ensuring they are production-ready, monitored and documented.
  • Operate and improve the data platform in production, including orchestration, CI/CD, automation and infrastructure.
  • Manage and evolve platform infrastructure across on-premise and cloud environments, ensuring security, stability, scalability and performance.
  • Promote best practices in DevOps, release management, observability and infrastructure as code.
  • Contribute to the platform roadmap by gathering user needs and helping prioritise capabilities based on business value.

Your profile

  • Degree in engineering, computer science or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • At least 5 years of combined experience in data engineering and DevOps within a modern data platform environment.
  • Strong programming and data querying skills.
  • Experience in data ingestion, pipeline development and large-scale data transformation.
  • Hands-on experience with CI/CD, automation and DevOps ways of working.
  • Practical experience with cloud, containerised environments and infrastructure management.
  • Familiarity with technologies such as Python, SQL, AWS, Kubernetes or Snowflake is an asset, but none is mandatory.
  • Interest in a product-oriented approach to platforms, with a sense of user needs and priorities.
  • Proactive, adaptable and solution-oriented, with strong communication skills and team spirit.
  • Fluent in English and French.
  • Swiss resident or willingness to relocate.

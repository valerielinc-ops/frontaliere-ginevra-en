CONTEXT

Within the Quality Department, you are responsible for supporting the development of Quality Assurance in order to make the processes and quality of the products manufactured (Manufacturing dressing – site de la Chaux-de-Fonds)

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

You provide technical support on the quality criteria

By sizing the quality level with production, by your presence in the field and in coordination with other production sites

By sharing quality criteria with product development teams on novelties

By analysing SPC data to define, propose and pilot the axes of continuous improvement Quality

Ensuring the application and evolution of quality standards and procedures in production workshops

You take care of quality problems

By bringing your support and expertise to production in the treatment of non-compliances and in our problem-solving process.

Working with production teams to analyse root causes and solve quality problems.

Monitoring the implementation and validation of the effectiveness of the actions defined

You are a major player in our approach to quality improvement

Based on our Quality Roadmap, you contribute to the definition of priority topics through an analysis of indicators, you lead monthly quality performance reviews and pilot identified quality projects

You perform product/process audits and routine tests according to the identified risk levels and follow up on the action plan

You are conducting continuous improvement projects related to your scope of action in partnership with Industrialization or actively participating in transversal technical or continuous improvement projects

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

Quality/production engineer or technician training with experience in watchmaking

Experience confirmed in the industrial field, experience quality a plus

Basic knowledge in mechanics and plan reading, technical skills

Attracted for the field in connection with production teams

Rigor and analytical spirit

Good relationship

Cross-cutting leadership

Place of work : La Chaux-de-Fonds

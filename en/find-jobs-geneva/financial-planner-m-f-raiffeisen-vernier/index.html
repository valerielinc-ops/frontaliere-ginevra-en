Full description

16.11.2026 Vernier

Be part of our team!

At Banque Raiffeisen Cities and Vineyards Geneva, you play a key role in the interface between our clients, our advisors and the various specialists of the bank. Internal reference in financial planning and heritage council, you bring your expertise on issues of provisioning, retirement, taxation and heritage transmission.

What are you waiting for?

You accompany our clients and advisors on issues of pension, pension, taxation, property transfer and financing.

Specifically, you

  • Conduct comprehensive financial planning and personalized heritage analysis.
  • Support client advisors in identifying their clients' needs and participate in high value-added appointments.
  • Contribute your expertise on complex heritage situations and contribute to the development of the Heritage Council.
  • Coordinate the various internal expertise to ensure a consistent and customer-oriented approach.
  • Host customer events and share your knowledge with teams.
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and compliance standards.

What are you bringing?

  • Federal financial planner patent.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in a financial planning or heritage advisory function at a bank, insurance or financial institution.
  • Experience in hosting conferences, workshops or customer events.
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss foresight, retirement, taxation and inheritance.
  • Experience in the conduct of customer interviews and the accompaniment of customer advisors.
  • Good knowledge of the Geneva market and its heritage specificities.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

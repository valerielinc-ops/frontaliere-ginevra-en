Full description

Introduction

For our Procurement, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Security Officer whose main mission will be to ensure the implementation and monitoring of safety and fire prevention measures.

Responsibilities

  • Ensuring the operationality of fire protection and safety systems at one of our sites in the Canton of Geneva
  • Control and order corrective actions necessary to maintain facilities
  • Coordinate with the representatives of our agent oversight missions and tasks
  • Monitoring and training fire and evacuation response personnel
  • Developing good safety practices and fire prevention
  • Promote the implementation of safety rules and standards in settlement and development projects
  • Develop operational and/or functional specifications for a new project
  • Manages building passes (keys-cylinders)
  • Participate in the picket service

Profile

  • Diploma in fire prevention AEAI
  • Minimum 5 years experience in fire protection and safety
  • Good command of spoken and written French
  • Volunteer firefighter experience would be a plus
  • Knowledge of technical safety and fire protection facilities
  • Very good mastery of common computer tools
  • Discreet, confidential and responsive to emergencies
  • Responsible, rigorous and excellent priority management

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Full description

For our Research & Development division, we are seeking an Eco-Design Engineer (M/F) whose primary mission will be to support design teams in the development of more sustainable products.

  • Responsibilities
  • Integration of environmental criteria from the design phase of a product or process to reduce its impact throughout its life cycle, and to accompany teams in this direction
  • Understanding of the environmental impacts of projects, products, and processes through life cycle assessments (LCA) to guide eco-design choices
  • Innovation in a sustainable manner while respecting other product and process requirements, such as quality and cost
  • Accompanying teams in the eco-design of products
  • Proposal of innovative technical solutions to reduce the environmental impact of products and processes
  • Profile
  • EPF/HES engineering degree in engineering sciences or equivalent training, with a minor in durability desired
  • Knowledge of materials and processes
  • Expertise in eco-design
  • Proposals for innovative materials, new technologies, and more sustainable manufacturing processes
  • Ability to translate environmental challenges into concrete solutions for design teams
  • Experience in life cycle assessments (LCA), an asset
  • Interest in regulations and technological developments in the environmental field
  • Teaching skills
  • Pragmatism and ability to simplify complex concepts
  • Friendly, relational ease, and collaborative spirit
  • Curiosity and perseverance

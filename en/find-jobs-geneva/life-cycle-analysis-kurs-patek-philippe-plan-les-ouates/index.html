Full description

Mission

As part of the roll-out of our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) strategy and the strengthening of our environmental impact measurement approach, we are looking for a 6-month Life Cycle Analysis Trainee for our CSR Service. The main task will be to contribute to a life cycle analysis of a product and/or process.

Responsibilities

  • Perform life cycle analysis (LCA) on one or more company representative products according to a defined benchmark.
  • Contribute to the collection and reliability of data required for analysis.
  • Evaluate and quantify environmental impacts and key contributors.
  • Develop ways of improving and valuing the results to feed environmental and eco-design approaches.
  • Contribute to the modelling of an internal procedure to reproduce and compare CVAs on different products.

Profile sought

  • Master in Environmental Sciences or Sustainable Management, or equivalent title, in progress.
  • You have a strong interest in environmental issues.
  • Climate science knowledge, environmental impact assessment, carbon balance and life cycle analysis (LCA).
  • Project management skills.
  • Analytic spirit, curiosity and strength of proposal.
  • Relationship relationship.
  • Good command of English.
  • Desired start date: January 2027.
  • Internship or in the framework of a Bachelor/Master Project, lasting 6 months.

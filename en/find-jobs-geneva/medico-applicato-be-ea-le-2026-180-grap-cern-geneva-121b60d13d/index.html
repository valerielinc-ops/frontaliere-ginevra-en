Full description

Join CERN's Experimental Area group and contribute to the development of beamlines and facilities for the next generation of fixed-target experiments. The project you will be supporting is called NA60+. You will carry out beam studies, simulate proton and ion beams and their associated backgrounds, and perform shielding optimisation calculations. Working closely with beamline experts and service and equipment groups, you will provide technical input to the design of future experimental facilities.

Your responsibilities

  • Conduct beam studies for CERN's North Area secondary beamlines.
  • Develop detailed simulations of proton and ion beams and associated backgrounds for the future NA60+ experiment.
  • Evaluate and optimise shielding for the upcoming NA61-LE beamline through detailed simulations using the FLUKA Monte Carlo code.
  • Collaborate with service and equipment groups to inform the design requirements of the NA60+ experiment.
  • Contribute to project technical documentation and present findings at technical and project meetings.
  • This role includes team supervision responsibilities.

Your profile

  • Experience performing physics simulations.

Skills

  • Knowledge in at least one of the following Monte-Carlo and accelerator optics codes: Geant4, FLUKA, BDSIM, MADx, Xsuite.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a collaborative and international environment.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Applied Physics, Accelerator Physics (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.

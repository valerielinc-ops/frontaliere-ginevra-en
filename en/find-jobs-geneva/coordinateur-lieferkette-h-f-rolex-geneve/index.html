Introduction For our Commercial Division, we are looking for a Management Coordinator/Supply Chain Coordinator (H/F) whose main mission will be to manage global and cross-cutting projects related to all of our flows and coordination of improvement processes. This position is part of a dynamic environment, linked to many internal departments, and requires a strong capacity to support change. Responsibilities

  • Being the SAP Key User for our entity, within a SAP Key Users network
  • Actively participate in the migration of tools for the Prediction, Planning, Supply and Sales Administration trades
  • Contribute to projects to develop and optimize processes and implement IT solutions
  • Identify and manage continuous improvement actions, in line with the objectives set
  • Collaborate with the teams Forecast, Planning, Supply and Sales Administration, as well as with all the entities of the company: Purchases, Finance, Stock, etc.
  • Support teams in leading change, as a leader, by fostering communication, versatility and team spirit Profile
  • HES engineering training, Supply Chain specialization or equivalent
  • Minimum experience 5 years in Supply Chain / SAP environment
  • Very good knowledge of Supply Chain and SAP processes
  • Advanced Master's degree in Excel
  • Analysis capacity and ease in data management
  • Experience in continuous improvement and process optimization
  • Ability to manage cross-cutting projects in an evolving environment
  • Ability to federate, accompany and reassure teams in the phases of change
  • Initiative, strength and direction solutions
  • Excellent communication Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas). #LI-Hybrid

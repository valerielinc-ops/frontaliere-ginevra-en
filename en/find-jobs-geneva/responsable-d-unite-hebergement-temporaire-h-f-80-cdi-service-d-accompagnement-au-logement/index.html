Full description

Background In a context of significant development of its activities, the Temporary Accommodation Unit (UHT) is undergoing a change in its organisation, marked by an increase in staff, the diversification of its accommodation facilities and the forthcoming opening of two new residences. In order to accompany this evolution, the unit will now be placed under the responsibility of two unit managers at the same level, directly attached to the head of service. Working in pairs, the two managers jointly manage the unit, while each has a reference area comprising teams and accommodation structures. This organization is based on close collaboration, regular coordination and mutual support to ensure continuity of activities throughout the unit.

Your Mission

  • You ensure, in close cooperation with your partner, the smooth operation and control and guarantee the quality of the services delivered by the UHT in accordance with its mandate;
  • You guarantee, in emergency and temporary accommodation and relay apartments, a high-quality reception and accompaniment allowing the guests to move towards a sustainable accommodation;
  • You ensure the quality of life and social cohesion in accommodation places, especially through collective actions;
  • You are piloting the opening up of new structures and the evolution of existing schemes and the implementation of projects with your partner;
  • You develop the network of community partners and contribute to the improvement of practices and organization of unit and service.

Your main activities

  • You frame, mobilize and animate the teams in a logic of trust, autonomy and shared responsibility, and support them in complex situations;
  • You monitor some of the employees (follow-up, evaluation and development interviews, management of absences and replacements) and participate in the recruitment, integration and training of new employees;
  • You ensure a regular presence on your reference sites and can decide for all structures in the absence of your binome;
  • You ensure that the host framework and procedures are respected, that the practices are consistent and that the unit's pedagogical project is carried out, and that projects are carried out with the teams;
  • You follow up on issues related to buildings, works and developments with Real Estate, Facility Management and Watch and Human Security services;
  • You prepare and coordinate the opening of new residences and participate in the development of devices (relay apartments, slippery leases, new accommodation solutions);
  • You contribute to the monitoring of the budget and indicators, validate the administrative decisions and invoices of your perimeter and ensure reporting to the Chief of Service;
  • You collaborate with service units, CAS/SAIS and specialized units, and represent the unit with institutional and community partners including allocation commissions.

Your profile

  • Bachelor's degree in social work or equivalent training; additional training in management or social policy: an asset;
  • Experience of at least 3 years in the social field, ideally in collective, emergency or temporary accommodation;
  • Confirmed experience in team management and knowledge of internal management rules and staff status;
  • Good knowledge of the legal framework, the Geneva social network and the public in precarious situations; strong interest in housing and accommodation;
  • Project management experience with multiple partners, ideally structural opening: an asset;
  • Working in pairs: continuous sharing of information, concerted decisions and ability to decide alone for the whole unit;
  • Posture of confidence in teams, ability to federate and responsiveness to emergencies and unforeseen;
  • Mobility between sites and time flexibility according to field needs;
  • Proposal strength, administrative rigor, ease of writing and mastery of the usual computer tools.

We offer you

  • An open and respectful corporate culture;
  • Continuous skills development through attractive training policy;
  • Opportunities for career development and internal mobility;
  • A work-life balance - privacy;
  • Many cultural and sporting activities to live together within the institution.

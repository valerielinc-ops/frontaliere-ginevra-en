Full description

Introduction

You will play a key role in building meaningful relationships with CERN’s current and prospective donors throughout the entire donor cycle, while managing formal obligations such as proposal preparation and reporting. In doing so, you will help generate financial and strategic support for CERN by identifying funding opportunities, securing donations and grants, and building partnerships with corporations and foundations.

Functions

  • Development and implementation of fundraising strategies for CERN fundraising projects.
  • Identification, qualification, cultivation, and stewardship of donors, including planning and conducting of donor meetings, events, and experiences.
  • Research and pursue of funding opportunities with foundations, including writing proposals and reports.
  • Track, analyse, and report on fundraising performance and partnership impact.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to align fundraising with project goals, prepare donor agreements and reports.
  • Conceptualise and produce communications materials for fundraising purposes.
  • Support Due Diligence process.
  • Bachelor's Degree or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Fundraising, International relations or a related field.

Experience

  • Demonstrated xperience in fundraising, grants management, donor reporting and stewardship within a non-profit charitable context.
  • Proven ability to meet financial targets and manage multiple projects.
  • Experience contributing to initiatives aligned with organisational mission and values, delivering measurable impact.
  • Experience in fundraising for Science and Education or a related field is an asset.
  • Experience in using software for Graphic Design is an asset.
  • Experience in an international context is also an asset.

Technical competencies

  • Fundraising; in particular in science and education fields in a international set up.
  • Stakeholder relationship management.
  • Event organisation and management.
  • Use of a fundraising CRM; and Office Software Packages.

Behavioural competencies

  • Achieving Results: delivering high quality work on time and fulfilling expectations.
  • Building Relationships: being able to put self in the shoes of others in order to understand their needs and interests.
  • Communicating Effectively: delivering presentations in a structured and clear way; adjusting style and content to the audience; responding calmly and confidently to questions.
  • Working in the Interest of the Organization: behaving consistently with CERN's values and goals.
  • Managing Self: maintaining a positive outlook even in difficult situations; demonstrating resilience and persistence in response to setbacks and adversity.

Language skills

  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

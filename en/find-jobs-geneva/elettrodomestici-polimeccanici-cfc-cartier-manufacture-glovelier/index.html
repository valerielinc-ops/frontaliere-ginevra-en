For the fall of August 2027, we offer apprenticeship places in the following occupation:

POLYMECANICIAN LEARNING CFC

Description

You will train for 4 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC) for Polymechanics.

During this training, you will learn to make small, very precise, unique or prototype parts.

You will use conventional machines and CNC machines (digitally controlled).

You will also learn to program these machines and use techniques such as wire erosion or diving. And of course, you will participate in quality control and you will always seek to improve manufacturing processes.

Your profile

You've finished your compulsory education.

You're comfortable with mathematics.

You love to understand how things work.

You have manual skill.

You can concentrate and reason logically

You are motivated, curious and persevering

What we bring

Our trainers will accompany you throughout your learning. They will pass on their knowledge and provide you with the necessary guidance to develop your technical skills and know-how. The training is held in various departments of the company in order to gain a broad vision of the profession.

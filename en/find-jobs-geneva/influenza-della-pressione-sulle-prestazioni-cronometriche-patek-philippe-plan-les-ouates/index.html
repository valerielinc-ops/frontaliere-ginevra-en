Full description

Mission

Attached to our Research and Development Division, you will be responsible for studying the variations in walking of a mechanical movement depending on the pressure and establishing the influence of the characteristics of the movement and its environment.

Responsibilities

  • To achieve a state of art of knowledge of the effect of pressure on walking (patents/articles).
  • To determine experimentally the relationship between pressure and motion, depending on the characteristics of the oscillator.
  • Quantify the relationship between the pressure variations around the movement and the box construction (emboiting, actuators, time, ...).
  • Put in place predictive models of pressure variation and walking according to box geometry and motion characteristics.
  • Validate measurements and models from production data.
  • Quantify the impact of these effects on walking dispersions from the assembly of the movement to the customer's wear.

Profile sought

  • Currently in Master in Mechanical Engineering or Microtechnics.
  • Interest in watchmaking.
  • Programming skills (including Python) and machine learning.
  • Good project management skills.
  • Analytical mind.
  • Autonomy.
  • Proposal force.
  • Master's project lasting 6 months.

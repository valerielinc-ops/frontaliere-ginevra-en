Full description

  • Ensuring the overall direction and progress of the programme . You ensure the overall coherence and progress of the programme. You coordinate the various projects, their deadlines and interdependencies, manage the overall budget and anticipate risks. You take care of the management and resolution of cross-cutting issues, the effective implementation of decisions, and the achievement of objectives, within the deadlines and budgets set.
  • Support services in defining their needs. In close cooperation with the Real Estate Service, you identify, analyse and formalise with the teams the functional needs, usages, flows, and adaptations necessary for the organization of work, taking into account the specific needs of the beneficiaries.
  • Conducting relocation projects and ensuring operational transition. You plan and coordinate transfers, engage stakeholders, and ensure that all organizational, human and material conditions are met to allow the resumption of activities in the new premises, within the planned time frame, with minimal disruption. You take care of the resolution of difficulties until the operation stabilizes.
  • Pilot support to change teams and beneficiaries . You identify the impact of changes on work organisation, and work practices, define and implement accompanying measures with the relevant officials. You encourage the information, consultation and participation of collaborators and beneficiaries, and accompany them in the transition in their new spaces and modes of operation.
  • Mobilize and coordinate project teams. You coordinate the contributions of internal and external services, clarify roles and responsibilities, and ensure that commitments are met. You define and develop common methods to harmonize the conduct of moves and accommodation.
  • Higher education (HES, university or equivalent) in organization, engineering, real estate, management or related fields.
  • Further training in project management (IPMA, PMI, HERMES or equivalent), as well as training or significant experience in support of change.
  • At least five years of experience in program management and in conducting complex and cross-cutting projects, ideally linked to organizational transformations, business reorganization or site transfers.
  • Very good control of programme management, including the coordination of interlinked projects, the management of their overall progress, the management of cross-cutting risks, as well as the conduct of complex projects and support for change.
  • A very good understanding of organizational and operational issues related to accommodation, relocation and business continuity, with attention to the needs of teams and recipients.
  • Ability to bring together multidisciplinary teams without hierarchical authority, facilitate consultation and find shared solutions.
  • Excellent oral and written communication and synthesis capabilities. Autonomy, rigor, capacity to anticipate, sense of priorities, and ability to solve complex problems.
  • Knowledge of the public or medical and social sector is an asset.

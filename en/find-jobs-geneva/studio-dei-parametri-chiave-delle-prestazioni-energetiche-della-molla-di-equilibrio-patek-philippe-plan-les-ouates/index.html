Full description

Mission

Attached to our Research and Development Division, you will be responsible for studying the parameters influencing the energy performance of the balance-spiral oscillator, the regulating organ of our timepieces.

Responsibilities

  • To achieve a state of knowledge of the energy losses of the balance-spiral (patents/articles/internal studies).
  • Model analytically or in finite element simulation to theoretically quantify the influence of the parameters identified in the state of art.
  • Establish laboratory tests to quantify effects in practice.
  • Analyze and interpret results to understand and prioritize different factors and their impacts.
  • Validate the theoretical models from experimental data collected in the laboratory.
  • Make improvements to our models and our product.

Profile sought

  • Currently in Master in Mechanical Engineering or Microtechnics.
  • Good skills in structural modelling by finite elements.
  • Rigor, analytical and synthesis spirit and strength of proposal.
  • Ability to solve complex problems.
  • Interest in watchmaking.
  • Master's project lasting 6 months.

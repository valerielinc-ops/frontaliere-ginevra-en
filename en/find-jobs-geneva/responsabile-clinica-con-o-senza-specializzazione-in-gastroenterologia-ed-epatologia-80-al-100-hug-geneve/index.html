Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Medicine (DMED) provides comprehensive and specialized care for patients in hospitals and outpatients. Driven by a holistic, human approach based on best medical practices, it offers reference care in many specialties (cardiology, gastroenterology, pneumology, angiology and nephrology), with 24/7 availability. Engaged in clinical excellence, teaching and research, DMED promotes innovation, continuous skill development and patient-centred care, while ensuring the quality of life at work and the efficiency of benefits. Within this department, the Department of Gastroenterology and Hepatology takes care of patients and patients suffering from diseases of the digestive tract, liver, biliary tract and pancreas on a diagnostic and non-surgical basis, taking into account the services provided by other HUG services. It provides the endoscopic, percutaneous and transhepatic interventional services of the above-mentioned organs. This position is newly created and is part of the ALTER deployment, which aims to ensure that work organisation complies with the Labour Act (LTr).

Description of post

As head or head of clinic, you ensure the care of hospitalized and outpatient patients with gastroenterological and hepatological pathologies. You participate in the diagnostic and therapeutic activities of the service, including endoscopic procedures. You work closely with assistant and assistant physicians and multidisciplinary teams to develop the most appropriate diagnostic and therapeutic strategies. You demonstrate resilience, clinical sense and a high analytical capacity in handling sometimes complex situations. Thanks to your organizational sense and ability to prioritize, you effectively ensure your clinical activities while responding to multiple demands (patients and patients, telephones, correspondence, meetings). You are evolving in a demanding environment, at the interface of care, teaching and research. As such, you work closely with the medical team as a whole and ensure clear, fluid and respectful communication with both your colleagues and patients, adapting your speech to their understanding. By relying on your team spirit, you show solidarity with the unexpected. As head or head of clinic, you are actively involved in the supervision and training of intern physicians and medical trainees in the department of medicine in the field of gastroenterology and hepatology, adopting a mentoring posture and sharing your expertise.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in medicine or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO).

For chefs or chiefs of clinics with speciality status, you are entitled to an FMH title in gastroenterology and hepatology or you have a title deemed equivalent recognized by MEBEKO and are in possession of the cantonal right of practice or are able to obtain it before you enter office with the cantonal doctor of Geneva. You have a 4 year experience in general hospital medicine followed by 3 years in gastroenterology and hepatology. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

You have excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with the patient, you are a dynamic, flexible, committed and discreet person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability and team spirit and solidarity with the team member.

Further information

You can work both in daytime, nighttime and weekend hours.

Full description

Reference code: JR135986

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

Join one of the iconic houses of high watchmaking, where excellence is a constant requirement. Take part in our collective success and the creation of our future heritage.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

We are looking for a person at the end of the engineering course for a 6 month internship to pilot continuous improvement projects within our Manufacturing perimeter.

Attached to the Head of Manufacturing, you will be an ambassador of the culture of performance and continuous improvement.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

Give a concrete dimension to your engineering training: at the heart of our workshops, you will lead projects of continuous improvement in production, from initial diagnosis to implementation of the actions.

In contact with field teams, you will observe practices, analyze data and identify the levers of progress to build solutions adapted to the realities of production.

You will test and deploy the solutions with the teams, measure the results achieved and help to sustain improvements in workshop practices.

An experience to move from analysis to action and see in concrete terms the impact of your work.

QUALITIES FOR EXCELLING

Success in this role depends on your ability to lead projects with successful direction and excellent communication.

Very comfortable with Lean tools, you stand out thanks to your agility and your analytical and synthesis skills.

You master French (oral and written) very well and have a good level in office automation, including Excel.

Pragmatic and listening, you easily immerse yourself at the heart of the workshops to establish a dialogue of confidence with the production teams.

An observer, attentive and methodical, you can translate the reality of the field into factual analyses, structured reports and concrete recommendations.

As a powerful proposition, you are moving forward with enthusiasm, intellectual curiosity and a constant willingness to advance practices.

Vacheron Constantin celebrated its 271th anniversary as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.

As a Quality Assurance Intern (m/f/d), you will directly report to our Quality Assurance Manager, and you will join our Quality Assurance Department in Vernier (Switzerland)for minimum 6 months.

In this paid internship, you will work in a stimulating and collaborative environment where your proactivity will be valued. You will also have the chance to develop competencies in cosmetic GMPs and broaden your understanding of Quality Systems.

In this exciting role you will

Redesign existing GMP risk assessments, and continue working on process risks that could affect Quality (with a focus on the distillation area).Review shopfloor practices against GMP requirements: identify any gaps, raise alerts, and propose improvements.Maintain the current dashboard of Quality Indicators in Power BI, automate data collection, and improve the content to match operational needs (using SAP, Microsoft tools).Help coordinate the cleaning validation program and propose actions in case of deviations or failures.Occasionally participate in the daily tasks of the Quality Assurance Manager (e.g., deviation and complaint management, audit preparation and participation).Help create short training videos illustrating GMP rules.

Your success profile includes

  • Pursuing university studies (a **convention de stage/university agreement** is mandatory) in Chemical Engineering or Chemistry.
  • Competency in Data Sciences required.
  • Genuine interest in learning on the shopfloor.
  • French language mandatory, knowledge of written English language.
  • A curious and rigorous mindset.
  • Comfortable with computer tools (Microsoft Office, SAP), and handling data collection and analysis (statistics).

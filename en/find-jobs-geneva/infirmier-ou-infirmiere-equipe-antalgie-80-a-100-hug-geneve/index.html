Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With close to 1200 collaborators, the Department of Acute Medicine (DMA) includes the services of anesthesiology, clinical pharmacology and toxicology, intensive care and emergencies. It responds to a quadruple mission through its services that bring their expertise in frequent interaction with all the university hospitals in Geneva, thanks to a cross-sectional plateau, with the aim of ensuring the best possible care for patients. The Anesthesiology Service is a transversal service of the HUGs at the heart of the technical plateau. With its 7 medical units and a research and innovation unit, its mission is to ensure the comfort and safety of patients during therapeutic or diagnostic, medical or surgical, as well as in post-intervention care, while ensuring the proper management of pain. As Switzerland's largest centre in number of active acts per year, the Anesthesiology Service is at the forefront of progress in this area. The service also provides pre- and post-graduate and continuing education for nurses and physicians in specialized training. The services are also provided in the field of interventional analgia and in emergency situations in the hospital or pre-hospital setting alongside the Emergency Service.

Description of post

As part of your function as an intra-hospital and outpatient nurse, you ensure that patients with acute, complex or chronic pain are managed in all areas of the Cluse-Roseraie site. You perform specialized assessments of pain by analyzing its characteristics and functional impact, and accompany the patient in understanding chronic pain. In close collaboration with the doctor, you adjust the treatments, monitor their effectiveness, evaluate compliance, and adjust the management to prevent dose errors and chronic pain. You set up and monitor advanced analgesic techniques or devices. Using a non-drug approach, you use complementary techniques and the help relationship to promote physical and psychological pain relief. You conduct therapeutic education with the patient and their loved ones to strengthen therapeutic adherence, autonomy, and identify beliefs, resources and aggravating factors. A key actor of the interdisciplinary approach, you are actively collaborating with all health professionals and professionals to conduct a personalized and coordinated therapeutic project. Finally, you act as a reference or referant to the various medical-care partners in order to ensure the quality and consistency of analgesic strategies within the institution. As part of the job, 20% is required to be performed in the SSPI (a wake-up room) and SINPI (peri-intermediate care).

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree in nursing or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Swiss Red Cross. A two-year experience is required. Training in antalgia is an asset. Training in hypnotic practice is to be provided. You master current institutional applications and have excellent command of the French language both orally and in writing. You have a strong sense of organization, priority management and stress resistance. You have versatility, adaptability and excellent relational skills. Of a dynamic and motivated nature, you are listening and ready or ready to invest in an institution whose relational values you share: trust, respect, teamwork, recognition, equality and inclusion. Finally, you exercise discretion and diplomacy in your daily practice.

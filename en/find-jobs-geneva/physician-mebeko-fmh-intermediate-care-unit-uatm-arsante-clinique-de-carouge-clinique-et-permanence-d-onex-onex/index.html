Full description

Part-time post (60-80%) – Without guards. We are looking for a graduate doctor (MEBEKO or FMH, advanced assistant doctor or head of clinic) to join the medical care unit of a health institution recognized for its offer of continuing education and its commitment to organizational innovation. Your missions

  • Provide access and daily medical follow-up to patients in the service (present 60-80% spread over 5 days).
  • Write reports and exit letters , in close collaboration with the healthcare team.
  • Be a key player in medical coordination, in connection with the specialists of the health home and the technical plateau.
  • Participate in a friendly hospital service, oriented towards the quality of the journeys and the relevance of the care.
  • Work in an innovative interprofessional context, with great freedom of clinical and organizational initiative.
  • Adjust the trajectories of patients, often complex, to ensure appropriate and progressive management.
  • Actively contribute to institutional development, including the evolution of the UATM concept as a credible alternative to heavy hospitalizations. Post conditions
  • Activity rate: 60-80%, regular presence over five days.
  • No penalty, thanks to the permanent presence of an internal emergency service.
  • Integration into a team that is welcoming, experienced and accustomed to collaboration.
  • Environment known for continuing training, support and clinical quality. Profile sought
  • Recognized diploma in Switzerland (MEBEKO).
  • Ideally FMH title in general internal medicine or equivalent experience.
  • Confirmed clinical experience (advanced assistant physician or head of clinic).
  • Interest in coordination, care pathways and transitional medicine.
  • A sense of teamwork, autonomy, adaptability, and a taste for organizational innovation.

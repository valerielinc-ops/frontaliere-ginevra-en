Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a New Products Planner (H/F) whose main mission will be to ensure the production planning of new products in order to ensure their availability, while respecting quality and timeliness objectives. You will coordinate the various players in Supply Chain and Production in order to anticipate needs, secure industrial launches and contribute to the overall performance of flows.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and manage production plans for new products
  • Analyze demand needs and translate them into manufacturing plans
  • Identify capacity risks and propose arbitration scenarios
  • Monitor performance indicators: plan adhesion, service rate, load/capacity, etc.
  • Participate in planning reviews (S&OP, PDP, etc.)
  • Coordinating the various internal actors: production, supply, logistics, methods, quality, industrialization
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of planning processes and tools.

Profile

  • Diploma of engineer HES, EPF or university training (Bachelor or Master) in Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain, Logistics, Production or equivalent field
  • First professional experience from 2 to 5 years in Supply Chain, ideally in an industrial environment
  • APICS (CPIM or CSCP) certification is an asset
  • Good knowledge of industrial planning principles and Supply Chain
  • Mastery of ERP tools, ideally SAP, as well as of Excel
  • Ability to analyse data and make decisions based on indicators
  • Good understanding of production capacity and supply issues
  • Organization, rigour and autonomy in the management of priorities, including in an evolving environment
  • Excellent relational skills, sense of collaboration and ability to unite stakeholders
  • Proposition strength, conviction, sense of responsibility and good resistance to stress.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificate and diploma).

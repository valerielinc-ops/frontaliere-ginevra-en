Full description

Introduction

For our Procurement, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Security Officer whose main mission will be to ensure the security of the equipment of the various sites.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the safety engineer and supplement him with the technical aspects of safety.
  • Ensure that safety requirements are taken into account when acquiring and making available new equipment, from the preparation of specifications until they are received on site
  • Ensure the application of safety rules and procedures by technical services
  • Conduct and monitor general and specific risk analyses, including isolated work
  • Coordination with stakeholders: maintenance, building and infrastructure
  • Participate in the drafting of technical specifications and safety requirements for new equipment
  • Analyze compliance and risks related to equipment prior to commissioning; participate in technical approvals
  • Ensure safety monitoring of construction sites and intervene with suppliers if necessary
  • Support a technical network with maintenance, building and infrastructure teams
  • Conduct risk assessments and analyses, propose preventive measures and monitor their implementation
  • Write awareness or training reports, procedures, instructions and materials
  • Inform, advise and sensitize technical teams on safety rules.

Profile

  • Initial training with technical orientation, ideally in maintenance, electromechanical or equivalent domain. Further training in occupational safety and health is an asset
  • Good technical knowledge of machinery and equipment operation
  • Mastering the principles of occupational risk analysis and assessment
  • Ability to identify safety requirements for equipment, construction sites and technical installations
  • Ability to draft clear and structured specifications, procedures, reports and safety instructions
  • Mastery of common computer tools and editorial convenience
  • Ability to design and implement awareness-raising actions or appropriate training
  • Rigor in the analysis, synthesis and monitoring of actions
  • Good communication, listening and outreach capabilities
  • Relationship strength and ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholders
  • Diplomacy, respect and sense of service
  • Rigor, organizational sense and reliability
  • Spirit of analysis, proactivity and ability to propose pragmatic solutions
  • Ability to enforce safety rules with pedagogy and sanctity.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

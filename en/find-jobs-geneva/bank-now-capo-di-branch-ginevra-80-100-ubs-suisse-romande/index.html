Full description

Key responsibilities Are you a committed leadership personality? Do you know how to develop and maintain customer relationships? Then contact us. We are looking for an experienced sales personality who will take the helm of our Geneva branch.

Your main responsibilities

  • You lead, coach and motivate a team on the Geneva site and ensure a high level of sales quality and service.
  • You manage the branch using key performance indicators (e.g., the following): volume, quality, efficiency) and ensure the achievement of objectives.
  • You autonomously identify the fields of action, define concrete measures and implement them with your team.
  • You monitor day-to-day operational activities, ensure effective processing, and apply risk and compliance guidelines.
  • You convince by your competent advice to our customers in all areas of private credit and act as a model for your employees.
  • On the local market, you represent BANK-now, develop networks and maintain existing partnerships.

The team Want to participate in a new success story? At BANK-now SA you will find the ideal conditions for this. A subsidiary of the UBS Group, we are a Swiss bank specializing in credit and leasing.

Join a young and dynamic environment in the heart of Geneva and enjoy real opportunities for evolution within BANK-now SA.

Your skills and experience

  • Commercial or equivalent training, supplemented by successful continuing training and/or university degree.
  • Persuasive leadership person, with several years of experience and able to motivate and develop his team.
  • Passion for selling, acquiring new customers and developing existing customer relationships.
  • Excellent ease with the numbers: you pilot your activity using KPI, analyze the results and deduct concrete improvement measures.
  • Stress resistance, flexibility and ability to effectively define priorities, including in intense times.
  • Mastery of French and German, both oral and written; any other language is an asset.

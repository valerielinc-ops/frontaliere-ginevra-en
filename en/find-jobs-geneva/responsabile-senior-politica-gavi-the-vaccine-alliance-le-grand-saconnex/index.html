Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities The Senior Manager, Policy develops programmatic, funding and vaccine policies and investment cases that shape Gavi’s future direction. include:
  • Manage the delivery of robust evidence bases, actionable options and recommendations, and implementation plans aligned with Gavi’s strategic priorities, working closely with cross-Secretariat project teams;
  • Develop high-quality, insight-driven deliverables—including policy documents, presentations, decision memos and vaccine investment cases/assessments—for Gavi’s CEO, Senior Leadership Team, and Board;
  • Manage stakeholder engagement across the Alliance and broader global health ecosystem at technical and leadership level, facilitating transparent, inclusive processes and fostering alignment;
  • Provide policy interpretation and translate policy priorities into practical implementation plans, including facilitating integration into Gavi Secretariat guidelines, processes, and programmes.
  • As examples, the initiatives that the Senior Manager will lead cover the following questions:
  • What new vaccines from its current Vaccine Investment Strategy and its assessments of vaccines with epidemic and pandemic potential will Gavi adopt into its portfolio?
  • What will be Gavi’s approach to identifying and prioritising new vaccine investments in future strategic periods?
  • What is the design and roll-out plan for Vaccine Budgets in Gavi 6.0 that enable countries to maximise the impact on Gavi 6.0 Strategic Goals while strengthening predictable planning?
  • How can the Alliance further transform its cash and partner funding architecture for countries to enable maximum impact on its strategic goals while increasing simplicity?

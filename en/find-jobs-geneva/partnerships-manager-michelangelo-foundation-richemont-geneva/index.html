Full description

Reference code: JR136214

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

Reporting to the Executive Deputy Director, the Partnerships Manager’s main objective is to increase the financial resources of the Michelangelo Foundation to allow the international expansion of its programmes in the short and long term.

She/he is responsible to secure new sources of funding as per yearly objectives by building on the fundraising unit created 1.5 year ago, defining the strategy and yearly action plan including its implementation, nurturing a cross-cutting fundraising mindset within the Foundation, applying for national and global funding opportunities from high-profile individuals, corporates, trusts & foundations and institutional public donors and enlarging and animating the Foundation’s community of individual donors.

The ideal candidate understands the cultural fundraising landscape at a global level and comes with a track record in securing funds. She/he has a hands-on approach going from developing the network of prospects of the Foundation to securing funds, preparing deck and following all administrative matters.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Strategic Development & Team Management

  • Prepare, implement and monitor a fundraising strategy and a multi-year plan to develop and grow philanthropic support from a variety of benefactors sources (corporates, institutions, individuals).
  • Develops strategic relationships within the philanthropy and business communities to increase visibility of the Michelangelo Foundation and to foster financial support and networking potential.
  • Prepare yearly reporting of projects and programmes in collaboration with the different departments of the Foundation necessary for Fundraising initiative.
  • Responsible for the financial reconciliation between philanthropy and finance.
  • Manage team, budget and operational planning.

Benefactor Engagement

  • Cultivate and strengthen existing benefactors relationships and manages accounts including administration and database management and development.
  • Leverage Michelangelo Foundation networks to secure new contacts, influencers, and funders working hand-in-hand with the Executive Team.
  • Develop a local and international portfolio of benefactors including corporates, institutions and individuals who have capacity and inclination for supporting Michelangelo Foundation programmes.
  • Animate and grow the community of individual donors of the Foundation with a one-to-one relationship management approach.
  • Actively pursue prospects by using multiple techniques including one-on-one meetings, events, and other touchpoints.
  • Develop tools for fundraising and align and update gift acceptance policy and due diligence process and have them endorsed by the Executive Team and Board. Make these policy and process evolve as needed.
  • Ensure consistency of messaging in all fundraising communications according to brand identity. Prepare and/or supervise preparation of fundraising content and materials to ensure that the information is accurate, up to date and reflects Michelangelo Foundation values and mission.

Budgets and fundraising results

  • Achieve the yearly KPI’s defined for FY28 and Y+1.
  • Forecast revenue and project fundraising expenses for the multi-year plan.
  • Design standards for fundraising success, with monitoring indicators to measure and identify areas of success and opportunity.
  • Take accountability for the delivery of the plan to agreed objectives and activities, with monthly reporting and updates to the Executive Team.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • In depth knowledge of philanthropy environment and techniques.
  • In-depth knowledge of arts and crafts related business.
  • 10+ years of management working experience with a demonstrable record of fundraising success securing high-value gifts with various donor sources.
  • Track record in developing, growing, hosting and animating benefactors base from corporates to institutions and individual donors.
  • Experience in building, implementing, and delivering significant strategic programmes.
  • Proven record of line-managing a successful fundraising team.
  • Experience with reporting and budgeting.
  • Proficient experience in the practical use of personal IT equipment and Microsoft Office 365 suite and donor relationship management tools/database.
  • Transversal project management or cross function working experience.
  • Strong written, verbal, and digital communication skills, including representing the organization at events and in-front of high-profile donors.
  • Organization skills, with the ability to multi-task and prioritize projects while working to a deadline, or in a fast-paced environment.
  • An analytical mind-set, with the ability to solve problems.
  • Professional demeanour and strong work ethic.
  • Self-motivation with the capability to work independently.
  • Team player. Proven ability to work supportively and effectively within and across teams and build good working relationships.
  • Innovative and strategic thinker. Intellectually curious to ensure strategy is evolved to maintain momentum and maximise impact.
  • French and English speaking, Italian a plus.
  • Willing to travel domestically and internationally.

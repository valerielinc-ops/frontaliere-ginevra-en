Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1'300 employees, the Department of Psychiatry is responsible for a three-pronged mission in the area of psychological disorders, training and research. Thus the department's mission is to effectively detect and treat increasingly frequent and polymorphic pathologies in the respect of patients and their relatives. The Adult Psychiatry Service is responsible for the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders in adults and mental disorders starting in young adults as part of a public health mission. In the outpatient and hospital units of the service of the multiprofessional teams offer care integrating biological, psychotherapeutic and social treatments into a network with external partners. The Adult Psychiatry Service works according to the principles of community psychiatry that organizes the care around the patient in her environment and network. This approach is intended to help those in care maintain or develop social and/or professional activity in the community. It is also suggested that patients retain a large share of initiative in their journey to recovery. To this end, the therapeutic approach consists mainly of outpatient, mobile and daytime interventions. Temporary hospitalization is possible, however.

Description of post

As a proposaler, you want to contribute to the projects of a large institution such as the HUGs and recognise yourself in our institutional values? As an occupational therapist in adult psychiatry, you have the responsibility to design and implement an individualized therapeutic project around human occupations and adapted to the patient's or patient's status. You play a key role in promoting autonomy, rehabilitation and psycho-affective support for patients with psychiatric disorders. You are involved in an outpatient centre for integrated psychiatry and psychotherapy (CAPPI) offering psychiatric, psychosocial and psychotherapeutic treatments to patients in the Jonction sector. You are responsible for the conduct of individual and group occupational therapy treatment plans and the seizure of invoicing services. You manage and update patient records by documenting the services provided and clinical information. You show yourself synthetic in your information transmissions. You measure and reassess therapeutic objectives at interdisciplinary meetings (visits and/or symposia). You guarantee the quality and continuity of care in connection with other health professionals and professionals. Your function leads you to participate in the interprofessional projects of your department and to share your practice with the occupational therapists of the HUG.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree in Occupational Therapy or a diploma deemed equivalent recognised and registered by the Swiss Red Cross. You benefit from a proven experience with patients with psychiatric conditions and have good knowledge of the Geneva health network. You are an autonomous, creative person and have good integration and adaptation skills. You are open to changes, to questioning your practices and being able to work in therapeutic co-conduction. You must demonstrate an interest in developing ergotherapeutic practice in outpatient settings while cultivating interprofessionality. You have a good knowledge of yourself and your own limitations. You are able to develop social skills and pro-social behaviours, foster occupational engagement, develop the patient's intrinsic motivation, establish temporal-spatial benchmarks, identify the patient's resources and difficulties, improve, remedy, re-appropriate and maintain the patient's skills.

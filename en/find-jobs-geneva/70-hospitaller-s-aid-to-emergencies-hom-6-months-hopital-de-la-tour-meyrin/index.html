Full description

The La Tour Hospital has an emergency and medical facility open seven days a week and 24 hours a day. Our service includes all the infrastructure necessary to establish a diagnosis for first aid and surveillance regardless of the severity of the case. It also has a resuscitation room, a plaster room and various treatment and consultation rooms.

In order to complete our service we are recruiting an

70% Hospitaller's Aid to Emergencies - HOM 6 months (ref: 2034)

Your mission

In a state-of-the-art facility you have a variety of positions on a multidisciplinary team including nurses, health care workers, doctors.

You will need to accommodate patients with acute overall care.

As such you are in charge of;

  • Welcoming the patient and collecting administrative data
  • Take care of the patient as a whole in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team and meet its basic needs (hygiene, pain, food, comfort, ...)
  • Ensure the supply of equipment, verify their operation and ensure the management of maintenance, apply cleaning and disinfection procedures
  • Participate in the proper functioning of the service, make transmissions on the computerized care file
  • Updating of professional practices to service or institutional care procedures

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • 1 to 2 years minimum experience with a hospital structure
  • Diploma of Compulsory Caregiver
  • Self-governing, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and sense of priority are essential qualities

We offer you

Type of contract: Contract with maximum duration of 6 months Percentage: 70% Type of schedule : Irregular Start date: Ideally November 1, 2026

Senior Strategic Transformation Manager — EFG International AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Main responsibilities

Enterprise & Strategic Transformation

  • Drive bank-wide transformation initiatives aligned with the long-term strategy of EFG
  • Translate strategic priorities (growth, efficiency, client experience) into concrete transformation roadmaps.
  • Support Executive Committee members in strategy execution and transformation governance.
  • Prepare decision material for senior management and Board-level discussions

Operating Model & Process Excellence

  • Design and implement target operating models adapted to a relationship-driven private banking environment.
  • Support optimization of front-to-back processes across:
  • Client Relationship Management and Client Advisory
  • Investment Services and Portfolio Management
  • Operations and Middle Office
  • Lead cost-efficiency and productivity initiatives while preserving service quality and client experience

Digital & Data Transformation

  • Lead business-driven digital initiatives including:
  • CRM and client lifecycle management
  • Advisory and portfolio management tools
  • Process automation (workflow digitization, RPA, AI use cases)
  • Act as bridge between business, IT, and data teams to ensure adoption and value realization.
  • Promote data-driven management through dashboards, KPIs, and management information.

Regulatory & Risk-Driven Change

  • Manage transformation initiatives related to FINMA regulation, AML/KYC, suitability, cross-border rules.
  • Ensure regulatory changes are embedded pragmatically into business processes.
  • Work closely with Compliance, Risk, Legal, and Internal Audit.

Change Management & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Act as an internal change leader and sparring partner for senior executives and department heads.
  • Drive change management: communication, training, and adoption across the organization.
  • Manage resistance to change in a traditional private banking culture.

Program & Delivery Management

  • Own end-to-end delivery of transformation initiatives:
  • Governance, milestones, KPIs, and benefits realization
  • Budget and resource management
  • Risk, dependency, and issue management
  • Coordinate internal teams and selectively manage external consultants and vendors.

Skills and experience

  • Pure Private Banking Expertise, with hands-on understanding of:
  • Relationship-based private banking business model
  • Investment products and services
  • UHNW/HNW client expectations
  • Strong sensitivity to confidentiality, risk culture, and reputational consideration
  • Transformation & Execution
  • Enterprise and functional transformation
  • Operating model design
  • Business case development and benefits tracking
  • Program and portfolio management
  • Digital & Analytical Skills
  • Ability to translate technology into business value
  • CRM platforms, Front office / Investment applications and private banking core systems
  • Process automation and digitalisation / AI in banking
  • Data analytics and performance management
  • Leadership & Personal Skills
  • Executive-level communication and presentation skills
  • Strong influencing skills without formal authority
  • Structured problem-solving and analytical rigor
  • High discretion, resilience, and ownership mindset
  • Strong cultural fit with Swiss private banking values
  • Experience & Background
  • Background / profile : Chief of Staff, Internal transformation, COO Office, or strategy roles within private banks and/or top-tier strategy/management consulting
  • 10–15 years of professional experience
  • Proven track record delivering in-house transformation initiatives
  • Strong exposure to Swiss private banking market and regulatory environment
  • International experience considered an asset
  • Education
  • MBA from a leading business school
  • Master’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Engineering, or equivalent
  • Languages
  • Fluent English (mandatory)
  • French and/or German (strongly preferred)

Our Values

  • Accountability: Taking ownership for tasks and challenges, as well as seeking continuous improvement
  • Hands-on: Being proactive to rapidly deliver high-quality results
  • Passionate: Being committed and striving for excellence
  • Solution-driven: Focusing on client outcomes and treating clients fairly with a risk-aware mindset
  • Partnership-oriented: Promoting collaboration and teamwork. Working together with an entrepreneurial spirit.

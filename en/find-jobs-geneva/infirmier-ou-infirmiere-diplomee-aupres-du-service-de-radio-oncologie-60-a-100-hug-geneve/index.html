Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Department of Oncology provides diagnostic and therapeutic support to adult patients and patients suffering from cancer and to support the patient and her relatives. The objective is to ensure access to the best anticancer treatment, to offer appropriate care and to anticipate the needs of the hospitalized patientel (mell transplant, cell therapy, immunotherapy, etc.). Together, the Cancer Centre allows for the coordination of cross-cutting activities dedicated to cancer issues between departments and departments. It consists of 4 departments (haematology, oncology, precision oncology and radio-oncology) and is composed of about 300 collaborators. The mission of the radio-oncology service is to assess the therapeutic indication of patients referred to it and to provide them with information on radiotherapy. Medical specialty responsible for the therapeutic use of ionizing radiation, radio-oncology is particularly used in the context of tumour diseases. The service applies ionizing radiation treatments and conducts clinical monitoring of patients, both during treatment and after administration, to assess their response and ensure their optimal treatment tolerance. He is a university-based department and is also dedicated to research and teaching.

Description of post

In the HUG radio-oncology department, you provide nursing consultations, technical care and clinical follow-up for patients in outpatient care. You practice on the plateau of the service, in its four dicasteries, especially those of the ORL and the breast. Your activity also includes nursing patients receiving high-dose vaginal curietherapy (HDR). You mobilize your oncology expertise to assess needs, provide clinical surveillance, and contribute to the prevention and management of adverse drug reactions. You adapt your interventions to the changing situation, from a preventive, curative or palliative perspective, and provide care within your autonomous role or medical prescription. Therapeutic education is an integral part of your activity. You adapt the information and advice to the needs of each person in order to promote their understanding of treatment, participation in care and autonomy. You work closely with radio-oncologists, medical radiology technicians, medical physicists, medical physicists and other service professionals. You participate in the coordination of the journey and the continuity of care with the intra and extrahospital partners. You document your assessments and interventions in the Integrated Patient Record (IPD) and in ARIA. You apply the radiation protection instructions specific to the activities of the service. You contribute to improving care practices and processes, enhancing nursing activity and service development projects.

Qualifications

Your clinical judgment, rigor and organizational sense allow you to set priorities and intervene independently in complex or urgent situations. You have 3 years of experience and you combine listening quality, communication accuracy and teamwork skills. You establish a relationship of trust with patients and contribute to respectful interprofessional collaboration. You can cope with the emotional burden of care situations, recognize your limitations and mobilize the necessary resources. You demonstrate reliability, availability and adaptability. You question your practices and engage in updating your knowledge and developing your skills. You participate in the internal mobility of the Department of Oncology according to the organization defined by the frameworks and the required skills.

Job Title Senior Consultant, Artificial Intelligence & Data (Data Management & Data Platforms focus) Business Line: Technology & Transformation Location: Geneva or Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80–100% Start date: Ongoing Experience Level: Experienced About the Role Join Deloitte AI & Data as a Senior Consultant focused on data management and data platforms. You will design and deliver solutions that help clients improve how they govern, structure and use data, translating business objectives into practical technical choices. Work across data strategy, architecture, transformation and Data & AI use cases alongside colleagues and clients in Basel, Geneva or Zurich. You can deepen your expertise through varied project work, knowledge sharing and the team’s learning and mentoring opportunities. How you can make an impact

  • Design and implement data management solutions covering data quality, master and reference data, and catalogues, as well as modern data platforms that make data usable for clients.
  • Shape data strategies around clients’ regulatory, cost and revenue objectives; translate diverse business and technical requirements into coherent solution designs and guide delivery of complex data and analytics transformations.
  • Drive Data & AI use cases from the business-value concept through to production, applying architectural, technical and organisational thinking to the changes clients need to make.
  • Build trusted client relationships through delivery, workshops and business-development activity; help turn prospective opportunities into new projects.
  • Guide colleagues and implementation teams, share your subject-matter knowledge and continue building expertise across industries and data topics. What you bring to succeed
  • Bring at least three years of relevant practical experience and technical knowledge of data topics gained in consulting, a technology company or industry; work independently across concurrent activities.
  • Translate between business and IT, consolidate diverse requirements into clear data-solution designs, and communicate architectural decisions and technical detail in writing, presentations, one-to-one discussions and workshops.
  • Bring hands-on delivery experience in at least one of two areas: two or more projects implementing master data management, data quality, reference-data or catalogue solutions; or two or more projects implementing cloud-based data lakehouses.
  • In your chosen area, have worked across vendor selection, implementation and operation, and helped lead nearshore or offshore implementation teams. For the platform route, also bring experience designing and documenting data and technical architectures and knowledge of common development methodologies.
  • Use English fluently to work with clients and colleagues across teams. What sets you apart
  • Hands-on experience with one or more relevant solutions: Collibra, Informatica, Azure or GCP for data management; or Azure, GCP, AWS, Snowflake or Databricks for cloud data platforms.
  • Fluency in German and/or French to support conversations with a wider range of clients and colleagues.
  • Experience preparing or leading workshops with prospective clients and shaping business-development opportunities. Your team Join a diverse, multinational AI & Data team of 50+ colleagues across Zurich or Geneva. The team works on how organisations structure, source, transform, store and use data across a range of client projects. You will work with colleagues across the practice and have access to training, mentoring and on-the-job learning. Meet our people Want to learn more about what it's really like to work at Deloitte? The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

Full description

Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years we have been joining hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, proposing them the best talents. We will not lie to you however: we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Electrician

Your assignment

  • Read, interpret and analyze with precision electrical designs and diagrams in accordance with the current NIBT standards.
  • Run independently the electrical tracking, ensuring a rigorous and conformable installation on site.
  • Build the recess of electrical systems in walls and in the sections, especially during the jet, ensuring a perfect and lasting integration.
  • Perform the installation of electrical pipes with great precision, respecting the rules of art for cable protection and ease of access.
  • Install the recessed boxes ensuring correct positioning and fixing, in order to ensure functionality and safety.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Professional admission (CFC) as an electrician installer or equivalent title recognized in the Swiss electric sector, as evidence of your solid technical skills.
  • Impeccable approach and rigorous application of the updated NIBT standards, to guarantee the conformity and safety of the plants.
  • Proven ability to read and interpret complex plans and diagrams, indispensable support for your operating autonomy.
  • Excellent ability in precise and autonomous electrical tracking, which optimizes the positioning of the equipment in compliance with the standards.
  • Technical competence for the incorporation of electrical systems in the walls and the sections, especially during the casting phase, ensuring the perfect integration of the networks.
  • Rigor and precision demonstrated in the recess of the electrical installations, guaranteeing durability and ease of maintenance.
  • Experience in the accurate installation of the recessed boxes, key element for the functionality and safety of the circuits.

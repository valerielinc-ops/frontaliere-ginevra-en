Full description

Reference code: JR132609

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

Within our Manufacture du Brassus (VD) you orchestrate and optimize all the physical and information flows related to production, from supply to shipping, in order to guarantee optimal adherence to the production plan.

To do this, you directly coordinate planning/scheduling activities (4 people) and indirectly, receptions/shipments (9 people) while conducting a global approach to optimizing flows and processes.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

OPERATIONAL PILOTAGE & TRANSVERSE COORDINATION

  • Actively participate in the S&OP process to ensure load/capacity matching and anticipate production needs.
  • Pilot performance analyses and associated action plans with relevant and results-oriented KPIs.
  • Ensure monitoring and control of operations in order to achieve quality, cost and timeliness objectives.
  • Work closely with cross-cutting teams (Movements Development, Purchasing, Quality, Production) to ensure effective coordination and proactive communication.
  • Coordinate production launches related to the Production Master Plan.
  • Supervise Central Warehouse operations and ensure smooth management of logistics flows.

OPTIMIZATION OF OPERATIONAL FLUX & EXCELLENCE

  • Support the transformation of flows and processes to strengthen organizational agility and robustness.
  • Identify, prioritize and lead continuous improvement initiatives consistent with the site's strategic issues.
  • Optimize logistics and industrial flows by applying the Lean Manufacturing principles (Kanban, CONWIP, stream drawn, standardization).
  • Structure processes and strengthen operational standards to ensure sustainable performance improvement.
  • Develop a problem-solving, innovative and operationally effective culture.

MANAGEMENT OF EQUIPMENT & DEVELOPMENT OF SKILLS

  • Manager and develop an efficient team: plan and organize the work, support and empower employees to achieve the expected results.
  • Promote skills development: managing the succession plan, promoting versatility, identifying training needs and encouraging the professional development of each team member.

QUALITY FOR EXCELLING

From an engineering training (or equivalent) in Supply Chain, Industrial Production Management or Logistics, you benefit from a 10-year managerial experience in an industrial environment.

You are able to position yourself both strategically and operationally to support the production activity of the site.

You are in perfect control of SAP, office tools including Excel and Power BI.

Vacheron Constantin celebrates 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a global network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

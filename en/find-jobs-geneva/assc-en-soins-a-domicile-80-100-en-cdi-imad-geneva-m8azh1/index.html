Full description

Attached to a home support team, the ASSC provides services independently, while respecting the client's lifestyle, beliefs, values ​​and culture. He/she works interdisciplinarily and interprofessionally, respecting the mission and objectives of the institution. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Provide basic care and medical-technical procedures, delegated by the referring nurse, in a global approach to the client
  • Promote the restoration of the client's state of health, the strengthening or maintenance of their autonomy to enable them to restore, continue or adapt their lifestyle so that they can stay or return home
  • Evaluate and re-evaluate practical help situations and assume reference to them
  • Carry out practical assistance services while respecting the client's lifestyle and their environment
  • Promote the maintenance of links with those around you and collaborate with them by determining resources and providing information and support.
  • Federal certificate of ASSC capacity and authorization to practice in the canton of Geneva
  • Sense of communication and relationships with others, quality of listening
  • Ability to assess situations and act responsibly
  • Resistance to stress and physical work
  • Ability to work independently and interprofessionally within a multidisciplinary team
  • Essential mobility (by bike in town / on foot / by car / by TPG).
  • Entry into office: to be agreed

  • an up-to-date curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.
  • Right to practice
  • We only consider complete application files

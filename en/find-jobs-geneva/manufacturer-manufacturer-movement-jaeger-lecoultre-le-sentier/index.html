Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its own inventive spirit through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

For our Manufacture located at the Sentier (Vallée de Joux - Canton de Vaud), and within our development department movement, you are in charge of designing and developing the core range and high watchmaking movements in collaboration with the Project Manager, Design, Marketing and Methods.

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT? YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Conduct pre-study, design and construction on CAD-3D software and manage PLM attributes.
  • Ensure the life cycle of our products and the availability of technical data via our PLM.
  • Monitor the plan record and ensure compliance.
  • Conduct design reviews.
  • Support development until product is approved.
  • Work closely with the watchmaker laboratory and methods.
  • Providing technical support and updating of technical files

THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS? YOUR SKILLS! To benefit from higher education in watchmaking construction or microtechnology with option in watchmaking construction, you have a proven experience of at least 5 years in the field of watchmaking movement construction, as well as solid concepts of materials, calculations of structures in numerical simulations as well as the mastery of NIHS, GPS ... . Passionate by the Haute Horlogerie and the complications, you are rigorous, methodical and creative. You enjoy working as a team, while Being autonomous on a daily basis. The mastery of a first PLM software and the mastery of the PTC Creo software are advantages.

Your assignment

  • Ensure installation, configuration and commissioning of electronic security systems: video surveillance (CCTV), access control and intrusion detection.

  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance of plants.

  • Knowledge of solutions such as Genetec, Milestone, Lenel or equivalent systems is an advantage.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical plans and schemes.
  • Autonomy, rigour, analysis and customer orientation.

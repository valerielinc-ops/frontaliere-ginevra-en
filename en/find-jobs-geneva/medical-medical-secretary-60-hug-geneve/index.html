Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. This department, consisting of nine medical services, is responsible for providing diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The mission of the Genetic Medicine Service is to provide the population with a state-of-the-art clinical advisory service and genetic diagnostic laboratory services, taking into account the analyses provided by other UGH services. It provides in-house and external laboratory services for outpatient and in-patient care. The genetic medicine department uses specialized methods (PCR, high-throughput DNA sequencing, MLPA, CGH array, etc.) to search for genetic changes (mutations, deletions, insertions, etc.) on any type of sampling. The objective is to make a pre- or post-natal diagnosis, confirm a genetic disease or diagnose malignancies.

Description of post

As part of your mission, you must register applications for a genetic medicine examination sent to the department, ensuring completeness of data, adherence to procedures and good management of priorities, in collaboration with medical teams and laboratories. You must support telephone calls and service emails and mails, format medical reports and ensure the effective management of their transmission. You work with other medical and medical secretaries to process the entire medico-administrative flow, since the receipt of applications for medical examination by guaranteeing speed and quality of execution. You work in a team under the supervision of Prof.ABRAMOWICZ Marc, to ensure the proper functioning of the activities of the medical secretariat.

You are also led or led to

Ensure that all requests to the medical secretariat are handled and followed up, as well as their rigorous and standardized documentation in the patient's administrative and medical records. Actively contribute to optimizing administrative flows and continuous process improvement.

Qualifications

You have a commercial maturity and/or ideally a medical secretary degree, or other equivalent administrative training. You justify at least 5 years of experience in a secretariat and have excellent command of the French language. Experience in the medical or laboratory environment, knowledge of medical terminology and interest in developing them are assets. Basic knowledge in English and German will also be appreciated. Pre-hospital experience and knowledge of institutional tools for HUGs such as IPR, DPA, GRECO, OPALE may also be an advantage. You master Office IT tools (ECDL advanced for Word, Excel and PowerPoint standard, as well as advanced for MS Outlook) and are comfortable with computerised systems such as laboratory management systems and databases. Rigor, teamwork, procedural compliance, a high level of priority, discretion and confidentiality are essential to the processing of health data. You are professional and efficient, even in demanding situations, whether dealing with urgent requests, managing sensitive communications or dealing with emotionally sensitive contexts. You are motivated or motivated to contribute to the efficiency, quality and excellence of the missions of the Service of Genetic Medicine, for the benefit of patients and all employees, in a key position within an interprofessional team composed of administrative staff, laboratory technicians, doctors, researchers, etc. We offer you a stimulating working environment in a dynamic team in close collaboration with medical-technical professionals and professionals, where you will have the opportunity to develop your skills and contribute to the excellence of the services and organization of the service. We are committed to support your professional development and value your contribution.

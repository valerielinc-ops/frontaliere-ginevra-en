Full description

Reference code: JR133189

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

Our SAP platform is at a pivotal moment; moving to RISE, migrating to s/4, embracing Clean Core, and building the BTP foundations that will power our next decade of innovation. The Platform Architect is the person who connects all these dots: architecture, extensibility, developer enablement, and governance. If you want to shape a global SAP landscape across a portfolio of iconic luxury Maisons, this is the role.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Within the SAP platform team, the SAP Platform Architect defines and governs the SAP platform architecture across S/4HANA (ERP, EWM, GTS…), SAP BTP, and all integration touchpoints. Shaping the technology landscape, setting guardrails, and ensuring every extension or customisation is justified, sustainable, and future-proof.

You will lead the OSBF Design Authority, promote Clean Core principles and SAP's extensibility strategy (side-by-side vs. in-app), and ensure alignment with both SAP's product roadmap and our enterprise architecture.

Your impact spans across a multi-stream technical roadmap covering 4 strategic pillars:

  1. Architecture Vision & Roadmap
  • Own and drive the architectural direction of the platform across Core Architecture, BTP & Development.
  • Participate in strategic architectural decisions such as use cases for BTP, Federated platforms (Mendix, Tulip, Pega…), ERP/EWM decoupling, S/4HANA continuous evolution, including associated topics such as Job Scheduling or establishing a Printing Platform for the group.
  • Collaborate with the Enterprise Architecture team on cross-cutting concerns such as Disaster Recovery design and enterprise-wide technology alignment.
  • Evolve the Platform to further integrate CI/CD practices.
  • Integrate Richemont AI strategy in the platform roadmap & design.
  • Define consumable and reusable services for product teams that accelerate delivery, increase product teams' autonomy, and reduce the need for custom development, duplicated effort, and reliance on central platform expertise.
  1. Clean Core Governance & Extensibility Strategy
  • Define and enforce Clean Core guidelines: minimise custom code, maximise standard, and leverage key-user extensibility and BTP side-by-side extensions.
  • Own the decision framework for "where to extend": in-app (BAdIs, RAP, key-user extensibility) vs. side-by-side (CAP on BTP, Integration Suite, or Federated Platforms).
  • Guide product teams on the right extensibility pattern for each use case.
  • Conduct architecture reviews for all significant developments.
  1. Standards, Patterns & Guardrails
  • Integrates Group Standards for reusable architecture patterns, in particular for API design and ownership, event-driven integration, data models, CDS views, and Fiori apps.
  • Champion configuration as Code for sap platforms, including BTP (Terraform) which adheres to Richemont guidelines in terms of security, Identity management and connectivity.
  • Publish and maintain Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) and reference architectures.
  • Guide the technical archiving and data volume management strategy and application retirement (ILM).
  • Provide architectural direction on data lifecycle decisions that impact platform performance, storage costs, and compliance.
  1. Community & Enablement
  • Participate in cross-team design reviews and lead the SAP platform Architecture Board.
  • Establish and animate an Architect Community across the product teams.
  • Stay ahead of SAP technology evolution through continuous learning, SAP community engagement, and vendor dialogue.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

Education & Experience

  • 10+ years of progressive experience in SAP ecosystems, with deep expertise across S/4HANA, SAP BTP, and hybrid integration landscapes.
  • 3+ years in an SAP platform architecture role.
  • Master’s degree in computer science, engineering, or equivalent practical experience.

Technical Expertise

  • SAP S/4HANA: Deep understanding of S/4HANA architecture, Clean Core methodology, custom code analysis (ATC, Custom Code Migration app), and extensibility options (BAdIs, RAP, key-user extensibility).
  • SAP BTP: Strong knowledge of the BTP service portfolio — CAP, Integration Suite, SAP Build Work Zone, HANA Cloud, AI Core / Generative AI Hub, Event Mesh, Destination & Connectivity services.
  • Architecture Patterns: Proven expertise in API-first design, event-driven architecture, side-by-side extensions, and Fiori/UX architecture.
  • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines, configuration as code - Terraform
  • Security Awareness: Solid understanding of SAML 2.0, OIDC, SSO, and identity management concepts
  • RISE: Understanding of the RISE with SAP operating model and SAP Cloud ALM.

Soft Skills & Behaviours

  • Visionary yet pragmatic: Balances long-term architecture ambition with short-term delivery reality (strategic vs. tactical).
  • Strong conviction with open mind: Defends architectural principles but listens to field constraints.
  • Excellent communicator: Explains complex architecture concepts to business stakeholders, product teams, and technical audiences alike.
  • Collaborative authority: Influences without commanding; earns trust through expertise. Works with security, operations, and enterprise architecture — not in place of them.
  • Systems thinking: Sees the whole SAP landscape as interconnected and integrated within a global, heterogeneous enterprise landscape. Never forgets the Enterprise Architecture guiding principles.
  • Intellectual curiosity: Continuously tracks SAP innovation and industry best practices.

  • Curiosity and sound judgement: you ask thoughtful questions, challenge assumptions constructively and stay open to new approaches .
  • A collaborative mindset, clear communication and a willingness to share knowledge and support others.

  • Expertise with at least one of the following platforms: Azure, Google Cloud Platform , AWS, Snowflake , Databricks or Informatica

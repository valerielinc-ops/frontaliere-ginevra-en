Location: Zurich, Geneva Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% About the Role Help our clients navigate the future with confidence by turning complex data challenges into long lasting impact. Deloitte AI & Data help leading organisations across industries rethink what their data can do. We work on the platforms that make information easier to trust, connect and use—giving clients the freedom to move faster and decide with confidence. We bring together data engineering, data science and architecture expertise to address complex challenges. Working closely with clients, we design and deliver solutions that meet today’s needs and create room for what comes next. This role is open to candidates with a background in data platforms, d ata e ngineering and/or data . During the interview process, we’ll explore how your skills, experience and ambitions align with the team’s growth targets. How you can make an impact

  • Design and implement data strategies and modern data solutions – including data management, platforms and architectures – that enable clients to achieve their business objectives
  • Provide architectural, technical and organizational thought leadership to guide complex data and analytics transformations with significant organizational implications.
  • Deliver impactful data solutions from value concept to production , to establish robust data foundation s for the client’s ambitions, including AI enablement
  • Build and maintain trusted client relationships through project delivery and business development, expanding your network internally and externally.
  • Be come a role model for people you work with by demonstrating strong work ethic, leadership, and subject matter competence.
  • Continuously grow your knowledge across industries and share insights with Deloitte talent. What you bring to succeed
  • A background in data, with hands-on experience using programming languages such as Python or SQL to analyse, transform or validate data.
  • The ability to absorb complex information quickly, structure a problem and translate between business needs and technical requirements.
  • Curiosity and sound judgement: you ask thoughtful questions, challenge assumptions constructively and stay open to new approaches .
  • A collaborative mindset, clear communication and a willingness to share knowledge and support others.
  • Fluency in English and professional fluency in German or French , enabling you to work confidently with clients and colleagues. What sets you apart
  • Expertise with at least one of the following platforms: Azure, Google Cloud Platform , AWS, Snowflake , Databricks or Informatica
  • Familiarity with tools or practices such as Git, DevOps, dbt or Fivetran is a plus
  • Experience working with clients or stakeholders across different business and technical teams.

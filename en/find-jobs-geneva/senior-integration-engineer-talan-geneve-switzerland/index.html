Full description

We are looking for a Senior Integration Engineer to strengthen a team specializing in application integration and APIs.

You will work on designing, developing, and maintaining robust, secure, and scalable integration solutions, enabling smoother data exchanges between different applications and platforms.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain integration solutions in Python, Java, and SQL Server.
  • Develop and manage APIs, particularly with FastAPI, and contribute to their governance through an API Management platform.
  • Implement real-time data flows with Kafka and event-driven and microservices architectures.
  • Deploy and maintain integration components in OpenShift environments.
  • Ensure the reliability, security, performance, and availability of critical services.
  • Participate in resolving complex incidents and continuously improving existing solutions.
  • Leverage generative AI tools to accelerate development, testing, log analysis, and documentation, while validating the quality of the results produced.
  • Collaborate with technical and functional teams and ensure the documentation of implemented solutions.

Desired profile

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in application integration.
  • Very good command of Python and Java, as well as SQL Server.
  • Practical experience developing APIs with FastAPI.
  • Good command of Kafka and streaming and messaging mechanisms.
  • Experience with an API Management platform, ideally Gravitee.
  • Experience with OpenShift and containerized environments.
  • Expertise in microservices architectures, event-driven architectures, and Enterprise Integration Patterns.
  • Knowledge of ETL/ELT processes and data transformation and exchange challenges.
  • Practical experience with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and its integration into complex application architectures.
  • Use of AI assistants for development and automation, with an ability to critically evaluate generated code.
  • Ability to design and maintain critical, secure, and scalable services.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Additional assets

  • knowledge of Talend ESB, Apache Camel or other integration tools.

Education

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in computer science or an equivalent field.

Technical environment

  • Python, Java, SQL Server, FastAPI, Kafka, Gravitee, OpenShift, API Management, Microservices, Event-Driven Architecture, ETL/ELT, MCP and AI tools applied to development.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

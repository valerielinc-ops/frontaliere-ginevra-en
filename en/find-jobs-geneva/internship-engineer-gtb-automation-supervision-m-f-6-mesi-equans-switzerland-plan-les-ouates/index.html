Full description

As soon as possible or to be agreed – duration 6 months – flexibility on start dates. To cope with its development, Equans Switzerland, based in the Geneva region, offers a student internship in the 5th year of engineering school within our department Security & Automation.

Title of the course

Integration of AI into the various tasks to carry out a project of Technical Building Management.

Your mission

As a first step you will appreciate with our teams the tasks of writing functional analyses, the design and programming of GTB controllers and supervisors as well as the specific techniques used in commissioning. Following this discovery phase, you will be asked to implement various AI tools to optimize the different tasks of our employees. This internship will allow you to explore cutting-edge technologies and contribute to the intelligent digitalization of our infrastructures. You will have the opportunity to familiarize yourself with automatons from the Beckhoff, Schneider, SAIA .... and supervisors Ignition, PC-Vue, Iconics....

Your profile

Student in the final year of engineering school or higher education level baccalaureate+5, with a specialization in computer science, industrial computer science or automation, you are looking for a six-month term of study. You have a good foundation in programming and have a special interest in artificial intelligence and its practical applications in technical trades. You want to discover or deepen your knowledge in computer programming, supervision and Building Engineering Management (GTB). Knowledge of building automation or technical facilities management (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, lighting and blinds) is an asset, but it is not essential: our teams will help you discover these areas. Rigoury and passionate about technique, you have a taste for teamwork and want to invest in a developing group.

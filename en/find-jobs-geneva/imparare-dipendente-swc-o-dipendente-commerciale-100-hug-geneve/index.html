Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The learning offer of HUGs is developing and diversifying. Apprenticeships cover 24 different occupations in several areas of hospital activity. Find out more about HUG learning or watch videos of HUG learning. To contribute to your success, your guidance is provided by certified trainers and, throughout your training, internships are organised within the institution or in collaboration with partner companies and institutions in order to guarantee you a diversified, innovative and quality training. For the fall of 2027, we are looking for our future apprentice commercial employees and apprentice commercial employees CFC.

Description of post

During your learning, you are in direct contact with patients and participate in various secretarial activities. In particular, you contribute to the management of correspondence, whether it is writing mail or e-mail. You also take care of certain accounting tasks, such as writing and tracking payments. In addition, you manage orders, from the receipt of material to the billing. Your training allows you to evolve in a variety of environments: reception and billing centres, admissions, medical secretariats, executive secretariats or human resources services. Throughout your training, you will discover a variety of administrative learning areas to support the reception and quality of patient care. Every year, a departmental tournus is offered, which is an added value in terms of employability.

Qualifications

You have successfully completed your compulsory education and are looking for an apprenticeship in the administrative field. Thanks to your relationship comfort and your sense of hospitality, you guarantee a suitable service. Your methodical mind and ease of writing allow you to make quality renderings. You show a motivation to discover a varied working environment, at the service of the population. You have an appeal for the hospital environment, you are ready or ready to invest in vocational training alternating work in company, practical and theoretical courses at school.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.

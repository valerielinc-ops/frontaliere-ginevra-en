Full description

Passionate about the world of luxury and driven by the sense of service, you will contribute to the success of our AP House in Geneva, offering our current and potential customers an exceptional experience. In line with our strategy, your mission will be to create positive and lasting relationships through comprehensive, meaningful and exciting experiences. Your responsibilities:

  • As a true ambassador of the House, you embody our values and develop a privileged relationship with each customer, with the aim of creating authentic and lasting ties.
  • You take care of the entire customer journey: reception, discovery, advice, sale and follow-up, while ensuring irreproachable operational and administrative support.
  • You contribute to pre- and post-sales activities, guarantee an experience of excellence and actively participate in the development of our brand thanks to your sense of detail, rigor and result orientation.
  • You work closely with your team and the various departments in a spirit of trust and solidarity. If individual performance is valued, collective success remains at the heart of our culture.
  • You develop and animate your client portfolio: prospecting, loyalty, attentive and personalized monitoring. Profile sought:
  • You justify at least 7 years of successful experience in the sale of luxury products, ideally acquired in prestigious houses operating in a demanding and highly customer oriented environment.
  • You master luxury codes and excel in the art of developing a customer base, maintaining lasting relationships and offering an exceptional customer experience.
  • With a strong sense of outcome, you excel in creating human relationships and place customer experience at the centre of your priorities.
  • You are comfortable with CRM tools and recognize the importance of rigorous customer monitoring. You ensure the quality of the data, the monitoring of commercial actions and the proper management of administrative tasks related to sales activity.
  • Driven by the taste of the challenge, you're also a real player, committed and positive.
  • You have a perfect command of French and English, communicate with ease and demonstrate listening, depathy and relationship maturity.
  • An experience in watchmaking or jewellery would be a valuable asset.
  • Finally, you cultivate a passion or personal talent that you practice regularly, and that you like to share: a wealth that will contribute to the singularity of your relationship with our customers. What we offer:
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual vacation weeks
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities

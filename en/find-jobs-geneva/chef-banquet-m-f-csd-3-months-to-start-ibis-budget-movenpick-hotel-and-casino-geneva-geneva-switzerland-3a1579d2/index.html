Job descriptionThe Chef de cuisine Banquet is responsible for the organization, production and quality of culinary services for events (banking, weddings, seminars, receptions, cocktails, conferences, etc.). It ensures compliance with quality standards, deadlines, costs and standards of food hygiene and safety (HACCP). MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES Production management

  • Plan, organize and supervise the culinary production of banquets.
  • Develop menus in collaboration with management and business teams.
  • Ensure the quality, presentation and regularity of the dishes served.
  • Adapt production according to the number of guests and logistical constraints. Team management
  • Coach, train and motivate the kitchen team.
  • Organize planning and assign tasks.
  • Ensure the development of skills and compliance with internal procedures.
  • Ensure effective communication with other services (banking, catering, purchasing, maintenance). Hygiene, safety and HACCP
  • Strictly enforce and enforce existing HACCP standards and health regulations.
  • Control storage, cooking, cooling and warm-up temperatures.
  • Supervise traceability of foodstuffs.
  • Ensure that cleaning and disinfection procedures are followed.
  • Conduct regular checks and corrective actions in the event of non-compliance.
  • Prepare and accompany food safety and hygiene audits. Cost management
  • Control material costs and optimize yields.
  • Manage orders, stocks and inventories.
  • Limit losses and food waste.
  • Control the quality of the goods received. Customer satisfaction
  • Ensure a high level of quality and satisfaction at events.
  • Respond to specific requests (allergens, diets, special requirements).
  • Participate in continuous improvement of culinary servicesQualificationsPROFIL RECHERCHED Training

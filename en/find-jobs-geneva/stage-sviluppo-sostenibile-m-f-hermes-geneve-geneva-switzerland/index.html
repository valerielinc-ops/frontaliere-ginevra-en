Full description

As part of its sustainable development strategy, Hermès Switzerland and Central Europe want to strengthen its CSR, ethics, environment and social impact actions. You will support the head of the department throughout the project deployment.

Your main tasks will be

  • Participate in the implementation of the CSR strategy of the group
  • Contribute to the monitoring of CSR indicators (environment, social, governance) and compliance with procedures
  • Consolidate annual CSR reporting
  • To lead existing associative partnerships and actions within the subsidiary, monitor indicators
  • To raise awareness and coordinate training with the shop and headquarters teams.
  • Support the CSR lead in working with warehouse, logistics and human resources teams to integrate sustainable issues
  • Support the coordination of well-being initiatives at work within the branch

Your profile

  • Student in Master's (business school, political science, sustainable development, etc.)
  • Strong interest in CSR and retail issues
  • Good analytical capacity and rigour
  • Spirit of synthesis and curiosity
  • Proactivity and very good relationship
  • Taste for cross-cutting project management and teamwork
  • Mastery of the Pack Office
  • Excellent communication in French and English

Internship condition

  • Start of internship on 22 February
  • Minimum duration of 6 months (preferable 12 months)
  • To benefit from an internship agreement

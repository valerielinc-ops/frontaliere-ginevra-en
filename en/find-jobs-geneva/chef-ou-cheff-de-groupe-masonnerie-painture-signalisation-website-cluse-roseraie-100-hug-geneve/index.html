Description of post

In this role, you manage a team of about 10 collaborators, whose mission is to intervene at first level for all requests for interventions emanate from the users of the buildings, premises and equipment, for the site of Cluse-Roseraie placed under your responsibility and always keeping in mind the 5 values of the institution: trust, respect, team spirit, recognition, equality-inclusion. You coordinate and control the activity of the group "Masonry-Painting and Signalization", whose mission is to maintain, repair, revise, control to transform, modify, improve, manufacture building structures in the hospital and medical fields. The area of intervention is particularly concerned with masonry, concrete-armed, drilling, civil engineering, wall protection, roads and roads, fire-proofing elements, tile and earthwork, false ceilings, floor coverings, scaffolding, dust protection of paint, gypsum, wall coverings, road markings, signs, etc. You ensure the study, coordination and execution of the small transformation and renovation work requested by the clients. You will participate in the studies and implementation of the projects entrusted to the maintenance sector by the technical engineering sectors of the building and the architecture of the master works department. You ensure rigorous management, offering the best value for money and at the right price, by optimizing the technology and materials used, the use of subcontracting and maintenance contracts by monitoring the adequacy of your team's professional qualifications with the tasks to be performed. You replace the head of the sector during his absences, in turn with your colleague head of the Menuiserie-Clés group. You ensure administrative and managerial management in relation to the activities of the group. You are assisting the head of the sector in setting up an organisation to improve customer satisfaction and the performance of the sector. Your leadership skills (respect, listening, team spirit, fairness, recognition, agility) and your rigour give you credibility that allows you to support employees by developing their team spirit. You ensure that you provide them with regular feedback to develop their skills. Your sense of organization and planning ensures that you take care of interventions and troubleshooting according to available resources and needs. Sensitive to patient presence and hospital constraints, you supervise and enforce the procedures and protocols defined in the sector quality assurance system and all good professional practices. Of a positive, optimistic and "client service" nature, you can motivate your team and maintain a climate of partnership in order to proactively manage complex situations involving several professional actors and actors. You update your team's knowledge of your field through training.

Qualifications

You have an ES technician or technician degree in building maintenance or an equivalent training, supplemented by team management training. Training as a leader or construction manager is an asset. You benefit from a minimum of 5 years' experience in building maintenance and 2 years' experience in team management. You master office software, Excel and Word. In addition, the mastery of GMAO CARL and QUALIAC software is an asset. You have a perfect mastery of the oral and written French language.

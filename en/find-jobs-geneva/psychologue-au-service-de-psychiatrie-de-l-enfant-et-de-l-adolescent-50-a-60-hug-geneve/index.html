Role overview

You join the Malatavie-Crisis Unit team, which offers a crisis follow-up for 13-18 year-olds with a suicidal risk and can engage in a specific care.

As a psychologist of the Prevention team, you participate in supervisions, in different meeting times and present clinical situations to your colleagues.

You also work closely with the Geneva network.

  • You join the Malatavie-Crisis Unit team, which offers a crisis follow-up for 13-18 year-olds with a suicidal risk and can engage in a specific care.

Contacts

  • You contribute to the care and guidance work of adolescents and adolescents, as well as young adults who have suicidal thoughts and a significant mal-being (hosting and orientation interviews, suicide risk assessment, responding to the Teen Line, responding to requests received by e-mail and responding to questions asked by young people on the theme of suicide via the Ciao.ch and tecit.ch platforms).

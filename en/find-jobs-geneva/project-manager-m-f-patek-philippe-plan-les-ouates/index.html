Full description

Mission

As part of this function, which is part of the International Client Service Division (SCI), the Project Manager will be responsible for managing data in accordance with the standards in place and for referring to the associated information processes. It will be responsible for supporting the trades (Geneva and international subsidiaries) as part of the information system flows and will lead the related projects.

Responsibilities

  • Manage item sheets, operating ranges, SCI specific service products and nomenclatures.
  • Analyze deviations from standards and system malfunctions (product structure, coding rules, indicators, etc.) and implement corrective actions.
  • Ensure consistency of data between different systems by applying changes resulting from the ERP.
  • Audit processes, client needs and working methods to evolve existing systems.
  • Provide support to all of Patek Philippe's divisions and subsidiaries for requests for basic data, coding and change management.
  • Defining and developing the repository of information systems by guaranteeing interfaces with other professions (Development, Logistics, Quality, Production, Subsidiaries, etc.), as well as applying protocols for testing and evolution of transversal systems.
  • Establish and provide training on existing products and on new processes and projects at the local and international levels.
  • Pilot projects for the transversal development of new features within information systems (ERP, Salesforce, etc.) and the integration of new tools internally and externally.
  • Define and ensure compliance with the objectives, specifications, deadlines, budget, standards and performance of solutions with the project team, while ensuring regular reporting.
  • Lead steering committees, keep indicators up to date, capitalize on feedback in order to feed the knowledge base and seek innovative solutions beyond the processes already controlled by Patek Philippe.

Profile sought

  • Master's degree in engineering, computer science, technology management or equivalent.
  • At least 3 years of experience in a similar position.
  • Confirmed skills in project management.
  • Master's degree in English, level C2.
  • Knowledge of an ERP (SAP is an asset) and a CRM platform (Salesforce is an asset).
  • Excellent ability to analyse and interpret results. Knowledge of a reporting system (Table is an asset).
  • Control of logistical and transversal flows.
  • Control of internal audit of information processes.
  • Excellent communication, knowledge transfer and training capabilities.

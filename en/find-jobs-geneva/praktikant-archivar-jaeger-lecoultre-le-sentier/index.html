Full description

Reference code: JR126627

Since 1833, driven by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

The Archivist Trainee will be responsible for supporting the Archives team in the management, conservation and enhancement of the Group's holdings. He/she will actively participate in ongoing projects to organize and make available historical and contemporary information, whether physical or digital.

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

  • Treatment of funds:
  • Conduct inventory, classification and description of physical and digital documents.
  • Participate in data entry and updating in archival databases.
  • Scanning:
  • Assist in digitizing collections, including document preparation, digitization itself and image quality control.
  • Participate in indexing and describing digitized documents.
  • Research and Communication:
  • Support internal documentary research.
  • Contribute to the valuation of funds (exhibitions, publications, internal, etc.) if the opportunity arises.
  • Daily management:
  • Participate in the day-to-day administrative tasks of the Archives Service.

THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS? YOUR SKILLS!

  • You are looking for an internship as part of your studies (Master) in Archivist, Information Sciences
  • You master the tools of the Pack Office and are familiar with archival software and have a sensitivity to digitizing digital preservation
  • You have a great interest in watchmaking
  • You are recognized for your rigor, your method and your sense of organization
  • You are autonomous, proactive and analytical.
  • You have a good team spirit and a very good relationship
  • You have mastered French (professional English would be an asset).

WE RECRUIT

RESTAURATION HORLOGY (M/F)

based in our Sentier offices in Switzerland.

Within the customer service department, the Horloger Restoration performs repair, restoration and maintenance of old watches and provides technical and historical monitoring.

HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT?

  • Ensure the rehabilitation of our oldest watches
  • To restore the part (angling, black polished, steel restoration etc.) and repair,
  • Exchange or re-manufacture defective components (winding wheel, balance axis, counting of a spiral...) using existing machines (turn 70, milling machine, pointer...)
  • Final control: walking, aesthetics, sealing, range simulation, functions (calendar, timing, etc.), winding, as well as re-emboiting of the part,
  • Manage the supply of supplies and ensure the design and manufacturing of the necessary tools.

THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS?

  • You benefit from a CFC watchmaker or equivalent
  • You benefit from several years of experience in a restoration workshop
  • You are passionate about watches and their history
  • You have worked in a VAS environment
  • You are customer oriented and bring solutions
  • Open and communicative personality
  • Accuracy and thoroughness

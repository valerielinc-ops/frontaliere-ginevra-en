Full description

Your Mission

At the head of the institution, you set the course and create the conditions for the General Hospital to fulfil its public service tasks in a sustainable manner. In close connection with the Board of Directors, as well as with the parent department, you translate strategic directions into institutional priorities and ensure that they are achieved. You lead the Directorate-General, including the Deputy Directorate-General and the General Secretariat, and you rely on the Executive Committee to ensure the coherence, performance and adaptability of the entire institution, to provide an appropriate and coordinated response to the needs of those who use the General Hospital. You represent the General Hospital to the cantonal authorities, its partners and key stakeholders. You carry its mission, positions, values and challenges in a constantly changing social, political and institutional environment. Your main responsibilities

In particular, you will be responsible for

  • develop and implement the general Hospice's strategic guidelines with the Board of Directors, as well as with the parent department;
  • rely on the General Secretariat to ensure sound governance, the functioning of bodies, the control of risks and the quality of controls;
  • ensure, with the Deputy Directorate General, the overall management of the activity, the coherence between the directorates and the implementation of institutional decisions;
  • leading a Directorate-General and a Management Committee capable of arbitrating, prioritizing and acting collectively;
  • leading the institution in a logic of public service, quality of services, efficiency and accountability;
  • anticipate social, political, legal, economic and technological developments that may influence its tasks;
  • create conditions for management and teams to exercise their responsibilities with autonomy, demand and cooperation;
  • ensure a controlled allocation of human, financial, real estate and digital resources;
  • maintain a relationship of trust and transparency with the Governing Council and its committees;
  • represent the General Hospice in its relations with the Council of State, the Grand Council, the Trusteeship Department, the municipalities, partner institutions and other actors in the network;
  • speaking to the institution in strategic, sensitive or crisis situations;
  • support the necessary transformations while preserving the continuity and quality of the services to the accompanied persons.

Your profile You have a higher education or a course deemed equivalent, supplemented by training in management, public governance or organisational conduct. You can take advantage of a proven experience of executive direction or direction in a complex organisation, ideally in the public, parapublic or in a highly regulated environment.

Your journey demonstrates your ability to

  • define a vision and translate it into concrete decisions and results;
  • leading a large organization and complex transformations;
  • work with a Board of Directors and in a public governance environment;
  • understanding political and institutional issues without losing sight of operational realities;
  • arbitrate and decide in situations with sometimes divergent interests or constraints;
  • federate leadership and develop genuine collective responsibility;
  • establishing strong relationships with institutional and political partners;
  • communicate with clarity, including in sensitive contexts;
  • combine performance requirements, quality of public service and attention to people.

Your leadership is based on clarity, trust and accountability. You can listen and associate, but also decide when the situation requires. You are comfortable in an exposed environment, where decisions must be explained, assumed and implemented. An important asset is a thorough knowledge of the functioning of Geneva's public institutions and social policies.

What we offer

A function at the heart of the social stakes of the canton of Geneva, with the possibility of taking concrete action on the evolution of a major public institution. You will join an organization committed to a transformation of its functioning, practices and tools, with a clear ambition: to strengthen its impact with the people accompanied while developing a culture of collaboration, responsibility and continuous improvement.

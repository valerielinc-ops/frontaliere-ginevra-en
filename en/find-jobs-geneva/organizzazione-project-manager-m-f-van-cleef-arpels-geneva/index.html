Reference code: JR135040

Who are we?

A High Jewelry Home with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence.

What do we expect from you?

You have confirmed experience in a similar position and/or in a strategy and/or organization consulting firm as well as experience in the retail universe.

You have excellent analytical, structuring and animation capabilities.

You are familiar with the exposure to the executive committees and have a perfect mastery of French and English.

Do you match the profile?

Attached to the Director Organization & Strategic Planning, you operate as a real internal consulting firm at La Maison, working on cross-cutting and structuring issues related to the organizational development of the House.

You are mandated by members of the Executive Committee.

As part of your missions, you

  • Support teams in formalizing a forward-looking strategic vision and identifying operational and organizational issues.
  • Lead projects: by defining objectives, framing and project approach.
  • Participate in the budget process in collaboration with human resources.
  • Provide methodological support to carry the project culture internally.

More than a job... We're recruiting for a career!

Integrating Van Cleef & Arpels is first and foremost a team where the development of employees is at the heart of our ambition.

From your integration, you will be immersed in the world of the House thanks to trainings on our history, know-how and culture.

The recruitment process

Apply directly online.

If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for an interview.

You meet the Director Project Organization.

Otherwise, you will receive an email informing you of the refusal of your application.

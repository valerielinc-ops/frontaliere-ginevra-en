Full description

Introduction

For our Communication & Image division, we are looking for a (M/F) Project Coordinator whose main mission will be to provide administrative and organizational support for the management of exhibition projects.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative and organisational support for exhibition projects, from preparation to closing
  • Prepare and update working documents: presentations, schedules, follow-up tables, minutes, contact lists and meeting materials
  • Update piloting tools, track deadlines, actions, deliverables and blocking points
  • Centralize information and facilitate coordination between internal teams, partners, suppliers and providers
  • Organize the archiving of documents and ensure reliability, confidentiality and correct updating of project data
  • Provide logistical and operational support in the preparation and conduct of exhibitions, including hospitality and guest management

Profile

  • University or HES training in communication, event or equivalent
  • Experience in event and project coordination
  • Aisance with Excel and PowerPoint desktop tools
  • Mastery of French and English, oral and written
  • Excellent relationship and team spirit
  • Rigor, autonomy and priority management
  • Reactivity and service sense
  • Discretion and confidentiality

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

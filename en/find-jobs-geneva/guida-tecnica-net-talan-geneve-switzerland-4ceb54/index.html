Full description

As a Technical Lead .NET, you will be responsible for defining and implementing the technical guidelines of the project while accompanying the development teams on a daily basis: Technical leadership

  • Work closely with the Internal Technical Leader to align technical choices with project objectives.
  • Define technical guidelines and ensure their effective implementation.
  • Be the technical reference to development teams.

Architecture & Design

  • Design robust, scalable and secure technical solutions.
  • Participate in architectural choices and selection of the most suitable technologies.
  • Ensure the technical coherence of the entire platform.

Team management

  • Lead a team of .NET developers.
  • Monitor Front-End developments to ensure smooth integration with Back-End components.
  • Support developers in their skills development and promote the sharing of good practices.

Quality & development

  • Supervise developments and ensure compliance with quality standards.
  • Conduct code reviews and support teams on complex technical topics.
  • Ensure performance, sustainability and security of developments.

Communication & coordination

  • Ensure smooth communication with different stakeholders.
  • Share progress, identified risks and proposed solutions.
  • To contribute to good coordination between technical teams and business interlocutors.

Experience

  • At least 10 years experience in .NET development.
  • An experience confirmed as Technical Leader, Lead Developer or Software Architect.
  • Experience in transforming or redesigning complex applications.

Technical expertise

  • Excellent mastery of C#, ASP.NET and Entity Framework.
  • Strong knowledge of application architectures, APIs and web services.
  • Good command of Front-End technologies such as Angular or React, as well as JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML and CSS.
  • Good understanding of the performance, security and scalability issues of applications.

Leadership

  • Ability to federate and accompany technical teams.
  • Experience in coordinating multidisciplinary teams.
  • Ability to make technical decisions and solve complex issues.
  • Natural leadership and initiative.

Languages

  • French current indispensable.

Assets

  • Experience in banking, financial services or fintech is a real plus.
  • Knowledge of the specific regulatory requirements of the banking sector is appreciated.

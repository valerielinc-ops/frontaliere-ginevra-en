UNITED MACHINING is one of the world's leading providers of complete solutions for precision components and tools manufacturers and the mold-making industry. The portfolio includes milling, EDM, laser texturing, laser micromachining, and additive manufacturing machines. UNITED MACHINING is looking for an experienced and hands-on Senior IT Systems & Infrastructure Engineer to manage, optimize and secure our enterprise IT infrastructure. You will play a key role in ensuring the reliability and performance of our global IT environment while contributing to a major IT transformation and carve-out project.

100%WorkloadMeyrinLocationUnlimitedJob type Apply now Your tasks

  • Manage and optimize enterprise IT infrastructure, including Windows servers, VMware, storage and backup systems.
  • Administer core infrastructure services, including Active Directory, DNS, GPO and PKI.
  • Ensure infrastructure availability, security, performance and disaster recovery readiness.
  • Automate infrastructure operations using PowerShell and Infrastructure as Code.
  • Troubleshoot complex infrastructure issues and drive continuous improvement.
  • Take a leading technical role in infrastructure migrations and separation activities as part of our IT carve-out

  • Maintain technical documentation and support security and compliance initiatives.

Your profile

  • Higher education completed, preferably with a degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • 10+ years of hands-on experience in enterprise IT infrastructure engineering and operations, primarily in

on-premises environments.

  • Advanced knowledge of Windows Server, VMware and enterprise storage and backup solutions.
  • Strong understanding of networking, Active Directory, DNS, PKI and infrastructure security.
  • Experience with scripting, automation and Infrastructure as Code.
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Azure and hybrid cloud environments.
  • Proven experience in infrastructure migrations and complex IT transformation projects.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with a proactive mindset and a strong sense of technical ownership.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively in international and

multicultural teams.

  • English at a professional level is mandatory.

UNITED MACHINING is one of the world's leading providers of complete solutions for precision components and tools manufacturers and the mold-making industry. The portfolio includes milling, EDM, laser texturing, laser micromachining, and additive manufacturing machines. UNITED MACHINING is looking for an experienced and hands-on Senior IT Systems & Infrastructure Engineer to manage, optimize and secure our enterprise IT infrastructure. You will play a key role in ensuring the reliability and performance of our global IT environment while contributing to a major IT transformation and carve-out project. This is an opportunity to take technical ownership, tackle complex infrastructure challenges and help build our future IT landscape. Learn more...

Your Purpose

As a Junior Architect, you will support the Port Development team by transforming design concepts and business requirements into clear architectural drawings, layouts, and visualizations. You will contribute to the development of private destinations, cruise terminals, port infrastructure, and related facilities, helping stakeholders make informed decisions through high-quality design documentation.

Your Impact

  • Translate business and design requirements into zoning studies, spatial diagrams, and architectural layouts.
  • Support the development of preliminary designs aligned with operational and project requirements.
  • Prepare conceptual design packages for internal reviews and coordination meetings.
  • Create presentation materials, visual narratives, diagrams, and material boards to communicate design concepts effectively.
  • Develop 3D models and real-time visualizations to support project reviews and executive presentations.
  • Produce and revise architectural drawings, including plans, sections, elevations, façade studies, and circulation diagrams.
  • Coordinate and adapt port and civil infrastructure drawings, site layouts, road networks, and hardscape elements.
  • Ensure accuracy, consistency, and timely delivery of design documentation in a fast-paced environment.

Your Journey so far

  • Degree in Architecture or a related field.
  • Experience producing architectural drawings, layouts, and design documentation.
  • Exposure to port, infrastructure, hospitality, commercial, or large-scale development projects is considered a plus.
  • Portfolio demonstrating technical drawings, architectural layouts, and rendered visualizations.
  • Familiarity with design development processes from concept through preliminary planning.

Your Essentials

  • Advanced proficiency in AutoCAD with strong drafting accuracy and speed.
  • Strong 3D modeling skills using SketchUp (preferred) or Revit.
  • Experience with real-time rendering tools such as Enscape, Twinmotion, or equivalent platforms.
  • Good knowledge of Adobe Photoshop for image editing and presentation enhancement.
  • Interest or basic knowledge of AI-powered design tools such as Stable Diffusion, Magnific AI, or similar.
  • Ability to independently produce clear and professional visualizations.
  • Strong attention to detail and excellent organizational skills.
  • Passion for architecture, interiors, materials, and spatial design.
  • Ability to work efficiently under tight deadlines and manage multiple priorities.
  • Proactive, execution-oriented mindset with strong visual communication skills.
  • Valid Internship Agreement

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

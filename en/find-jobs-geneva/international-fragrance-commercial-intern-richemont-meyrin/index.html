Full description

Reference code: JR134527 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

Are you a highly analytical self-starter eager to launch your career in luxury beauty? Join our global team as an International Fragrance Commercial Intern.

In this role, you will bridge day-to-day commercial operations with high-impact strategic analysis. From tracking market performance to enhancing e-commerce platforms and supporting trade initiatives, you will build a solid foundation while taking on progressive ownership of projects.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

  • Data Management & Business Reporting
  • Serve as the central point of contact for monthly sell-in/sell-out data, distribution mapping, and performance updates across global markets.
  • Consolidate Trade Marketing plans and document actionable outcomes from monthly market reviews.
  • Audit market submissions to identify data inconsistencies and drive accuracy.
  • Strategic Analysis & Market Insights
  • Perform ad-hoc benchmarking and market analyses to uncover new growth opportunities (including Travel Retail).
  • Evaluate pricing alignment across regions and escalate potential gaps.
  • Conduct post-activation reviews for major trade campaigns to quantify ROI.
  • Trade Marketing & E-Commerce
  • Support the global rollout and business impact analysis of Haute Parfumerie deployments.
  • Partner with e-commerce teams and local markets to assess and enhance brand performance on retail partner sites.
  • Special Projects & Event Support
  • Help organize major commercial events, including vendor negotiations for the annual Fragrance Seminar.
  • Lead a strategic side-project aimed at pitching innovative solutions to the team.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • You have a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business, Marketing, Data Science, related field or a prior experience in marketing, commercial analysis, or the beauty/fragrance sector are strongly preferred.
  • You are proficient in Excel and digital tools, data visualization tools (e.g. looker) and have strong slide-design skills (PowerPoint) are a major plus.
  • You are highly meticulous when it comes to data integrity, reporting, and presentation quality.
  • You are highly organized, agile, and a strong communicator, bringing a proactive, autonomous, and curious mindset driven by continuous learning and tangible business impact.
  • You are fluent in English
  • French is a plus.

WHAT DO WE OFFER?

  • We create a positive environment and highlight your personal and team performances.
  • We’re fully transparent and share with you: Maison strategies, key information, needs of others Corporate Departments.
  • We constantly educate you with: Cartier’s savoir-faire, competitive landscape, industry news, client experience best practices.

YOUR JOURNEY WITH US

If your application is selected, we will give you an introductory call to further discuss the opportunity, and next steps. If things go well on both ends, you will have a chance to meet the hiring manager and the HR Partner.

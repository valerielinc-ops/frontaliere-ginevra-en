Description of post

As a data manager, you collect and control clinical data using the institutional databases and platforms required by MHS organs. You ensure the completeness, consistency, traceability and timeliness of submission to the MHS. You ensure the implementation of qualitative data management by participating in the establishment of standardized data collection media, participating in the definition of data entry rules and implementing a data validation process. You are extracting data from the Hospital Information System (IPS) to design dashboards and track the activity volumes required by the MHS (minimum case threshold per year and per operator). To ensure reliability and traceability of data, you work with multidisciplinary teams, senior physicians, medical secretariats, medical coding and nursing teams to document the patient journey. You participate in the sessions and meetings of the teams. You participate in the training and supervision of new employees.

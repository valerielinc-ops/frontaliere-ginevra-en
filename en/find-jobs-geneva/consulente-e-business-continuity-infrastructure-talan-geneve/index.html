Full description

Background As part of strengthening its IT resilience, our client is looking for an Infrastructure Operating Engineer to support the Business Continuity Management Office (BCMO). The aim is to ensure the continuity of critical IT services and the proper integration of business continuity requirements into projects (BUILD) and operating environments (RUN). Main missions

  • Follow up on Business Continuity requirements in IT projects and services.
  • Contribute to the drafting, updating and improvement of PCA/PRA.
  • Reliability of BCM and critical services data.
  • Prepare and accompany review campaigns, continuity tests, exercises and audits.
  • Identify technical dependencies, risks and vulnerabilities of infrastructure.
  • Coordinate remedial plans and follow up corrective actions.
  • Produce KPI/KRI, reporting and dashboards related to IT resilience.

Business Continuity / IT Resilience

  • Business Continuity Management business experience in an IT environment.
  • Mastery of PCA/PRA, BCP/DRP and BIA.
  • Experience of Disaster Recovery tests and continuity exercises.
  • Knowledge of ISO 22301 and BCM good practices.
  • Knowledge of the popular ParaSolution tool.

IT infrastructure

  • Good understanding of multisite environments and data centers.
  • Windows, Linux, virtualization, networks, Cloud.
  • Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, storage, backups and databases.
  • ITSM and CMDB tools.

Tools and reporting

  • Excellent mastery of Excel: TCD, advanced functions, Power Query, consolidation and data control.
  • Mastery of SharePoint: libraries, access rights, versioning and documentary lists.

Transverse competencies

  • Spirit of analysis and ability to identify dependencies and technical risks.
  • Coordination of multiple contacts and facilitation of workshops.
  • Documentary rigor, capacity for synthesis and monitoring of action plans.

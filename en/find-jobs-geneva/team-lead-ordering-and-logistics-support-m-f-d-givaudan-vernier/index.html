Team Lead Ordering and Logistics Support (m/f/d) - Your next job?

AsTeam Lead Orders and Logistics Support (m/f/d) to Vernier, you are attached to our department Logistics and you report to ourLogistics Director. You will be in charge of managing the Logistic Support and Ordering team and you will lead the coordination of the logistics flows and packaging of the site by ensuring effective collaboration between the various departments. You guarantee business continuity through proactive business monitoring, anticipation of risks and resolution of constraints affecting Supply Chain. You contribute to continuous process improvement and ensure the role of business expert for the department's tools and systems.

Are you interested in this job? This could be the perfect opportunity if, as for us, your goal is to always meet the needs of customers and touch the emotions of the world around you.

Your Main Mission

Leadership and Team Management

  • To supervise, support and develop a team of 5 employees by promoting skills, versatility and performance.
  • Define operational priorities, organize the distribution of activities and ensure regular monitoring of performance indicators.
  • Conduct performance and development interviews, identify resource and training needs.
  • Promote a collaborative, service-oriented and EHS-compliant working environment.
  • Participate in the management bodies of the department and contribute to the coordination of cross-cutting activities, the management of major incidents and crisis situations.

Logistics operations and packaging piloting

  • Supervise logistics flows to ensure a high level of customer service, ensuring the management of priorities, emergencies and proactive monitoring of delays.
  • Ensure alignment between operational capacity and demand to maintain departmental processing times.
  • Pilot activities related to returns, subcontracting, waste, loss investigations and analysis of causes of malfunction.
  • Ensure the availability of the packaging required for operations and ensure efficient management of stocks, supplies and storage areas.
  • Coordinate the introduction of new packaging and ensure compliance with operational standards and continuous improvement.

Professional expertise, systems and continuous improvement

  • Supervise inventory activities and ensure reliability of logistics data.
  • Develop the team's expertise on logistics tools and SAP in order to ensure professional support of the first level.
  • Ensure the quality, consistency and maintenance of data and processes related to logistics operations.
  • Maintain procedures and working instructions, while ensuring their proper application.
  • Identify and implement opportunities for continuous improvement, while contributing to the department's cross-cutting projects.

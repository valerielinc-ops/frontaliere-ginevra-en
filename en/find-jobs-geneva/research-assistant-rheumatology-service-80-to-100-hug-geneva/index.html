Description of post

Within the rheumatology department you conduct research in epidemiology and applied statistics, with a view to increasing scientific knowledge and developing practical applications, including medical, industrial and public health. You work mainly on longitudinal records and observational studies. You are studying new statistical analysis techniques and their applications. You take part in teaching practical work, seminars and symposia.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor in science or statistics. You have experience in research (minimum 2 years) and scientific and technical literature in the field. You have writing skills in French and English. Knowledge of R and/or python would be real assets. You are a conscientious and committed person. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

Full description

Mission At the heart of the governance of the Group HRIS, you are the functional and technical referent of the Oracle HCM Cloud solution for the entire Group. As a platform expert, you guarantee the availability, quality and continuous evolution of the tool, ensuring alignment between the needs of the HR teams and the capabilities of the solution. Your scope covers all Oracle HCM Cloud modules: Core HR, Talent Management, Performance & Goals, Absence Management, Compensation & Benefits, Learning, Workforce Management / Time & Labor, HCM Analytics & Reporting. Activities RUN – Maintenance in operational conditions

  • Provide level 2 and 3 functional support to HR teams and key users
  • Manage incidents and anomalies, coordinate their resolution with the publisher and the TMA
  • Monitor the availability and performance of the solution, produce regular reporting
  • Maintain functional documentation and operating procedures up to date
  • Lead and train Key Users and contribute to the skills development of HR teams

Updates & version upgrades

  • Manage Oracle quarterly updates: analysis of release notes, impact assessment, organization and creation of recipes, deployment
  • Define and execute non-regression test plans
  • Coordinate all stakeholders (HR, IT, service providers) during updates
  • Ensure communication with users on developments in the solution

Evolution of the tool

  • Collect, analyze and prioritize development requests related to HR professions
  • Configure and configure Oracle HCM Cloud in response to identified needs
  • Monitor new Oracle features and propose their activation when relevant
  • Build, feed and maintain the functional roadmap of the HCM solution

Managing projects related to Oracle HCM

  • Contribute to the framing and implementation of module implementation or extension projects
  • Ensure the role of functional referent on interfacing projects (payroll, ATS, LMS, BI, etc.)
  • Write functional specifications and validate technical deliverables
  • Participate in governance bodies (steering committees, business workshops)

Profile

Technical skills

  • In-depth mastery of multi-module Oracle HCM Cloud
  • Significant experience in HRIS project management
  • Confirmed practice of Oracle HCM configuration/parameterization
  • Good knowledge of HR processes (payroll, talent, training, time management, etc.)
  • Experience in managing Oracle quarterly updates
  • Ability to manage a TMA and service providers
  • Comfort in writing functional specifications
  • Mastery of ticketing tools (JIRA, ServiceNow, etc.)
  • Solid experience in reporting and designing decision-making dashboards (HCM Analytics, BI)

Personal qualities

  • Rigor, sense of organization and priorities
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis skills
  • Excellent interpersonal skills and ability to work transversally with HR, IT and business teams
  • Autonomy, proactivity and strength of proposal
  • Sense of pedagogy and ability to make technical subjects accessible to non-specialist interlocutors

Language

  • Fluent French
  • Fluent professional English, both spoken and written (minimum C1 level)

Training & experience

  • Master’s degree in Information Systems Management, IT or Human Resources, or title deemed equivalent
  • Minimum 5 years of experience on Oracle HCM or equivalent HRIS solution, ideally in a group/international context
  • Oracle HCM Cloud Implementation Specialist certification is a real asset

What we offer you

  • A key role at the heart of the Group's HR digital transformation
  • The possibility of directly contributing to the optimization of HR processes and operational efficiency
  • A stimulating and demanding work environment, based on proximity, trust and accountability
  • Quality working conditions within an international Group on a human scale

If you do not hear from us within a month, you may consider that your application has not been accepted.

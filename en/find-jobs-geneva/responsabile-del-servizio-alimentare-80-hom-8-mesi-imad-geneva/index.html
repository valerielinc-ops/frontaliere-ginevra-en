Full description

The mission of the Nutrition and Dietary Centre is to help keep the Genevan population home by promoting its nutritional health. It consists of two complementary services, the nutrition service (delivery of meals at home) and the diet service. The dietary service consists of a team of dietitians and an administrative assistant working throughout the canton of Geneva. They carry out dietary consultations at home on medical prescription, actions in prevention / health promotion. They also supervise the offer and the quality of the IMAD meals. As part of the replacement during her maternity leave of the current holder, attached to the Director of the Nutrition and Dietary Centre, the person in charge of the Dietary Service will assume responsibility for the Dietary Service. Your missions and responsibilities

  • As an ad interim team manager of the dietetics department, you provide direction, coordination and supervision of the activities of dieticians and the administrative assistant of the department. You ensure the evaluation, adequacy and quality of the services provided to the beneficiaries, the follow-up of the interventions and the associated billing, on the delegation of the Director of the Centre for Nutrition and Dietetics. You guarantee human resources management in accordance with the institutional policies and the legal framework in force.
  • You promote coordination with management teams and partners in the health and social network to strengthen the prevention, detection, assessment and monitoring of nutritional health risks. You contribute to the continuous development of skills, to the valorisation and dissemination of home-based dietary expertise, in collaboration with the internal actors concerned. You work closely with nutrition officials to ensure consistency and continuous improvement in the delivery of the Nutrition and Dietetics Centre, while participating in the implementation of the projects.

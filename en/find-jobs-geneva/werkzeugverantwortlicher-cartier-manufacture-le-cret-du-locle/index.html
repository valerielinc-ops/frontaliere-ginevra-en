CONTEXT

Attached to the Production Support department, you ensure the day-to-day management of the Tools sector in order to guarantee the uninterrupted supply of cutting tools and consumables, the gains on planned purchases and the deployment of the transformation roadmap of the department. Your success will help improve the performance of our workshops.

WHAT YOUR IMPACT IS

Responsibilities

As a team manager

  • You optimally organize the department, define and develop the technical potential and expertise of the team as well as its versatility and autonomy
  • You ensure smooth and regular communication (ascending, descending, transversal) with employees and promote the values of the House
  • You help develop a healthy and stimulating working environment
  • You work closely with the different interfaces

As a "pilot" of your activity

  • You rationalize production costs
  • You are putting in place a purchasing strategy that respects our ecosystem, consolidating partnerships in place
  • You negotiate with suppliers the fair price of the items to be supplied
  • Optimize inventory

As a guarantor of continuous improvement

  • You deploy the transformation roadmap of the department
  • You digitize flows
  • You automate processes
  • You rationalize the portfolio Cartier Watches articles

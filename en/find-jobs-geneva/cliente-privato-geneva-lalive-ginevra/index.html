Full description

For our Geneva office, we are looking for a committed and qualified employee with prior experience in the field of private clients, including inheritance law, family law and the protection of adults. This role requires excellent technical knowledge, rigour and motivation. He will ask you to be able to work independently and as a team. The positioning of the Study on the Swiss and international market – with offices in Geneva, Zurich and London – also requires a taste for cosmopolitan working environments. Who are we? The prevention and resolution of disputes is at the heart of our study. Our private customer team is internationally recognized and has strong expertise in all types of litigation involving important issues, usually with an international component. In particular, we represent many wealthy personalities and/or families and advise them on the challenges they face. The issues are as varied as the clients who consult us, and include not only front-line litigation but also transactional accompaniment. They cover inheritance or family matters, almost systematically in an international context, the treatment of trusts and their integration into the Swiss legal system, the accompaniment in disputes or transactions in the art market and family businesses, the resolution of disputes of a commercial but personal nature – with the current generational transition in watermarking. We are looking for a candidate for the following qualities:

  • Legal training in Swiss law and patent of Swiss lawyer required;
  • Two years of post-patent experience in the field of private clients (inheritance law, art law, adult protection, family law);
  • Excellent academic references (diploma in Swiss law); priority to persons holding a doctorate, a post-graduate specialization or authors of publications concerning private clients;
  • French mother tongue and excellent English proficiency; good knowledge of German; knowledge of other languages appreciated;
  • Structured and precise working methodology;
  • exemplary research, analysis and writing skills;
  • Very good interpersonal skills, communication and coordination skills. We offer a first-rate opportunity and services:
  • Recognized and stimulating international environment with experienced lawyers;
  • Multi-purpose work on complex mandates;
  • Strong support for personal development, including an individual budget for continuing training;
  • Freedom of involvement in professional associations and potential for developing an individual professional profile on the market;
  • Wages and competitive working conditions. If you wish to join our dynamic team, we invite you to submit your complete application (CV, cover letter, copy of your diplomas, university notes and other qualifications) to the attention of Human Resources by clicking on this link.

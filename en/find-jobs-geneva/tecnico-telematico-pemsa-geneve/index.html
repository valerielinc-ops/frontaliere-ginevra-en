Your assignment

Install and maintain low voltage systems You will take care of the installation, maintenance and repair of different low voltage solutions: video surveillance (CCTV), access control, anti-intrusion and fire alarm systems, computer and telephone networks, intercoms and other electronic security systems. Configure and program equipment You will take care of the commissioning, configuration and programming of equipment and software in order to ensure its proper functioning, reliability and adequacy to the specific needs of customers. Diagnosing and solving malfunctions You will identify faults, analyze their origin and implement rapid and effective technical solutions in order to restore the operation of the plants and ensure the continuity of the service. Perform harvests and controls Run the tests, adjustments and controls necessary before setting up systems. Check their compliance and ensure their performance and reliability. Ensure quality technical monitoring You will publish your intervention reports, document your work and ensure professional follow-up with your customers. You will also ensure the correct transmission of the information necessary for the maintenance and operation of the plants. Your contribution Thanks to your technical expertise, responsiveness and service orientation, you directly contribute to the performance, safety and reliability of low voltage systems installed at our customers. And now we're talking about you? A recognised technical training You are in possession of an AFC as a telematic technician, network address, or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland. It offers solid knowledge in the fields of telecommunications, computer networks and communication systems. Proven Experience You have matured at least 2 years of professional experience in a similar function. This experience allows you to intervene independently and effectively on different types of plants and in various technical environments. Solid technical skills You are comfortable with configuration, commissioning, diagnostics, maintenance and optimization of communication systems. A true spirit of service Rigorous, autonomous and organized, manage your interventions with method and professionalism. Your ease in interpersonal relationships and your spirit of service allow you to establish a relationship of trust with customers and users. Mobility suitable for field needs In possession of the B category driver's license, you appreciate the on-site interventions and are willing to go to customers to ensure a responsive, effective and quality service. Team spirit and sense of initiative Proactive and involved, you appreciate both work independently and collaboration with technical teams . Your ability to adapt, reactivity and orientation to solutions enable you to respond effectively to the challenges encountered in the field. What will make the difference Your technical expertise, your sense of service and your ability to operate in different technological contexts will make you a real resource to ensure the performance of communication infrastructure and the satisfaction of our customers.

Full description

Work Flexibility: Field-based

What You Will Do

As part of our Mako team, you will provide hands-on clinical and technical support to surgeons and operating room teams across Western Switzerland .

Your key responsibilities will include

  • Training and supporting surgeons and OR staff before and during procedures using the Mako System.
  • Providing clinical and technical guidance on Mako and its applications for partial knee, total knee and total hip arthroplasty .
  • Supporting surgeons with pre-operative planning , including the preparation and use of CT data within the Mako software.
  • Planning and coordinating upcoming Mako procedures.
  • Organizing and delivering training sessions and presentations for healthcare professionals in surgery and radiology .
  • Supporting the onboarding and coordination of new customers in close collaboration with our Joint Replacement sales team .
  • Providing product expertise and sales support at conferences, congresses and trade fairs .

What You Need Required

  • A minimum of 2 years of professional experience in a medical or clinical environment , such as operating room technology, medical technology/application support, clinical support or physiotherapy.
  • Strong technical aptitude and the ability to understand complex topics quickly and communicate them clearly.
  • A strong customer and service mindset .
  • Excellent organizational and planning skills, with a reliable and proactive approach.
  • A valid Category B driving licence and willingness to travel extensively.
  • Fluent in English and French skills at C1 level , both spoken and written. German language skills would be a plus.

Preferred

  • A degree in Medical Engineering, Bioengineering, Natural Sciences or a comparable field.
  • Previous experience working in an operating room environment .

Additional Information We value diversity and welcome applications from people regardless of ethnic, national or social background, gender, disability, age or sexual identity.

What to Expect

  • Comprehensive Mako product training , followed by an assessment and ongoing certifications, all conducted in English .
  • A company car , also available for private use.
  • Travel of up to 80% , primarily by company car, to support customers and procedures across the region. Occasional overnight stays will be required.
  • Depending on your weekly schedule, you can spend the remaining time working from home .

Your salary level will be determined based on the depth and relevance of your professional experience .

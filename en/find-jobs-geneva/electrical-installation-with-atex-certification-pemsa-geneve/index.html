Your assignment

Building electrical installations in the industrial field You will participate in the construction and installation of power plants with strong current and weak current (CFO/CFA) in industrial sites, in compliance with the projects, Swiss standards, safety requirements and the specific requirements of each project. Operating in ATEX environments You will work in areas at risk of explosive atmospheres. An ATEX training is mandatory for this position. Strictly apply the specific procedures, regulations and security measures of these environments. Install and connect electrical equipment The installation, installation and connection of the cables, as well as the installation of electrical equipment, cable channels and the various infrastructures necessary for the proper functioning of the systems. Read and interpret electrical schematics You are able to read and interpret schematics, drawings and technical documents in order to make plants that meet the quality requirements of the project. Contribute to the success of the yards In collaboration with the various subjects involved, you contribute to the proper performance of the work, to the compliance of the deadlines and quality standards, while guaranteeing the constant respect of safety standards. And now we're talking about you? A recognised technical training You have an AFC as an electrical installer or electrician or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland. Proven experience in industry You have gained professional experience in the electrical industry, ideally acquired in industrial sites or in complex technical facilities. An updated ATEX training You have a valid ATEX training and know the safety standards and requirements applicable to environments with explosive atmospheres. Solid technical skills Master the strong current and weak current systems (CFO/CFA) as well as reading electrical plans and schematics, laying and connecting cables, laying equipment and installing cable channels. A good understanding of the current NIBT norms and an experience in the Swiss electric market are important strengths. A rigorous and reliable professional Autonomous, conscientious and organized, appreciates teamwork and knows how to move in demanding industrial environments. It attaches particular importance to the quality of work, compliance with procedures and safety. Your strengths to succeed Your industrial experience, your ATEX training, your technical skills and your knowledge of Swiss standards will allow you to integrate quickly and effectively contribute to the success of the yards.

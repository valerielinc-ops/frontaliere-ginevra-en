Description of post

You provide personalized and comprehensive nursing services, on a self-reliant basis, to children in situations with different levels of complexity. Dexterity and ease in technical care are essential qualities for taking care of children hospitalized in paediatric surgery for the management of dressings, probes, drains, catheters and infusion pumps. You demonstrate rapid and effective management of complex care for children in post-transplant liver surgery and ensure careful and structured follow-up during new hospitalizations. Your learning skills allow you to continuously progress in your practice. You are a reactive person, with a very good management of stress and priorities, you know how to assess, analyze complex clinical situations and adapt to the context as well as to unforeseen and peak activity. You are able to manage pain, control hygiene and asepsis and administer paediatric medicines. You are a precise and rigorous person in the care and respect of procedures. Your ability to work collaboratively allows you to work with the medical-care team and ensures accurate and thorough transmission of information within multidisciplinary teams. You are involved in the supervision and clinical teaching of new healthcare staff and other health professionals and professionals.

Qualifications

You will receive a B.A.H.S. in nursing or an equivalent title recognized by the Swiss Red Cross. You have a professional practice of at least 3 years acquired preferably in a surgical department and especially in patients in gastrohepatology. An experience in pediatrics would be an asset. You have a real motivation for pediatrics and show patience in a paediatric context. You have excellent relational skills that demonstrate ease in communicating with the child and his or her family, creating a bond. You are able to adopt a language adapted to his age and reassure him. Your ability to adapt and your critical mind feed a continuous improvement approach. You have a strong sense of organization and priority management with several patients in charge and different levels of complexity. You are able to work in interdisciplinarity and demonstrate versatility, autonomy and proactivity. You are a motivated, dynamic, creative and listening person, ready to invest in a department where you share the relational values of trust, respect, teamwork, recognition, equality and inclusion as well as the institutional values of quality, innovation, service and responsibility. Finally, you exercise discretion and diplomacy in your daily practice.

Full description

Who you are

  • You have already worked in a graphic communication team in an IKEA organization, an advertising agency or a commercial design studio.
  • You like to take responsibility and be an inspiring and motivating leader.
  • You studied graphic design and typography.
  • You speak English and English fluently.

Your responsibilities

  • Supervise all communications from the store to the customer.
  • Mobilize your knowledge and understanding of home life and people's purchasing behaviours to improve graphic communication inside and outside the IKEA store to facilitate the purchase process.
  • Use your graphic knowledge and IKEA concept and IKEA visual identity to create and deploy graphic solutions that convey clear, simple and consistent messages. Supervise all communications from the store to the customer.

Teamwork

Where others see furnishings, we see clever solutions to improve daily life. With a lot of passion, imagination and brainstorming, we transform colours, textiles and furniture into inspiring atmospheres that enthralle, attract and convince visitors to IKEA stores that they can reproduce the same thing at home.

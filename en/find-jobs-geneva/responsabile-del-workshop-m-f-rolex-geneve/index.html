Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a (M/F) workshop manager whose main mission will be to manage the organization of the resources of an assembly workshop by putting in place the actions to ensure that the production objectives are met.

Responsibilities

  • Organize and coordinate daily goals by ensuring optimal workload distribution.
  • To inform and analyse production indicators and the monitoring of the workshop's short-term objectives.
  • Ensure cross-linkage and cross-linkages with production services.
  • Contribute to daily sessions related to the management scope.
  • Manage inventory organization.
  • Conduct ad hoc and operational analyses for the proper operation of the service.

Profile

  • Bachelor level HES or HEG oriented logistics or management, or experience confirmed in production management.
  • Training in production organization and management or experience deemed equivalent.
  • Mastery of computer tools (MS-Office, SAP and production monitoring indicators).
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis.
  • Communicative nature, flexible and excellent team spirit.
  • Ability to take initiatives.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

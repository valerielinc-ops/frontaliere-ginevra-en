Full description

Introduction For our Information Systems Division, we are looking for a Data Protection Advisor (DPO) (H/F) whose main mission will be to ensure compliance of personal data management for the Rolex Group. Responsibilities

  • Ensure the application of the Personal Data Protection Regulations within the Group
  • Maintain the documentary base, including policies and guidelines
  • Publish monthly and annual activity reports
  • Managing the relationship and contracts (DPA) with the Group's companies in Switzerland and abroad
  • Ensuring the processing of requests relating to the rights of persons
  • Ensure the relationship with the Group's trades and maintain the register of processing activities
  • Identify and track compliance gaps, participate in compliance audits
  • Advise project and operational teams on compliance issues
  • Training the Group's staff
  • Ensuring legal oversight
  • Identify and carry out treatments requiring risk analysis (RAA)
  • Managing incidents related to the security of personal data, including the relationship with supervisory authorities
  • Integrate European standards into compliance processes (IA Act, Data Act, CRA, DSA, DMA, ...) Profile
  • Higher education in computer, economic or legal fields
  • Good knowledge of computer architecture and cyber security principles
  • Risk management skills
  • Mastery of personal data protection standards and laws, including LPD and GDPR
  • Certification(s) on Swiss and/or European data protection standards
  • Excellent communication capabilities, particularly in interactions with treatment managers and the legal service
  • Perfect command of French and very good level of English Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas). #LI-Hybrid

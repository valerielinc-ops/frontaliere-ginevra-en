Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the General Internal Medicine Service of the Department of Medicine. Internal medicine is a speciality centred, as its name indicates, towards the inside of the body and organs. The mother-discipline has given rise, over the last three decades, to multiple disciplines, which have become full-fledged specialties for several years and have obtained the same status as internal medicine itself. The specialist who brings the latest knowledge of his/her specialty to patients and to university centres, is able to develop sophisticated research programs, either clinical or laboratory, that will allow to integrate this knowledge through translational medicine into practice. The General Internal Medicine Department is responsible for the care of hospitalized patients with a problem or condition related to internal medicine, in collaboration with the departments of the Medical Specialties Department.

Description of post

You ensure in close cooperation with your hierarchy, the management of a care unit, the coordination of practical activities, the clinical, therapeutic or surgical interventions of the patientess entrusted to you. You are responsible, under the supervision of your direct hierarchy, for medical procedures within the department. You guarantee the quality of medical services in care units including acute intermediate care activities and in the adult emergency department, including on-call periods. You perform diagnostic or therapeutic interventions in the areas defined by the department's chief physician. You contribute to training and efficiently support in-house physicians and trainees in their skills development. You conduct and contribute to training activities, such as attending seminars, seminars or in-house theoretical training conferences. You pursue your training objectives according to the post-graduate training plan and/or the requirements of ISFM-FMH for the proposed specialty qualification or retention. You participate in ongoing research activities in the service and participate in the evaluation of the training and the degree of achievement of its objectives.

Qualifications

You have a federal doctor's degree or training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO). You have a solid background in general internal medicine. An experience in acute medicine is a plus. You benefit from or are close to obtaining an FMH specialty in general internal medicine. If necessary, you are in possession of the cantonal Geneva law of practice. You have or have excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with patients, you are dynamic, flexible and engaged or engaged. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability. You have the will to participate in clinical research. A knowledge of the hospital world is necessary. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees and collaborators representing 180 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights and key figures of 2025 by clicking here . The Department of General Internal Medicine (SMIG), attached to the Department of Medicine, provides diagnostic and therapeutic support to adult patients and patients with a problem or assignment related to internal medicine, in collaboration with other HUG services, in particular the Department of Medicine.

Description of post

Within the Service de Médecine Internale Générale (SMIG), you join the secretary pool and ensure operational support to the various units, especially in case of overload, replacement or to guarantee the back-up of the team. In the units, you work closely with doctors and the Patient Managers Routes (IPM) You play a key role in the continuity of the service and develop a relationship of proximity and trust with your colleagues. Your main tasks cover all administrative tasks related to the patient's hospital path, from admission to discharge. In particular, you ensure: The rigorous monitoring of clinical documentation (input processes, anamnesis corrections and summaries on the hospital record, letters from their creation to validation, etc.)

Managing daily physician requests, mail, telephone calls, external and/or internal appointments, insurance and DRM, etc.

Proactive updating of electronic medical records (review results, corrections, repatriation of documents) You actively participate in the life of the service and units, especially via the Huddles, and are in direct contact with patients at their bedside. Your sense of organization, autonomy and initiative allow you to effectively manage priorities, even in situations of stress. Versatile or versatile and responsive or reactive, you can easily adapt to everyday changes and unforeseen events. Your flexibility allows you to intervene in different services according to the needs of the institution, while maintaining a high level of quality in your work. You also contribute to specific missions entrusted by your manager and ensure that you maintain a team spirit and constant rigour in your activities.

Qualifications

You have an SWC of a commercial employee or employee/a commercial professional maturity and/or a medical or medical secretary degree, or any training deemed equivalent. You have a successful experience of at least two years in a similar position in a medical secretariat. You have a good knowledge of the administrative structure, procedures and organisation of the HUGs. You have a perfect command of French in both oral and written terms and a proven ability to write. Medical terminology is easy to understand. Knowledge of English is an asset. You have very good knowledge of computer tools (standard ECDL/advanced level on Word, Excel, Powerpoint, Outlook and/or Web) as well as software in force at the HUG (DPA, DPI including the hospital file, OPALE, GRECO, Winscribe and Request Lab). While you have a good sense of organisation and priorities, you are an autonomous, rigorous and precise person in the tasks entrusted to you. You communicate easily with your colleagues and superiors. You have the ability to manage unforeseen events and to work with many constraints. You have a keen sense of confidentiality.

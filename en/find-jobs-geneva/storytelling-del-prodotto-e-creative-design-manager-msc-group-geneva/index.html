Your Purpose

As a Product Storytelling & Creative Concept Manager, you will transform early-stage innovation ideas into compelling stories, visual narratives, and market-ready concepts. Acting as the creative bridge between concept development and go-to-market readiness, you will craft the messaging, storytelling, and visual expression that make new experiences, venues, products, and spaces clear, desirable, and memorable.

Your Impact

Creative Storytelling & Concept Narrative

  • Craft the core story behind new product innovation concepts.
  • Turn early ideas, spaces, and guest experiences into simple, emotional, and memorable narratives.
  • Develop original hooks, headlines, claims, naming directions, taglines, and key messages.
  • Clearly define what a concept is, why it is different, and why guests should care.
  • Transform concepts from simple descriptions into commercially compelling stories that create excitement and desire.

Copywriting, Claims & Messaging

  • Write concise and impactful copy for concept one-pagers, presentations, launch packs, and executive materials.
  • Create headlines, claims, elevator pitches, concept descriptions, and storytelling assets.
  • Simplify complex ideas into clear, premium, and distinctive messaging.
  • Adapt communication styles for different audiences, from senior leadership to commercial teams.
  • Challenge generic language and ensure originality in storytelling.

Graphic Design & Visual Expression

  • Design polished one-pagers, concept sheets, mood boards, storytelling decks, and visual toolkits.
  • Define the visual identity, tone, and creative world of each concept.
  • Use typography, layout, imagery, colour, and visual hierarchy to communicate ideas effectively.

Go-To-Market Readiness

  • Build storytelling packs that enable downstream teams to successfully launch and promote concepts.
  • Translate innovation ideas into practical marketing assets, including key messages, claims, visual narratives, and content blocks.
  • Collaborate closely with Marketing, Brand, Communications, Commercial, and Activation teams.
  • Ensure each concept has a strong creative foundation before launch planning begins.
  • Act as the transition point between internal innovation development and external market communication.

Creative Collaboration & Workshops

  • Partner with Product Innovation, Design, Newbuild, Brand, Communications, Revenue, and Operations teams.
  • Facilitate creative discussions to uncover what makes each concept distinctive.
  • Lead workshops to refine positioning, strengthen storytelling, and test creative ideas.
  • Consolidate diverse inputs into one coherent creative direction.
  • Present creative concepts confidently and clearly communicate the rationale behind them.

Creative Quality & Originality

  • Own the quality, originality, and clarity of all Product Innovation storytelling outputs.
  • Ensure deliverables are engaging, memorable, and never generic.
  • Monitor trends across travel, hospitality, lifestyle, entertainment, food & beverage, retail, culture, and design.
  • Bring fresh ideas, references, and creative perspectives to the team.
  • Continuously elevate how innovation concepts are communicated and remembered.

Your Journey so far

  • 5–8 years of experience in creative storytelling, graphic design, copywriting, brand narrative, creative studios, agencies, hospitality, lifestyle, travel, or product innovation environments.
  • Proven experience developing concept narratives, storytelling frameworks, and creative communication assets.
  • Strong portfolio showcasing graphic design, visual communication, storytelling, and concept development work.
  • Experience translating abstract or early-stage concepts into clear and persuasive outputs.
  • Experience creating one-pagers, creative decks, concept sheets, launch toolkits, storyboards, and brand storytelling materials.
  • Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams across creative, commercial, product, and operational functions.
  • Confident presenting creative work and engaging senior stakeholders.

Your Essentials

  • Excellent copywriting skills with the ability to create compelling headlines, claims, hooks, and narratives.
  • Advanced proficiency with creative tools such as Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, Canva, Keynote, PowerPoint, or similar.
  • Strong visual design skills, including typography, layout, hierarchy, mood boarding, and presentation design.
  • Ability to transform abstract ideas into visually engaging and commercially compelling concepts.
  • Strong conceptual thinking and storytelling abilities.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and collaboration skills.
  • High attention to detail and strong judgment regarding design, tone of voice, and content quality.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced and evolving innovation environment.
  • Passion for travel, hospitality, guest experience, lifestyle, entertainment, and cultural trends.
  • Fluent English communication skills; additional languages are an advantage.
  • Ability to travel occasionally as required.
  • EU Passport or the right to work in Switzerland

Your Purpose

Are you the person who can turn a blank page, a bold idea and a half-formed concept into a story people instantly want to believe in? We are seeking a Product Storytelling & Creative Design Manager to bring new product innovation concepts to life through exceptional copywriting, compelling storytelling and strong visual design. You will turn new spaces, venues and guest experiences into clear, memorable stories, claims, visuals and high-quality creative materials.

Sitting within Experience Innovation, you will act as the creative bridge between Product Innovation, Architectural Design and Marketing, ensuring Brand & Marketing and other relevant commercial functions have the stories, visuals, renders and briefing materials they need, at the right time and aligned with commercial needs. This is a hands-on creative role for someone who can write exceptionally well, think visually and synthesise complex information into something simple and compelling, while helping raise the overall quality and consistency of how concepts are documented and presented at MSC Cruises.

Your Impact

  • Craft the core story behind new product innovation concepts.

  • Develop original hooks, headlines, claims, naming directions, taglines, and key messages.
  • Make every concept answer clearly: what is it, why is it different, and why should guests care?
  • Push concepts beyond description into desire — making them feel exciting, ownable, and commercially compelling.

Copywriting, Claims & Messaging

  • Write concise and impactful copy for concept one-pagers, presentations, launch packs, and executive materials.
  • Create headlines, claims, elevator pitches, concept descriptions, and storytelling assets.
  • Simplify complex ideas into clear, premium, and distinctive messaging.
  • Adapt communication styles for different audiences, from senior leadership to commercial teams.
  • Challenge generic language and ensure originality in storytelling.
  • Create visually polished one-pagers, concept sheets, mood boards, storytelling decks and visual toolkits.

Marketing & Creative Briefing Readiness

  • Build creative storytelling packs that help downstream teams take concepts to market with clarity and confidence.
  • Translate innovation ideas into usable assets: key messages, claims, hooks, copy blocks, concept descriptions, and visual narratives.
  • Work closely with Marketing, Brand, Communications, Commercial, and activation teams to understand what they need to launch, sell, and amplify each concept.
  • Ensure each concept has a clear creative foundation before moving into broader launch planning.
  • Act as the handover point between internal innovation thinking and external expression.

Creative Collaboration & Workshops

  • Partner with Product Innovation, Design, Newbuild, Brand, Communications, Revenue, and Operations teams to understand each concept at depth.
  • Ask smart, provocative questions to uncover what makes an idea truly distinctive.

Creative Project Management

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

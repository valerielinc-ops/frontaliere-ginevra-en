Full description

Introduction

For our Human Resources division, we are looking for a HR Project Manager (M/F) whose main mission will be to manage and monitor HR and HRSI projects and contribute to the continuous improvement of HR processes and tools.

Responsibilities

  • Managing and monitoring HR and HRIS projects from inception to deployment of solutions
  • Organize and drive change: team support, internal communication and development of training materials
  • Identify areas for HR process improvement and monitor HR IT tool developments
  • Work closely with HR teams, IT teams and other stakeholders
  • Provide access management and functional maintenance of HR tools

Profile

  • Higher education
  • First successful project management experience and strong interest in HR and associated IT tools
  • Very good control of Microsoft tools, including PowerPoint, SharePoint and Teams
  • Knowledge of SAP, SuccessFactors and SmartRecruiters is an asset
  • Excellent editorial skills
  • Excellent organization and management of priorities, with methodological approach
  • Ability to evolve in a confidential environment
  • French mother tongue with good English knowledge
  • Strong customer orientation, autonomy, rigour, versatility and curiosity

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The reception sector and the telephone centre are the first points of contact and image of the HUGs. Their aim is to convey the values of the HUGs from the very beginning by ensuring a professional, warm and efficient reception, orienting and teaching patients, relatives, visitors and collaborators with listening, discretion and benevolence. Their mission is to ensure quality management, and a reassuring experience from the first exchanges.

Description of post

As a reception officer or bailiff, you ensure the reception, information and guidance of patients and patients, their loved ones, the public and collaborators. With your professionalism and sense of service, you guarantee a friendly, friendly and efficient reception, both on the face and on the phone, thus contributing to the quality of the support and brand image of the HUGs. As such, you manage the HUG telephone exchange and respond to requests by providing suitable solutions or by directing interlocutors to the appropriate services. You contribute to the quality of the data by searching for missing information or confirming it, making sure it is accurate, and tracing the discrepancies found. Whether in reception or concierge, you also take part in various logistical support activities, including the management of found objects, the sorting and distribution of mail, as well as the management of the material made available to employees (keys, bikes, cleaning/dressing services, sales).

Qualifications

You have an employee or business employee CFC, or a hotel school, or training deemed equivalent. You have gained experience in a team offering a reception service for a large number of clients. You are perfectly comfortable with computer tools. You have excellent command of the French language, both oral and written and have the ability to speak English (minimum B1 level), and German as an asset. You demonstrate excellent relational qualities, customer orientation and proven sense of service. You are a smiling, organized and methodical person in your work. Team spirit and collaboration are required as well as capacity to manage work independently. You demonstrate a strong motivation to continually change the sector at the qualitative and quantitative level. You are a versatile and geographically mobile person and are able to work on several sites.

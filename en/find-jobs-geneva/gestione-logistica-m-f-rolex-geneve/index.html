Full description

Introduction

For our Logistics Resources department within our Procurement, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Logistics Manager whose main mission will be to manage the activities of its perimeter within the defined deadlines and objectives, while ensuring customer satisfaction.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and organize workload to meet service contracts
  • Host sectu sessions
  • Piloting actions and priorities
  • Providing critical feedback to the right interlocutors
  • Provide the first level of assistance or support (Helpdesk niv 1)
  • Analyze and address anomalies and discrepancies
  • Planning and managing inventories according to defined objectives
  • Optimize stock occupancy
  • Propose changes and improvements
  • Reliability and integrity of stocks
  • Respect and enforce existing rules and procedures
  • Participate and actively contribute to service projects
  • Train on procedures and management tools related to its perimeter
  • Ensure compliance with security rules and report deviations
  • Participating in the process of change

Profile

  • CFC in logistics or equivalent. Federal certificate of logisticsian, an asset
  • Workshop management, stock management
  • Mastering computer interfaces made available (ERP, SAP, BI/TABLEAU, FLEXNET, Office pack...)
  • Analysis and synthesis capacity
  • Supply chain terms
  • Experience in administrative tasks
  • Respecting company rules and values
  • Management of priorities, proactivity
  • Benevolence and integrity
  • Leadership, Team spirit, listening, easy communication
  • Self-reliance, decision-making ability and discernment
  • Organized, good stress resistance and adaptability
  • Polyvalent, ability to work in multi-tasking

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

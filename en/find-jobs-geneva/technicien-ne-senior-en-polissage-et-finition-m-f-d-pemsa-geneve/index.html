Your assignment

  • Perform high precision polishing operations on components for watches and jewelry
  • Perform accessories such as rejuvenation, felting, sanding, satin finishing, sanding
  • Ensure rigorous aesthetic control
  • Perform assembly maintenance operations
  • Working on coating components and precious materials such as gold

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have at least 5 years of proven polishing experience, ideally in watchmaking or jewellery sectors
  • Complete mastery of polishing with skills in finishing, felting, sandblasting, satin finishing and sanding for high value pieces.
  • AFP and/or CFC in polishing and finishing
  • Experience with precious materials, coating components, overhaul, as well as excellent manual dexterity for minuziose business operations.
  • Rigore, sharing knowledge, attention to detail and autonomy in work are essential to grow in a demanding environment and oriented to excellence.

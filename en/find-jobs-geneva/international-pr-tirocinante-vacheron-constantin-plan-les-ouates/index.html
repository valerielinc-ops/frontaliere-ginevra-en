INTERNATIONAL PR INTERN

Internship, from January 2027

With internship agreement (“convention de stage”)

Plan-les-Ouates

FOR THOSE WHO SEEK EXCELLENCE

Join one of the most respected Maisons in fine watchmaking where excellence is both a given and a never-ending quest. Play your part in driving our collective success.

YOUR ROLE

We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented International PR Intern to join our dynamic team. She/he will assist with press office duties, coordinate the traveling collection, and ensure smooth communication and coordination across various stakeholders. This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of international public relations within a luxury environment.

PLAYING YOUR PART

TRAVELLING COLLECTION COORDINATION (80%)

  • You will handle the worldwide coordination of the travelling collection
  • You will follow up on local PR activations and photo shoots, maintaining efficient communication with key contacts regarding logistics, transport and security
  • You will ensure diligent daily planning and follow-up, working closely with internal teams at HQ and within our markets

CONTENT CREATION & PR ACTIVATION SUPPORT (20%)

  • You will cover press office duties such as press manufacture visits and interviews, including media briefs and shootings set up
  • You will support markets to answer their requests and improve earned media locally

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU SUCCESSFUL?

Success in this role will require

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree with a strong interest in luxury and watchmaking
  • A first internship in Press Relations and/or Public Relations would be a plus
  • Solid communication and organizational skills
  • Excel and PowerPoint skills
  • Rigor, curiosity, proactive attitude and teamwork
  • Fluency in English and French

Vacheron Constantin celebrates more than 270 years as the world’s oldest watchmaker in continuous production since 1755. Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, we operate a global retail network of nearly 200 points of sale across 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional global community of watchmakers, artisans and dedicated professionals share a common devotion to our exceptional heritage and a passion to trailblaze an even brighter future.

Full description

Mission We are seeking a visionary Global Head of CRM to lead the next phase of Chopard's customer transformation journey and establish CRM as a strategic business capability across the Maison. Reporting directly to the Chief Digital Officer, this role will be responsible for defining and executing Chopard's global CRM vision, transforming customer data into actionable client intelligence, and enabling seamless client experience across channels. The successful candidate will play a pivotal role in shaping Chopard's future customer ecosystem by establishing customer-centric processes, developing advanced CRM capabilities, building a strong CRM organization, and driving the adoption of a global client culture across markets and functions. Beyond campaign management, this position will be responsible for turning customer data into a strategic asset that supports clienteling excellence, customer development, client retention and business growth worldwide. Main responsibilities CRM STRATEGY CLIENT DEVELOPMENT Define and execute the global CRM roadmap in alignment with business priorities. Drive CRM contribution to business performance by identifying opportunities to increase client conversion, repeat purchases and customer lifetime value. Monitor market trends and luxury industry best practices to continuously evolve Chopard's CRM approach. Partner with Retail, Marketing and Digital teams to translate client insights into measurable commercial growth and revenue generation initiatives. Promote a culture of client-centric decision-making across the organization. CLIENT INTELLIGENCE PERSONALIZATION Develop a deep understanding of client behaviors and purchasing patterns. Lead client segmentation, targeting and profiling initiatives. Translate client data and insights into actionable business recommendations Drive the evolution of personalization capabilities across client journeys Support strategic decisions through client analytics and performance measurement. OMNICHANNEL CRM CLIENT EXPERIENCE Ensure consistency and relevance of CRM initiatives across all client touchpoints. Develop personalized and seamless client journeys across online and offline channels. Support the ongoing enhancement of clienteling capabilities. Foster collaboration between Retail, Marketing, Digital and Regional teams. Contribute to deliver a differentiated luxury client experience worldwide. CRM OPERATIONS, GOVERNANCE LEADERSHIP Lead, coach and develop the Global CRM team. Establish CRM best practices, processes and operating frameworks. Partner with Digital and IT teams to ensure the evolution of CRM capabilities and tools. Ensure high standards of client data quality and operational excellence. Define and monitor CRM KPIs and business impact. Ensure CRM investments and initiatives deliver measurable business outcomes and commercial value. Manage CRM partners and external agencies.

Full description

Do you like it when it's all right? Join our team and become an essential link in the restaurant. Thanks to your energy and your sense of organization, you guarantee a clean, safe and welcoming environment. Between diving, goods management and team support, you play a key role in providing our clients with a quality experience every day.

Luogo di lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Genève, Restaurant Chêne-Vert, Place de la Gare 2, 1225 Chêne-Bourg

Important remarks

The use of women aims for simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You clean dishes, cutlery, glassware and kitchen equipment. You run the washing equipment and guarantee an impeccable result for every service.
  • You keep kitchens, technical premises and storage areas clean and safe. You apply the hygiene rules rigorously and you help create an irreproachable environment.
  • You greet clients with a smile when you intervene in the room. You answer simple questions, help with storage and help maintain a pleasant atmosphere.
  • You support the team where the need arises. You show versatility, adapt your activity to the pace of service and help advance the day in the right direction.
  • You are involved in waste management and compliance with environmental regulations. You keep the sorting spaces clean and contribute to the daily functioning of the restaurant.
  • You receive the goods, check the deliveries and store the products in the right storage areas. You're looking out for the right rotation of stocks to limit losses.

Required

  • 1 year in a similar position: in the catering or production sector.
  • You're being rigorous and unorganized. You follow procedures, apply hygiene standards and keep your efficiency even when the pace accelerates.
  • You know how to work with others. You exchange easily with your colleagues, share useful information and help maintain a positive working atmosphere.
  • You keep heading under pressure. You stay focused during intense periods, prioritize important tasks and find simple solutions to unforeseen events.
  • You are available at least 2 days a week, between 7:30am and 5:30pm.
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Djaina Julliard Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Job Description

As a Commvault Backup Administrator, you will be responsible for the administration, operation, availability, security and continuous improvement of EFG’s enterprise Commvault Backup & Recovery platform. You will ensure that backup and restore services are reliable, timely and compliant with agreed RPO/RTO, security, audit and regulatory requirements across on‑premises and cloud environments. Leveraging your knowledge of SAN storage technologies (particularly HPE Alletra and Hitachi Vantara HDS VSP), you will help safeguard the bank’s critical data and support the resilience of our infrastructure. This is an individual contributor role with significant technical ownership and close collaboration with infrastructure, application and security stakeholders.

Key

  • Responsibilities

· Administer, operate and maintain the Commvault Backup & Recovery platform across on‑premises and cloud environments, ensuring high availability and performance. · Monitor daily backup operations, analyse failed or degraded jobs, perform root‑cause analysis and implement permanent corrective actions to meet agreed SLAs. · Execute, validate and document restores for files, virtual machines, databases and Microsoft workloads (including Active Directory, Exchange, SharePoint and Microsoft 365), ensuring reliable end‑to‑end recovery. · Define, create and maintain backup plans, schedules, retention rules, Storage Policies and protection requirements in line with business, security, audit and regulatory needs. · Administer Commvault CommServe components, Media Agents (Windows and Linux), HyperScale X, disk libraries and object‑storage targets, including connectivity and capacity management. · Manage Commvault agents, clients, subclients, access permissions, alerts, dashboards and operational reporting, providing Level 2/3 expertise for backup and recovery incidents. · Plan, test and implement Commvault upgrades, feature releases, security updates and hotfixes under formal change‑management procedures, in coordination with Infrastructure, Security and Risk stakeholders. · Apply and continuously improve backup‑security controls (encryption, immutability, privileged access, data isolation and ransomware‑resilience measures) in collaboration with Information Security and Compliance. · Lead or participate in recovery tests, disaster‑recovery exercises and cyber‑recovery scenarios, documenting results, remediation actions and audit evidence for internal and external auditors. · Provide operational support for SAN‑backed backup infrastructure and coordinate activities involving HPE Alletra and Hitachi Vantara HDS VSP (capacity, performance, provisioning, zoning and connectivity). · Participate in a 24/7 on‑call duty rotation and support critical incidents, including planned maintenance and recovery activities outside normal business hours.

Requirements and

  • Qualifications

Education

· Minimum: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a closely related field, or an equivalent combination of education and professional experience. · Preferred: Commvault Professional‑level certification; HPE or Hitachi storage certifications are considered strong assets.

Professional Experience · At least 3 years of hands‑on experience as a Commvault administrator in a complex enterprise environment. · Experience in a regulated or banking environment is an advantage. · Proven track record managing backup operations, resolving failures, tuning performance, managing capacity and conducting reliable end‑to‑end restorations. · Experience participating in disaster‑recovery and cyber‑recovery exercises and supporting 24/7 on‑call operations is preferred.

Technical and Functional Knowledge · Solid knowledge of Commvault Backup & Recovery, including core components such as CommServe, Command Center, Media Agents, clients, subclients, plans and Storage Policies. · Experience protecting and restoring key enterprise workloads (for example Windows, Linux, VMware, Active Directory, Exchange, SharePoint, Microsoft 365 and major databases). · Understanding of Commvault upgrades, security hardening, role‑based access, encryption and immutability, with the ability to apply these in line with security and audit requirements. · Ability to define and maintain backup policies, retention standards, recovery procedures, operational dashboards and RPO/RTO reporting. · Good understanding of SAN and storage concepts; exposure to HPE Alletra and Hitachi Vantara HDS VSP or similar enterprise storage platforms is an advantage. · Familiarity with basic automation or scripting for monitoring, reporting and recurring administrative tasks is an advantage. · Strong analytical, troubleshooting and documentation skills, with the ability to prioritise operational work and respond effectively to critical incidents. · Strong collaboration and communication skills when working with technical teams, application owners, Information Security, Risk, auditors and external vendors.

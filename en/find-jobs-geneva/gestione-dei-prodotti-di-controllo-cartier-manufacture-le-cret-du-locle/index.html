CONTEXT

Attached to the Manager of Management Control of Horological Manufactures, the Control Manager "Pôle Produits" controls the costing and performance of the activities of the Products and Services Directorate (Development, Quality and Reliability, Customer Service).

He/she guarantees the reliability of management data, supports operational and strategic decisions, supervises the main financial processes and accompanies the transformation of the perimeter, while ensuring the management of a team composed of 2 industrial controls.

MAIN MISSIONS

  • Managing performance and reporting: monitoring development and production costs, investments and key monthly indicators, analysing variances, risks and opportunities and providing a clear reading for decision-making by operational managers. Supervise the cost and cost statements to the House and the Group
  • Ensure product cost control: calculate, analyse and secure cost prices throughout the product development cycle and provide financial expertise to Development teams (including Product Development Managers)
  • Coordinate Budget and Forecasts: build and value production plans with Finance and Operations teams, explain the volume/price/mix/performance effects to justify possible variances and manage the budget of costs, investments and hourly rates
  • Improving industrial performance and piloting tools: changing indicators, management control processes, management of precious metals and stocks, and contributing to the evolution of LES and digital tools
  • Ensuring financial governance and internal control: ensuring compliance with Group and IFRS rules, coordinating inventories and leading corrective actions.
  • Managing a team and supporting digital transformation: mentoring and developing the Industrial Management Control & Costing team, anticipating skills and succession needs, while contributing to projects to transform, digitize and harmonize Finance practices.

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

You have a Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Management or Engineering and have at least 7 to 10 years experience in industrial management control (perfect mastery of standard cost concepts, gap analysis, inventory, cost costs, hourly rate, Capex, budget and forecasts).

You already have a first experience in management and you speak fluent English.

You are expert in using Excel and you have already used SAP, Anaplan, Power Bi and ideally Looker.

Rigoury and endowed with an excellent spirit of analysis and synthesis, you can turn complex problems into concrete and actionable recommendations. Close to the field and oriented to business, you stand out for your leadership, relationship ease and ability to challenge constructively, to unite your team with two collaborators and to accompany change.

We offer a field-oriented experience with challenging and varied missions. In this way, you help meet our challenges by bringing and developing key skills such as: project management, critical thinking and leadership.

Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship as a Finisher - Numerical Control option? Ready to become a reference in your trade thanks to high-quality training in a leading company? Join us! The watchmaking finisher receives the various parts of a watch once they have been machined. He/she subjects them to a series of finishing operations (polishing, satin-finishing, highlighting, etc.) designed to give their surface the final aesthetic appearance desired by the customer. This profession allows you to tackle precision mechanics as well as aesthetic and dimensional control. After initial manual training, the Numerical Control (NC) orientation allows you to specialize in carrying out operations using automated machines on large volumes. This apprenticeship is suitable for young people who are comfortable in technological and mechanical environments, with a keen eye for detail.

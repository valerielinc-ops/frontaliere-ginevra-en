Introduction

As an Applied Physicist, you will join the ATLAS Computing Section, which is responsible for the development, operation, maintenance and future upgrades of the Tier-0 and Rucio data processing/management systems for the ATLAS Experiment. You will be in charge of maintaining, operating and evolving the ATLAS Tier-0 system, overseeing all aspects of central data handling and processing for the ATLAS Experiment, and providing ATLAS physicists with high-quality data products for analysis and searches for new discoveries. The ATLAS Tier-0 is the central hub of the ATLAS offline computing infrastructure, responsible for the prompt reconstruction, registration and archiving of raw detector data, as well as its distribution to Tier-1 and Tier-2 centres worldwide. It also supports essential data-preparation activities, including detector calibration, alignment and data-quality monitoring. Its critical role in the ATLAS data-processing chain demands reliable, continuous operation, rapid problem resolution and flexible support to meet the evolving needs of the Experiment and its clients and users.

Functions

  • Take a leading role, as part of a small team of experts, in the operation, maintenance, and evolution of the Tier-0 system to meet the needs of the ATLAS Experiment, with the goal of providing the physics community with data of excellent quality. This includes contributing to all operational aspects of central Tier-0 data handling and processing.
  • Engage with a broad and diverse community of stakeholders and clients (including CERN IT and the ATLAS Trigger/DAQ, Software and Computing, Data Preparation, Physics and Detector communities) to understand their data processing requirements and translate them into solutions implemented within the Tier-0 system.
  • Contribute to the maintenance and development of the Tier-0 software suite in response to evolving requirements, as well as to the development of applications, tools and interfaces supporting its operation.
  • Take a leading role in the preparation and commissioning of the Tier-0 system for the HL-LHC era. This includes activities like the commissioning of calibration and alignment workflows for the new or upgraded detectors; the implementation of new data handling and data management strategies (with CERN IT, Trigger/DAQ, Rucio); the exploration of possible synergies with the ATLAS Grid Production System; and the development and commissioning of innovative workflows for optimising the usage of computing resources (CPU, memory, storage).

Master's Degree or PhD or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Large-Scale Computing-System Operations / Physics, Data Processing or a related field.

Experience

  • Experience in the development and operation of large-scale data processing systems for scientific experiments or in comparable industrial environments.
  • Experience in supporting a diverse community of stakeholders, including prioritising and balancing competing requirements.
  • Proven proficiency in Python, SQL-based databases and Git for version control.
  • Familiarity with web interface development technologies (HTML, JavaScript and CSS), including backend frameworks.
  • Knowledge of calibration requirements and procedures for particle physics experiments would be an advantage.
  • Understanding of configuration management and orchestration tools (e.g. Puppet and Kubernetes) is considered an advantage.

Technical competencies

  • Architecture and design of ICT systems: in particular of physics data processing and analysis frameworks.
  • Verification and optimisation of ICT architectures and designs.
  • Simulation, design and development of data processing and analysis workflows.
  • Conceptualising, designing and developing user experiences and interfaces.
  • Development of application software.

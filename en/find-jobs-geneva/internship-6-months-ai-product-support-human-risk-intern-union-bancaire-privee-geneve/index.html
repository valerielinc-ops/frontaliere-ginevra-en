Full description

Job Title: AI Product Support & Human Risk Intern Mission As a Human Risk Officer Intern, you will play a key role in supporting the Group HR Risk Team in identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks associated with human behavior, ethics, and conduct within the bank. Your primary focus will be on preventing risks such as fraud, misconduct, conflicts of interest, insider threats, and non-compliance with regulatory requirements. In this role, you will collaborate closely with internal stakeholders, including the Group Head of HR Risk, HR Recruiters, and employees, as well as external stakeholders on matters related to Human Risk Management and Background Checks for new hires. You will additionally work closely with the UBP Client Lifecycle AI Team to support HR AI initiatives across testing, evaluation, business requirements, governance, and product rollout. This role offers a unique opportunity to deepen your understanding of background screening processes and to translate these requirements into product development requirements, supporting the team on testing, evaluation, governance, and post-launch care for in-house HR-related AI initiatives. Key responsibilities

  • Support the AI initiative by translating background screening requirements into product development specifications.
  • Perform testing and evaluation of AI tools and processes.
  • Contribute to governance frameworks and compliance standards for AI initiatives.
  • Provide feedback on user experience, usability, and business requirements for AI-enabled workflows.
  • Support post-launch care activities including monitoring, feedback collection, and process optimization.
  • Oversee and execute background check procedures for current employees at the group level.
  • Communicate directly with employees to gather necessary information and documentation.
  • Collect, review, and analyze data and supporting documents.
  • Conduct additional research to verify and validate information as needed.
  • Maintain an up-to-date and accurate record of ongoing cases.
  • Summarize findings and prepare comprehensive final reports.
  • Identify and escalate any discrepancies or risks detected during the process.

What we are looking for

  • Bachelor’s or master’s Degree in engineering, with deep interest in wealth management, economics or similar
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills with attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities to interact with diverse stakeholders.
  • A proactive mindset with the ability to detect and address potential risks effectively.
  • A commitment to maintain confidentiality and handling sensitive information with discretion.
  • Familiarity with risk management, compliance, or HR processes is a plus.
  • AI knowledge is not required but any prior experience or interest is a strong asset.
  • Perfectly bilingual and operational in French and in English (written and speaking)
  • Team oriented
  • High working ethics
  • Discretion

This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in human risk management and AI product development within a dynamic and fast-paced environment. You will work closely with HR experts and AI engineers to learn how background screening works and translate those requirements into effective AI solutions. If you are detail-oriented, eager to learn, and passionate about fostering ethical and compliant workplace practices, we encourage you to apply.

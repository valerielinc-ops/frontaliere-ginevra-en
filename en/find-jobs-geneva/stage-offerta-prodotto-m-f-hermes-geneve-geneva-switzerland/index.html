Full description

The main mission of the Intern is to assist the Product Offering / Retail Merchandising team in managing the product assortment of the 10 stores located in Switzerland, Poland and the Czech Republic.

Your missions

Analysis of results

  • Monthly update of product Sell-Through (sales rate)
  • Analysis of the subsidiary's results and updating of sales and inventory tracking files by collection and department: Best Sellers, Slow Movers, sales by product families
  • Preparation of the annual sales report

Collection tracking

  • Monitoring deliveries of new collections and new products in store
  • Sharing information relating to new collections with stores and headquarters
  • Performance monitoring of new products
  • Updating and sharing the product launch calendar

Optimization of subsidiary stock

  • Weekly transfer proposals between central stock and stores
  • Proposals for stock rebalancing between stores and monitoring of transfers

Placing and Tracking Orders

  • Management and tracking of customer orders
  • Restocking suggestions

Purchases

  • Creation and updating of the necessary analyzes before purchasing sessions in the different departments
  • Preparation of binders and documents necessary for buyers
  • Assistance in preparing buyer briefs and proofreading Purchasing

Updating and sending the Product Offer Newsletter

  • Preparation of documents and sending of the monthly Newsletter to stores and headquarters

Your profile

  • Excellent knowledge of IT tools and particular ease with Excel and Powerpoint
  • Very good analytical skills
  • Attention to results and the demand for quality
  • The sense of collective and service
  • Good interpersonal skills
  • An excellent level in French and English
  • Prior 6-month internship experience is desired

Internship conditions

  • Start of course on February 22
  • Minimum duration of 06 months (renewable up to 12 months)
  • An internship agreement is necessary

Full description

In your capacity as head or head of clinic with speciality in Gynecology and Obstetrics, you guarantee the quality of medical services in the care units of your field of competence. You ensure, under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, a position of head or head of clinic in the Gynaecology Service, focused on Urogynecology. Within the Perineology Unit, you ensure the consultations of the neurogeology, perineology and the neurodynamics. Your activity also includes a weekly operating period focusing on urogynecological procedures (cured urinary incontinence and genital prolapsus treatment, among others). Unity is at the cutting edge of minimally invasive techniques and allows you to learn them. You coordinate the practical activities as well as the diagnostic and therapeutic strategies of the patients entrusted to you in close cooperation with the assistant and assistant physicians.

  • You also participate and contribute to the supervision and training of intern physicians and trainees.
  • Demonstrating a confirmed interest in teaching, you participate in pre- and post-graduate and continuing training in urogynecology.
  • A dynamic, flexible and committed person, you develop interdisciplinary clinical projects and participate in research projects in the Gynaecology Service.
  • You take part in the chief and chief clinic guards of the gynaecology and obstetric services, as well as the supervision of gynaecological emergencies during the day.

Reference code: JR130718 Who are we? A High Jewelry House with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment. Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself. At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence. Do you match this profile? You are dynamic, flexible, communicative and a good lister. You are passionate about Jewelry, the Maison universe and art craftsmanship. You are comfortable in a sales environment in boutique in contact with both international and local customers. You are looking for a team spirit environment focusing its energy on customers, needs and satisfaction. You speak fluently in English and French and Russian would be a more. You have preliminary experience in sales and in the luxury industry. If so, we!d love to get to know you! What do we expect from you? As a Sales Associate in our boutique, you are the voice of Van Cleef & Arpels for our customers and you build and maintain strong relationships with them. You are an ambassador of the House and you promote its image both internally and externally. You demonstrate excellent commercial sense and actively participate in the recruitment and development of new customers. You like to offer a unique experience to our loyal customers You are conveying the refinement, elegance and innovation that inspires the Maison creations. More than a job... an experience! Joining Van Cleef & Arpels is before all joining a team where the development of our employees is at the heart of our ambition. You will have the opportunity to join our boarding program with a wide range of trainings on our history, our know-how and creations, as well as daily support. The recruitment process: Apply online and if your profile is selected, then: You will be contacted by the Talent Acquisition team for an initial online digital interview Boutique based interview to meet the management team. You will then meet the Retail Manager and HR Business Partner for the Swiss market. If your application is not selected, you will receive a email rejection.

Full description

The movement has been in our DNA since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow up with us. Join our family and shape with us the future of transport and storage logistics.

Aufgaben

  • Like a pro, you load and unload trucks by controlling every commodity and pallet.
  • Carefully manage transport and customs documents (import/export).
  • You keep the warehouse clean and tidy and help keep everything fluid and efficient.
  • You work with internal and external partners to ensure that the info always circulates correctly.

Profile

  • You already have a first experience in the field and you know how to handle a transpalette like a pro.
  • You have a carist license.
  • You have strength and endurance, and you don't hesitate to give a hand when it comes to handling goods.
  • You work reliably and accurately, and quickly adapt to new situations or procedures.
  • Even when the rhythm accelerates, you keep your calm and your sense of organization.
  • You know how to communicate clearly in French and strictly abide by all OFAC rules.
  • Clear legal record and clear history are mandatory for this position.

Benefits

  • You'll be part of our family business culture. - respect, trust and courtesy are our key values
  • We live with our time - you have modern and safe working tools
  • With us, you can park for free

As a company, we attach great importance to collaboration and a good working atmosphere. This is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, availability and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis of a reliable and customer-oriented method of work.

Full description

Socio-administrative assistance

  • Within the reception, contribute to the reception and guidance of beneficiaries at the social and administrative offices of the centre, as well as to internal and external partners as requested.
  • Within the social services, in support of social workers, participate in interviews and registration of applications and responses in our systems.
  • Upon delegation of the social workers, provide additional support to the beneficiaries when necessary (support to complete certain forms, guidance of beneficiaries to facilitate their travel in the canton, etc.).
  • During dedicated periods, participate in the delivery of financial benefits.
  • Within the reception, support social secretaries in the tasks of distributing internal mail, making appointments in the agendas of social workers, and transferring calls and information to employees.
  • Within the collective accommodation centre, support administrative assistants in the preparation and reception of new beneficiaries and the transfer of beneficiaries to other units, by contributing to the preparation of records, the editing of various documents, filing and archiving.
  • Computer tool mastery (Word, Excel, specific tool);
  • Adaptability;
  • Ease of writing in French;
  • A good knowledge of the English language would be an asset.

Further information Civil service is an alternative to military service, which cannot be reconciled with conscience. They perform alternative civilian service upon request.

To be able to apply for the positions below, it is imperative that you have completed all the procedures with the Civil Service so that it recognizes you as a civilist. The General Hospital as a priority assignment institution engages civilists in various areas of expertise.

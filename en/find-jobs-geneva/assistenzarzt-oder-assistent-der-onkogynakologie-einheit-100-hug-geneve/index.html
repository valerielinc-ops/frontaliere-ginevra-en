Full description

As an assistant doctor or assistant doctor, you guarantee the quality of medical services in the surgical onco-gynaecology unit. You collaborate with the medical-care staff and ensure the coordination of practical activities, as well as the diagnostic and therapeutic strategies of patients entrusted to you. Evolving in a stimulating academic environment, in a multidisciplinary team, you demonstrate leadership and use your excellent organizational, relational and communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with the patient. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability.

  • You support, supervise and efficiently supervise the unit's in-house physicians and heads of clinics in their skills development.
  • Demonstrating a confirmed interest in teaching, you participate in pre- and post-graduate and continuing training in the unit and in the gynaecology department.
  • You take part in the guards of the chiefs and chiefs of clinics of gynaecology and obstetric services.
  • Demonstrating dynamism, flexibility and commitment, you develop interdisciplinary clinical projects and deploy and ensure research in the unit.
  • On request, you will participate in the gynecology department's picket system.

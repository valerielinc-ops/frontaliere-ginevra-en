Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Department of Clinical Neurosciences. This department provides care to patients of all ages, children and adults, with neurosurgical, neurological, ophthalmological and oto-rhino-laryngological pathologies. Care is tailored to the needs and difficulties of the patient and patient, from the most acute sectors (intermediate care units) to the rehabilitation sectors (neuroreeducation). The aim of the department is to bring the patient to a recovery of her autonomy through teaching, accompaniment and outpatient follow-up. It ensures the operation of patients and patients such as their accompaniment, in order to promote an early return home. In the same vein, outpatient care is also preferred and operational activity optimized to facilitate access to care within a reasonable time frame.

Description of post

At the Bellerive , Cluse-Roseraie and Beau-Séjour sites, you ensure the supply and efficiently manage the department's inventory by proposing optimal resupply and storage measures, with the aim of minimizing the cost of possession and limiting disruptions. You continuously determine stock levels and resupply thresholds, based on consumption and supply times in collaboration with teams. You participate in inventories, manage orders necessary for the proper operation of the stock, ensure receipt, control the equipment and its replacement. You are the contact person for any questions and/or problems related to stock management. You define in partnership with the care team leaders, the necessary care items. You ensure maintenance of premises and equipment as well as liaison with the various technical and logistical services.

Qualifications

You benefit from training in logistics or equivalent. The CFC level is required. The CAS in Industrial Supply Chain is an asset. You have proven skills in using IT management tools as well as the package office. Knowledge of an ERP (Qualiac and Eproc) is a considerable asset. You also have good knowledge of the French language as well as editorial convenience. You benefit from a successful experience in the field of inventory management and customer/supplier relationship, customer/provider. You have good physical resistance and can justify handling ability. With entrepreneurship, you have a relational ease, a spirit of collaboration and a sense of service. You have a capacity to work in a large multidisciplinary team and in collaboration with other logistics managers. Autonomous, you are very rigorous and professional, with a strong sense of organization and priorities. You also have good stress resistance, can manage priorities and can adapt to geographic mobility.

Full description

Mission

Attached to our Research and Development Division, you will be responsible for measuring and simulating the shock behaviour of a reference mechanical device to improve the accuracy of our digital models.

Responsibilities

  • Analyze, measure, simulate and document impact behaviour from a reference device.
  • Measure and determine modelling parameters.
  • Develop methodologies to optimize measurements in numerical simulations.
  • Validate parameters and evaluate the impact on the reliability of models Shock measurement (acceleration, fast camera).
  • Modelling using the finite element method (FEM) to ensure the operation and integrity of the impactors.
  • Implement action plans associated with these activities.

Profile sought

  • Currently in Master in Mechanical Engineering or Microtechnics.
  • Competencies in data analysis and dynamic numerical simulation.
  • Knowledge of numerical modelling tools.
  • Good project management skills.
  • Rigor, precision, analysis and synthesis.
  • Orientation "solutions" and strength of proposal.
  • Master's project lasting 6 months.

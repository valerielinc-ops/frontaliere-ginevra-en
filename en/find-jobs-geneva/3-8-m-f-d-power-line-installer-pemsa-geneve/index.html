Your assignment

  • Ensure the operation of waste incineration and energy recovery plants.
  • Monitor the operation of equipment and plants through control systems and during regular field inspection turns.
  • Check the operating parameters and identify any anomalies or deviations.
  • Contribute to the optimization of energy production of the plant.
  • Ensure compliance with operational instructions and contribute to the regulatory compliance of environmental waste.
  • Perform routine operations necessary for the proper functioning of the plants.
  • Participate in the diagnosis and resolution of the first level problems related to waste treatment equipment and energy production.
  • Report the malfunctions that require specialized intervention and collaborate with maintenance teams.
  • Record operating information and events to ensure good continuity between different teams.
  • Strictly apply the operational procedures and site safety rules.

And now we're talking about you?

  • At least 5 years of experience in a similar job in industry.
  • Experience in the management, monitoring or management of industrial plants.
  • Good understanding of industrial processes and ability to monitor different operating parameters.
  • Good skills in first-level diagnostics on mechanical, electrical or automated systems.
  • Ability to quickly identify anomaly and take the first necessary measures.
  • Particular attention is paid to safety, environment and operational continuity.
  • Ability to work independently while maintaining excellent communication with other team members.
  • Rigore, responsiveness and ability to maintain good control of priorities in emergency situations.
  • Needless to work with 3×8 shifts.

This is a long-term temporary assignment, starting from the last quarter of 2026. Retribution based on the profile and the wage table.

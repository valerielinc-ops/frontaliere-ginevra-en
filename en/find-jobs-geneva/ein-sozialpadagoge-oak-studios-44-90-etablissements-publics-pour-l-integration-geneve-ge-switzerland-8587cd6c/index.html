Full description

  • Integrate the people concerned into community life by highlighting their skills and resources, while respecting their rights and duties.
  • Develop personalized socio-educational support plans, ensure their follow-up, and engage team members in their day-to-day implementation.
  • Carry out delegated duties as the designated contact person for one or more people concerned and maintain regular contact with their network (legal representatives, close contacts, attending physician...).
  • Ensure that their educational practice is consistent with the principles set out in the institutional policy “Integration and Support”.
  • Maintain positive relationships with the people concerned in keeping with the socio-educational values of EPI.
  • Support people in their daily lives and provide assistance suited to their needs and resources.
  • Design, carry out and evaluate socio-educational activities, independently and/or as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Facilitate socialization, cultural, leisure and/or work activities.
  • Respond appropriately to difficult and/or complex situations.
  • Maintain direct, effective contacts and relationships with all departments and external partners.
  • Carry out the administrative tasks inherent to the position.
  • A Swiss degree in Social Work (HES level), Educational Sciences with a specialization in Special Education, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Knowledge of psychosocial and/or intellectual disabilities and/or alcohol-related issues, and a willingness to develop knowledge in these areas.
  • Previous experience in a similar position and with this population is an asset.
  • Demonstrate a service-oriented attitude toward populations in vulnerable situations.
  • Demonstrate a strong sense of responsibility focused on serving users, and an open mind.
  • Have strong interpersonal skills, communicate with ease, and have a pronounced taste for teamwork.
  • Have schedule flexibility.
  • Be proficient in standard office software (Word, Excel and Powerpoint) and the specialized tools available within the establishments.
  • Be proficient in French.

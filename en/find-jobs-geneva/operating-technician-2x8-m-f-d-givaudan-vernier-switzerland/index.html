Industrial Equipment Maintenance Technician 2x8 (m/f/d) - Your next job? As an operating technician 2x8 (m/f/d) in Vernier, you are attached to the service of Production Composition *and you report to our Technical Unit Supervisor. You will be responsible for coordinating, planning and supervising the provision of production facilities and equipment to ensure preventive, corrective and troubleshooting maintenance. You will need to ensure that each intervention is carried out safely and in the best operational conditions. You will also need to appropriate the machines and handle them according to the different needs of the interventions You're interested in this job? This could be the perfect opportunity if, as for us, your goal is to always meet the needs of customers and touch the emotions of the world around you. Your Main Mission:In our Production Composition, you coordinate and organize the provision of facilities as part of preventive, corrective and troubleshooting maintenance interventions, ensuring that the impact on production activities is limited. A real facility specialist, you ensure the preparation, monitoring and coordination of interventions in collaboration with maintenance, production and external service providers. Ensuring the safety of stakeholders and facilities, you ensure the strict application of the recording and deconsignment procedures (LOTOTO) and compliance with the applicable HSE rules. You prepare and manage the documentation required for the interventions and ensure that all safety and operational conditions are met prior to the start of the work. At the end of the interventions, you check the equipment's compliance, perform or coordinate the necessary tests and are responsible for making the facilities available to the operator. Thanks to your knowledge of the equipment and key contacts of the site, you facilitate the proper coordination of operations and help ensure the reliability and availability of the facilities. Confirmed user of the SAP software system, you will be in charge of entering repair orders, improvement or reliability projects. Consultation and stock correction, you are a reference in the use of this software. At ease with Office tools, you have a mastery in word processing and spreadsheet. Support for production, your availability and your technical skills will provide the necessary support to maintain the best operational efficiency, both on the part driven industrial installation or even first level troubleshooting. Consumables managers in this service, you will be in charge of maintaining a database of these references in order to ensure stock availability, place orders if necessary and make them available You will also be in support of the improvement project and will be the voice of the operator in order to jointly define operational needs.

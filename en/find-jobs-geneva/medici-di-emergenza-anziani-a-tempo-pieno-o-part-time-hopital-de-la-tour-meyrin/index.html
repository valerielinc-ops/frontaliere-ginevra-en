Full description

'Hôpital de La Tour is one of the leading private health facilities in French-speaking Switzerland, recognized for the excellence of its care and its demanding but human work environment. Located in Meyrin, at the gates of Geneva, it enjoys a unique strategic position: a centre of reference for the right bank Geneva, international organizations and neighbouring France.

As part of its development, the Medical Emergencies Service recruits several senior emergency physicians to join a structured, caring and quality-of-care team.

Senior full-time or part-time emergency physicians (ref: 1933)

Recognized by the Swiss Society of Emergency and Rescue Medicine (SSMUS) and a member of the Genevois Emergency Network (RUG), our service ensures complete care, 24 hours a day and 365 days a year:

  • More than 33,000 annual passages, with varied and sustained medical activity.
  • An emergency outpatient unit and a line of patients "bed down" (vital and semi-emergency emergencies).
  • A short-term observation unit (UOCD), equivalent to the French UHCD.
  • A post-emergency referral unit for close patient monitoring.
  • Direct access to hospital specialists, without delay and in a spirit of collaboration.

What You Will Do

As a senior physician, you exercise with real clinical autonomy, while actively contributing to the life of the service:

  • Management of adult patients in all the diversity of medical and surgical emergencies, from vital emergency to unscheduled consultation.
  • Clinical supervision of assistants and internal physicians: supervision, validation of decisions and daily transmission of knowledge.
  • Participation in continuous improvement of service protocols and practices.
  • Cross-cutting collaboration with health care teams and hospital specialists.

The profile we're looking for

We recruit both a confirmed emergency specialist who wants to leave the public hospital for a more serene setting, and an ambitious young specialist who is ready to build his career in an environment of excellence. What matters to us:

  • Diploma in medicine obtained in an EU Member State, eligible for MEBEKO recognition.
  • Specialised in emergency medicine: DES, DESC, European emergency medicine certificate, Swiss SSMUS training or recognised European equivalent.
  • Significant experience in high-volume emergency service: minimum 5 years in UAA or equivalent position as head of clinic.
  • Mastery of essential current French; language of work and daily transmission.
  • Ability to manage emergency with autonomy, discernment and team spirit.
  • A sincere interest in the clinical supervision and training of junior physicians.

What we offer

Organization and working conditions

  • Full-time or part-time employee, in a Swiss legal framework.
  • 40 hours per week (for a full time), with mixed hours (8am and 12h) distributed equally between days, nights and weekends.
  • Compensation for nights in accordance with the Labour Act (LTr) and Swiss practices.
  • Free parking for night guards, weekends and public holidays.
  • Direct public transport (bus stop in the immediate vicinity of the hospital).

Compensation

Attractive salary conditions, consistent with Swiss standards for senior doctors in emergencies. Details will be provided during the first interview.

Professional environment and living environment

  • An institution of human size, where medical autonomy is real and collegial decisions.
  • A senior team experienced, welded and accessible, with a culture of self-help.
  • Personal support.
  • Prospects for developing skills in emergency medicine.

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Department of Medicine, which includes medical specialty services and general internal medicine Within the Department of Medicine and the Department of Infectious Diseases, the HIV Unit is responsible for the care and prevention of people living with HIV and other sexually transmitted infections and viral hepatitis. Within the HIV unit has developed a multidisciplinary consultation specialized in the practice of chemsex, whose mission is to accompany people affected by this practice in a personalized way in terms of prevention, risk reduction, sexology, addictology, infectious disease, peer-help, and mental health by people sensitized to the topic.

Description of post

You integrate chemsex consultation into the HIV unit and ensure, within a multidisciplinary team and psychological and psychotherapeutic follow-up of patients followed. You participate in the monthly co-construction sessions of the consultation with the other members of the specialized consultation. You ensure clinical assessments, individual and/or group follow-up in collaboration with experienced professionals and professionals. You comply with the institutional guidelines for all matters related to the situations followed and the seizure of your benefits.

Qualifications

You hold a Master's degree in Psychology and you have the title of "recognized psychotherapist at the federal level". You have a particular sensitivity to the LGBTQIA+ population and the subject of addiction. You have the billing authorization at the AOS, so you can charge on an outpatient basis. You can easily integrate into the care teams and demonstrate autonomy and involvement in your work. You are a conscientious and committed person. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a stock agent, you will join the Logistics team at our Meyrin site.

In this context

  • You will prepare kits of watch components for mounting boxes and bracelets
  • You'll do pick-up tasks
  • You will have to exchange components between stock and production
  • You will guarantee the proper storage of the stock
  • You ideally have a Logistician CFC or any other equivalent level title
  • You have at least 3 years of experience in a similar position
  • You are recognized for your rigor, organizational qualities and thoroughness
  • You like to work as a team, show relational ease and have a positive mind
  • A prior experience in watchmaking is a plus

What we offer

  • Insurance coverage - disease (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities as well as subscriptions encouraging soft mobility (e.g. SBB)
  • Possibility of working remotely from several sites (GE/VD/NE)
  • An LPP with favourable rates

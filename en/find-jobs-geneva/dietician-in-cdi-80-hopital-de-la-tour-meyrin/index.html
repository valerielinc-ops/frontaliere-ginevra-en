Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Anchored in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, La Tour Hospital has placed continuous improvement and the development of medical excellence at the heart of its priorities.

In order to complete our teams, we recruit a

Dietician in CDI 80% (ref: 2009)

Your mission

You will need to help maintain or rehabilitate the health status of our patients by accompanying them in the implementation of good food practices.

In this capacity you are in charge of

  • Collaborate with physicians on ongoing and necessary projects at the Institution
  • Provide outpatient consultations for patients with obesity (surgical, pharmacological or conservative treatment), metabolic disorders and paediatrics.
  • Conduct prevention and/or patient information workshops in research for therapeutic education to improve their quality of life
  • Provide nutritional assessment and follow-up consultations with patients hospitalized in intensive care, surgery, medicine.
  • Participate in the development of our digital platform Engage for patients, aimed at therapeutic education
  • Promoting successful communication with the various actors of the Centre and the Institution in general

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Dietician HES or CRS recognition (for foreign diplomas)
  • A first prior experience, ideally in the hospital field
  • Practiced in Obesity (Bariatric Surgery) and Pediatrics
  • Case of Practitioner Trainer, an asset
  • Fluid communication in English

Want to combine professional wealth and flexibility? Our private hospital offers you a part-time dietitian position in a stimulating multidisciplinary team. Discover a wide variety of specialties while enjoying a working rhythm adapted to your life balance.

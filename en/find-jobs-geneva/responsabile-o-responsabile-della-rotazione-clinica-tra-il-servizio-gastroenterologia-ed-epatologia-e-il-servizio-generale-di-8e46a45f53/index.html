Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Medicine (DMED) provides comprehensive and specialized care for patients in hospitals and outpatients. Driven by a holistic, human approach based on best medical practices, it offers reference care in many specialties (cardiology, gastroenterology, pneumology, angiology and nephrology), with 24/7 availability. Engaged in clinical excellence, teaching and research, DMED promotes innovation, continuous skill development and patient-centred care, while ensuring the quality of life at work and the efficiency of benefits. Within this department, the Department of Gastroenterology and Hepatology takes care of patients and patients suffering from diseases of the digestive tract, the liver, the biliary tract and the pancreas, taking into account the services provided by the other services of the HUG. It provides the endoscopic, percutaneous and transhepatic interventional services of the above-mentioned organs. The Department of General Internal Medicine (SMIG) provides diagnostic and therapeutic support to adult patients and patients with a problem or assignment related to internal medicine, in collaboration with other units of the HUG, in particular the Department of Medicine. This position is newly created and is part of the ALTER deployment, which aims to ensure that work organisation complies with the Labour Act (LTr).

Description of post

As head or head of clinic, you are involved in the management of inpatient and outpatient patients in the Gastroenterology and Hepatology and General Internal Medicine Services through 6-month rotations. In rotation within the Gastroenterology and Hepatology Service, you participate in the diagnostic and therapeutic management of patients with digestive tract, liver, biliary tract and pancreatic diseases. You also take part in endoscopic procedures according to your level of training and skills acquired. During your transition to general internal medicine, you ensure the medical supervision of the care units entrusted to you and contribute to the management of complex clinical situations, in close collaboration with the various specialties of the department. You work closely with assistant and assistant physicians, medical teams and hospital partners to develop the most appropriate diagnostic and therapeutic strategies. You actively contribute to the supervision and training of intern doctors and trainees, participating in their daily clinical supervision as well as in the teaching activities of the department. You participate in seminars, seminars and training conferences, and contribute to academic and research activities developed in both departments. Thanks to your sense of organization, your analytical capacity and your ability to work in a dynamic environment, you ensure quality management while responding to different clinical demands. You have excellent relational skills and promote clear and respectful communication with patients, their loved ones and all professionals involved in care.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in medicine or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO).

You are seeking an FMH title in Gastroenterology or General Internal Medicine or you have an equivalent title recognized by MEBEKO and are in possession of the cantonal right of practice or are able to obtain it before you enter office with the cantonal doctor of Geneva. You have a 4-year experience in general hospital medicine followed by a year in gastroenterology and hepatology. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

You have excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with the patient, you are a dynamic, flexible, committed and discreet person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability and team spirit and solidarity with the team member.

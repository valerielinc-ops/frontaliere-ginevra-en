Full description

Join a team where clinical expertise, autonomy and commitment make a difference.

The cardiopulmonary unit and intermediate care accommodate patients with complex medical and surgical conditions, whose health status requires close clinical surveillance, confirmed nursing expertise and individualized management.

In this demanding and stimulating hospital environment, the quality of care depends on the ability of each professional to evaluate, anticipate, decide and act with discernment.

To strengthen our team, we recruit one

Night Nurse – Cardiopulmonary and Intermediate Care CDI 90% (ref: 2083)

Your mission: expertise, vigilance and autonomy at the heart of care

Within a multidisciplinary team, you ensure a comprehensive and secure management of patients with sometimes complex and scalable clinical situations.

As a night nurse, you play an essential role in continuity of care. Your ability to perform clinical analysis, anticipation and responsiveness are critical to ensuring patient safety and quality of care.

As such, you are responsible for

• Ensure the overall reception and care of patients taking into account their pathologies, needs and degree of dependence. • Conduct a rigorous and continuous clinical assessment, early detection of any deterioration in health status and adjust interventions accordingly. • Organize and prioritize care according to the complexity of situations and the degree of urgency. • Provide nursing care within your jurisdiction and perform prescribed treatments in accordance with protocols and best practices. • Ensure adequate monitoring for patients hospitalized in cardiopulmonary and intermediate care. • Discretion and responsiveness in emergency situations, alerting and mobilizing appropriate resources. • Ensure traceability of interventions and quality of transmissions in the computerised patient file. • Ensure coordination with physicians and stakeholders to ensure continuity and consistency of care. • Contribute to the organization of patient inputs, outputs, transfers and movements in collaboration with the team. • Accompany patients and their loved ones with professionalism, availability and kindness. • Participate in the supervision of students and the integration of new collaborators. • Maintain and develop your skills through active participation in training and continuous improvement of the quality and safety of care.

Your profile: clinical rigor, commitment and responsibility

We are looking for a professional who can combine technical skills, clinical reflection and human qualities. You appreciate the demanding environments in which autonomy, vigilance and adaptability are essential.

You have the following qualifications and skills

• Diploma of recognized nurse and permission to practise in Switzerland. • Confirmed hospital experience in Cardiology, Pneumology, Acute Medicine or Intermediate Care. • Strong capacity for clinical evaluation and detection of signs of decompensation. • Management of priorities and ability to make appropriate decisions in complex situations. • Occupational autonomy and a high sense of responsibility, particularly in the context of night work. • Ability to manage emergency situations with calm, method and discernment. • Rigor in care delivery, clinical surveillance and traceability. • Excellent relational abilities and ability to collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team. • Commitment, reliability, versatility and ability to cope with unforeseen events. • Constructive attitude, teamwork and willingness to actively contribute to the quality of care.

Beyond your skills, we expect a real professional posture: you know how to take initiatives in your field of expertise, assume your responsibilities, question your practices and place patient safety at the centre of each of your decisions.

We offer you: an environment that meets your commitment • A stimulating work environment, within a hospital recognized for its expertise and diversity of care. • A varied and enriching clinical activity in patients requiring close monitoring and specialized care. • Multi-disciplinary collaboration, promoting the sharing of skills and quality of care. • Continuing training opportunities, to maintain and develop your professional expertise.

Post conditions

• Type of contract: CDI • Participation rate: 90%.

The post is attached to the Directorate of Real Estate (DI), whose mission is to define a strategy of the housing stock, to develop the coherence of the follow-up of the major projects according to the hospital master plan 2022-2040, in strong partnership with the main State departments attached to the Department of the Territory, the Department of Infrastructure and the Department of Health, to optimize the monitoring of the surfaces and its future planning, while guaranteeing the proper functioning of the services. More than 230 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility.

Description of post

As a real estate and local manager you play a key role in the allocation, optimization and strategic management of workspaces. You receive and process requests for premises made by employees via dematerialized channels in support of the team and propose solutions. You analyze occupancy rates and propose suitable allocation and development solutions (flex-office, redevelopment...). You carry out monitoring visits to ensure the proper use of the premises according to our standards. You are involved in the development of ring road projects and the digitization of the management of the premises while ensuring the updating of the occupation repository. You draw up statistics and prepare the files to be presented to the Commission. As part of the management of the common rooms, you ensure that they are listed on the intranet and that their information (photos, equipment, capabilities, plans) is updated. You optimize their use, redefine the terms of reservation and access, and frame the responsibilities of room managers. Also, you contribute to cross-cutting real estate management projects.

Qualifications

You have a degree in real estate management or a Bachelor's degree or an equivalent. You have a solid experience in real estate management or planning projects. You are comfortable with digital tools, data analysis, and you demonstrate an excellent sense of organization. You know how to work independently while working effectively with different actors and actresses.

Full description

Reference code: JR135994

Roger Dubuis has been at the forefront of contemporary Haute Horlogerie since 1995. Its expressive timepieces house the finest watchmaking mechanisms, created in its integrated manufacture in Geneva and certified by the Poinçon de Genève.

The quest for excellence, the long search for the right gesture, the development of unique skills are all constants of Roger Dubuis. We are crafting a vibrant work environment where collaboration is celebrated, audacity is encouraged, and pleasure in what we do create truly enriching experiences. We are looking for unique, audacious and creative individuals who are looking to take part in the Roger Dubuis adventure.

Under the responsibility of the Event Manager, you will work on the different projects of experience of the brand and on the events of the House.

Among other things, your main impact will be to contribute to the success of a key moment of the Maison: Watches and Wonders Geneva 2027.

You will work to prepare events on, among others, the following aspects:

  • Supporting the event leader in setting the event guidelines
  • Management of accreditation processes and accommodation logistics
  • Organize the arrival of representatives of our different markets
  • Organize catering service for guests and staff
  • In connection with our providers, plan the different schedules (hostesses, cleaning)
  • Coordinate the preparation and delivery of all event material
  • Preparation of the stand
  • Logistics support

During events, you will have a key role to play in

  • Be the point of contact with Watches and Wonders Geneva Foundation
  • Managing accreditation
  • Provide daily logistical and operational support at the stand
  • Coordinate the actions of different providers
  • Manage ad-hoc requests

Your profile

  • In the course of training, ideally in luxury hotels, you are looking for a contracted internship for 6 months
  • You have already carried out missions in the luxury or event sector
  • Operational Field Experience at Watches and Wonders Geneva would be a plus
  • Motivated and open-minded
  • Details and solutions
  • Proactive and fast learning
  • Curious, agile and voluntary
  • The mastery of English, French at a common level is essential and any other language is an asset.

Our friendly, open-minded and talented team is looking forward to welcoming a new talent. We promise that your daily activities will be interesting, stimulating and diverse.

If you are available from 1 December for a period of 6 months, and you meet all the above criteria, you must apply and join our team! We look forward to receiving your CV and cover letter.

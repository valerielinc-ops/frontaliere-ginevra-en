Scheduling Coordinator (h/f/d) - Your next job?

As Scheduling Coordinator (h/f/d) to Vernier, you are attached to our department Logistics and you report to our Logistics Scheduleting Team Leader.

You are a key player in the coordination of flow and supply chain activities. Your main mission is to ensure optimal management of materials and packaging, while ensuring smooth communication between different departments.

You also play an active role in continuous improvement and development projects, helping to optimize processes and implement innovative solutions.

You're interested in this job? This could be the perfect opportunity if, as for us, your goal is to always meet the needs of customers and touch the emotions of the world around you.

Your Main Mission

Transverse Coordination & Collaboration

  • Ensure daily coordination with different departments (Production, Planning, Quality, etc.) in order to anticipate and solve problems affecting deliveries;
  • Acting as a key point of contact to ensure smooth logistics operations;
  • Support Logistics and other departments in their operational needs.

Operational management

  • Manage and monitor material allocations;
  • Perform internal and external commands (SO / STO / TR);
  • Monitor inventories and manage variances;
  • Manage packages (availability, stock, monitoring, projects);

Continuous improvement & projects

  • Actively participate in departmental improvement projects;
  • Identify areas for improvement, define priorities and contribute to their implementation;
  • Establish and monitor performance indicators (KPI);
  • Conduct operational analyses to optimize processes and decision-making.

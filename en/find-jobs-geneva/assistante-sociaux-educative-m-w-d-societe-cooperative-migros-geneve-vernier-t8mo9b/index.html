This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you wish to welcome the children in a safe, joyful and living environment? At the Balexert Centre's Stop-Games, every day you help young people play, learn and trust. You connect with families, support the team and bring calm in the rhythm of everyday life.

Mansioni

  • You welcome children as parents with kindness and in accordance with internal or legal regulations.
  • You accompany children in games, meals and quiet times while guaranteeing their safety and well-being.
  • You offer adapted activities that awaken their curiosity and actively participate in organized anniversaries.
  • You exchange with the parents, give them useful information and promote activities.
  • You ensure the opening and closing of the Stop-Games and collaborate with all the actors (parents, colleagues, external partners).
  • You ensure that the rules of hygiene, safety and organisation of the Stop-Games are respected.

Required

  • 2 years of experience: in a similar position
  • Completed professional learning (CFC): Socio-educational assistant
  • Ability to keep your calm always smiling, versatile, dynamic and creative
  • Excellent relational capacity, active listening and with clear and simple communication
  • Reliable, respectful, available and with team spirit
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: 2% and 5% reduction depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Department of Surgery. This department provides care for patients, both elective and emergency. It represents a centre of excellence, not only Swiss, but also European in several fields and in several of its surgical specialties.

Description of post

As part of your function, you contribute, under the responsibility of nurse and in collaboration with the interprofessional team, to the overall management of adult patients hospitalized in cardiovascular, chest and plastic surgery, visceral surgery, in a context of acute care. You provide delegated basic and medico-technical care, assist patients in their daily activities, and actively contribute to their comfort, safety and well-being during hospitalization. You intervene with a patient in a pre- and post-operative period requiring rigorous management, high adaptability and constant clinical vigilance. You are involved in the monitoring of the general condition of patients, in the prevention of post-operative complications and in early mobilisation, in close collaboration with healthcare teams. You also support patients and their loved ones in understanding care measures, particularly in relation to functional recovery, pain and return home. Thanks to your organizational sense, team spirit and relationship skills, you work closely with different health professionals and professionals to ensure continuity and quality of care. You also contribute to the reception and integration of new employees and to the supervision of trainees.

Qualifications

Having obtained the SWC as an assistant in community health and care, you hold your right of practice valid in the Canton of Geneva. Experience in surgery or acute care is an asset.

You are a reactive, organized person, with a good management of stress and priorities, you know how to analyze clinical situations and adapt to the context and to the unforeseen circumstances inherent in the activity of a care unit. Your ability to work collaboratively allows you to work hand in hand with a team of health care professionals. You ensure accurate and rigorous transmission of information within multidisciplinary teams. You are involved in the supervision and clinical education of new health care staff and other health professionals and professionals. You have excellent command of the French language both orally and in writing.

You can work both in daytime, nighttime and weekend hours.

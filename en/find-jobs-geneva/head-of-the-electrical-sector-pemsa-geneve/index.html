Your assignment

Manage electric yards with high requirements You will take care of strong current and weak current projects (CFO/CFA) and ensure its implementation in compliance with the highest quality, safety and performance standards. Ensure complete project management From preparation to testing, you will coordinate all phases of the project. You will plan resources, supervise the progress of the works and ensure effective collaboration between the internal teams, subcontractors and the various stakeholders involved. Ensure technical mastery of the work You will analyze and interpret the projects, schemes and technical documents in order to ensure compliance with the standards and requirements of the customer. You will also optimize the technical solutions implemented. Process and follow commercial offers You will prepare technical and financial offers by proposing relevant solutions tailored to the needs of customers. You will ensure the follow-up while at the same time reconciling technical, regulatory requirements and profitability objectives. Ensure safety and compliance of yards Ensure strict compliance with safety standards, internal procedures and applicable regulations. Contribute thus to the safety of employees, to the protection of plants and to the conformity of the realizations according to the Swiss standards. Your contribution Your technical expertise, your sense of organization and your ability to effectively coordinate the different subjects involved allow you to complete complex electrical projects with rigor and effectiveness. And now we're talking about you? A solid experience in the management of electric yards In possession of a certificate of electrician capocantiere or an equivalent qualification, master perfectly the electrical plants and have a proven experience in the management and supervision of teams on the field. A proven experience in high level renovation You have gained experience in challenging renovation projects, especially in historic buildings, stately residences or prestigious residences. Know the technical specifications of these environments and pay particular attention to the quality of the execution and the care of the finishes. A recognised technical competence Reading and interpreting projects, schemes and technical documents have no secrets for you. Ensure the compliance of the plants to the current standards and ensure an impeccable level of execution. Structured and efficient yard management Organized, methodical and reactive, manage your yards from planning to testing. Coordinate the different subjects involved, anticipate difficulties and provide effective solutions to technical and organizational issues. Excellent relational capacity You can establish a trusted relationship with customers, suppliers, partners and teams. Your disinvolvement in interpersonal relationships and your ability to communicate effectively contribute to good project coordination and customer satisfaction. A culture of performance and excellence At ease in the processing and monitoring of technical and financial offers, you can reconcile quality of execution, cost control and customer satisfaction. Your attention to detail and your need encourage you to constantly search for the highest level of achievement. A unifying leadership Recognized for your professionalism, your sense of responsibility and your need, you can mobilize teams around common goals. You are able to instill a true culture of quality, rigour and excellence in your yards. What will make the difference Your ability to combine technical expertise, field leadership and service sense allows you to manage complex electrical projects with effectiveness, rigour and professionalism.

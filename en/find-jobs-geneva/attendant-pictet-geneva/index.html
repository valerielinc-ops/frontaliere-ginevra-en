Full description

Your team

In order to strengthen the Receptionists & Waiters service of its Group Corporate Services division in Geneva, we are looking for one:

Husband/Ear (100%)

Your role

Mission To ensure the reception of our customers.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome external and internal clients.
  • Direct customers to the floors so that they are taken over by receptionists, their respective managers or the client caisse.
  • Call taxis.
  • Ensure the security of the customer lobby.
  • Please provide tourist information (hotels, restaurants, etc.).
  • Participate in the service at external and internal events.
  • Provide valet service on an ad hoc basis.

Your profile

  • Hotel training.
  • Experience in reception in luxury hotels (5 stars), with a minimum of 10 years of professional experience.
  • Good knowledge of common office tools; knowledge of the Lotus Notes collaborative work software: a plus.
  • Excellent presentation and irreproachable reception.
  • Absolute discussion, integrity, diplomacy, accuracy and punctuality.
  • A sense of customer service.
  • Possession of a driving licence (type B121/TPP: an asset)
  • Spoken languages: French, English; other language: an advantage.
  • Home in Switzerland required.

Your ability to work as a team and your relationship will be valuable assets that will enable you to successfully carry out the missions you will be responsible for.

Ref. H/SBP/FP

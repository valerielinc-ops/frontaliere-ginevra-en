Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive own brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service business in Switzerland and has around 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • You actively develop your client portfolio in the gastronomy sector, acquire new customers and maintain sustainable business relationships.
  • You analyze customer needs, offer customized solutions and develop tailored offers to increase revenue and profitability.
  • You plan and implement commercial measures to develop your sales sector and achieve the budget targets.
  • You follow the evolution of the market, competition and customer needs to identify new business opportunities.
  • You work closely with Transgourmet Switzerland's Key Account Management and internal services to ensure optimal customer tracking.
  • You ensure the updating of customer data in CRM and ensure compliance with defined business processes.

Qualifications and qualifications

  • You have several years of experience confirmed in external sales, ideally in the gastronomy sector.
  • You have a strong commercial sense, enjoy contact with customers and are comfortable in prospecting and negotiation.
  • You know how to recognize the needs of clients, to attract interest and to present tailored solutions convincingly.
  • You stand out by a structured, autonomous, efficient and objective-oriented method of work.
  • You have excellent communication skills and know how to maintain professional relationships with different people.
  • You master MS Office tools and effectively use CRM systems as well as other internal applications.
  • You have an excellent command of French in oral and written language. Knowledge of German is an advantage.
  • You are a Class B driver's licence holder and are willing to travel regularly in your sales area.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Directorate of Real Estate (DI), whose mission is to define a strategy of the housing stock, to develop the coherence of the follow-up of the major projects according to the hospital master plan 2022-2040, in strong partnership with the main State departments attached to the Department of the Territory, the Department of Infrastructure and the Department of Health, to optimize the monitoring of the surfaces and its future planning, while guaranteeing the proper functioning of the services. More than 230 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility.

Description of post

As a representative or representative of the contractor, you conduct and manage complex institutional projects by ensuring that they are integrated into the institution's value chain in organizational, technological and cultural dimensions. This will help you achieve the real estate strategy. You conduct advisory missions for governance and interact with members of the Board of Directors, Board of Directors and State Policies in the context of major projects. You carry out tasks of mastering public works by conducting all the phases of the organisation of projects, from the feasibility study to the reception of the work according to the Hermes and SIA methodologies. You are an internal consultant and carry out studies and diagnostics of the institution's buildings. You provide business expertise in one or more areas of real estate as part of institutional projects. You develop, maintain and disseminate a strategic watch in the field of architectural concepts and the organization of real estate projects.

Qualifications

You benefit from a diploma of architect or engineer REG A or from a title deemed equivalent to the Master level supplemented by a certification training in project management. You have a solid knowledge of building standards and regulations. You have excellent mastery of MS Office office tools. You have the capacity to abstract and conceptualize and like to work as much as in team as in autonomy. You have the sense of organization and are rigorous in mind. You have a proven experience of 15 years in construction including 5 years in mastering large-scale projects. A hospital experience is an asset.

Further information

You have a regular schedule but you can make yourself available off schedule including night, weekend and public holidays for picket service according to turns and urgent interventions.

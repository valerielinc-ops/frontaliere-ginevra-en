Data Analyst – Microsoft Fabric (6-month contract)

As part of the HR team, you will contribute to a strategic transformation aimed at building a modern, scalable, and AI-ready data architecture based on Data Mesh principles, Data Products, and the Microsoft Fabric platform . The objective is to evolve from a traditional reporting-driven model to a self-service data platform that enables users to directly access trusted, governed, and reusable data assets.

Working closely with the HR Data Officer , you will help define the target architecture, design Data Products, model data structures, and implement governed semantic models that ensure consistent interpretation of HR metrics across analytical tools.

You will also contribute to the development of a data portal that enables users to discover, understand, and independently leverage certified datasets while benefiting from a strong governance and security framework.

You will work with complex but well-established datasets that already benefit from robust quality controls and a solid analytical foundation. The challenge is not to fix data quality issues, but to transform existing data into reusable, secure, and scalable analytical assets that can be leveraged by business users, analytics tools, and AI solutions.

This role combines hands-on delivery with genuine architectural responsibilities. As the Microsoft Fabric platform is currently being built, you will take part in key decisions regarding data modelling, security, governance, and self-service analytics, helping establish a sustainable foundation for future data and AI initiatives .

YOUR ROLE

  • Gather, analyze, and formalize HR business requirements and translate them into data and analytics solutions.
  • Design and model Data Products following Data Mesh and Medallion Architecture principles.
  • Develop and maintain data transformation pipelines from Silver to Gold layers, including associated dimensional models.
  • Build and maintain semantic models and enterprise HR metrics for Power BI, Excel, and Copilot.
  • Define and implement data security mechanisms, including Row-Level Security (RLS) and Column/Object-Level Security (CLS/OLS), to protect sensitive information.
  • Contribute to the implementation and continuous evolution of the Microsoft Fabric platform in collaboration with Group IT and Data Office teams.

YOUR PROFILE

  • 4-5 years of experience in Data, Business Intelligence, Analytics, or Data Engineering.
  • Very good command of Microsoft Fabric environments is strongly desired (Microsoft Fabric, Lakehouse, Medallion Architecture)
  • Excellent command of SQL and Microsoft SQL Server (MSSQL).
  • Excellent command of Data Modelling (Star Schema), Power BI Semantic Models, and DAX .
  • Excellent knowledge of Data Product design and Data Governance principles, with a strong user-centric mindset and focus on self-service analytics.
  • Comfortable operating in both hands-on delivery and solution architecture capacities.
  • Strong ability to collaborate with business stakeholders and technical teams in a data-driven environment.
  • Strong analytical and structured thinking, at ease with business requirements analysis and formalization.
  • Excellent communication skills with both business and IT stakeholders.
  • Autonomous, pragmatic, and service-oriented approach.
  • Resident in Switzerland or willingness to relocate.

The eHealth and Telemedicine Service co-develops innovative digital solutions (web and mobile applications) to improve the practice of professionals and the experience of patients. You develop both front-end and back-end, using microservices API architecture and state-of-the-art technologies. You are evolving from a current and demanding stack based on Java/Spring-Springboot and oriented events with Kafka as well as angular/TypeScript. You create the component and guarantee its maintenance, while ensuring rigorous monitoring in consultation with the product manager. You work closely with the team to develop reliable and scalable solutions and features. You share best practices and take a critical and constructive approach to continuous improvement. Throughout the product life cycle, thanks to your sense of communication, you contribute to the development of the software in an optimal and structured way, ensuring an essential link between the eHealth service, partner teams, the professions involved and external stakeholders. You adapt your language according to your interlocutors to popularize technical terms, challenge proposed solutions, and actively collaborate with stakeholders to find the most relevant adaptations. In addition, you ensure that the necessary documentation is updated and written. You use your synthesis capacity and pragmatism to deliver your mission on time and ensure a quality result. You rely on your synthesis capacity and pragmatism to move forward and conduct your activity within the time constraints. Joining these teams is integrating a stimulating environment dedicated to digital health innovation, collaborating with multidisciplinary teams (Innovation Centre, APPS & UX, eHealth & Telemedicine, the entire HUG DTN-IA, external partners), and contributing to projects with high impact for professionals and professionals and patients. You are evolving in an agile, collaborative and solutions-oriented work culture.

Full description

Bracco is an international Group active in the healthcare sector and a leader in diagnostic imagining. The Group的s main products are contrast agents sold in over 100 countries, both directly and indirectly through branches, joint ventures and licensing and distribution agreements.

Bracco Suisse SA, based in Geneva, is a company of the international group BRACCO (www.bracco.com), a leader in the development and marketing of contrast agents for medical imaging. The Group relies on two R&D centres, located in Italy (CRB – Bracco Research Center) and Switzerland, which combine scientific expertise, know-how and innovation. Our production centre, located in Plan-les-Ouates, is approved and regularly inspected by the main international regulatory authorities (FDA, Swissmedic, etc.). As part of the replacement of our HSE manager, we are looking for one/one site in Geneva (Plan-les-Ouates):

HSE MANAGER (M/F)

Mission

Attached to the Site Manager, you will play a key role in promoting a strong HSE culture, complying with Swiss regulatory requirements and Bracco Group guidelines, as well as continuously improving health, safety and environmental protection practices. You will also be responsible for managing the management system (SMI), in accordance with the requirements of ISO 14001 (environmental management) and ISO 45001 (health and safety at work). As the site's HSE reference, you will work closely with management, employees, various internal committees and external partners and authorities to ensure a safe, healthy and environmentally friendly working environment.

Your main activities

Health, health, safety and environment

  • Advise and accompany management and staff on HSE issues.
  • Ensure compliance with Swiss legal requirements and Bracco Group guidelines.
  • Identify and assess occupational and environmental risks, identify and monitor preventive measures.
  • Supervise the management of hazardous substances and waste and emergency preparedness.
  • Analyze accidents and incidents, identify their causes and coordinate corrective actions.

Integrated Management System (IMS) Pilot

  • Ensure the deployment, operation and continuous improvement of the management system according to ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 standards.
  • Coordinate internal and external audits and monitor action plans.
  • Maintain the HSE documentation and procedures.
  • Monitor performance indicators (KPI), prepare HSE reports and contribute to management reviews.
  • Ensure integration of HSE requirements into site projects and activities.

HSE culture and coordination

  • To lead the HSE Committee of the site and coordinate the actions resulting from it.
  • Develop a culture of prevention and raise awareness of good HSE practices among employees.
  • Organisation of training and communication activities on health, safety and environment.
  • Collaborate with the group's HSE teams, external partners and competent authorities.
  • Represent the Geneva site in the HSE domains, with the prior agreement of the Site Manager.

Your Profile

  • University training or equivalent vocational qualification. The Swiss Federal Patent for Occupational Safety and Health Protection (STPS) is an asset
  • Experience confirmed in HSE function, ideally in a pharmaceutical, chemical or industrial environment
  • Good knowledge of Swiss regulations and ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 standards, auditor certification is in advantage
  • Knowledge of risk assessment, prevention and continuous improvement tools
  • Excellent communication, organizational and coordination capabilities
  • Pragmatic, proactive and solutions oriented spirit
  • Current English and French, Italian a plus

What we offer

  • Join a human-sized site in an international group
  • A key function within an international company, with a direct role in the development of the HSE culture of the site
  • A diverse position combining operational responsibilities, regulatory compliance and continuous improvement
  • Close interactions with the management of the site, the employees and international teams of the Bracco Group
  • The opportunity to make a concrete contribution to the creation and maintenance of a safe, healthy and environmentally friendly working environment

If you recognize yourself in this profile and are looking for a new challenge, send us your complete file (CV, cover letter).

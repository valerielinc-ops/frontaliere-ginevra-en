Full description

The centralized long-term pool (PCLD) is made up of nurses, community care and health assistants (ASSC) as well as home helpers. They operate throughout the canton of Geneva to carry out replacements, lasting approximately ten to twelve months, in our branches. This pool's mission is to guarantee continuity and optimal quality of services for our customers. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Provide basic care and medico-technical acts delegated by the nursing advisor in a global approach to the client
  • Promote the increase, recovery or maintenance of the client's autonomy to enable them to re-establish, continue or adapt their lifestyle at home
  • Carry out practical assistance services while respecting the client's lifestyle and their environment
  • Work interdisciplinarily, respecting the mission and institutional objectives
  • Evaluate and re-evaluate practical help situations and assume reference.

Your profile

  • Ability to diversify one's experiences in different Geneva sectors, different teams and clienteles
  • Loving challenges and discoveries
  • Federal certificate of ASSC capacity and authorization to practice in the canton of Geneva
  • Sense of communication and relationships with others, quality of listening
  • Ability to assess situations and act responsibly
  • Resistance to stress and physical work
  • Ability to work independently and interprofessionally within a multidisciplinary team
  • Essential mobility (on foot / by bike / by TPG / by car).

Full description

Introduction For our Information Systems Division , we are looking for a Business Analyst SAP PP/ePPDS (H/F) whose main mission will be to ensure the harmonization and consistency of production planning and scheduling processes and to accompany the transition to SAP S/4HANA for these strategic areas. Responsibilities

  • Assist users and business teams in functional analysis of planning and scheduling needs
  • Participate in configuration, testing, validation and support activities on SAP PP and ePPDS modules
  • Work with stakeholders to identify, analyse and resolve process issues
  • Develop and pilot initiatives to improve data quality, optimize time processing and anticipate future needs
  • Ensuring good governance and consistency of data related to the trade
  • Take part in cross-cutting/inter-domain projects and coordinate the transition to the new SAP S/4 system for planning and scheduling
  • Ensure that changes in the solution are controlled and are consistent with the company's roadmap. Profile
  • University training in computer science, EPF engineer or equivalent
  • Minimum 3 years experience in Supply Chain, ideally in industrial environment
  • Confirmed mastery of SAP PP and SAP ePPDS modules (configuration, customizing, support)
  • Knowledge of APS tools such as Kinaxis or SAP APO is an asset
  • Strong project management skills: organization, planning, flexibility, teamwork
  • Excellent command of French and good knowledge of English
  • Strong autonomy, initiative and ability to seek innovative solutions
  • Analytical spirit, rigour, sense of confidentiality and excellent relational qualities. Conclusion We offer a stimulating environment, attractive working conditions, flexibility schemes and top social benefits.

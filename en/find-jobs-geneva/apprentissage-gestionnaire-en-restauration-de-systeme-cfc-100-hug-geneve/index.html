Full description

The Catering Service consists of seven kitchens and eleven food centres (brasserie, cafeteria, coffee, sandwich shop) serving some 9,000 meals a day. The mission of this service is to offer quality services and pleasant and friendly moments. During your apprenticeship, you discuss the different aspects of management and operation of a restaurant. You participate in the reception and control of goods and in the preparation of dishes and the preparation of drinks. In addition, you ensure reception, sale, and customer advice at restaurants. You also manage the implementation of the products by ensuring their presentation. In addition, you are involved in the planning and organization of field activities such as the management of planning and the implementation of work processes. Food safety, hygiene, quality monitoring and marketing are all concepts that complement your training. The variety of services provided and the requirement imposed by the hospital environment allow the Catering Service to offer apprentices a varied and comprehensive training course. The apprentice is learning at several sites: Trois-Chêne Hospital, Belle-Idee Hospital and Bellerive Hospital.

Full description

As part of the strengthening of its administrative and financial organisation, an EMS partner seeks an accounting officer of 80%. Your Mission You independently and rigorously maintain the institution's accounting and contribute to the reliability of financial tracking. Your main responsibilities include:

  • maintenance of general accounts;
  • preparation and participation in periodic and annual loops;
  • control, imputation and monitoring of supplier accounts;
  • preparation and monitoring of payments;
  • management of accounts receivable;
  • Invoicing of residents and monitoring of receipts;
  • monitoring of open debtors, reminders and regularizations;
  • reconciliation of accounts and accounting controls;
  • collaboration with the Directorate, the various departments of the institution, as well as external partners and the review body. Your profile
  • Business training with specialization in accounting or training deemed equivalent;
  • proven experience in indispensable accounting , allowing for autonomous management of the tasks entrusted ;
  • Mastery of general accounting and experience of loop operations;
  • good control over the management of suppliers and debtors;
  • excellent mastery of common computer tools, including Excel;
  • experience in an EMS, a health institution or a medical-social structure as a real asset;
  • knowledge of the Geneva accounting and administrative environment appreciated. We are looking for a rigorous, reliable and organized person, with a good analytical spirit, able to work independently while enjoying the collaboration within a team. The function also requires discretion and confidentiality, particularly in the processing of residents' financial data.

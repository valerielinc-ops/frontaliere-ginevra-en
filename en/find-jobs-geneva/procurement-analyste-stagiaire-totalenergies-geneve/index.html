The Trading activity accounts for almost 90% of Gas and Power’s revenue, representing over £10 billion per annum of delivered natural gas, LNG, power, coal and emissions. Transactions average in excess of 10,000 per month covering both intra-desk and external counterparty deals. The counterparts for these operations and contracts take various forms (such as long-term contracts, forwards, futures, options, swaps, transportation and storage). You will join the credit team whose role is to assist in the day-to-day management of the counterpart portfolio risk under the Gas and Power sectors mainly. You will be a member of a team of professionals handling over 1.000 counterparties representing billion of US Dollars in credit lines.

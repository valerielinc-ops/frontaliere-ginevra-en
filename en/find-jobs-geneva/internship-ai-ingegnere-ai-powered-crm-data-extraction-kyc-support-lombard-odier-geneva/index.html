Internship – AI Engineer - AI-Powered CRM – Data Extraction & KYC Support

Know Your Customer (KYC) processes require the review, interpretation and validation of large amounts of client documentation. Artificial Intelligence offers significant opportunities to automate repetitive tasks and improve operational efficiency. As part of our AI-Powered CRM initiative, you will contribute to the development of intelligent solutions capable of extracting, organizing and validating information from client documents and onboarding processes.

YOUR ROLE

  • Leverage AI technologies to simplify document processing, improve data quality and support KYC activities within the CRM platform.
  • Analyse document processing workflows.
  • Explore OCR and document intelligence solutions.
  • Develop information extraction capabilities.
  • Build validation and quality-control mechanisms.
  • Integrate extracted data into CRM workflows.
  • Collaborate with compliance, operations and development teams.
  • Evaluate opportunities for further automation.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Master's student in Computer Science, Data Science or Engineering.
  • Interest in AI, document processing and automation.
  • Experience with backend, frontend and database technologies.
  • Knowledge of data processing frameworks is a plus.
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary environment.
  • Residence in Switzerland or willingness to relocate

WHAT YOU WILL GAIN

  • Exposure to real-world AI applications in regulated environments.
  • Opportunity to work on high-impact operational challenges.
  • Experience with document intelligence technologies.
  • Participation in a strategic transformation programme combining AI, operations and customer experience.

Full description

Introduction CERN is looking for an experienced full-stack software engineer to lead the design, development and optimisation of software modules for the Glance project. Glance is a web-based information and management framework used by several high-energy particle physics experiments to manage collaboration information (including membership records, committee appointments and speaker nominations), publication tracking and authorship qualification tracking. The successful candidate will be responsible for ensuring the reusability of Glance modules across multiple experiments and for coordinating the work plan according to the experiments' needs. Functions

  • Coordinate and contribute to all stages of the software development lifecycle - from requirements gathering and design to implementation, testing, deployment, support and long-term maintenance.
  • Collaborate closely with physicists and computing experts to translate experimental and physics requirements into robust and efficient computing solutions.
  • Implement and validate modules that take full advantage of modern computing architectures.
  • Profile, monitor and optimise software performance to ensure efficiency, reliability, and scalability.
  • Participate in the integration and validation of new software releases within heterogenous production environments.
  • Ensure high-quality documentation of software components, maintain configuration control, and contribute to internal knowledge sharing.
  • Promote best practices in coding, testing, and software design, fostering a culture of collaboration and continuous improvement across the team.
  • Provide technical support and mentoring to team members and collaborators, helping to disseminate expertise within the Glance community.

Master's degree or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field.

Experience

  • Experience in providing technical leadership for software projects.
  • Proven ability in software development; familiarity with databases, PHP, vue.js would be an advantage.
  • Practical knowledge of configuring and developing software for UNIX-based architectures.
  • Good understanding of software engineering practices, including test-driven development, version and release management, and quality assurance.
  • Experience in providing support to users of customisable software applications.
  • Good communication skills.

Technical competencies

  • Conceptualising, designing and developing user experiences and interfaces.
  • Testing, diagnosing and optimisation of software.
  • Development of application software.
  • Knowledge of programming techniques and languages.
  • Re-use, refactoring, integration and porting of existing software.
  • Installation, operation and maintenance (preventive and corrective) of computing systems.

Behavioural competencies

  • Achieving Results: having a structured and organised approach towards work; being able to set priorities and plan tasks with results in mind.
  • Working in Teams: debating at the table and engaging in constructive confrontation of ideas.
  • Demonstrating Flexibility: readily absorbing new techniques and working practices; proposing new or improved ways of working.
  • Demonstrating Accountability: presenting information accurately and objectively.
  • Solving Problems: identifying, defining and assessing problems, taking action to address them.

Language skills

Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, linked to the Commercial and Marketing Division, you will actively participate in the implementation of the social media strategy by ensuring the piloting, analysis and optimization of the performance of our content on the various platforms in order to increase the visibility, commitment and visibility of our brand.

In this capacity, you will participate in the management of local and international projects related to product launches, communication campaigns and digital activations, while ensuring compliance with quality standards, budgets and deadlines.

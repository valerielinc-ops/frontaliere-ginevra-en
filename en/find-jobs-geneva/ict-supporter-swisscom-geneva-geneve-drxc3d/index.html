# ICT Support

He tuo futuro inizia who

In this role, you support users and solve workplace level two problems at a large customer in the watchmaking sector. On site and remotely, you take care of support and workstations. You ensure quick resolution of incidents and requests to provide a smooth experience. Microsoft environments, including Windows, Microsoft 365 and Active Directory, are your responsibility to ensure their smooth functioning. Finally, you install, troubleshoot and maintain the computer fleet, from fixed and mobile stations to applications and peripherals.

  • Provide support on site level two and by telephone level one overflowing the support center
  • Quickly resolve incidents and respond to user requests
  • Install, troubleshoot and maintain the computer fleet (fixed and mobile posts, applications and peripherals)
  • Maintain and troubleshoot Windows, Microsoft 365 and Active Directory environments
  • Inform clients of the progress of requests and respect service levels

Your skills

  • Diploma (EPF/Uni/HES) in computer science or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in a similar role
  • Solid control of Windows, Microsoft 365 and Active Directory environments
  • Good knowledge of security systems, networks and practices
  • Excellent communication, sense of service and ability to clearly explain technical information
  • Autonomy, reliability and adaptability to various environments
  • Team spirit and close collaboration with clients and colleagues
  • Very good French and good English

You will become familiar with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working time arrangements to meet your personal needs.

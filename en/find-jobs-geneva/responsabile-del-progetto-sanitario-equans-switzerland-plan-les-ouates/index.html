Full description

In order to strengthen our health team in Geneva, we are looking for a 100% health project leader.

Your Responsibilities

  • Planning, coordinating and supervising the implementation of sanitation facilities;
  • Establish technical and commercial offers and bids;
  • Ensure technical, financial and administrative monitoring of projects;
  • Manage implementation schedules and monitor progress;
  • Be the primary contact point for clients, partners and subcontractors;
  • Ensure compliance with existing standards and quality and safety requirements.

What characterises you

  • Training in health;
  • Experience in managing health projects in Switzerland;
  • Excellent control of financial management and budgetary monitoring of projects;
  • Good bargaining and customer service skills;
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and initiative.

Here's what we offer

Full description

Damiani Group, an international luxury group with a portfolio of prestigious jewellery, watchmaking and design Maisons, is looking for a Brand Content Designer to join its team, with a specific focus on Baume & Mercier.

The role will contribute to the development and execution of the Maison's visual identity and content initiatives, combining strong graphic design skills, creative sensibility and a solid understanding of luxury brand codes. Working closely with the Marketing team, the successful candidate will create visual content, support campaign development and coordinate external creative and production partners.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Brand Identity & Creative Development

  • Ensure the consistent application and visual quality of Baume & Mercier's brand identity, guidelines and creative materials.
  • Contribute to campaign concepts, visual storytelling and the evolution of the Maison's visual language.

Graphic Design & Content Production

  • Create and adapt visual assets across print, digital, social media, web and retail channels.
  • Support content planning and ensure the quality and consistency of all creative deliverables.

Production & Project Coordination

  • Coordinate photo and video shoots, working with photographers, videographers and production houses / external creative specialists.
  • Develop and coordinate in-house production of visual content for social media and digital channels.
  • Manage project briefs, timelines, deliverables and production budgets, ensuring smooth execution.

Market Support & Events

  • Develop campaign toolkits and visual guidelines for our local markets.
  • Adapt visual materials for local markets, trade activities and retail.
  • Support the creation of visual materials for events and activations, including Watches & Wonders, collaborating with internal stakeholders.

REQUIREMENTS

  • Degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, Fine Arts or a related field.
  • 8+ years of experience in graphic design, brand content creation or creative production, preferably within the luxury, watchmaking, jewellery or fashion industries.
  • Strong design skills, refined aesthetic sensibility and advanced knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and Premiere Pro/After Effects).
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and coordinate external partners, ensuring compliance with briefs, timelines and budgets.
  • Hands-on approach, with strong autonomy, proactivity, attention to detail and collaborative skills.
  • Fluency in English is mandatory; proficiency in French is a strong asset.

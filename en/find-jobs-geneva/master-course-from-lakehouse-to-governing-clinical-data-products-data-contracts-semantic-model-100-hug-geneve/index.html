Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) is a national and international reference institution. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Digital Transformation and AI Directorate (DTN-IA) supports the institution's digital transformation. She designs, pilotes and develops the HUG information system, and plays a key role in the development of innovative digital products that serve hospital performance and quality of care. In particular, it aims to improve the path of patients before, during and after hospitalisation, to optimize hospital flows and to support health professionals and professionals through appropriate tools that reduce administrative burden. It also contributes to the development of clinical research and innovation, notably through the integration of artificial intelligence technologies, and contributes to the promotion of HUGs as a major player in digital health The DTN-IA Data & Decision Support Domain runs the HUG institutional data platform. It provides reliable data for care, research, quality and pilotage.

Description of post

Within the DTN-IA, you contribute to the institutional data platform of the HUGs. The project concerns the central concepts of the data warehouse which are now modeled in a heterogeneous way according to the sources (laboratory, drugs, medical coding, etc.). The HUGs wish to describe them in a common reference model, published in the form of data products contractualized and searchable by trades according to shared definitions. The course focuses on four priority concepts: demographic data, hospital stays, patient and patient movements, laboratory results. It can be supplemented by interventions, diagnostics and drugs (prescriptions and administrations). Within a multidisciplinary team (data architect, analysts, data engineers, clinical expertise), you build and evaluate this chain from end to end from data to contracted data products until they are questioned by the trades. You work with recent open standards (ODCS, ODPS, Apache Ossie, ex-Open Semantic Interchange) on a modern lakehouse architecture (Apache Iceberg, dbt, Airflow, OpenMetadata catalogue). Your main objective is to create data products governed and contracted for these four concepts, with definitions and metrics common to all trades. Whenever possible, you are working on a prototype AI tool that uses these data products to respond to requests for data extraction addressed to the infodesk. It is not a delivery commitment and will use only synthetic or anonymous data in an environment that meets the data protection requirements of the HUGs. To carry out your missions, you analyze the existing one and inventory sources, tables and quality problems for the selected concepts. You design conceptual, logical and physical models, including historical and temporal management. You formalize data products: data contracts in ODCS (schema, quality rules, SLA, responsibilities) and product descriptors in ODPS. You define the semantic model: entities, relationships, dimensions, clinical metrics and glossary according to the Apache Ossie specification, with annotations for AI tools. You then link it to the OpenMetadata catalogue (business glossary, data products, contracts, lineage). You prototype on the lakehouse by making Apache Iceberg tables, SQL transformations with dbt, Airflow orchestration, quality controls from contracts (dbt tests). You expose data products via a query interface (API, SQL or dashboard). As an option and depending on the advancement you develop, with IA assistants, a prototype tool that relies on these data products and the Ossie model to meet the demands of the infodesk. You evaluate the set on a case of real use (exports for research, quality indicator or infodesk request) taking into account the following elements: completeness, consistency of metrics, performance, ease of use by the trades.

The deliverables expected for this internship are

You create a reference model and data dictionary You develop ODCS contracts and ODPS data product descriptors You conceptualize a semantic Apache Ossie model You are developing a data product query interface; optional, prototype IA tool for infodesk You write an internship report and prepare a final presentation

Qualifications

You have good knowledge of SQL and Python You have good knowledge of data modeling: conceptual, relational and dimensional (star and flake schemas, Data Vault) You show a strong interest in the hospital world and health data Your curiosity about emerging standards, your rigor, your autonomy and your ability to popularize technical concepts with clinical teams are proven You have perfect command of the French language Your knowledge of Cube, dbt MetricFlow or ontologies; dbt, Apache Iceberg, Airflow, OpenMetadata; data products/contracts; IA generative (LLM, text-to-SQL, agents) is an asset.

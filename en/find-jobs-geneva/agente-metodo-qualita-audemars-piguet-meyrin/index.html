Full description

Integrated into the Quality Manufacturing team of the Meyrin site and directly attached to the manager, your role will be to ensure the definition and validation of the three-dimensional controls of the watchmaking components.

In this context

  • You will provide programming and program management on three-dimensional Zeiss Duramax machine
  • You will inform the control ranges on the Ellisetting software and quality of service indicators
  • Machine optics knowledge Mahr and Marcel Aubert would be a plus
  • You will bring your experience in managing non-compliance
  • You will provide support for production at metrology level
  • Knowledge of Calypso software and Zeiss machines is imperative, you justify a minimum of 2 years experience in programming
  • With a metrology-related technician degree or equivalent, you have a 2-5 year experience in a similar position
  • You are autonomous, rigorous and versatile. You have a good spirit of analysis and initiative. You're positive and you appreciate teamwork

What we offer

  • Insurance coverage - disease (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities as well as subscriptions encouraging soft mobility (e.g. SBB)
  • A business restaurant
  • An LPP with favourable rates

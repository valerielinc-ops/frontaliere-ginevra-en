Activities: As an AI Engineer Intern within the Front Innovation Tools team, you will contribute to the design and development of an agentic AI platform supporting our trading desks and operations teams. Based on the Geneva trading floor, you will work closely with a team of quantitative analysts and software engineers responsible for derivatives pricing tools, risk management applications and digital innovation. Your main responsibilities will include: 1. Agentic AI Platform Development

  • Contribute to designing and developing a platform enabling the creation, deployment and execution of autonomous AI agents.
  • Develop solutions connecting AI agents to internal trading applications, pricing libraries and financial data sources.
  • Explore and implement agent orchestration frameworks, allowing multiple AI agents to interact, collaborate and execute complex tasks.
  • Support the development of human-in-the-loop workflows to enable effective collaboration between AI systems and users. 2. Automation & Intelligent Workflows
  • Design event-driven and automated workflows triggered by market developments, data updates, communications or user requests.
  • Develop AI-powered solutions to streamline information gathering, processing and analysis.
  • Contribute to monitoring frameworks assessing agent behaviour, execution performance and operational costs.
  • Explore practical applications of AI to improve the efficiency and reliability of trading and operational processes. 3. AI Applications for Trading & Market Intelligence
  • Collaborate with trading desks to identify opportunities for AI-driven innovation.
  • Participate in developing intelligent assistants capable of analysing market information, trading communications and operational data.
  • Explore AI applications for market intelligence, including analysis of commodity flows, logistics, refinery activity and other market fundamentals.
  • Contribute to prototypes supporting market analysis, data visualisation and decision-making. 4. Architecture, Testing & Implementation
  • Support the definition and documentation of the platform's technical architecture.
  • Develop and test functional prototypes integrating agent execution, orchestration, evaluation and tracing capabilities.
  • Collaborate with internal technical teams to ensure solutions meet relevant security and operational requirements.
  • Participate in developing and validating end-to-end business use cases with trading and operational stakeholders. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

Full description

Join CERN's Storage and Data Management group, to develop data storage systems for physics experiments at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). The Storage and Data Management Group is responsible for enabling data access for the laboratory, in particular the long-term archival, preservation and distribution of LHC data to a worldwide scientific community. CERN, take part! As a Data Storage R&D Engineer you will join the CERN Tape Archive (CTA) team, responsible for the archival and management of 1.5 Exabytes of physics data from the entirety of CERN's experimental programme. Your job will include R&D, software development and operations tasks.

Your responsibilities

Your responsibilities will include

  • Analyse metadata and logs to optimise physical data placement on storage media.
  • Research appropriate mathematical models and propose optimisations to CTA's scheduling algorithms within specified cost constraints.
  • Develop and maintain software in C++/Linux.
  • Participate in operations, troubleshooting and support as part of a team.

  • Prior experience with tape storage systems and robotic libraries is not expected.

Eligibility criteria

  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.

Your Purpose

Your Purpose The Business Intelligence & Automation Intern supports Shipboard Hospitality operations by providing structured analysis, KPI tracking, dashboard development, and actionable insights. The role ensures accurate and timely data visibility across Hotel, F&B, SRE, Beverage, YC, and Ground Ops teams, enabling day-to-day operational decisions and continuous performance improvement.

Your Impact

  • Build, update, and maintain Power BI dashboards for consumption, revenue, cost, stock rotation, venue performance, package penetration, and other operational KPIs.
  • Support KPI definition, ensuring alignment across ships, venues, and departments (Hotel, YC, F&B, SRE, Housekeeping).
  • Run weekly and monthly reporting, highlight key trends, risks, and anomalies, and share insights with business owners.
  • Conduct ad-hoc analysis on consumption patterns, cost deviations, stock inefficiencies, menu yield gaps, and initiative performance.
  • Assist with automation of data extraction (PMS) and validate data integrity.
  • Support business case preparation, ROI modeling, and sensitivity analysis for initiatives.
  • Collaborate with BI, IT, Procurement, Demand Planning, and Finance teams to ensure alignment between operational and financial data.

Your Journey so far

  • Bachelor's degree in data science, Statistics, Economics, Engineering, Computer Science, or related field.
  • Experience with data analysis, reporting, and dashboard tools (Power BI mandatory
  • Tableau a plus).
  • Proficiency in Excel and basic SQL; experience with Python or R is a strong advantage.
  • Strong analytical mindset with ability to translate data into insights.
  • Clear communication skills and ability to work with multiple stakeholders.
  • Detail-oriented, structured, and capable of managing multiple tasks.

Your Essentials

  • EU Passport Holder or Right to Work in Switzerland.
  • Valid Internship Agreement

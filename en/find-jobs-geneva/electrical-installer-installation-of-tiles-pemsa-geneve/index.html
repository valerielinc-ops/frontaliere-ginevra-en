For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, proposing them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Electrical Installer – Installation of tiles

Your assignment

  • Read, interpret and accurately analyze electrical designs and patterns, in compliance with current NIBT standards.
  • Automately carry out the tracking of electrical installations, ensuring precise execution and conform to the requirements of the yard.
  • Provide for the collection of electrical installations in the walls and in the suns, in particular during casting works, ensuring accurate, lasting and consistent integration.
  • Realize electrical ducting with rigor and precision, according to the rules of art, in order to ensure optimal protection of the cables and easy access to the plants.
  • Place and accurately fix the recessed boxes, respecting the positioning and alignment, in order to ensure the quality, functionality and safety of the systems.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as electrician/electricist installer or equivalent training recognized in Switzerland.
  • Excellent mastery of the NIBT rules in force and their application on site.
  • Excellent ability to read, interpret and understand electrical projects and schemes.
  • Mastery of electrical tracking, with precise, autonomous execution and conform to the requirements of the construction site.
  • Solid skills in the collection of electrical installations in the walls and in the suns, especially during casting.
  • Good mastery of the laying of electrical pipes, performed with rigor and respect for the rules of art.
  • Experience in laying and placing recessed boxes, with particular attention to precision and quality of execution.
  • Autonomy, rigour and attention to detail, with real attention to quality and safety.

How you can make an impact As a Senior Manager/Director in Finance Transformation, you will:
  • Spearhead ERP/EPM-enabled finance transformation initiatives, including ERP/EPM assessment / Phase 0, also leveraging leading platforms to optimise financial processes and reporting.
  • Lead and deliver end-to-end finance transformation projects focused on the future of finance, including the design and implementation of Finance Strategy and Target Operating Models definition and implementation of performance steering models.
  • Drive Day 1 readiness activities post-integration to ensure seamless finance operations and compliance from day one following mergers or acquisitions.
  • Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams and technology partners to integrate advanced finance solutions, ensuring alignment with strategic business objectives.
  • Advise clients on finance strategy, process optimisation, and technology adoption to enhance decision-making, performance management, and value creation.
  • Drive business development activities with the team Who we are looking for
  • You hold a university degree in Finance, Business, IT, or a related field.
  • You bring at least 10 years of experience in Finance Strategy, transformation, or consulting, ideally with exposure to ERP/EPM-enabled finance projects, strategy design, Target Operating Model design and integration readiness.
  • Experience in consulting gained within a Big Four firm or a leading consulting market player is required.
  • You have a strong understanding of finance processes including controlling, consolidation, planning and reporting.
  • You possess excellent communication, stakeholder management, and analytical skills.
  • You are fluent in English, fluent in German or French with a willingness to travel within Switzerland and internationally.
  • Experience working with leading ERP and finance technology platforms is highly desirable.

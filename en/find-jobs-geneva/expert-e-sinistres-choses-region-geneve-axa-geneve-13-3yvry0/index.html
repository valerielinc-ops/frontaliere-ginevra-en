What's waiting for you

In this role, you deal independently with complex insurance claims things, including for the commercial and industrial sector.

  • You conduct site visits, inspections and investigations to address issues of coverage and accountability.
  • You assess the extent of the damage and calculate the insurance benefits.
  • You communicate with insured persons, their representatives, brokers, service providers and internal services.

Your profile

  • You have strong insurance training (federal patent or equivalent degree) and experience in managing complex claims as an expert/claim inspector.
  • You have good knowledge of civil liability law and you have knowledge of construction technology.
  • You have excellent customer orientation, ease in human contact and exemplary perseverance.
  • You have a sense of organization and are flexible.
  • You have perfect command of the French language (mother tongue); good knowledge of English and/or German would be an asset.
  • Residing within the range of activity would be an advantage.

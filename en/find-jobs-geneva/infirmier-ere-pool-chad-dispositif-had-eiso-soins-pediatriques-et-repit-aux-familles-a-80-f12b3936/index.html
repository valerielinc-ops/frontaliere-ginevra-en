Full description

The Home Hospital Pool (CHAD) performs short-term replacements within 24-hour teams within the multi-purpose adult HAD teams, interprofessional team specializing in oncology (EISO) and the paediatric care team and respite families. Attached to a team of dynamic and versatile poolists, the nurse provides services in a diverse and complex environment. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Respond effectively to specific care by providing autonomously and on medical delegation of technically complex nursing care at home clients, usually provided in hospital (blood transfusion, peritoneal dialysis, antibiotic IV ...)
  • Provide comprehensive personalized care to clients of all ages and high risk of hospitalization.
  • Provide comprehensive personalized care to adult clients with an oncological pathology within a global vision, taking into account their direct and social environment, their psychological constraints.
  • Provide and ensure individualized care for children, taking into account physical, psychological, emotional, spiritual, social and cultural needs
  • Act in accordance with the principles of the nursing code of ethics to ensure the overall care of the client in his or her usual environment.
  • Participate in the institutional mission to foster home care in a stimulating environment for our clients
  • Bachelor of Nursing from HEDS or equivalent.
  • Minimum 2 years of professional experience in acute care.
  • Adaptability, flexibility and agility.
  • Great relational ease, ability to manage stress and sense of responsibility
  • Knowledge of the Geneva care network.
  • Professional experience in the oncological and paediatric fields would be an asset.

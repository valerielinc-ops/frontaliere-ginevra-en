Full description

Your team

Corporate Functions brings together the Group's non-banking activities and has over 900 employees. The core functions are carried out by specialists who are fully familiar with all business lines and are divided into various departments: Communication, Compliance, Finance, Human Resources, Information Security, Internal Audit, Legal, Corporate Office, Risks and Taxation.

As part of your master's work and the writing of your memoir, you will actively participate in the various activities of the Group Information Security department.

Your role

  • Contribute to cyber security risk analysis, particularly in the areas of artificial intelligence and cloud computing.
  • Participate in security reviews conducted as part of the Group's strategic projects.
  • Monitor intrusion tests and assess identified vulnerabilities.
  • To carry out awareness-raising activities and to participate in the promotion of a safety culture, by exploiting where relevant the opportunities offered by artificial intelligence.
  • Analyze safety solutions and technologies, including AI-based solutions, in terms of their effectiveness, level of risk and compliance.

Your profile

  • Master's degree in cybersecurity, information security, computer science, data science or risk management.
  • Deep interest in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and emerging technological issues.
  • Good foundation in information security and strong motivation to develop your skills in a demanding environment.
  • Spirit of analysis, intellectual curiosity and ability to address complex issues.
  • Knowledge of programming languages and interest in data exploitation and AI technologies.
  • Knowledge of key cloud services (Azure, AWS, GCP): an asset.
  • Excellent communication skills in French and English, both oral and written.
  • Rigor, autonomy, proactivity, discretion and ability to evolve calmly in a demanding environment.
  • Home in Switzerland required.

Entry into office: from February 2027

Curious, rigorous and well-analyzed, you like to understand a variety of issues and meet new challenges. You enjoy working as a team while showing initiative in the missions entrusted to you. This course will provide you with the opportunity to develop your skills and gain practical experience in cybersecurity, risk management and information security in an international banking environment.

Note

We do not accept any CVs via agency.

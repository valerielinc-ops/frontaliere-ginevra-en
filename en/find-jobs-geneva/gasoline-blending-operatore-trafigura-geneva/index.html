Role overview

Main purpose

Responsible for execution of Gasoline Blending operations and trader support.

The role co-ordinates all aspects of post-deal trading activity to ensure contract execution runs efficiently, provides commercial and market information to the trader as necessary, and reviews and comments on contracts and tenders to ensure consistency with the trade ticket and recap.

Main responsibilities

  • Key responsibilities
  • For all Gasoline blended cargoes, be the key point of contact between the commercial desk and external stakeholders.
  • Liaise with terminals and vessels to ensure commercial blending plans are followed in line with the traders’ expectations.
  • Ensure with inspection companies that quality is as per blend plans and in line with contractual provisions.
  • Investigate Quantity losses and explore remedial action.
  • Trouble shoot when plans change and provide solutions.
  • Actively manage the terminals line up to mitigate demurrage.
  • Review stowage plans and monitor vessel progress during voyages.
  • Appoint third-party providers in line with group policy; oversee clean acceptance of vessels on subjects and chartering progress.
  • Liaise and co-ordinate with Finance, providing accurate and timely data needed for the issuance of L/Cs and P/Us.

Additional details

  • Identify operational issues and escalate to the Regional Manager as appropriate.
  • Good understanding of different types of financial instruments, their uses, and the importance of LOIs and shipping documents within the trading process.

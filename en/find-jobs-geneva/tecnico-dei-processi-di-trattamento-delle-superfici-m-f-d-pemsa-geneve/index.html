Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team to position (M/F/D) Surface Treatment Process Technique

Your assignment

  • Process, update and optimize production cards and work instructions.
  • Participate in the industrialization of new components and the qualification of the first series: FAI, DVI, PPAP, evidence...
  • Provide your technical expertise to the production teams on the treatment, removal, passivation and surface finishing processes.
  • Participate in industrial tests and process qualifications.
  • Analyze the technical problems encountered in production and contribute to the reduction of non-compliance.
  • Perform and interpret the necessary controls: thickness measurements, XRF, corrosion tests, dimensional controls...
  • Participate in the analysis of internal and external non-compliances.
  • Manage or participate in corrective and preventive action plans.
  • Use the main quality tools: 8D, 5 Why, 6M, Ishikawa...
  • Participate in internal audits and technical exchanges with customers.
  • To actively contribute to continuous improvement projects and process optimization.

And now we're talking about you? First of all, we are looking for a profile that possesses a concrete and consolidated technical expertise in surface treatment.

  • Ideally, you have gained several years of experience directly on the field before moving towards roles in the Methods, Industrialization or Quality sectors.
  • Masters in particular:

The treatment processes of surfaces and/or galvanoplasty. The problems related to treatment, removal of treatment, passivation and finishing. The reading and interpretation of technical drawings. The use of measuring and control tools related to surface treatment. The methods of analysis and resolution of quality problems. The demanding industrial environments, ideally in subcontract.

  • You are recognized for your technical rigour, your analysis ability and your autonomy.
  • Your organization allows you to efficiently structure your working methods and quickly analyze data in order to identify the actions to take.
  • Appreciate your work in close contact with Production, Methods, Quality, Technique and Customers.
  • A fluent mastery of French is indispensable. A professional level of English or German, ideally B2, is a real advantage for technical exchanges and customer audits.

Location at 40 hours per week / Indefinite contract / Retribution on 13 months, salary based on profile

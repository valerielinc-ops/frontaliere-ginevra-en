HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT

Reporting to the Head of Strategic Projects & A&P Controlling within Cartier’s International Finance team, you will play a key role in shaping and driving strategic Finance initiatives and supporting the assessment of the Maison’s strategic priorities through a strong financial lens.

At the crossroads of strategy, finance and transformation, you will combine strategic thinking, financial expertise and project leadership to turn complex business questions into clear insights, recommendations and actionable roadmaps.

Working closely with senior Finance leaders and a broad range of functions, Regions and Markets, you will act as a trusted thought partner and hands-on project leader, helping translate the Maison’s ambitions into robust financial frameworks and sustainable business outcomes.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Among various responsibilities, your main missions are

Strategic Planning

Where is the Maison going, and what does it mean financially over the long term?

  • Drive key analyses contributing to the Maison’s strategic planning exercises, translating long-term ambitions into clear financial trajectories, scenarios and priorities, including the Strategic Plan, MTSP and 5-year plan updates.
  • Partner with Finance teams to strengthen the connection between strategic ambitions, financial planning and operational performance, ensuring consistency across planning horizons.
  • Synthesize complex business and financial dynamics into clear insights and recommendations to support senior management decision-making.

Transversal projects

How do we evaluate and deliver the Maison’s strategic transversal priorities ?

  • Lead financial and strategic analyses supporting key transversal Maison projects, developing robust business cases through financial modelling, scenario analysis and the assessment of risks, opportunities and value-creation potential.
  • Translate analyses into clear recommendations and compelling executive-level materials for senior leadership and Steering Committees.
  • Drive cross-functional project workstreams, coordinating stakeholders across departments.
  • Act as a Finance partner for selected strategic initiatives (e.g. Cartier 180, procurement savings controlling), establishing the appropriate financial framework, consolidating inputs and monitoring progress and financial impact over time.

Finance Transformation & Community roadmap

How does Finance itself evolve to support those ambitions?

  • Shape and drive the Finance improvement roadmap, identifying key priorities, pain points and opportunities across the Finance community and translating them into concrete transformation initiatives.
  • Lead or contribute to the development of frameworks, processes and tools that enhance transparency, collaboration, efficiency and decision-making across Finance, including the 5-year plan Anaplan platform, Global Cartier Finance Calendar and Finance intranet.
  • Support initiatives aimed at strengthening the Finance community, ways of working and knowledge sharing across Central, Regional and Local Finance teams.
  • Contribute to the preparation of Central Finance content and interventions for key global and regional Finance moments, including World Meeting, town halls and community events.

WHAT ARE WE LOOKING FOR

  • You have at least 7–8 years of relevant professional experience gained within Finance, Strategy, Consulting, Investment Banking or a similar environment, ideally with exposure to international and matrix organizations.
  • You have a Master’s degree or equivalent in Finance, Economics, Business or Engineering.
  • You have strong financial acumen and analytical capabilities, with demonstrated experience in financial modelling, business cases, scenario analysis and strategic decision support.
  • You are able to navigate complex and ambiguous business questions, with a proven ability to connect detailed analysis with the broader strategic picture.
  • You have strong synthesis skills, with the ability to translate complex financial and business topics into clear, compelling and actionable recommendations.
  • You have experience preparing executive-level presentations and recommendations for senior management, with excellent PowerPoint and storytelling capabilities.
  • You have strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to build trusted relationships, challenge constructively and influence stakeholders across functions, cultures and levels of seniority.
  • You have solid project management capabilities, with the ability to structure complex initiatives, mobilize diverse stakeholders and drive multiple priorities forward.
  • You are proactive, curious and solution-oriented, with the ability to anticipate challenges and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • You have a strong team spirit, combined with high standards of integrity, discretion and accountability.
  • You have advanced proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint; knowledge of Anaplan, Looker and SAP BI is a plus.
  • You are fluent in English
  • French is a plus.

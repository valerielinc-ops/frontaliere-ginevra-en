Full description

The Centre Santé de Lancy – Pont-Rouge is looking for a medical specialist in general internal medicine, wishing to practise as an independent, in order to complement his team of health professionals. Ideally located in the heart of the dynamic district of Lancy Pont-Rouge, our Health Centre is a medical facility of proximity, accessible to both the inhabitants of the area and to people working there or coming from outside. Our approach is based on quality family medicine, based on listening, continuity of care and a relationship of trust with patients. The Centre also welcomes various independent professionals from the paramedical fields and complementary practices, including in podology, dietetics, shiatsu, art therapy and physiology, thus promoting multidisciplinary management. We offer you

  • A modern and fully equipped office, ready to welcome your activity.
  • An independent status and practice.
  • An existing patientel and an important development potential.
  • A multidisciplinary environment conducive to exchanges and synergies between health professionals.
  • Administrative and logistical support allowing you to focus fully on your medical practice.
  • A great autonomy in the organisation and development of your activity.

Your profile

  • Medical specialist in general internal medicine.
  • Holder of an authorisation to practise in Switzerland and, ideally, of an authorisation to charge for or meet the conditions for compulsory health insurance.
  • Wants to develop an independent, sustainable and care-oriented activity.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit, autonomy and a sense of collaboration.
  • Interest in local medical practice and networking with other healthcare professionals.

Do you want to exercise independently while enjoying a modern, dynamic and multidisciplinary professional environment? We would be delighted to talk to you and introduce you to our Centre and our project.

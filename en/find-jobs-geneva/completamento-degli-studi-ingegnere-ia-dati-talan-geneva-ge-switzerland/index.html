Full description

BACKGROUND

Within Talan's Business Line Data & AI, you join a team that designs, prototypes and industrializes artificial intelligence solutions (from generational AI to agent systems) to service our customers and products (LLMs via Azure AI Foundry, data platforms and new generation BI: Power BI, Microsoft Fabric...). Depending on the current projects and your profile, you can contribute to various topics related to AI: generic AI applications, AI agents, LLM integration, data/BI process automation, POCs for our customers or internal accelerators. One of our flagship projects, for example, is an agentic Migration Factory that automates the migration of BI legacy platforms (Tableau, Cognos, MicroStrategy...) to modern platforms.

MISSIONS

Boxed by Business Line Data & AI experts, you will intervene (depending on the projects) on three complementary axes:

  1. Design and develop AI solutions:
  • Develop components, prototypes and POCs around the general AI and LLMs (prompting, agent orchestration, RAG).
  • Design connectors, parsers and pipelines to integrate data, tools and various usage cases (e.g. BI/data sources, APIs, cloud platforms).
  • Expand the capabilities of our solutions to support new Data & BI platform environments, technologies and artifacts.
  1. Reliate AI and strengthen code quality:
  • Improving the design of AI agents and chains (prompting, orchestrating, task cutting, context management) to make AI-assisted steps reliable.
  • Strengthen automatic validation, non-regression tests and the confidence level produced by our solutions.
  • Refactor existing code and improve test coverage, typing, modularity and IC/CD practices.
  1. Explore, benchmark and capitalize:
  • Benchmark LLM models (accuracy, cost, execution time) and prototype recent agentic approaches.
  • Document the architecture of the solutions and capitalize on reusable patterns for the benefit of other projects.
  • Measure the impact of your contributions on actual use cases.

Training

  • You are in the final year (5th year / Master's degree) of an engineering school or university with IA orientation / computer science (EPFL type, EPFZ, Swiss high schools or equivalent) and you are looking for an agreed completion course (PFE).

Technical expertise

  • Solid bases in Python and software engineering: typing, automated tests, Git, good practices in CI/CD.
  • General AI/LLM knowledge (prompting, agent orchestration, RAG); the experience of Azure OpenAI / Azure AI Foundry is a plus.
  • Data bases science / machine learning and taste for experimentation (model evaluation, fast prototyping).
  • Curiosity or first experience of the Data & BI ecosystem (Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, dbt, Snowflake, Databricks...).
  • Current French and technical English (reading documentation and code).

Expected qualifications

  • Engineer rigor: taste of clean code, tested and documented.
  • Autonomy and strength of proposal.
  • Curiosity for the state of AI art (agents, LLMs, new models).
  • Team spirit and good communication skills, written and oral.
  • Impact oriented pragmatism: ability to transform experimentation into a reliable and measurable result.

Why should we join?

  • Contributing to real and innovative AI projects (generative AI, agents, LLMs) on various customer cases.
  • Competing on GenAI applied in a demanding software engineering framework (Azure AI Foundry, MLOps, CI/CD).
  • Be supervised by Data & AI experts and benefit from a true sharing of know-how.
  • Measure the tangible impact of your work on concrete and diverse projects.
  • Evolve in a rapidly growing group, with real prospects for continuation at the end of the internship

PRACTICAL MODALITIES

  • Type of contract: Contracted completion period (PFE).
  • Duration: about 6 months (according to academic calendar).
  • Location: Geneva or Lausanne, hybrid format.
  • Start: depending on availability.
  • Remuneration: paid internship, according to current profile and grid.

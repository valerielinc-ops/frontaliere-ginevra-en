Full description

Do you like fresh products and contact with customers? At Migros Geneva, you live our Fruit & Vegetables department every day. You ensure the freshness and quality of the products, treat their presentation and advise customers. Thanks to your attentive eye and energy, you help make the ray attractive from morning to evening.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, MM Lancy-Onex, Rue des Bossons 78/80, 1213 Petit-Lancy

Important remarks

The use of women aims at simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You guarantee the freshness of products. You control their quality, appearance and maturity every day. You remove those who no longer meet our requirements.
  • You're making your department live. You fill the stalls, ensure a nice presentation and adapt the setting up throughout the day to make the customers want.
  • You advise customers. You share your knowledge of fruits and vegetables, answer questions and help everyone find the products that fit their needs.
  • You handle the goods carefully. You receive deliveries, checks products and rows properly to preserve their quality and freshness.
  • You're monitoring the stock and the needs of the department. You anticipate missing products and contribute to orders to offer an assortment available throughout the day.
  • You keep your ray clean and welcoming. You store your workspace and respect hygiene and safety rules to ensure an impeccable environment.

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of experience: in the field of retail trade in fruit and vegetables.
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • You know fruits and vegetables and their peculiarities. You know how to recognize a quality product, handle it carefully and give simple advice to customers.
  • You have a sense of business and service. You naturally go to customers, listen to their needs and take pleasure in advising them to make them want to come back.
  • You're dynamic and you like field work. You enjoy active days, work carefully and keep an eye on your radius and priorities.
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and free of charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Michaël Lassère Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100%

Location : Geneva Work Pattern Percentage : 80% - 100% Start date : from February 2027 About the Role In Deloitte Forensics you will use the power of data to detect, prevent and investigate financial crime. You will help our clients across a broad spectrum of industries to fight fraud, money laundering, or sanctions breaches. Working side by side with a diverse group of talented professionals from a science, business or legal background, you will turn data into actionable insights to protect our clients and society — while continuously expanding your own skills. How you can make an impact

  • Help clients navigate evolving financial crime threats and regulatory expectations, strengthening their resilience against fraud, money laundering and sanctions breaches.
  • Use data science, analytics and modern technology to design tailored financial crime prevention and detection solutions.
  • Assess and enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of clients’ existing financial crime controls, models and processes.
  • Support forensic investigations by analysing complex data sets, identifying patterns and translating findings into clear, actionable insights.
  • Work independently while benefiting from the advice of experienced colleagues in multidisciplinary project teams. Your active contribution is pivotal to making an impact that matters for our clients and to drive your own professional growth. What you bring to succeed
  • You have a Master’s degree or PhD in Data Science, Computer Science, Mathematics, Engineering, another related quantitative field or Criminal Sciences.
  • You have a strong interest in fighting financial crime to protect individuals, organizations and society.
  • You are a curious, structured problem solver who enjoys turning data into actionable insights.
  • You take on tasks flexibly as required by engagements and clients.
  • You have excellent interpersonal, written, and verbal communication skills in English and French mandatory What sets you apart
  • You are interested in working independently while collaborating effectively in multidisciplinary teams.
  • You are willing to invest in continuous learning to get up to speed quickly and stay on top of a dynamically changing environment.
  • You have additional language skills beyond English and French. Your team Deloitte Forensic is the leading Swiss Forensic & Financial Crime team, based in Zurich and Geneva. We are a team of 90 highly competent colleagues from a wide variety of backgrounds, including chartered accountants, lawyers and technology experts. The team helps clients across a broad spectrum of industries to address issues related to financial crime, fraud, and corporate misconduct – protecting them and society from ever‑evolving threats. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.

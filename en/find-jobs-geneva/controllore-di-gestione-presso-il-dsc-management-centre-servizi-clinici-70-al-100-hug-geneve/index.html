Full description

As part of your function, you support the Administrative and Financial Officer (RAF) and integrate his or her team by actively collaborating in the effective management of the management hub and its directorates by bringing your financial expertise. You work together to develop the budget and objectives of the management centre and its directorates in accordance with the budget framework set and verify their implementation. You are involved in the development of steering tools and the monitoring of indicators and dashboards to verify their implementation. You identify, identify and assess the financial impact of the objectives and the planned means / budgetary demands of the services in order to prepare them for the negotiations undertaken by the administrative and financial manager with the relevant directorates. You participate in the operational management control of the directorates, the reporting (financial and activity) and assist managers in their operational management. To this end, you analyze the pilotage indicators, use them statistically and highlight key points, analyze the variances (budgets/forecasts/realizations) and propose corrections with the financial manager and his deputy. You are involved in the analysis of management accounting results. You also carry out economic and financial analyses as well as business plans related to the development of the business.

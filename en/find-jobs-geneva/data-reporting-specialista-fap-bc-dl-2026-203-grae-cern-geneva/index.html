Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Extract, transform, model, and visualise data in reports, dashboards, and analytics platforms;
  • Interact with relational databases, mainly Oracle, using SQL and PL/SQL;
  • Develop, maintain, and test reporting data models and semantic models supporting CERN's Finance and Procurement systems;
  • Maintain, support, and enhance existing reporting and data solutions while helping define and implement their future evolution;
  • Contribute to data profiling, mapping, quality checks, validation, and reconciliation activities during migration and transition phases;
  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of ETL/ELT pipelines and other data-processing activities where required;
  • Collaborate with business users, functional experts, architects, and developers to ensure data consistency, usability, and technical feasibility;
  • Support testing, troubleshooting, documentation, maintenance, and third-level support for reporting and data solutions.

  • Experience analysing data structures, flows, and dependencies in complex enterprise systems. Data Engineering:

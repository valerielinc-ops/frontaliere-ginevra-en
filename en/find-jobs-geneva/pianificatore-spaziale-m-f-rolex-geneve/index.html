Full description

Introduction

For our Purchasing, Support & Infrastructure division, we are looking for a Space planner (M/F) whose main mission will be to develop and implement administrative and industrial development projects within the company.

Responsibilities

  • Collect and analyse the needs of internal clients in order to propose technical solutions adapted, realistic and aligned with the objectives of the project. Providing advice throughout the various phases of implementation
  • Design and prepare implementation files: development of plans, specifications, technical documents, specifications and details necessary for the successful completion of the work
  • Develop budget estimates and monitor the financial performance of projects, including cost control, monitoring of commitments, gap analysis and corrective action where necessary
  • Plan, organize and coordinate work with internal teams, suppliers and subcontractors, while ensuring timeliness, budgets, quality requirements and safety standards
  • Ensure overall monitoring of projects, from the expression of need to the receipt of work, maintaining clear and regular communication with all stakeholders.

Profile

  • Architect, SWC of Building Designer or Diploma of Interior Architect
  • More than 5 years' experience in managing development and/or building projects
  • Mastery of Archicad software (licence 28), MS Project and PC environment (Windows suite)
  • Proactivity, autonomy and great attention to detail
  • A sense of communication and good relational qualities.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

Full description

Within the OBB House and as part of a multi-disciplinary and multi-weekly care, you independently provide psychomotor care (balance sheet, individual and/or group sessions with body mediation) for children aged 0 to 2 years and their parents. You accompany and support parenting, child psychomotor development and parent-child relationship. You participate in the synthesis meetings, the development of therapeutic projects and guidance. Within the OBB House, a structure attached to the Office for Children and Youth, focusing on particularly vulnerable families, you have the opportunity to provide support in the development of parent-child ties for families with high psychosocial risk. You carry out psychomotor assessments and then a parent-baby accompaniment in close collaboration with the team of the House OFB (educational team of the FOJ) and that of the SPEA/HUG (pedopsychologist, child psychiatrist of the unit Early childhood guidance, pediatrician, adult psychiatrist and social worker). In addition, you support and evaluate the establishment of parent-baby/child ties during daily care (bathing, change, meals, sleep, interactions/games...) and the skills of the baby/child as well as his/her psychomotor development by accompanying his/her parents in reading, decoding and responding to his/her signals.

  • You support and accompany the parent according to his or her mental state (postpartum depression, personality disorder, non-decompensated psychiatric disorder...).
  • In addition, you support and evaluate parenting skills.
  • You can participate in joint interviews with the child psychiatrist or psychologist.
  • Participation is also required in team symposia, supervision, psycho-social networks around the family

Finally, from an administrative point of view, you will take care of the follow-up notes of the daily observations of the child and the parent-baby relationship for each family. You are involved in the preparation and writing of reports and in billing.

Full description

Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The HUG Finance Directorate is responsible for the financial, budgetary and accounting management of the hospital, the administrative management of the patient, the setting of tariffs, the planning of investments, the development and maintenance of the economic intelligence system. It manages a budget of almost 2 billion francs, processes and controls over 1 million supplier invoices and over 1 million patient bills. It proposes and publishes indicators and dashboards to monitor activity and institutional objectives. Finally, it is responsible for the production of all official statistics transmitted to the health authorities. As part of the development of our activities, we are looking for a management supervisor responsible for analysing and developing the system of private fees, in accordance with the procedures and institutional rules in force. At the interface between financial, operational and institutional issues, you help ensure data reliability and process efficiency.

Description of post

Attached or attached to the Finance Directorate's Information Collection Space, you ensure the piloting, analysis and development of the activity related to the private fees of CCAG physicians. As part of piloting and reporting, you ensure regular reporting of the activity related to private fees. You set up and develop monitoring indicators and dashboards for activity management, and produce institutional and individual reports for physicians. You conduct in-depth financial analyses and implement analytical tools to monitor the evolution of private fees and to measure their financial impacts. You also control the quality, consistency and reliability of financial and administrative data. You are involved in setting up the business, technical and financial structure necessary to manage and pay the private fees of the CCAG doctors. You analyze existing processes and propose improvements to enhance their effectiveness and security, and drive or participate in projects to optimize tools and procedures. Finally, you provide support and advice to the various interlocutors and internal interlocutors and assist stakeholders in understanding and using management indicators and steering tools.

Qualifications

You have a higher education such as HES/HEG, University or equivalent in the fields of management, management control, finance or economics. You benefit from 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar position, ideally in the hospital or medical field. You have good knowledge of the billing and cashing process. You have very good skills in data analysis and financial information interpretation. You are able to build consolidated reporting devices and decision support indicators. You have excellent command of office tools, especially Excel (advanced level), BO with the ability to develop automated tools and macros. You have strong analytical, synthesis and extension capabilities. You are a rigorous, autonomous person with a sense of responsibility.

Full description

The La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Anchored in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, La Tour Hospital has placed continuous improvement and the development of medical excellence at the heart of its priorities.

For the office of orthopaedic surgery, Dr. Faundez, specialist in orthopaedic surgery and traumatology of the musculoskeletal system, we are recruiting a

50% medical secretary (ref: 1983)

Your mission

We are looking for an experienced medical secretary for Dr Antonio Faundez's secretariat. You will perform all administrative functions relating to the proper functioning of the orthopaedic surgery secretariat, in pairs with the incumbent secretary.

As such you will be primarily responsible for the following tasks;

  • Making an appointment & organising the doctor's agenda
  • Preparation of the file, convocation, patient reception
  • Management & tracking of admissions folder
  • Verification & follow-up of insurance requests
  • Organization of radiological examinations & laboratory
  • Reservations of operating rooms via Opale software
  • Snapshot of operational reports & reports
  • Seizure of outpatient and hospital benefits
  • Mail management, telephone calls, incoming and outgoing mail
  • Scanning & archiving documents.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Graduated from Medical Secretary
  • Minimum experience, three years, with knowledge of orthopaedics
  • Knowledge of the Swiss health system (Tardoc) & social insurance
  • Snapshot of operational reports and consultations
  • Rigor, organizational sense, discretion & know-how
  • Multiple task resistance, versatility & autonomy
  • Mastering Opale, Xplore, Rap software would be a major asset
  • Mastery of the French language & irreproachable spelling
  • Level of English C1, imperative.

We offer you

  • Type of contract: Contract with Indeterminate Duration
  • Percentage: 50%
  • Presence: Tuesday afternoon, Wednesday & Thursday all day. 100% available for school replacements
  • Hours : 12:30-16:30 & 7:45 - 4.30pm

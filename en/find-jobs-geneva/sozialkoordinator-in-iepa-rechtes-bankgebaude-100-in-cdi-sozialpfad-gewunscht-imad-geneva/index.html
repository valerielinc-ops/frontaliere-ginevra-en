Full description

The elderly housing (IEPA) consists of self-catering housing, with full tenants for the elderly, as well as common premises for various activities, allowing them to remain autonomous in a secure environment. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Ensure the safety and well-being of tenants by making the necessary arrangements
  • Organize collective life within the building and provide entertainment services
  • Provide social and administrative assistance to tenants who so request (e.g. ensuring tenants' access to social rights, assisting them in their financial management, as well as in the processing of mail and solicitations from partners internal or external to IMAD, etc.)
  • Provide services specific to home care and contribute to the interdisciplinary care of tenants through coordination with different partners
  • Work closely with IEPA team leaders and ensure the administrative management of social stewardship in accordance with IMAD procedures
  • Ensure monitoring and partnership with building-owner foundations, agents (regimes, companies) within the framework defined between the different partners
  • Provide guards on weekends and holidays.
  • Bachelor HES-SO in social work or diploma recognized as equivalent by the Swiss Conference of High Special Schools and Higher Schools of Social Work (SASSA)
  • 3-5 years experience in social or care settings (geriatric or psychiatric experience required)
  • Ability to work independently AND mobility on the Canton
  • Knowledge of the social network / Geneva health
  • Age in the relationship with the elderly
  • Administrative rigor and organizational qualities
  • Good mastery of standard IT tools.
  • Deadline for application: 1.11.2026
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.

We only consider full applications We guarantee complete confidentiality in the processing of your file.

Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Operations. The mission of this department is to ensure the stewardship and logistics necessary for the proper functioning of all HUGs, in the restoration, cleaning and disinfection of premises and hygiene of linen. It also manages the sterilization service, the biomedical service and equipment as well as Cressy-Santé. More than 1'300 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. The Catering Service consists of seven kitchens and eleven food centres (brasserie, cafeteria-restaurant, coffee, sandwich shop) serving some 9,000 meals a day. The mission of this service is to provide quality services and pleasant and friendly moments for patients, staff and customers, with a service attentive to the criteria of a balanced and varied diet that meets the high requirements of hygiene and flavour. The Catering Service also offers a catering service (banquets, aperitifs, etc.) on the various sites of the institution. In all the HUGs, the Catering Service focuses on fresh products and local products, labeled Geneva Region Terre Avenir.

Description of post

As part of the strengthening of our organization, we are looking for an experienced administrative manager to coordinate administrative activities, procurement and monitoring the maintenance of catering equipment. A true interface between operational teams, suppliers and support services, you ensure the administrative control of activities, the monitoring of contracts and budgets, the management of procurement processes and the monitoring of the equipment fleet through GMAO tools. You are actively contributing to continuous process improvement, development of performance indicators and monitoring of service projects. You also accompany the teams in the formalisation of procedures, the monitoring of corrective actions and the coordination of technical interventions in order to ensure the continuity and effectiveness of the services provided by the catering service.

Qualifications

You have completed your course with additional training in administrative management, procurement, logistics, maintenance or project management (federal patent, CAS or equivalent). You also have the certificate of trainer in company, or demonstrate the willingness to get it quickly after you start working. You justify a minimum of 5 years of experience in an administrative management function, complemented by a proven experience in procurement, logistics, maintenance or equipment management. Your journey also demonstrates a successful experience in accompaniment of collaborators or training, as well as in project management or continuous improvement. Technically, you have strong knowledge of administrative and budgetary management, good control of procurement and procurement processes, and knowledge of computer-assisted maintenance management (CAM). With excellent Microsoft 365 mastery and ease of use with ERP and integrated management systems, you have excellent analytical, synthesis and high priority. Finally, your sense of organization and rigor combine with a strong sense of autonomy and responsibility. Recognized or recognized for your excellent relationship and coordination capacity, you stand out for your proactivity, initiative, customer orientation and your strong taste for continuous improvement.

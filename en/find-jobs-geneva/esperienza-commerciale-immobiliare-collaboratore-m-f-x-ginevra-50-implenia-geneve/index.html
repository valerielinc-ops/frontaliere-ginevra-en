Full description

For our Geneva branch, in a team dedicated exclusively to the management of commercial buildings, we respond with expertise and negotiation to the individual and complex needs of our institutional clients. As a strategic partner, we focus on offices and commercial goods.

Through targeted and tailor-made marketing strategies, we pursue the ambition to rent each surface successfully while building lasting and trusting relationships with business owners and tenants.

If you're passionate about real estate, you're entrepreneurial and you like to work as a team, we'd be happy to receive your application as:

Collaborator in Experienced Commercial Real Estate (M/F/X) Geneva, 50%

What you contribute to

  • You support the management team in all administrative tasks related to the management of commercial buildings
  • You are the contact person for tenants, owners, service providers, artisans and authorities
  • You establish and manage correspondence, contracts, amendments and other administrative documents
  • You are involved in the marketing of commercial surfaces and in the processing of rental applications
  • You are responsible for monitoring the mandates given to external service providers and for monitoring the work carried out.
  • You support the processing of the records of places of entry and exit and the follow-up of tenant requests
  • The processing of supplier invoices, the control of debtors and the various administrative tasks complete your field of activity

What you bring

  • You have successfully completed commercial training
  • Professional experience in real estate, ideally in the field of commercial buildings, is an asset
  • Continuing training in real estate (federal certificate or patent) is an advantage
  • You appreciate teamwork and stand out for your sense of responsibility, customer orientation and reliability
  • You work independently, structured and oriented towards the objectives
  • You have excellent communication skills and appreciate contact with different people
  • You express yourself perfectly in French, both oral and written. Good knowledge of German is an advantage

Some of our benefits include

  • Working time: we live in mobile work with flexible working hours (annualisation of working time) and/or part-time work. Telework is possible up to 40%. Work-life balance is essential to us. Additional holiday purchase is also possible.
  • Indemnities: you receive a contribution to mobile phone charges and a contribution to meals.
  • Training and development: the development of our employees is a central element of our culture. We generously support internal and external training and offer opportunities for evolution in the functions of specialists, project managers or management.
  • Salary: We offer market-compliant remuneration with absolute equal pay. Our pension fund offers attractive benefits with different models of choice. Accident insurance also covers the private division in case of hospitalization.

Find out more about our benefits and conditions of employment on our Careers page.

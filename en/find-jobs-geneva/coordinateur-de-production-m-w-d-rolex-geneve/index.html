Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a Atelier Manager (M/F) whose main mission will be to meet the production objectives and to be the guarantor of the monitoring of manufacturing activities.

Responsibilities

  • Organize and coordinate, in relation to the various production respondents and providers, the daily objectives by ensuring an optimal distribution of the workload
  • Pilot actions to control outstanding, late or advance delivery in order to meet deadlines
  • Contribute, in a logic of permanent progress, to support cells towards autonomy and accountability
  • Manage inventory organization
  • Conduct and/or participate in daily coordination sessions
  • Monitor and manage the proper functioning of the precious material management process (inventory, balance sheets).

Profile

  • ES technician or equivalent
  • Training in production organization and management or experience deemed equivalent
  • Experience in an industrial environment, an asset
  • Mastery of IT tools (MS-Office, SAP and production monitoring indicators)
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Communicative, flexible and excellent team spirit
  • Ability to take initiatives. & FocusChain

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Full description

Cardio-respiratory physiotherapy is intended for patients who require specialized accompaniment in the fields of heart, respiratory, oncological, diabetes, obesity.

These areas represent the 5 pillars of the outpatient rehabilitation of our service. Our care is for both adults and children. For the hospital sector, cardio-respiratory physiotherapist intervenes within the various services: intensive care, continuous care, medicine, surgery.

Our team's mission is to improve, maintain and/or restore patients' cardiac and respiratory functions while improving their ability to work and their quality of life through physical activity.

In order to meet the needs of the service we recruit one

Physiotherapy Cardio-Respiratory 90% CDMax 10 months CDI possibility (ref: 2010)

Your mission

You work in a shared environment between hospital services and the outpatient rehabilitation technical plateau, within a motivated and dynamic team.

Your objectives

  • Evaluate the patient's functional abilities and set treatment objectives.
  • Develop and deliver individual or group physiotherapy sessions
  • Participate in the interdisciplinary therapeutic process
  • Participate in multidisciplinary symposia with other specialists

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • You have a degree in Swiss physiotherapist (or equivalent). Pre check positive required.
  • You are a force for proposals and are at the origin of initiatives
  • You show dynamism, motivation, curiosity and open mind
  • You like human relations and have a good team spirit
  • You are motivated and able to convey this motivation to others
  • You exercise autonomy and responsibility in your professional activity

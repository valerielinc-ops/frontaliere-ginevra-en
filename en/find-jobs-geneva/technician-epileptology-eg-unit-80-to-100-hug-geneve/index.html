Full description

You are in charge of receiving and caring for patients who come to perform neurophysiological examinations. You operate in two areas, both stationary and outpatient, but also in patient bed at different sites. You perform different examinations: EEG, Polysomnography, evoked potentials (motors, sensory, visual and auditory) which are prescribed by the medical team, for a patientel hospitalized and/or outpatient whose age may vary from newborn to elderly.

  • You accompany the patient and her entourage to the runaway of the disease of epilepsy for the preparation for possible surgery by relying on therapeutic education.
  • You work closely with the medical team.
  • You provide specific autonomous care and perform delegated medical procedures.
  • You handle emergency situations.

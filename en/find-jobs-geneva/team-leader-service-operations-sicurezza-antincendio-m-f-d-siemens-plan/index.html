Full description

We connect real and digital worlds in collaboration with our customers. Siemens is a technological leader who employs approximately 6,000 people. Building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry: our innovative solutions span many fields and shape the world of tomorrow. In order to support the strong growth of our activities in the field of fire detection and to continue to guarantee a high level of excellence for our customers, we are strengthening our Fire Safety Service organization in Geneva. As part of this evolution, we are creating a second team of technicians and looking for a Team Leader Service Operations Fire Safety. This key function will actively contribute to the development of our organization and to the support of a team of nearly 35 employees, divided under several team leaders. This job creation aims to strengthen the managerial proximity, technical support and quality of our field services, in a context of sustained growth in our operations. Want to take on a new managerial challenge and participate actively in the development of Fire Safety Geneva? Then this post is for you! Apply today as Fire Safety Operations Team Leader and start now or according to availability - your team is looking forward to welcoming you! Your new tasks As Team Leader, you will be responsible for:

  • Manager and accompany one of the Fire Safety service technicians teams.
  • Ensure the development of the technical and professional skills of employees.
  • Ensure monitoring of accreditations, PPE, security training and compliance with EHS requirements.
  • Ensure the quality of our clients' services (maintenance, troubleshooting, commissioning, periodic checks, etc.).
  • Collaborate closely with the Geneva Fire Safety Team Manager as well as with the head of the second team of technicians.
  • Coordinate the treatment of recurring troubleshooting in collaboration with the referent technicians and technical experts.
  • Monitor strategic clients and participate in the development of incident reports.
  • Actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes and to the satisfaction of our clients. Your talents and experience
  • Excellent communicator, able to motivate and unite a team.
  • Natural leadership and strong collaboration orientation.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and ability to take initiatives.
  • Great rigor in the application of safety processes, standards and instructions.
  • Excellent organizational, coordination and priority management skills.
  • Ability to manage contingencies and emergencies with autonomy.
  • Very good sense of customer service and relationship ease.
  • Good technical understanding of fire detection systems to support teams in diagnostics and troubleshooting actions. Your Benefits
  • A unique opportunity to participate in the construction of a new organization.
  • A role with high human and operational impact.
  • A dynamic and growing environment.
  • Close collaboration with the technical, commercial and operational teams of Fire Safety Geneva.
  • The possibility to support the development of our employees while contributing directly to the satisfaction of our customers.
  • 2 to 3 days/week of mobile work by default
  • Public transport subscription at reduced prices and free parking
  • Many opportunities for development and further training
  • Access to employee share plans

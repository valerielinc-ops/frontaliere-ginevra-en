Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The Private HUG Division guarantees access to state-of-the-art therapies and treatments and high-end hotel services. It consists of seven treatment units covering more than 24 medical and surgical specialties and offers patients a high quality reception and care, state-of-the-art services and access to an ultra-performance technical tray, while ensuring their comfort and well-being.

Description of post

As part of your activity, in a medical unit that hosts both general medicine and on-the-hematology, you accompany patients on a daily basis in their care journey. The Private Division seeks to strengthen and develop the role of ASSC within its teams. Thanks to your strong ability to position yourself and you are able to develop and strengthen the role within the service. Under delegation and responsibility of nurses, you assist patients in their daily activities and provide care by adapting to their needs and rhythm of life. You communicate appropriately to the patient, you know how to listen to her and you know how to reassure her if necessary. In a good spirit of collaboration, you report your interventions to the healthcare team. You perform the medical and technical procedures that fall within your competence in complete autonomy, such as assessing vital signs, measuring blood pressure, preparing medication treatments, changing dressings and taking injections and samples. You are actively involved in the prevention of complications related to hospitalization in collaboration with other health professional bodies (pain, escarre prevention, prevention of falling and infections, etc.), in addition you help the patient in the preparation of her exit. You ensure the maintenance of equipment, furniture, monitoring and documentation of your patients' and patients' computerized records and carry out administrative and logistical activities within your unit related to your healthcare profession.

Qualifications

Having obtained the SWC as an assistant in community health and care, you hold your right of practice valid in the Canton of Geneva. You have demonstrated your technical skills in the practice of care and are a recognized professional or professional for your relationship with both the patient and the care team. You are a reactive, organized person, with a good management of stress and priorities, you know how to analyze clinical situations and adapt to the context and to the unforeseen circumstances inherent in the activity of a care unit. Your ability to work collaboratively allows you to work hand in hand with a team of health care professionals. You ensure accurate and rigorous transmission of information within multidisciplinary teams. You are involved in the supervision and clinical education of new health care staff and other health professionals and professionals. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in the ASSC function. You are a motivated, listening and dynamic person ready to invest in an institution whose relational values you share: trust, respect, teamwork, recognition and equality and inclusion.

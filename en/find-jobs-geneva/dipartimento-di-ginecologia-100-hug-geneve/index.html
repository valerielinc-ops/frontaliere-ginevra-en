Description of post

Within the gynaecology department, you assist the medical officer in charge of the management and administrative organisation of the service, in the clinical, academic and research fields, ensuring the interface between the various professional fields. You organize appointments, sessions and seminars, managing the electronic agenda of the physician head of department. You prepare the files, prepare the minutes of meetings, and coordinate the follow-up of decisions made. You manage the correspondence independently. Your self-initiative ability allows you to analyze situations in order to submit draft responses. While responding to the various requests, you carry out the administrative activities related to your function, such as the management of the schedules (HVR) of senior physicians, chiefs of clinics and internal doctors, the administrative management of private or faculty funds, the organization of the medical profession's congresses and symposia, the monitoring of the budget allocated to the service and research. Your proactivity allows you to perform logistical tasks arising from the life of a secretariat, such as orders, requests for commitment, reimbursement of costs and training. You are also involved in the administrative coordination of the Service's consultations. As such, you ensure appointment management, follow-up of consultation schedules, preparation of medical records, and liaison with patients, physicians, and various stakeholders to ensure the smooth running of outpatient care. You ensure the processing and follow-up of requests related to the consultation activity. Within the team, you develop a close relationship with your fellow medical secretaries and ensure continuity of service during absences. And more generally, your sense of service makes you an agile person ready to put your versatility at the service of the institution to practice in different services according to needs.

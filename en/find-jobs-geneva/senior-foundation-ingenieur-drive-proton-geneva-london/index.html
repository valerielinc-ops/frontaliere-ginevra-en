Drive Foundation

Drive Foundation is the backbone of Proton Drive. We ensure that cross-client initiatives are executed consistently and efficiently. Whether it’s aligning multiple clients, maintaining documentation or the RFC process, or building robust SDKs, our team lays the groundwork that makes the entire Drive experience work seamlessly.

  • Current Projects:
    • We’re currently focused on developing SDK that encapsulate the core functionality of Proton Drive. The SDK comes with two core implementations, one in C# for desktop and mobile clients and second in TypeScript for web client, and higher-level modules in Rust. Our SDK primarily serve internal clients today, but our long-term vision includes integrating them across the Proton ecosystem—and even enabling third-party applications to interface with Drive.
  • Tech Stack and Tools:
    • Languages we see daily is C#, TypeScript and Rust. We also need to provide bindings for Swift and Kotlin. We use Grafana and Prometheus to instrument our SDK, Sentry to get details issues from the SDK usage, GitLab CI for running tests including e2e tests written using Playwright, or GitLab in general to work on the code base.
  • Future Plans:
    • We are planning to revamp our cryptographic model to boost performance and efficiency across all clients. We will finish ongoing first generation of SDK to cover all core Drive features and new crypto model. Then we will do second generation in Rust with lessons learned and include full support of all features such as Photos, Public Sharing, and Docs.
  • Impact Opportunities:
    • Foundation work touches every aspect of Drive. The SDK and infrastructure you build will be used by every Drive client—and getting it right is both critical and deeply rewarding.
    • Drive is rapidly expanding. As it evolves into a true platform, we expect more teams to emerge—each owning different verticals built on top of Drive. This opens up immense opportunities to grow as an IC, tech lead or engineering manager.
    • You’ll have the chance to shape not only the future of Drive, but also your own career path in a variety of domains and technologies.

As a Senior Drive Foundation Engineer, you will play a crucial role in designing, implementing, and maintaining the foundational components that power Proton Drive applications. You will work collaboratively with Drive teams, including backend, web, mobile, or desktop developers, to ensure seamless integration and optimal performance across various client applications.

What you will do:

  • Architect and develop core libraries: Lead the design and development of core C#/TypeScript/Rust libraries that serve as the backbone for Proton Drive's applications.
  • Cross-platform compatibility: Ensure compatibility and optimal performance across various platforms.
  • Code review and optimization: Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to ensure code quality, maintainability, and performance optimization.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with backend, frontend, mobile and desktop developers to integrate core libraries seamlessly into Proton Drive's applications.
  • Testing: Develop and implement comprehensive testing strategies, including unit tests and integration tests, to ensure the reliability of core libraries.
  • Documentation: Create and maintain thorough documentation for core libraries to assist other developers in understanding and utilizing the components.

Job requirements:

  • 5+ years of professional software development experience, with a focus on the Rust programming language.
  • Strong expertise in Rust language features, concurrency, and ownership models.
  • Web, mobile, and/or desktop development: Experience in developing libraries for client applications.
  • Proven ability to work collaboratively in a team environment and excellent communication skills.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to troubleshoot issues efficiently.
  • Proton is an office-first company. We expect you to spend most of your time in the office, and while we do offer some flexibility to work from home, we believe in-person collaboration helps us build the best products.

Even if you don’t meet all the requirements listed above, but feel you could still be a great fit, please still apply.

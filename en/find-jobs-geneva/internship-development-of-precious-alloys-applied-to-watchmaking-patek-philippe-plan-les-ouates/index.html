Full description

Mission

Attached to our Horloger Research Division and for a period of 6 months, you will be in charge of the development and characterization of precious alloys as a whole for watch applications in order to improve their properties.

Responsibilities

  • Analysis of the state of art.
  • Propose an experiment plan for the implementation of the tests.
  • Make prototypes via alloy fusions and then thermomechanical treatments.
  • Characterize prototypes: microstructure, mechanical properties, termination, etc.
  • Correlate results with process parameters and draw conclusions.
  • Write a written report, make a written and oral presentation.

Profile sought

  • Master in Materials Science and Engineering or title deemed equivalent, in progress.
  • Knowledge of metallurgy and method of material characterization.
  • Good project management skills.
  • Autonomy, power of proposition.
  • Analytical mind.
  • Relationship relationship.
  • Master's project lasting 6 months

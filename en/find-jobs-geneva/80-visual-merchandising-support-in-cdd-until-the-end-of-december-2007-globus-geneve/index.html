Full description

It is with great passion and love of detail that our employees make GLOBUS the first address of the Swiss Luxury and Premium. They are people like you who make the difference and, with us, give meaning to "the place we love to be".

IT'S WHAT YOU DO DIFFERENCE

  • You support the Visual Merchandising team in presenting the goods and setting up decoration on the store's sales surface.
  • You install, climb and disassemble promotions.
  • You are involved in various and varied workshop work (painting, carpeting, flower bouquet composition, etc.).
  • You prepare the marking equipment and install the posters in store.
  • Occasionally, you support sales teams in making gift packaging for customers.

WHAT DISTINCE YOU

  • You have a CFC of 3D polydesigner (decorator) and a small experience in retailing.
  • Your sense of organization and good physical condition allow you to perform manual work efficiently.
  • Excellent presentation, you are autonomous, versatile, orientated, know how to be flexible (times etc.) and appreciate teamwork.

BENEFITS FOR YOU With more than 1,700 other retail enthusiasts, you are an integral part of our fascinating world of high-end and luxury. You are recognized and valued, you work with an exciting and motivated team and we give you our trust so that you can enjoy free time and make a difference with your know-how. If you're an expert in your profession and you're putting your heart into it, many doors will open to develop your career. We will gladly support you in your internal and external trainings.

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • an exceptional working environment with a horizontal hierarchy in the city center.
  • performance-related premiums and attractive social benefits .
  • flexible work models and at least 5 weeks of vacation (PS: You can benefit from more free time thanks to the option "unpaid leave" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 30% discount on Globus universe products for you and your family.
  • and much more...

We are at your disposal.

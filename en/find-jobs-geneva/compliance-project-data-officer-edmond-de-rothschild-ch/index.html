Compliance Project & Data Officer — Edmond de Rothschild

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

RESPONSIBILITIES

Data

You actively contribute to the Compliance function’s data strategy and help promote a data culture:

  • Strengthen the quality, reliability and availability of Compliance data in order to support decision-making, risk management and compliance with regulatory obligations;
  • Make the data needed to produce recurring and ad hoc reports available;
  • Lead initiatives to transform how data is accessed and distributed, and participate in the continuous improvement of its quality;
  • In close collaboration with the Group’s cross-functional teams (Front, Legal, Data Office, Finance, etc.), you contribute to structuring a robust and shared data foundation;
  • Support the upskilling of teams, in order to firmly embed a data culture in day-to-day activities by sharing knowledge and best practices.

Projects

You lead key Compliance projects, from design through implementation:

  • Identify opportunities to transform ways of working in order to improve performance and efficiency (integration of AI tools);
  • Manage projects methodically through all their phases;
  • Define and validate the functional specifications for processes and tools, in compliance with the regulatory and internal framework;
  • Ensure coordination between Compliance teams, project management teams and the other stakeholders of the Group (IT, business lines, support functions);
  • Identify problems and propose solutions to ensure smooth execution of projects;
  • Represent the Compliance function in working groups and project committees;
  • Participate in testing phases to ensure that deliverables meet the stated requirements;
  • Manage relationships with suppliers, in coordination with the relevant internal teams.

Operations

You ensure risk is managed and Compliance productivity is continuously optimized:

  • Ensure alignment between the internal framework and tool configuration;
  • Ensure no regression when tools are updated;
  • Analyze and resolve operational incidents, in conjunction with the relevant stakeholders;
  • Contribute to proper-functioning checks and the implementation of preventive measures.

PROFILE

This position requires a strong ability to adapt and the ability to move from a broad perspective to concrete actions.

  • Mandatory proficiency in data analysis and reporting tools (advanced Excel, BI tools) ;
  • Significant experience managing compliance projects in the financial sector ;
  • Strong interest in teamwork and cross-functional collaboration ;
  • Rigor and organization in handling emergencies and prioritizing multiple deliverables ;
  • Analytical and synthesis skills, with the ability to simplify seemingly complex situations ;
  • Sense of responsibility and ability to add value ;
  • Very good listening skills and the ability to secure buy-in ;
  • Good verbal and written communication skills, in both French and English ;
  • Positive attitude, ability to find solutions and align stakeholders with the project ;
  • Excellent command of French and English ;
  • Residence in Switzerland.

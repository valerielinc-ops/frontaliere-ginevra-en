Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of a fixed job? We will not lie to you however: we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Operator in charge of the 5 axis grinding (M/F/D)

Your assignment

  • To realize and optimize the programs of processing with Alphacam and to make the adjustments in ISO language (Fanuc).
  • To ensure the complete regulation of CNC grinding machines with 5 axes and to validate the put into production.
  • To ensure the start and monitoring of productions in compliance with the objectives of quality, costs and timing.
  • To read and interpret the technical drawings, operating cards and specifications of customers.
  • To carry out the changes of series, the assembly and the adjustments necessary for production.
  • Perform the controls of the flow and vision during production.

And now we talk about you?

  • Training in precision mechanics, micro-technics or equivalent sector.
  • Proven experience in the regulation and programming of CNC machines for grinding in the watchmaking industry
  • Fingering of 5-axis machines of Crevoisier and Recommatic type
  • Alphacam approach and good knowledge of ISO / Fanuc language.
  • Solid skills in the reading of technical drawings and dimensional control.
  • Knowledge of quality requirements related to high precision environments.
  • Analytical spirit, autonomy and rigour.
  • Ability to work in a team and to transmit their know-how.
  • Pay on 13 months / 78k91k gross per year

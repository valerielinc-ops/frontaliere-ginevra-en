Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Real Estate Directorate (DI) has the task of renovating and building real estate infrastructure, managing, maintaining, maintaining and valuing the existing housing stock, being a committed player in the ecological transition of real estate, and cultivating operational excellence. As such, it defines the real estate strategy of the HUGs, leads the implementation of the Hospital Master Plan 2022-2040, and ensures the management and coordination of major real estate projects More than 230 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, a strong sense of service and a strong commitment to environmental responsibility.

Description of post

The Real Estate Directorate, as part of the deployment of its strategy and the achievement of institutional objectives in terms of risk control, security, compliance and sustainability of real estate assets, is seeking a real estate risk manager responsible for the project to ensure the safety, compliance and quality of hospital infrastructure. As such, you propose, define and structure a comprehensive risk management approach within the framework of an asset management adapted to the real estate assets you lay the foundations of, in relation to the challenges of continuity of hospital operations, patient and patient safety, decent reception conditions and the sustainability of infrastructure. You drive this dynamic under the governance of the real estate committee. In conjunction with the Director of Real Estate and in close collaboration with the heads of the Real Estate Directorate and other branches of the institution, you design and maintain a risk mapping, ensuring a methodical and comprehensive vision in order to develop a consolidated vision of real estate risks. Guaranteeing an integrated vision, you ensure the consolidation of the risk portfolio and map its interdependencies. You define and then implement, after validation, a rigorous methodology for analysing, assessing and prioritizing real estate infrastructure risks. You guarantee its dissemination, appropriation and application to the services and sectors concerned. You are involved in the progressive implementation of asset management by integrating a robust approach to real estate risk management, based on the principles of ISO 55000. You conduct risk and/or compliance analyses in relation to the sectors of the owner. In coordination with the real estate OMP, you propose corrective action planning taking into account financial and operational constraints. You provide methodological and organizational support to industry leaders, service managers and Real Estate Branch, and participate in the process updates. You facilitate coordination between different stakeholders, accompany change and contribute to the development of a risk culture in consultation with the various stakeholders of the institution.

Qualifications

You have a Master's degree (or equivalent) in civil engineering, engineering, architecture or relevant field, complemented by certification in risk management and certification in project management. You have at least 7 years of experience in a similar position in a large housing stock in a complex environment During your journey, you have acquired a mastery of the methods of risk analysis (AMDEC, tree of causes, CAR, ...) and a thorough knowledge of the quality and regulatory standards of the real estate field in Switzerland. You have developed strong analytical, negotiating and communication skills, and interact with the governing bodies and operational professions, based on your experience. The practical knowledge of the ISO 31000 and 55000 family of standards and its associated repositories, applied to complex real estate assets in operation, is highly desired. You are independent, versatile and adaptable to a variety of contexts. Your organizational qualities, rigor, pragmatism and ability to work in a complex environment are recognized skills. Good oral and written knowledge of English, in addition to the French you master perfectly, is an asset.

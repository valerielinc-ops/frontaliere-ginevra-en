Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The learning offer of HUGs is developing and diversifying. Apprenticeships cover 25 different occupations in several areas of hospital activity. Find out more about HUG learning or watch videos of HUG learning. To contribute to your success, your guidance is provided by certified trainers and, throughout your training, internships are organised within the institution or in collaboration with partner companies and institutions to ensure you a diversified, innovative and quality training. For the new school year 2027, the nursing teams are looking for their future apprentices and apprentices to help with AFP care and support.

Description of post

You are part of a department of care for the HUGs and when you learn under the responsibility of the healthcare staff, you provide support to patients in daily care. You manage body hygiene, food, mobilization and comfort care. You also perform stewardship and logistics. This includes disinfection of equipment, surfaces and material management. As a result, you ensure a hygienic and safe environment within our institution.

Qualifications

You will have completed 16 years of age at school, successfully completed your compulsory education and passed a test EVA https://www.eva-evatech.ch/ . You show interest in the health and social sectors. You are a listening person who wants to understand the needs of others and wishes to contribute to the care teams. You show courtesy, tact and discretion. Your hygiene and sense of cleanliness are irreproachable. Sociable, you appreciate teamwork and communicate easily with your interlocutors. You have an interest in the hospital environment, you are ready or ready to invest in vocational training alternating work in company, practical and theoretical courses at school.

Job Description

Attached or attached to the management of the crèches, you ensure the administrative management of the structures and provide essential support to the management and the assistants and educational teams. You manage the processing of mail, writing and formatting of documents, emails, presentations and communication media. You are involved in the preparation of various administrative documents for teams and families. You are responsible for the administrative follow-up of child and family files, the management of registration lists and associated databases. You also monitor children's presences as well as some data on employees' absences. You are developing scoreboards and supporting documents for decision-making on personnel management. You perform monthly billing in dedicated management systems and perform monitoring and updating of tariff data. You are involved in the tracking of orders and perform various administrative tasks related to the day-to-day operation of structures. You contribute to the updating of collaborative spaces and intranet, manage mailing lists and make public postages from existing databases. Through your daily presence in day-care centres, you play an interface role between management, educational teams and families. You participate in the quality of the reception and ensure the proper flow of information.

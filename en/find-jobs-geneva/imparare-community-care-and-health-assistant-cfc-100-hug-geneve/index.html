Description of post

Under the responsibility of nurses and medical staff, you support patients in daily care consisting of body hygiene, food, mobilization and comfort care. You develop and promote human relations. During your apprenticeship, you provide medical-technical procedures, such as medication administration, injections and dressings, on delegation and in collaboration with nurses. In collaboration with the medical-care team, you manage emergency situations that detect signs, give alerts, and manage first actions with the nursing or medical corps. In addition, you accompany people in crisis situations with an appropriate attitude. In addition, you support people in end-of-life situations by identifying their needs and assessing their pain. Stewardship of care materials (use, cleaning and maintenance of patient and patient environment) and logistics (admission of patient, appointment for examination and exit) are also included in the ASSC competency profile.

Qualifications

You will be 16 years old at the beginning of the year and successfully complete your compulsory education. You are showing interest in the health sectors. You are a listening person who wants to understand the needs of others and wishes to contribute to the care teams. Sociable, you appreciate teamwork and communicate easily with your interlocutors. The organization is one of your strengths and your analytical spirit allows you to identify problems and find solutions. Your hygiene and sense of cleanliness are irreproachable. You have an interest in the hospital environment, you are ready or ready to invest in vocational training alternating work in company, practical and theoretical courses at school.

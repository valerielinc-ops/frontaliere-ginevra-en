As a Finance Intern, your main responsibilities will include

  • Assisting with the processing and review of daily accounting operations.
  • Verifying and recording financial data within accounting systems.
  • Supporting the preparation of month-end closings and financial reports.
  • Participating in invoice tracking, account reconciliations, and internal control procedures.
  • Ensuring proper filing and accuracy of financial documents.
  • Collaborating with various departments to ensure compliance with company procedures.
  • Respecting confidentiality requirements and company policies at all times.

You'll Be a Great Fit If

You also possess the following skills and qualities

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Strong numerical skills and analytical abilities.
  • A strong sense of confidentiality and responsibility.
  • A team-oriented mindset and excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Good command of both French and English.

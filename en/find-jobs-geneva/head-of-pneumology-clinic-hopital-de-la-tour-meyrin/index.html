Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Driven by the desire to offer patients the best possible quality of life, it places medical excellence, innovation and continuous improvement at the heart of its priorities. Thanks to recognized doctors, highly qualified healthcare teams and infrastructure with the latest technology, La Tour Hospital offers a demanding, challenging and humane work environment.

In order to strengthen our pneumonology service, we are looking for one. e

Head of Pneumology Clinic (ref: 1978)

Term contract from 12 to 24 months Entry into office: 1 November 2027

In a state-of-the-art medical environment, you join a multidisciplinary team of doctors, nurses, respiratory physiotherapists and caregivers. You are actively involved in overall patient care while developing your clinical skills within a dynamic and collaborative framework.

As part of your function, you will be prompted to

  • Ensure independent medical care for patients in pneumology.
  • Conduct daily outpatient consultations.
  • Actively participate in respiratory rehabilitation program.
  • Conduct hospital consultations under the supervision of senior physicians.
  • Work closely with medical and paramedical teams.
  • Attend service meetings and training activities.
  • Contribute, according to your interests, to a clinical research project within the department.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Holder of the Federal Medical Diploma or MEBEKO recognition for foreign diplomas.
  • Prior clinical experience in pneumonology corresponding to a level of clinical director and allowing for independent management of medical situations encountered in hospital.
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written; knowledge of English is an asset.
  • A sense of contact, listening and service in the context of a private establishment.
  • Autonomy, benevolence, versatility, organizational sense and priority management.
  • Team spirit and positive attitude to foster a climate of trust and collaboration.

What we offer you

  • A varied position within a reference establishment in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A rich clinical activity, combining hospital and outpatient care.
  • Close collaboration with recognized specialists in their field.
  • The possibility of participating in clinical research projects.
  • Access to continuing training and an environment conducive to professional development.

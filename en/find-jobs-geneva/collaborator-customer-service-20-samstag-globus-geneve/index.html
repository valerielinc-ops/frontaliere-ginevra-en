Full description

It is with a great passion and love of detail that our collaborators make GLOBUS the first address of the Swiss Luxury and Premium. They are people like you who make the difference and who, with us, give meaning to "the place we love to be".

That's what you're doing.

  • Ensuring customer satisfaction and hospitality by applying our principles of excellence
  • Respond to requests from our in-store customers as well as those made by phone
  • Process special requests and client claims
  • Treatment of claims and returns of goods

WHAT DISTINCE YOU

  • You are currently in higher education (Bachelor EHL type or HES degree) and have experience at a reception or concierge service in a luxury establishment.
  • You can justify good administrative management skills (impeccable spelling).
  • You have an excellent relationship, a very good presentation and offer excellent quality customer service.
  • You can cope with a large workload, be flexible and versatile. Your team spirit allows you to collaborate with different sectors.
  • Excellent knowledge of English and French (oral and written) is required. Any other spoken language is an important asset.

BENEFITS FOR YOU With more than 1,700 other retail enthusiasts, you are an integral part of our fascinating high-end and luxury world. You are recognized and valued, you work with an exciting and motivated team and we give you our confidence so that you can enjoy free time and make a difference with your know-how. If you're an expert in your profession and you're putting your heart into it, many doors will open to develop your career. We will gladly support you in your internal and external trainings.

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • an exceptional working environment with a horizontal hierarchy in the city centre.
  • attractive social benefits.
  • flexible work models and at least 5 weeks of vacation (PS: You can enjoy more free time thanks to the option "unpaid leave" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on Globus universe products for you and your family.
  • And much more...

Your contact We look forward to receiving your application online. It's very simple. Instead of a cover letter, we look forward to your answers to our three questions - please use the link provided in the announcement to this effect.

We are at your disposal.

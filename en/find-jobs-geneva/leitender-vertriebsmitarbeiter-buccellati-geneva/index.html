Full description

Reference code: JR132534

Buccellati is a renowned Italian high-jewellery Maison founded in 1919, admired for its craftmanship and the uniqueness of its creations. Today, the Buccellati jewels stand out for their design and for the techniques used to create them, such as the hand-engraving technique, dating back to the ancient goldsmithing traditions of the Italian “bottega” during the Renaissance times.

Buccellati has been part of the Global Luxury Group Richemont since September 2019, but the Buccellati family is still present in the Company with top level managerial functions.

Buccellati boutiques are present in the most important cities and in the most prestigious Malls of the world.

We are looking for enthusiastic, passionate, dynamic people to join our new retail team in Geneva!

Main purpose

The Boutique Senior Sales Associate is a fully engaged individual who is committed to contributing to the success of Buccellati boutique in Geneva, with the ambition to grow with the Maison.

He/she is developing his/her client portfolio while investing time and efforts in the boutique life, operations and actions and transmitting knowledge and passion to the clients.

Your mission

You will become a Brand Ambassador of Buccellati Maison in the Boutique as well as at external events, and you will be responsible for delivering a unique client experience by sharing memorable inspiring moments. You will foster trusted relationships with clients all along the journey with the Maison.

You will contribute in developing a deep understanding and knowledge of the Maison to convey its brand DNA, recognizable craftmanship and innovation in all its creations.

You will be part of a team, transmitting and sharing knowledge with the other team members, actively participating in the day-to-day boutique life.

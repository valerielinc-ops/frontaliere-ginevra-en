Description of post

Within the mechanical sector, you maintain, monitor, troubleshoot, revise, improve and ensure the efficient operation of the mechanical installations and equipment and in particular the maintenance of the tractor fleet, electrical lifting equipment, motorized machinery of gardeners, autowashers, diesel engines of emergency units, trolleys, dump trucks, pallet trucks and roadside equipment. You autonomously decide on the actions to be undertaken in accordance with the priorities defined in terms of vital or secondary parameters for the safety of patients, collaborators and heritage. You ensure preventive and corrective maintenance. You assist with periodic inspections carried out by accredited bodies and conduct regular check tours and surveys. You follow the condition of the facilities and support the alarms of the monitoring systems (technical management of the building). You keep the working documents up to date, you carry out the hardware resupply orders. You are actively involved in the development of procedures based on technical developments. You act as a technical advisor to users on mechanical equipment and monitor and monitor the work performed by outside companies. You like to find solutions to complex technical problems and you also thrive in performing simpler and more routine tasks. You are self-sustaining and also appreciate teamwork while respecting an established schedule. You ensure urgent interventions on request from the control centre and during the picket service.

Qualifications

You have a mechanic's or mechanic's CFC in Auto maintenance or diploma deemed equivalent with at least 5 years of professional experience in the maintenance and troubleshooting of complex facilities and handling equipment. You have very good theoretical and practical knowledge and are comfortable in the repair and maintenance of speed variators, mechanical and electromechanical equipment, welding... etc. You master perfectly the spoken and written French in a clear and concise way. You have a good knowledge of the usual office software (Microsoft Office) as well as the use of GMAO software. Your keen sense of customer service, team spirit and openness to change allow you to operate smoothly and integrate quickly. Thanks to your calm, laid and discreet personality, you can easily establish a relationship of trust with your interlocutors, you can cooperate and collaborate to help colleagues while being sensitive to the patient's presence and hospital constraints.

Further information

You have a regular schedule, while being available off schedule including night, weekend and holidays for picket service according to turns and urgent interventions.

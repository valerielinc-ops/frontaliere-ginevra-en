Reference code: JR133844

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

In the world of the House, our collaborators from all walks of life share with passion a common goal: to achieve a perfection that we are proud of and recognized for. We consider everyone unique, and every moment of our journey within the House is an exceptional experience.

Discover new challenges and be part of this adventure where excellence, belonging, exploration and accomplishment inspire us.

You are attracted to movement development and team management, this opportunity can meet your aspirations!

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

Attached to the Technical Directorate, you supervise a team of project leaders and supervise the entire development cycle of our movements, from the upstream scoping phase to the monitoring of production in series life.

Your mission is to ensure that quality, time and cost standards are met while promoting technical innovation.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

Upstream, Feasibility and Innovation

  • Collaborate with Creation, Marketing and Technical Bureau and Innovation teams to identify the needs and opportunities for developing new calibres.
  • Translate aesthetic and functional concepts into technical specifications and specifications.
  • Pilot technical and economic feasibility studies to secure project initiation decisions.

Development management and risk management

  • Framework and structure the activity of the Project Leaders team.
  • Ensure rigorous application of project management standards (jalons, deliverables, budget management).
  • Proactively identify project drifts (time, costs, quality), define associated remediation plans and pilot the climbing process with management if necessary.
  • Supervise the prototyping, testing and certification phases to ensure the technical reliability of the calibres in accordance with the requirements of the Poinçon de Genève.

Industrialization, serial life and partner relations

  • Ensure smooth transfer of projects to industrial and production teams.
  • Pilot the optimization of serial life products (continuous improvement of reliability, reduction of cost, resolution of obsolescences).

QUALITY FOR EXCELLING

Success in this role depends on

  • A Diploma of Engineer or Higher University Education (Master, Doctorate) with specialization in Mechanical, Physics or Microtechnics.
  • A proven and successful experience with a strong link in the development of movements or watch components.
  • Your excellent knowledge of the network of watch suppliers and subcontractors, allowing you to select and challenge the best technical partners.
  • Your proven mastery of project management methodologies and risk management.
  • Your excellent capacity for synthesis and outreach, allowing you to explain complex technical issues to non-technical interlocutors (Marketing, Management).
  • Your rigor and structure: with natural leadership to animate and unite a team.
  • Your passion for watchmaking: you demonstrate a natural curiosity and a strong interest in technological and competitive watchmaking in order to follow closely the evolution of the market.

This post is located on our Brassus site. Regular trips are planned on our site of Plan-les-Ouates (1 to 2 times a week).

Vacheron Constantin celebrated his 270th anniversary as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a global network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

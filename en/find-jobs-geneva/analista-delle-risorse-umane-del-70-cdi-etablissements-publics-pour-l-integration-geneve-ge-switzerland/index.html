Full description

HR Pilotage and Analysis

  • Analyze the needs of PPE directorates and executives in terms of HR indicators and HR steering tools;
  • Design, produce and develop scoreboards to monitor key HR issues and support the management of their activities, including absenteeism, staffing, departures, evaluation interviews and training;
  • Ensure the reliability, consistency and readability of the data and indicators produced;
  • Analyze results, identify trends, deviations and significant elements and explain trends;
  • Identify points of vigilance, make recommendations and propose options for action;
  • Contribute to the production of institutional and annual activity reports related to HR data.

Governance and Data Quality HR

  • Structure HR databases with a view to simplification, readability and reliability;
  • Establish consistency checks;
  • Ensure the accuracy of items entered into our HR software to enable data to be used.

Institutional reporting

  • Produce the statistics requested by the Department of Social Cohesion, the Geneva State Staff Office, the Federal Statistical Office, our auditors or any other partner, for HR topics involving processing and analysis of HR data;
  • Work closely with the Management Control Team to ensure consistency of data, indicators and institutional analysis;
  • Elaborate clear and structured documents (e.g. powerpoint, dashboards) with the ability to adapt their content and level of detail to the different recipients.

HR Application Functional Administrator

  • Provide a reference role for HR applications, be a driver in terms of digitalization and automation of HR data;
  • Supervision of the Mobatime time management software for all employees (creation of work schedules, user profiles, absences, queries and statistics);
  • Update the organizational chart and manage HR software access rights.

Training and experience

  • University-level bachelor's training relevant to the post, including human resources, economics, management, statistics, management information technology or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience of at least 3 years in HR data analysis, HR reporting or HR management control
  • Experience in operating a HRIS and time management software

Technical expertise

  • Mastery of Excel and tools for data processing, control and visualisation
  • Ability to construct relevant indicators and document their calculation rules
  • Very good analytical, synthesis and writing skills
  • Ability to interpret and make data available to non-specialist audiences
  • Good command of office automation and HR information systems

Personal Skills and Other Knowledge

  • Rigueur, method and sense of data quality
  • Critical spirit, ability to identify inconsistencies and propose pragmatic solutions
  • Autonomy, focus and ability to manage multiple demands
  • Relationship strength and ability to collaborate with stakeholders with diverse needs
  • Service orientation, listening and the ability to support users in the appropriation of tools
  • Interest in the social mission and integration of PPE
  • Knowledge of the legal framework applicable to the staff of the cantonal administration of Geneva or ability to appropriate it quickly
  • Knowledge of HR data protection, confidentiality and security issues

PPE offer

  • A diverse function with responsibilities within a public institution with a social purpose, pursuing ambitious and innovative objectives
  • A motivating and dynamic framework for leadership and professional development
  • A diversified continuing training process
  • Public Service Working Conditions (PACA/CARP).

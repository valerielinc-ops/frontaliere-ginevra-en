Role overview

Are you passionate about cloud infrastructure and curious about how large-scale services are operated and evolved?

Join CERN's IT Cloud team and help run and develop the OpenStack-based cloud infrastructure that underpins most CERN and LHC experiment services.

In this role, you will work on the systems and software that provide compute, storage, networking and automation capabilities to thousands of users across the organisation.

Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • Run and help evolve CERN IT's OpenStack-based cloud infrastructure.
  • Contribute to the operation and development of cloud services, including compute, storage, networking and related infrastructure components.
  • Develop and maintain automation and tooling using Python and configuration management systems.
  • Work closely with other IT teams and user communities to design and integrate new capabilities and services.
  • Contribute bug fixes and improvements upstream, leveraging CERN's strong links with the OpenStack open-source community.
  • Participate in troubleshooting and performance optimisation of large-scale distributed systems.
  • Further enhance the service's automation to ensure scalability and manageability.

Full description

To support the Programming activity, the Manufacture d'Horlogerie Audemars Piguet SA, Meyrin branch, is looking for a Programmer-Regulator.

In this context, your missions will be as follows

  • Programming our components in autonomy on Alphacam (freshing and shooting)
  • Ensure support with the adjusters to ensure the set-up (Willemin and Bumotec machines)
  • Establish manufacturing records and programs for various programming clients (terminations, boxes, bracelets)
  • Holder of a CFC in micromechanical, polymechanical or equivalent training, you benefit from 5 years of experience in a similar position.
  • You master Alphacam, Alphadoc and NC simul manufacturing software, and you are familiar with FANUC commands.
  • You also have strong skills in simultaneous 5-axis machining, and you are able to adjust Bumotech and Willemin machines (bibroches).
  • Your knowledge of watchmaking and post-processor components is an asset.
  • Equipped with internecine, you are recognized for your ability to work in collaboration with various services. You are autonomous, rigorous and versatile. As a proposal force, you report regularly to your hierarchy and give feedback in a clear and constructive way.

What we offer

  • Insurance coverage - disease (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities as well as subscriptions encouraging soft mobility (e.g. SBB)
  • Possibility of working remotely from several sites (GE/VD/NE)
  • An LPP with favourable rates

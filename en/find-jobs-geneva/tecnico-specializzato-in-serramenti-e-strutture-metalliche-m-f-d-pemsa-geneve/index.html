So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Technician specializing in locks and metal structures (M/F/D)

Your assignment

  • Perform industrial metal carpentry works on the different equipment and plants of the site.
  • Welding works, choosing the most suitable techniques for metals and operations to be carried out.
  • Intervention on metal structures and lamyeristic elements in maintenance, repair or modification operations.
  • Read and interpret the drawings, the drawings of detail and the isometric drawings necessary for the preparation and realization of the interventions.
  • Participate in pressure equipment interventions, in compliance with the applicable requirements and regulations.
  • Ensure technical monitoring of work entrusted to external enterprises.
  • Coordinate and guide subcontractors during their interventions on the plants concerned.
  • Ensure compliance with quality and safety requirements while performing the work.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as a metallurgical worker or training considered equivalent.
  • At least 5 years of experience in a similar job in industry.
  • Solid knowledge in the field of metal carpentry and metal construction.
  • Excellent knowledge of the different welding techniques and metal materials.
  • Experience in the works of metal structures and lamyeristics.
  • Excellent ability to read and interpret overall, detail and isometric designs.
  • Knowledge of the requirements and regulations applicable to pressure equipment.
  • Experience in the supervision and coordination of suppliers and subcontractors.
  • Ability to work independently and effectively organize their own interventions.
  • Rigore, sense of responsibility and strict compliance with procedures and safety standards.

This is a long-term temporary assignment, starting from the last quarter of 2026. Retribution based on the company’s profile and salary table.

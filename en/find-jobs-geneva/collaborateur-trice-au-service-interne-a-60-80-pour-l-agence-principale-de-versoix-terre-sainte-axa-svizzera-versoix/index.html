60-80%, Versoix

Shape your future with us!

Have you ever been able to apply your skills in service and business support? You're a customer-oriented person and you have a strong commitment to your work?

Are you comfortable with the phone contact? the objective of contributing to the success of the Agency by setting appointments to our advisors enthusiastically?

Our Versoix agency wants to strengthen its internal service team. If you recognize yourself in this description and your interest in joining us is real, we would be delighted to receive your file!

Are you a professional insurance professional or from another horizon? Start your career in one of Axa's agencies, the insurance leader. We are the trusted partner of our clients, in all situations of life. You too?

What's waiting for you

  • Advice and assistance to clients, mainly by telephone or reception upon arrival at the agency
  • Telephone contact with clients to obtain and set appointments for advisors
  • Preparation and follow-up of meetings with clients, support to teams of advisors in their daily tasks, correspondence and general administration
  • The drafting of offers and insurance proposals
  • Close and permanent contact with the Agency's teams of advisers
  • Frequent exchanges with the various departments of the regional headquarters of D'AXA

What you bring

  • Business training and first experience in administration, knowledge of the insurance sector are a real advantage
  • A strong interest in advice and sales, a strong motivation to train you on our products and internal processes
  • A service oriented personality and the desire to always find the best equitable solution for all
  • Positive and dynamic temperament, reliability, flexibility and ease of contact with others and excellent teamwork
  • An excellent mastery of French in both spoken and written language, an ease in English
  • A place to live in our area of activity and a good knowledge of the local economic fabric
  • Easiness with common computer systems and learning new digital tools

What we offer you We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to fully develop and exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-tariff subscription/participation in general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities

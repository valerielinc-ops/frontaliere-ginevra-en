Full description

Within the Secretariat of the Policlinic d,oncologie, you are a specialist medical or medical secretary and actively contribute, on rotation with the team, to the coordination of the outpatient care path for patients. In close collaboration with oncologists, nursing teams and internal and external partners, you contribute to the smooth running of the care and the fluidity of the patient path. You ensure the reception, information and orientation of the patient and their relatives, as well as the admissions and recording of administrative data according to institutional requirements. You organize and coordinate simple and complex appointments, including medical consultations, complementary examinations (blood tests, radiological examinations or other) and oncological treatments. You guarantee strict monitoring of medical and health care agendas, respecting the therapeutic constraints and the specific requirements of the various pathologies. You also manage internal/external phone calls as well as the joint courier of the secretariat. You ensure the typing, correction and layout of medical reports, as well as their billing. You take charge of managing incoming and outgoing mail, its filing and archiving, as well as digitizing and updating computer patient files to ensure that they are complete before consultations. With or with reactivity, rigour and a developed relational sense, you are evolving in a specialized medical environment where l, listening, discretion and empathy are essential. Your team spirit and collaboration with multidisciplinary teams directly contribute to the quality of care and support of patients throughout their oncology journey.

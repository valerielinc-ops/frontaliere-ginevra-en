Full description

Reference code: JR130552 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Professions from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striking to continuously rich our Maison

Your Mission

As an Operational Analyst, you will be an essential pillar for optimizing our processes, the reliability of our operations and the development of our teams. You will actively contribute to the success of the department by identifying opportunities for improvement and implementing innovative solutions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Analyze logistics flows (reception, storage, preparation, shipping, production), reduce costs and improve service quality. You will be actively involved in implementing these improvements and leading change.
  • Define and optimize daily organization, manage workload, supervise training and updating procedures. You will supervise newcomers and contribute to the versatility plan. You will also act as a key support and backup for the manager.
  • Ensure rigorous monitoring of key performance indicators (KPI), ensure interdepartmental collaboration and act as a trade reference and Key User on your scope of expertise.
  • Coordinate the preparation and execution of inventories and destruction processes, ensuring regulatory compliance.

Your Profile

  • Strong understanding of operational flows and knowledge of the principles of continuous improvement (Lean, Six Sigma).
  • Mastery of computer tools and information systems (SAP ERP), as well as advanced data analysis tools (e.g. Excel).
  • Proven analytical abilities, excellent communication and collaboration skills, proactivity, initiative, strength of proposal, rigour and organizational sense.
  • Project management knowledge is an asset.

Your Journey

  • Diploma in Supply Chain or equivalent.
  • Minimum 3 years of significant experience in a similar role, with exposure to process optimization, project management and team support.

Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Founded in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, La Tour Hospital places continuous improvement, medical excellence and the support of its staff at the heart of its priorities. The institution employs more than 1,200 employees, representing more than 60 professions, and collaborates with more than 550 registered doctors.

For the Human Resources Directorate, we are recruiting a

80% Training Officer (ref: 2015)

Maximum term contract – maternity leave replacement

Entry into office: early December 2026 – End of mission: late August 2027

Linked to HR Development and Expertise, you are the driving force behind the institution's skills development policy. You deploy and follow the training plan, as close as possible to the needs of the field, and contribute to the development of the skills of more than 1,200 employees, in a demanding, regulated and deeply humane environment.

An ideal position for a person who likes to reconcile organization and proximity with teams, and who wants to invest fully on a full and autonomous mission of 9 months.

Your Mission

As such, you will be required to

  • Deploy the annual training plan;
  • Collect and analyse training needs with managers and employees, and identify skills to develop in relation to strategic objectives;
  • Design, organize and implement appropriate training devices (face-to-face, digital, blended);
  • Organize and participate actively in reception days;
  • Administer the LMS and participate in the design of internal modules;
  • Coordinate and monitor external providers (if necessary) and schedule sessions related to the teams involved;
  • Manage the entire administrative process of training (registrations, convocations, attendance sheets, certificates and certificates);
  • Follow the training budget by optimising costs (validation of invoices, follow-up files, follow-up of fee notes) and following the training fees;
  • Measure the effectiveness of training through indicators (participation, satisfaction, competence building) and propose corrective or optimisation actions;
  • Ensure communication around training arrangements;
  • Answer employees' questions on training issues;
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders (managers, HRBP, schools, training bodies);

Your profile

We are looking for a person with 5 to 10 years of experience in training management, ideally in companies, supplemented by:

  • Higher education (federal patent, Bachelor or Master's degree) in human resources, training management or equivalent;
  • Good control of vocational training arrangements and regulations;
  • An ease with training management tools (LMS, HR software, excel mastery);
  • Strong fiscal management capacities;
  • A true sense of organization;
  • An excellent sense of relationship, listening and communication;
  • A spirit of synthesis and the ability to report clearly;
  • Self-reliance, a sense of initiative and guidance solutions, which are essential in the context of a replacement;
  • An excellent mastery of French;
  • Experience in the health field or in a highly regulated environment is a plus.

We offer you

The opportunity to join a recognised, human-sized institution in a complete and autonomous role, with direct contact with managers, teams and operational issues. You will integrate a committed HR team into a dynamic environment where collaboration, continuous improvement and a sense of service are essential.

  • Type of contract: maximum term contract (replacement of maternity leave)
  • Activity rate: 80%
  • Start date: early December 2026
  • Duration: until 31 August 2027
  • Workplace: Meyrin, Geneva

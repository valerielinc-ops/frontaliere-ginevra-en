Responsable de rayon boulangerie - convenience (F/H/D) — Société coopérative Migros Genève

Full description

Get in charge! Manages with passion and innovation one of our bakery areas and convenience. Give our customers a unique experience in our Eaux-Vives store: quality, freshness and proximity. Your curiosity and your sense of detail will make a difference. Join us to meet this challenge!

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, MM Eaux-Vives, Rue des Eaux-Vives 17-19, 1207 Geneva

Important remarks

The use of women aims for simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You receive and check the arrivals to ensure the quality and freshness of the products.
  • You ensure that the products are beamed and rotated for optimal presentation.
  • You analyze the performance of the rays and propose strategies for improvement.
  • You manage orders, inventory and inventory to maintain a smooth supply.
  • You ensure cleanliness and comply with HACCP standards and safety standards.
  • You help cook bakery products and guarantee the freshness of your products.

Required

  • 3 years minimum experience: in the retail sector
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail trade or equivalent title.
  • Rigor, dynamism, versatility, autonomy and strong desire to work as a team.
  • Excellent mastery and interpretation of the numbers to improve the performance of the rays.
  • Expertise of the field, strong proposal and excellent customer orientation.
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Michaël Lassère Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we conduct an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Individual video or telephone interview (FFB-Group): When the number of candidates is large, we may arrange an individual telephone or video interview.
  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100%

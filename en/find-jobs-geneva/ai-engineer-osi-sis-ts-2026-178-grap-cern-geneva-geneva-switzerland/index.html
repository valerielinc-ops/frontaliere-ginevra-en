Full description

Join CERN's Scientific Information Service and contribute to the evolution of INSPIRE, a key information platform serving the global high energy physics community. INSPIRE provides access to scientific literature, research data, author profiles and other scholarly information, supporting researchers in discovering, exploring and connecting scientific knowledge. As an AI Engineer, you will explore and develop AI capabilities to enhance scientific information discovery, access and curation within INSPIRE. Your work will focus on evaluating and improving existing AI prototypes, investigating retrieval augmented generation (RAG), semantic search and agentic approaches, and exploring how emerging technologies can support researchers and information specialists. You will also contribute to the development and evaluation of AI integration capabilities, including the Model Context Protocol (MCP), enabling AI assistants and other applications to interact with INSPIRE's scientific information and services. Working closely with software engineers, platform engineer and domain experts, you will design prototypes, establish evaluation methodologies and assess the feasibility of integrating promising solutions into INSPIRE, considering performance, reliability and CERN's computing infrastructure.

Your responsibilities

  • Evaluate existing INSPIRE AI prototypes and explore approaches such as LLMs, RAG, semantic search and agentic workflows to improve scientific information discovery and curation.
  • Develop and assess AI integrations, including MCP services, enabling AI assistants to interact with INSPIRE's data and services.
  • Design and implement evaluation methods to assess accuracy, relevance, reliability, performance and resource requirements of AI solutions.
  • Investigate deployment options within CERN's infrastructure, integrate and release validated AI features into production, and monitor their performance and reliability.

Your profile

  • Practical experience developing AI or machine learning applications through professional, research or substantial project work.
  • Experience implementing solutions using modern AI technologies, such as LLMs, embeddings, retrieval systems or agentic approaches.
  • Experience designing experiments, evaluating model outputs and comparing technical approaches.
  • Experience developing Python applications and integrating APIs or external services.
  • Experience deploying AI applications or integrating them into production systems.

Skills

  • Strong Python programming and software engineering skills, including API development, testing and integration.
  • Hands-on experience with AI/ML frameworks and modern AI technologies, such as LLMs, embeddings, RAG and agentic workflows.
  • Ability to design, develop and evaluate AI solutions, including benchmarking, quality assessment and performance optimisation.
  • Experience integrating AI models and services into existing applications.
  • Knowledge of containerisation (Docker), deployment practices and CI/CD workflows.
  • Familiarity with MCP, model serving, Kubernetes or cloud infrastructure would be an asset.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently, investigate emerging technologies and collaborate with multidisciplinary teams.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Computer Science (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.

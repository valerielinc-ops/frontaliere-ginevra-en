# ICT Network Engineer

Your future starts here

Within the Network Operations team, you support and operate our customer's networks and systems. You solve incidents, participate in projects and develop infrastructure. In parallel, you share your knowledge and contribute to clear documentation. You enjoy autonomy, collaboration and a service-oriented environment.

  • Level two support on local (LAN), extended (WAN) and security systems (Firewall)
  • Incident analysis and resolution, problem management
  • Participation in the implementation of network and security solutions and architectures, as well as infrastructure projects with agile methods
  • Point picket service
  • Contribution to the creation and updating of technical documentation and resolution articles

Your skills

  • Minimum two years of experience in a similar function
  • Diploma (EPF/Uni/HES/HF) in computer science or equivalent
  • ITIL Foundation, network certifications appreciated like Cisco, Azure or automation
  • Mastering the configuration of Cisco switches and routers and good knowledge of network and security concepts (routing, switching, Wi-Fi, DNS/DHCP, NAC, micro-segmentation, PKI, reverse proxy, latest generation firewall), Kubernetes, comfortable on Windows and Linux
  • Service sense, clear communication and teamwork
  • Autonomy, rigor and ability to prioritize
  • Very good French and English B2 level at least, English appreciated with a very good level of French

We offer the possibility to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or at your home. You will use agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible work schedules tailored to your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you have attractive advantages that will enrich your professional experience. These include a pleasant work environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for career development. Discover your advantages.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.

Full description

Introduction

For our Information Systems Division , we are looking for a PIM - Akeneo (H/F) Functional Manager whose main mission will be to pilot and evolve the Akeneo PIM tools, in interaction with ERP, MDM and PLM systems, to contribute to the reliability, consistency and performance of the data management produced within our organization.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in the different phases of PIM projects: framing, functional design, recipe, deployment and post-go-live support
  • Conduct business workshops, collect user needs, challenge applications and formalize them in the form of functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Contribute to the modelling of data produced in Akeneo PIM: families, attributes, categories, channels, local, enrichment rules and workflows
  • Define, run and document functional tests prior to delivery of solutions to end users
  • Contribute, in collaboration with QA or technical teams, to the automation of non-regression tests where relevant
  • Coordinate the activities of resources for development, platform maintenance, integration and data migration
  • Provide Level 2 functional support to users
  • Monitor incidents, anomalies and requests for evolution with editors, integrators and internal teams until resolved
  • Contributing to continuous improvement in the quality of data produced and enrichment processes
  • Participate in functional documentation and user support.

Profile

  • University training, HES engineering school, EPFL or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed on a similar function, ideally in a PIM context, product repository, e-commerce or data management
  • Strong functional administration experience of Akeneo PIM: data modelling, family management, attributes, channels, imports/exports, user rights and enrichment workflows
  • Good understanding of flows between PIM, ERP, MDM, PLM, DAM and related platforms
  • Mastery of project management methods, including Waterfall and Agile
  • Ability to write clear and structured functional specifications
  • Experience in functional recipe, integration tests and abnormal monitoring
  • Good ability to interact with business contacts, IT, publishers and integrators
  • Rigor, organizational sense and analytical and synthesis capacity, combined with a good team spirit and sense of service
  • Autonomous and endowed with good oral and written communication, you know how to prioritize your actions and challenge needs with a constructive approach.

Conclusion

We offer a stimulating environment, attractive working conditions, flexibility schemes and top social benefits.

Please send a complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, certificates).

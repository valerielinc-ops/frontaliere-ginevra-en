Role overview

Introduction

You will help design, integrate, and operate complex test and measurement systems in cryogenic, electrical, and controls environments.

Your work will support the characterisation and validation of superconducting magnets and related components under demanding protection, synchronisation, and reliability requirements.

  • Introduction
  • You will help design, integrate, and operate complex test and measurement systems in cryogenic, electrical, and controls environments.

Application process

  • Develop, maintain and integrate applications in C++, Python, LabVIEW and CERN controls technologies, including FESA/CMW equipment control interfaces for front-end and test-bench systems.
  • Integrate and support electronics, instrumentation, timing, interlocks, protection and data acquisition hardware on Linux-based, real-time and front-end platforms.
  • Operate, monitor, debug and improve complex superconducting magnet test systems, including cryogenic measurements, high-current/high-voltage tests, fast transients, quench detection and safe data acquisition.
  • Collaborate with CERN teams responsible for magnets, powering, protection, cryogenics, electronics and controls; document and present results, and promote good software engineering practices, including GitLab, code reviews, automated testing and clear documentation.
  • Master's degree or PhD or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Computer Science,
  • Software Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, Applied Physics or a related field. Experience:
  • Proven experience in C++ and Python software design and development for data acquisition, automation, testing, analysis or controls applications.
  • Experience developing software for hardware control and data acquisition systems, preferably on Linux-based, real-time or front-end platforms.

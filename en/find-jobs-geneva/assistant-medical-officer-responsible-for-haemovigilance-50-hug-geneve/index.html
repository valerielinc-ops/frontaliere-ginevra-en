Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 180 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Medical and Quality Directorate's mission is to ensure the quality, safety and excellence of the medical services provided to HUG patients. In this context, it ensures compliance with regulatory requirements and supports the development of institutional approaches that promote quality and safety of care. In order to strengthen the institutional system of haemovigilance, the Medical Directorate is looking for an advanced clinic chief or deputy or assistant doctor as head of institutional haemovigilance.

Description of post

Attached or attached to the Medical Directorate, you are the person responsible for haemovigilance (PRHv) in accordance with the requirements of Swiss legislation on therapeutic products and Swissmedic guidelines. As such, you guarantee compliance with the legal obligations of vigilance, declaration and transfusion safety within the HUGs. You are responsible for the institutional system of hemovigilance and ensure its compliance with regulatory requirements. You supervise the reporting, monitoring and analysis of transfusion reactions, incidents, adverse events and near miss situations. You conduct the necessary investigations and contribute to the implementation of corrective and preventive actions to enhance patient safety. You ensure the continuous management and improvement of hemovigilance processes, including traceability, data archiving, declaration validation and revision of institutional protocols. You also ensure strict compliance with the statutory reporting deadlines with the competent authorities. In close collaboration with the Blood Transfusion Centre, laboratories, prescribers and departmental quality officers, you develop a culture of transfusion safety and help improve professional practices. You also play an important role in education and training. You are involved in pre- and post-graduate training, the integration of new physicians, as well as in raising awareness of good transfusion practices among caregivers. Finally, you contribute to research activities, quality audits, practice reviews and the preparation of the annual hemovigilance report for the Medical Directorate. You also participate in various working groups and institutional strategic projects.

Qualifications

You have a federal or recognized medical degree as well as an FMH in haematology, internal medicine or anesthesia. A FAMH qualification in haematology is an asset. You have a solid hospital experience and experience in the areas of quality, patient safety and/or haemovigilance. You control the regulatory framework for labile blood products and vigilance activities. You are recognized or recognized for your rigour, sense of responsibility and ability to evolve in a complex environment involving many internal and external partners. Your mindset allows you to assess risks, interpret vigilance data and propose appropriate improvement measures. With or with excellent relational and communication skills, you can bring together different actors around the issues of quality and transfusion safety. You appreciate interdisciplinary work and demonstrate autonomy, pedagogy and organizational sense. A strong interest in training, applied research and continuous improvement of professional practices completes your profile.

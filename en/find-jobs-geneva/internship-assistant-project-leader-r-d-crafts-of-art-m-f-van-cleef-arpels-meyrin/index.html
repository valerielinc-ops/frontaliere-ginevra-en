Who are we?

A High Jewelry Home with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that diverse perspectives foster creativity and knowledge to deliver excellence.

Location : Meyrin, Switzerland

Start of internship : March 2027

Duration: 6 months - full time

Do you match the profile?

Are you a student in engineering school and have an aptitude for mechanics, materials or industrial engineering?

Are you dynamic, rigorous and responsible?

Autonomous, do you know how to prioritize your tasks and work in project mode?

Are you curious and interested in new projects?

You have a good relationship and enjoy team spirit?

Do you speak English and master CAD tools?

What do we want from you?

As part of the development and industrialization of new product development processes, you will assist the Chief R&D Group in its day-to-day missions.

In particular, you will be responsible for carrying out in-house testing plans (manipulations, experiments) in close connection with the workshops (crafts of art).

In addition, you will analyse and formalize the results of the experiments in a readable and succinct manner.

You will support the need for production and development tools through the construction of 3D technical solutions (CAO).

You will follow up with supplier applications.

You will also have the opportunity to collaborate in deploying methods that promote the artistic expression of our artisans.

In order to facilitate your integration, we will offer you a training course in the history and values of the House within the School of Joailliers Arts.

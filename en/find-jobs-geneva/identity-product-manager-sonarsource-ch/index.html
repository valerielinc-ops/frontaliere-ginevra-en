Who is Sonar?

Sonar enables organizations to ship agentic code with confidence. Code creation is abundant, but confidence is scarce. Our core platform, SonarQube, verifies over 750 billion lines of code every day, delivering agentic verification and governance for all code. Trusted by more than 7 million developers and 75% of the Fortune 100, including teams at Nvidia, ServiceNow, Booking.com , Goldman Sachs, AstraZeneca, and Ford Motor Company, Sonar enables organizations to reduce outages, improve security, and lower the cost and risk of agentic software development.

Founded in Geneva and now headquartered in Austin, Texas, Sonar has a global team working across offices and hubs, including Silicon Valley, London, Bochum, Barcelona, Singapore, Tokyo, and the Washington, D.C. area. At Sonar, we’re enabling enterprises to build agentic software factories that can move quickly while maintaining the control, consistency and evidence required to ship with confidence.

Position description

Sonar is looking for a customer-centric Identity Product Manager to drive the authentication, authorization, and access management capabilities that let every user, from an individual developer to the largest regulated enterprise, get into Sonar products securely and without friction. You will work with the Identity squad, in the Storefront domain, which provides the operational foundation enabling seamless customer access to Sonar's products, underpinning core business processes such as billing and entitlement.

This role sits at the intersection of customer experience, security, and compliance. As a Product Manager, you will shape how organizations authenticate into Sonar (SSO via SAML/OIDC, with enterprise identity providers such as Okta and Entra ID), how permissions and roles are managed at scale, and how user and organization identities are provisioned and kept secure throughout their lifecycle. To achieve this, we need a passionate Product Manager who will research, clarify, and prioritize the needs of external IT administrators, security teams, and enterprise buyers, and translate those needs across our product line.

You will join the Product Management team, composed of other passionate Product Managers eager to collaborate with you. You will also work closely with the Identity engineering teams to ensure that proposed solutions meet the expected outcome, and that we collectively deliver an identity experience that is both secure by default and simple for administrators to operate at scale.

By joining us, you will bring your deep knowledge of identity and access management (SSO, IdP integrations, RBAC/permissions, user provisioning) and your experience as a Product Manager to help us reach the next level and provide a trusted, frictionless experience to millions of developers and thousands of enterprise administrators.

What you will do • Contribute to the different areas of the Identity vision and communicate about them, internally and externally. • Research, clarify, and prioritize opportunities to build and maintain an effective roadmap, focused on enterprise-grade authentication, authorization, and access management. • Interact directly with our users and customers to support them, integrate their feedback, and identify opportunities. • Develop a deep understanding of enterprise identity needs (e.g., SSO/SAML/OIDC, IdP integrations such as Okta and Entra ID, SCIM/user provisioning, and fine-grained permissions) and how we can enable organizations to onboard and administer users at scale. • Drive modernization of enterprise identity and access management, including expanding IdP support and hardening permissions and role management for the largest, most complex organizations. • Work closely with other product managers to design cross-product features. • Align with the engineering and UX teams on the problems to solve and support them during the end-to-end development stage of the solutions. • Sync with the engineering teams to ensure that there is a good balance between innovation, consolidation, and stability. • Attend conferences and give talks if you like. • Help the Product Management team grow in its organization, tools, and processes.

Experience and qualifications • Passionate about identity and access management, and the practices that lead to seamless and secure authentication systems. • Deep understanding of identity protocols and patterns (e.g., SAML, OIDC/OAuth2, SSO, SCIM/provisioning, JWT) and enterprise identity providers (Okta, Entra ID/Azure AD, or similar). • Technical comprehension of permissions and access control models (RBAC/ABAC) and multi-tenant SaaS access control. • Understanding of the compliance and security expectations of enterprise buyers (e.g., SOC 2) as they relate to identity and access. • Experience with the latest developments in AI assisted coding. • Experience in software product management, preferably managing identity in an agile context. • Highly autonomous and pragmatic, with an obsession for solving external customer problems. • Open-minded and very positive can-do attitude. • Comfortable in dealing with change, complexity, and uncertainty. • Self-confident enough to challenge the status quo as well as to receive feedback. • Leadership: you take initiative and demonstrate your ability to lead a subject from beginning to end.

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) is a national and international reference institution. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Human Resources Directorate's main mission is to ensure the development of the human capital of the institution in view of organizational evolution. She constantly seeks to innovate, challenge and improve her practices while basing her strategy on the positive experience of collaborators. The HR policy of the HUGs is based on the development of skills and career paths in line with institutional needs and focuses on the consideration of the person, work/life balance and occupational health and safety. The Centre de développement des compétences des Hôpitaux universitaires de Genève is looking for a person or persons in charge of training to join the continuing training team. As part of the Human Resources Directorate's strategy, this sector supports trades managers and departments in their continuing training activities, and contributes to the development of the training offer in order to meet the needs of the institution and the various vocational sectors.

Description of post

As part of your function, you have the responsibility to analyse the needs and requests for training from applicants at different levels of the institution, to advise them and to accompany them in the implementation of appropriate training actions. Your professional experience in adult education and your knowledge of its concepts allow you to position yourself as a key actor or actress and influencing your various interlocutors (managers, human resources managers, etc.). You are an agent of change and innovation. You have the strength to propose and mobilize your ability to understand the requirements of the trades in order to meet the training needs of the institution. You are an actor or actor in the transition of continuous training devices in the hybrid learning age, in a logic of collective intelligence. You also have to ensure a professional watch, in partnership with trades and other HR management departments, in order to anticipate the development of tomorrow's skills and to ensure that the training implemented meets these new needs. As part of the ongoing organizational transformation of the Training and Development Centre, you do not hesitate to make proposals for improvement. In regular contact with the different members of the teaching team and your administrative colleagues, you promote collaborative work and teamwork, emulation and a dynamic of innovation. You are working alongside them to support the transformation of the Training and Development Centre and particularly the continuing training sector.

Qualifications

You master the concepts, methods and teaching methods of reference. To the benefit of a university master's degree ideally in the field of adult training supplemented by a post-graduate training of the CAS type in the field of adult training, accompaniment to change, HR, skills development or coaching, you have a professional experience of at least 5 years in a similar position. An experience in the health and/or social sector would be an asset to facilitate your success in this mission at the service of HUG and partner institutions.

Experience in a similar role or/and as a consultant in training or/and coaching and in accompanying change are also important assets.

You have a methodical and structured approach to managing your projects, allowing you to coordinate, prioritize activities, manage internal and/or external resources, while responding to budgetary aspects. This is done in a spirit of continuous improvement, serving the needs and strategic issues of your clients, clients and partners.

This position requires excellent oral and written communication skills in order to be able to complete the various negotiations and identify the expectations of the actors. Thanks to your customer orientation, analysis and synthesis capabilities, you are able to provide the advice expected by the various stakeholders. Excellent relational skills, adaptability and team spirit are required as well as great rigour and availability.

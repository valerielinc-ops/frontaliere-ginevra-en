Full description

Location: Geneva, Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80% - 100% Facilitate international mobility for global organisations As a (Senior) Consultant in our Global Employer Services (GES) immigration team, you will be providing immigration advisory and compliance services to our global and local clients. You will be working on different engagements and together with the team provide international organisations with leading immigration services - ultimately enabling our clients to successfully navigate international mobility. How you can make an impact

  • Together with other subject matter experts, you help our clients and their employees to guide them through the Swiss immigration process
  • You are managing and driving your own case and client portfolio and stay on top of your pending tasks – from the initial client contact, to document collection, application drafting and filing and receipt of the final approvals
  • Develop strong client relationships and have direct contact with the authorities
  • Maintenance of up-to-date client records and files in our case management tool
  • You prioritize multiple client request – immigration cases can be fast-paced and high-stakes Who We Are Looking For
  • Holder of university or university of applied sciences degree (Bachelor or Master) in law or a similar field
  • Initial experience in the field of immigration or HR/Global Mobility
  • You enjoy direct client contact, coordinating multidisciplinary projects and collaborating in international team set-ups as well as with immigration authorities
  • You are structured, resilient, and solution oriented and enjoy working in a fast-paced environment
  • You like to challenge the status quo and are interested in technology and process efficiencies
  • You communicate confidently and fluently (professional level in oral and written) in English and German or French.

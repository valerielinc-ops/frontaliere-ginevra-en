Full description

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) is a national and international reference institution. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. As part of the optimization of their structures, the HUGs set up an Executive Directorate for Operating Blocks (DBO) directly attached to the Deputy Directorate General (the Director assumed his duties on 16 March 2026). This new centralised structure is entirely dedicated to the management of operating blocks with a clear objective: to strengthen governance, interprofessional synergies, optimisation of patient care and processes, while propelling innovation and research. The operating units of the HUGs are a state-of-the-art technical platform made up of 8 modern blocks representing nearly 41 operating rooms, designed to provide optimal 24 hour support. Each year, more than 25,000 interventions are carried out by 1,500 dedicated professionals, covering all the leading surgical disciplines (gynaecology-obstetrics, ORL, maxillofacial, cardiac, vascular, visceral, neurosurgery, orthopaedics, paediatrics, etc., including robotic surgery). The context: Biomedical at the heart of the transformation of the operating room Today, the operating unit has become a technological environment of extreme complexity (surgical robotics, electrosurgery, advanced imaging, 4K/3D audiovisual integration, network interconnections, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity of medical devices). At the HUG, we are convinced that the role of this biomedical engineer is to embody a true role as an application engineer and strategic partner. A real bridge between clinical teams, surgeons, industrial and technical services, you do not just repair or do "disturbingshooting": you anticipate, advise, secure, optimize and accompany the teams in the constant technological evolution of the block and you ensure the safety and availability of the technical trays of the operating blocks on a daily basis with the technicians of the block.

Description of post

Within the new Operating Block Directorate (BOD), you play a central and cross-cutting role in the strategic, digital and operational management of biomedical equipment: As part of Strategic Governance & Planning, you must contribute to the multi-year planning of medical devices (equipment and associated consumables), maintain the inventory and manage capital assets. You also have to draw up the BOD equipment plan; support the needs of users, assess existing solutions and challenge requests in relation to institutional orientations. You are actively involved in multi-professional groups for acquisition and renewal files led by the Biomedical Service. You will coordinate tests and commissioning with the block technicians. As a referent or referent of technology & Active interface, you bring your technical and technological expertise during exchanges within the Directorate and the governance of the operating blocks. As far as digital integration, video and networks are concerned, you are involved in managing complex interfaces and interoperability between equipment, working closely with IT services on network and audiovisual integration (PACS, video feed, etc.) of the operating unit. For Operational Support & Continuous Improvement, you frame the first-level support, coordinate technical interventions, optimally plan preventive maintenance through the GMAO (in conjunction with the Biomedical Maintenance Sector and block technicians), contribute to the management of medical devices (DM/DMI), loans and associated contracts, while contributing to cross-cutting projects. For Training & Support for Change, in connection with the suppliers, you must design the training of the different equipment of the operating blocks. Organize and relay these trainings to healthcare and medical teams to optimize and secure use. For the Major Modernization & Infrastructure projects, you will intervene for the DBO, in connection with the biomedical service and the real estate management, as a consultancy to architects and project managers during the renovation and development of the blocks. You must define the needs, structure the technical programmes, anticipate the constraints of implementation and help ensure the overall coherence between infrastructure and clinical practices. With respect to Technology Watch, Security & Risk Management, you need to ensure active oversight of innovations (robotic, AI, assisted surgery). You also need to ensure regulatory compliance, anticipate obsolescence, work with the Materialovigilance Commission to secure traceability and actively participate in quality and risk mitigation (including radiation protection, in collaboration with the Medical Directorate).

Qualifications

You have a university degree in biomedical engineering (or equivalent training supplemented by a solid specialization in hospital). You have at least five years of successful experience in a hospital or clinical setting in a function similar to the heart of the interventional environments. You have excellent mastery of state-of-the-art medical technologies (especially operating units). Proven strength with IT tools, networked systems and health data architectures. You have a perfect mastery of French both orally and in writing, and good professional knowledge of English (watch, technical documentation, exchanges with industrialists). You have a pronounced curiosity for technological innovation and openness to the new paradigms of the block (IA, robotics, interoperability). You have a spirit of analysis, synthesis and strong decision-making autonomy. You have excellent communication skills, listening skills, diplomacy and pronounced customer/service orientation. You have a demonstrated ability to support change, to work as a multi-professional team, and to exercise rigour and discretion.

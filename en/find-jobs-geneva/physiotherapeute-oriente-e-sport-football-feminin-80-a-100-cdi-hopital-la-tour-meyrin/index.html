Full description

Women's sports and football therapist 80 to 100% CDI (ref: 1914) The Movement Physiotherapy Department of La Tour Hospital is supported by a team of more than 45 physiotherapists and is accredited by the Swiss Olympic Medical Center. Our organisation is based on clinical expertise, a strong evidence-based practice culture, and active engagement in research and innovation. Knowledge sharing, specialization in the service of clinical excellence and the continuous evolution of practices are an integral part of our identity. As part of the development of our medico-sports collaborations, we recruit a physiotherapist sharing his activity between the Hospital of La Tour and Servette FC Chênois Féminin, within the women's professional team. The post shall: The overall activity rate is between 80 and 100 %, divided between an employed activity at the Hospital of La Tour in CDI and a complementary activity within the Servette FC Chênois Féminin, according to contractual arrangements defined with the club. Hospital activity is carried out mainly within the technical plateau of ambulatory rehabilitation, with orientation in musculoskeletal physiotherapy. The activity within the club concerns the accompaniment of the professional women's team in the framework of a position of physiotherapist, exercised in close collaboration with the medical coordinator of the club, also member of the department of physiotherapy movement of the Hospital of La Tour. Your missions at La Tour Hospital

  • Ambulatory care for patients with musculoskeletal, orthopaedic and sports pathologies, as well as for hospitalized patients.
  • Participation in specialized support within the various areas of expertise of the department, depending on the profile and skills of the candidate.
  • Implementation of active rehabilitation, re-athletization and return to physical or sporting activity programmes.
  • Use of tools and equipment specific to the technical ambulatory rehabilitation platform.
  • Active participation in interdisciplinary clinical exchanges, case discussions and changes in service practices.
  • Integration of a practice based on scientific evidence, oriented towards the quality of care and the continuous development of care.
  • Participation, according to the profile and interests of the candidate, in the development projects and initiatives of the department. Your missions within the Féminin Chênois FC Servette
  • Player follow-up, daily pre-training care and post-training recovery.
  • Participation in the design and supervision of group and individual neuromuscular activation and development sessions in the room.
  • Reeducational care for injured players: table-based care, active rehabilitation, on-field rehabilitation and back-to-play support, based on evidence-based and criteria-based approaches.
  • Attending training sessions and managing emergency care in the field.
  • Attended some Swiss championship games as well as European competitions (UWCL/WEC).
  • Participation in early and mid-season screening and data collection, analysis and use.
  • Maintenance of medical reporting and epidemiological monitoring through the club's data centralization tools. This organisation allows to develop a practice articulated between specialised hospital environment and high-level sport, within a structured and collaborative project. Your profile
  • Swiss physiotherapist or foreign diploma recognized by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • Experience or interest in the physiotherapy of sport and/or the environment of football.
  • An experience in female football or in the management of female athlete is an asset.
  • High-level sport and field work.
  • Ability to evolve in a collaborative and interdisciplinary organization.
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and excellent communication skills.
  • Sensitivity to scientific approaches and quality of care.
  • Availability compatible with sports activity (training, games and travel according to organization). We offer you
  • An original and stimulating mixed activity between specialized hospital environment and high-level sport.
  • A physiotherapist position of 80 to 100%.
  • A distribution of the activity between the Hospital of La Tour and the Chênois Féminin Servette FC.
  • A dynamic, structured and recognized working environment.
  • A pole organization that promotes skills development and specialization.
  • Access to continuing training, clinical research and therapeutic innovation.

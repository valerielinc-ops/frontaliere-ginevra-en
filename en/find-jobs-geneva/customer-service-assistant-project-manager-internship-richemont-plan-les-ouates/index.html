Full description

Reference code: JR133788

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

Localisation: Plan-les-Ouates, Genève

MISSION

Within an international and multi-Maison environment, Richemont Customer Service (RCS) works to continuously improve Customer Service operations across its worldwide network. You will support projects aimed at improving operational efficiency, harmonizing processes and supporting our service centers worldwide.

RESPONSIBLITIES

  • Contribute to the definition and documentation of operational standards, including lean management, process optimization, 5S, HSE and compliance
  • Support the implementation of new digital solutions and operational tools
  • Help document procedures and guidelines across service centers
  • Identify opportunities to improve processes and ways of working
  • Review and enhance tools used to measure operational efficiency
  • Perform data analyses and prepare clear insights and reports for management

YOUR PROFILE

  • Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Engineering, Management or a related field
  • Strong interest in project management, operational excellence and continuous improvement
  • Strong analytical skills and ability to translate data into actionable insights
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and French
  • Good command of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint
  • Knowledge of SAP is a plus
  • Available for a 6-month full-time internship

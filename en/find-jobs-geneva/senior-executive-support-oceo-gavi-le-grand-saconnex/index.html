Position title: Senior Executive Support, Office of the CEO Contract type / duration: 5-year fixed-term contract duration Location: Geneva Department: Office of the CEO Team: Office of the CEO Reports To: Chief of Staff N° of positions supervised (if applicable): 0 Career Step: 3 1. About the Role The Senior Executive Support, OCEO plays a pivotal role within the Office of the CEO. The role works closely with C-suite leaders and their teams across Gavi. Its primary purpose is to manage the CEO’s daily cadence, ensuring tangible alignment across teams to ensure coherence and execution of a cohesive and strategic leadership agenda supporting the delivery of shared priorities. It combines a strong service focused mindset with hands-on execution capability, enabling not only what needs to be delivered, but how it is delivered with pace, discipline and impact. The role provides dedicated day-to-day executive, administrative, logistical, and personal support to the CEO and is responsible for ensuring the smooth daily management of the CEO’s schedule, correspondence, files, logistics, devices, expenses, hosting arrangements, and related support needs. Reporting to the Chief of Staff and working in close coordination with the Office of the CEO team, the role helps ensure that the CEO is well-prepared, responsive, and supported across daily priorities, while contributing to effective information sharing and a strong team approach across the Office and cross functional initiatives. 2. Key Responsibilities

  • Integrate and prioritise the OCEO portfolio: bring all priority initiatives into one clear portfolio view. Manage the CEO’s daily schedule and calendar, screening and prioritising requests using input from across the Office of the CEO and escalating significant prioritisation decisions to the Chief of Staff as needed.
  • Provide day-to-day CEO management support: maintaining real-time visibility on delivery by keeping an up-to-date view of priorities, risks and bottlenecks, revising CEO time and attention real time as priorities shift.
  • Distil content into usable first drafts: triangulating key issues to draft correspondence on behalf of the CEO and prepare and maintain the CEO’s daily files by receiving, following up on and compiling briefs, pre-reads, background materials and supporting documentation.
  • Run the CEO operating rhythm: coordinating meeting flows and agendas and ensuring pre-reads/materials are ready. Support the management of the CEO’s email flow and draft responses in close coordination with the Senior Specialist, Internal Management and Planning, and the wider Office of the CEO team, ensuring timely routing, follow-up and handling of correspondence.
  • Manage CEO correspondence and related records: ensuring effective tracking, organised filing, accessibility and appropriate confidentiality.
  • Provide personal support to the CEO where required: exercising discretion, sound judgment, and professionalism.
  • Manage Geneva-based administrative support for the CEO and Office of the CEO: including expenses and hosting arrangements for guests, meetings and events, ensuring timely coordination and professional delivery. Note: The essential functions listed in this section are not exhaustive of the job responsibilities; other duties may be assigned consistently with the department needs. 3.

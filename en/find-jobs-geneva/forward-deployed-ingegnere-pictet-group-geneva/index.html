Full description

Your team

Corporate Functions encompasses the Group’s non core-banking activities, with over 900 professionals supporting all business units, including Communications, Compliance, Finance, Human Resources, Information Security, Internal Audit, Legal, Corporate Office & Digital, Risk, and Tax departments.

Within this environment, Group Finance is responsible for robust financial management and reporting, supported by reliable data, effective controls and increasingly automated processes. As part of the Accounting, Data & Reporting team, you will join a function undergoing significant transformation, supported by a new enterprise data platform, enhanced end-user computing (EUC) capabilities and the growing use of low-code and AI technologies.

Embedded within Group Finance, this role combines Finance, Data and Technology expertise with hands-on engineering. You will work alongside Finance users to understand operational challenges, build practical solutions and support their adoption, in close collaboration with Data & Technology and IT teams.

Your role

As a Forward-Deployed Engineer, you will turn Finance business needs into working technology solutions that improve how financial information, reporting and controls are produced and delivered. You will combine business understanding with hands-on development, owning delivery from problem discovery and rapid prototyping through to testing, controlled deployment, documentation, user adoption and ongoing improvement. You will select the most appropriate technologies for each use case and agree clear support and handover arrangements with Finance and technical teams. Illustrative assignments include automating reconciliations, building reporting-control workflows and developing exception-management tools.

Understand business needs

  • Acquire a strong understanding of our financial accounting processes and related controls.
  • Work alongside users to understand their workflows, capture business requirements and pain points, and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Challenge existing processes and prioritise opportunities for simplification, standardisation and automation with users.
  • Translate business needs into clear solution designs, technical requirements and agreed acceptance criteria.

Design and develop solutions

  • Design, build and maintain applications and EUC solutions supporting Finance reporting, controls, analytics and operational processes.
  • Use programming, SQL, low-code platforms and AI-assisted development as appropriate.
  • Develop workflows, data pipelines, analytical tools, dashboards and controls, integrating with approved data sources and APIs where relevant.
  • Prototype rapidly with end users, validate solutions against acceptance criteria and iterate based on feedback before controlled deployment.

Deliver sustainable outcomes

  • Ensure solutions are robust, documented, maintainable and aligned with data, security and EUC governance frameworks, including appropriate access controls, data traceability, reconciliation and auditability.
  • Work with IT teams to determine whether a solution should remain an EUC solution or transition to an IT-managed application, with explicit ownership, support responsibilities and handover arrangements.
  • Own testing, controlled deployment, documentation and user adoption, and maintain and enhance delivered solutions within agreed support arrangements.
  • Reduce key-person dependency through reusable components, version control, automated testing, code review, clear documentation and shared development standards.

Support Finance transformation

  • Combine embedded collaboration with Finance specialists and hands-on engineering, working closely with technical teams to deliver solutions.
  • Identify practical AI and automation use cases that improve efficiency, reliability and quality, with appropriate validation and human review of AI-enabled outputs.
  • Promote adoption through user engagement, practical training and knowledge sharing across the users.

Your profile

  • A relevant degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Finance or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • At least five years of relevant experience delivering technology or data solutions in a business-facing environment.
  • Strong understanding of accounting and control processes environment, and ability to translate domain knowledge into reliable solutions.
  • Demonstrated delivery of applications, data pipelines or workflow automation used in real business workflows, from problem discovery through deployment and adoption.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with Finance users and technical stakeholders and turn business needs into actionable requirements.
  • Hands-on proficiency in Python or another relevant programming language, alongside SQL and data manipulation.
  • Knowledge of platforms such as Snowflake, AWS, Sigma Computing, Oracle APEX or Streamlit.
  • Strong experience in generative AI, AI-assisted development, workflow automation and low-code development.
  • A proactive, pragmatic and solution-oriented mindset.
  • Ability to work autonomously within agreed engineering and governance standards, involving IT specialists when solution criticality, complexity or scale requires additional expertise.
  • Curiosity and a hands-on attitude, with a willingness to experiment responsibly, learn and share knowledge.
  • Fluency in French and English.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.

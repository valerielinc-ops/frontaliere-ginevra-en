Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1'400 collaborators and collaborators, the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics is a medical center center centered on the elderly is spread over 5 sites: Trois-Chêne, Loëx, Bellerive, Beau-Séjour and Joli-Mont. It takes care of acute situations, thanks in particular to the emergencies of the hospital of Trois-Chêne, the specific problems related to age, medical rehabilitation and palliative medicine. The Memory Centre is located in Cluse-Roseraie and responds to the many challenges posed by memory disorders. It offers a diagnosis based on latest generation techniques, multidisciplinary management and individualized therapies. Patients can integrate research projects and test new drugs.

Description of post

Do you want to contribute to the advancement of knowledge about dementia and Alzheimer's disease and collaborate with recognized experts in this field? The Memory Centre is looking for its future laboratory or laboratory to join its laboratory in several research projects. Under the supervision of the scientific team, you are actively involved in the proper functioning of the laboratory and in the development of research activities. You ensure compliance with procedures, test protocols, and applicable quality, hygiene and safety standards. As part of your missions, you ensure the reception, sorting, processing and preparation of biological samples. In particular, you perform aliquoting, manual or automated decanting operations and ensure pre-analytic quality of samples by reporting any detected non-compliance. You prepare the reagents, solutions and equipment necessary for analyses and experiments. You perform various laboratory manipulations in the fields of biomedical, biological or chemical sciences, including analyses, assays, titrations, identifications, synthesises and other experiments according to established protocols. You are also involved in the development, adaptation and improvement of analytical methods and techniques for the Centre's research projects. You contribute to the collection, treatment, critical analysis and interpretation of experimental results. You are involved in the preparation of scientific reports, the presentation of results and the production of statistical or graphic representations where necessary. You ensure the installation, use, control and first level maintenance of laboratory equipment. You ensure the maintenance of the instruments and your workspace to ensure optimal conditions for research activities. Finally, you take part in activities necessary to maintain the accreditation and quality system of the laboratory, including audits, document management, traceability of samples and document filing. You contribute to the quality and reliability of the work carried out by the Memory Centre.

Qualifications

You are in possession of a laboratory or laboratory CFC and have practical laboratory experience in the management of biological samples. Mastery of the English language (B2 level or higher) is expected. You have scientific knowledge in collaboration with projects.

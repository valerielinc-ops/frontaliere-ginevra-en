Main responsibilities

  • In this evolving team, you have a variety of responsibilities related to attractiveness and recruitment, and you are contributing to the development of the HR Roadmap axes.
  • You provide full support to the five HR recruitment specialists for all recruitment mandates under their responsibility.
  • Managing the various recruitment channels used at HUGs (JobBoards, LinkedIn, specialized press) is one of your responsibilities.
  • As such, you analyze specific requests for publications and undertake the associated steps.
  • In pairs, you provide support to the users of the SmartRecruiters ATS, you can provide the platform's hand-in-hand training and help HR service members, solving problems to ensure optimal use of the tool.
  • We offer a collaborative and stimulating environment, an active team, participation in ambitious HR projects.

Application process

  • During the pre-selection phase, you ensure the qualitative analysis of the applications received for cross-sectional and volume campaigns as well as for more specific mandates.
  • You follow the various stages of the recruitment process, including interview planning, situation management, and response to applications.
  • In addition, you manage the entire summer replacement process representing more than 1,000 applications during the summer.
  • In your role, you ensure the publication of the announcement, evaluation and orientation of applications, then the contractual management of all persons recruited until the writing of the work certificates.
  • You occasionally supervise a team of 4 people dedicated to this process.
  • You are involved in organising recruitment events such as recruitment campaigns, job placements and trade shows and regularly contribute to service projects.
  • Periodically, you establish indicators for your activity.
  • You are comfortable with the current use of Excel for dynamic cross tables, tracking tables, direct mail etc.

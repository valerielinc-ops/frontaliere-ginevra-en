Security Software Engineer — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Pictet Tech designs, integrates and operates a wide range of software applications, from portfolio management systems and trading platforms to banking and corporate solutions. The division drives some of the Group’s key innovations while ensuring the continuity of its business operations.

The Infrastructure Security team serves as the first line of defence in protecting Pictet’s infrastructure and ensuring secure, resilient communications.

As a Security Software Engineer within the team, you will shape the future of automation across our security platforms, helping to deliver best-in-class protection, stability and ease of implementation for our internal partners throughout the Group.

Your role

  • Drive automation across our security platforms and champion infrastructure-as-code practices within the Infrastructure Security team and among internal partners.
  • Promote software craftsmanship practices within the team.
  • Design, develop and continuously enhance our security infrastructure automation landscape : APIs, scripts and CI/CD pipelines
  • Contribute to the architecture and delivery of secure cloud integrations for our data-centre environments.
  • Support daily operations and contribute to the team’s 24/7 on-call service.

Your profile

  • Strong software engineering skills: you write production-grade code in Python, Go or an equivalent language and have the discipline required to turn automation into a maintainable self-service platform.
  • Exposure to or interest in firewalls, proxies, IdPs, VPNs is a plus; we’ll teach you the rest through our experts' mentoring.
  • A strong interest in automation, infrastructure-as-code (terraform, ansible) and continuous integration technologies and tools.
  • A curious mindset and a strong willingness to learn new technologies and develop new skills.
  • A collaborative approach, strong communication skills, intellectual curiosity and a willingness to learn from and teach others.
  • Fluency in both spoken and written French and English.
  • Residence in Switzerland or willingness to relocate.

Your missions

As a Maintenance Technician, your main tasks will be

  • Conduct preventive and corrective maintenance of hotel equipment.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical failures in rooms, public spaces and service areas.
  • Maintenance of electrical, sanitary, heating, ventilation and cooling facilities.
  • Respond quickly to requests for assistance from different departments.
  • Conduct safety checks and ensure compliance of facilities.
  • Participate in renovation, improvement and general maintenance of the building.
  • Follow safety procedures and comply with existing and company standards.
  • Follow-up of interventions and indicate needs for repair or replacement of equipment.
  • Help maintain a safe, functional and comfortable environment for customers and associates.

You'll recognize yourself if... You like to work as a team, meet challenges, take initiatives and have a sense of service. You are curious, passionate and want to evolve in an environment where the requirement is accompanied by benevolence.

You also have

  • Technical training in maintenance, electricity, plumbing or similar fields.
  • Experience in building maintenance, ideally in hotels.
  • Good knowledge of electricity, plumbing and CVC.
  • Ability to diagnose and resolve technical failures.
  • Service sense and responsiveness.
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of responsibility.
  • Team spirit and good communication skills.
  • versatility and ability to manage priorities.
  • Time availability and flexibility.

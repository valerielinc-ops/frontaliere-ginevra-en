Full description

Do you want to practice your TRM profession in radio-oncology with some of the most advanced radiotherapy technologies, in a team recognized for its collaboration and expertise?

Join La Tour Hospital , a private referral hospital located in Meyrin, recognized for the quality of its oncological management and its latest generation technology platform. Our radio-oncology service offers a human-sized working environment where collaboration between TRM, medical physicists and radio-oncologists is at the heart of daily practice.

In order to strengthen our growing service we are recruiting a

Radiology Technician with 90% Radio-Oncology (ref: 1996)

Technology platform

  • 2 Varian accelerators: EDGE and ETHOS with Hypersight
  • Simulations: scanner, MRI, PET-CT
  • Adaptive radiation therapy
  • SRS, SBRT
  • Photobiomodulation
  • Surface hyperthermia
  • Overfacial monitoring AlignRT

This environment allows TRMs to work with cutting-edge technologies and evolve into innovative practice.

Your role

  • Perform radiotherapy treatments with precision and safety
  • Perform scanner, MRI or PET-CT simulations
  • Participate in adaptive treatments (OAR / CTV bypass under medical delegation)
  • Perform CT/IRM/PET-CT transfers and image fusions
  • Accompany patients throughout their therapeutic journey
  • Working closely with medical physicists and physicians

The position offers a great versatility , the TRMs working on all the modalities of the service.

Team

You will integrate a team recognized for its collaboration and good atmosphere:

  • 7 TRM
  • 3 medical physicists
  • 2 radio-oncologists
  • 2 dosimetrists
  • 3 secretaries
  • 1 nurse
  • 1 neuroradiosurgeon
  • 1 service manager

Conditions

  • CDI – 90%
  • Hours : 8am – 4.30pm (while keeping some flexibility to meet the needs of the activity)
  • Annual base salary 90%: between CHF 70,000 and CHF 84,000 depending on experience

What We Offer

  • Personalized in-house training to take up positions
  • Continuing training
  • Advanced technology platform
  • Stable team and collaborative environment
  • Hospital in the immediate vicinity of Geneva

Benefits

  • 13th salary
  • 5 weeks of vacation (possibility to opt for 6 or 7 weeks)
  • LPP and loss of gain insurance
  • Cafeteria, partner reductions, wellness programme
  • Co-op programme and training
  • Internal events

Your profile

  • Diploma of Medical Radiology Technician / TRM
  • Recognition of the Swiss Red Cross diploma required for foreign candidates (pre-check required)
  • Radio-oncology experience (ideally minimum 2 years)
  • Interest in innovative technologies
  • Patient sense, team spirit and professional rigor

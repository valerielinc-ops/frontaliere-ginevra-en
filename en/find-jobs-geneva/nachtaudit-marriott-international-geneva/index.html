As a Night Audit, your main tasks will be

To ensure the reception, check-in and departure of guests during the night.

  • Provide attentive and personalized service, responding to requests and ensuring client satisfaction.
  • Ensure continuity of receipt operations and follow-up of client requests.
  • Perform the Night Audit and monitor the compliance of financial transactions.
  • Verify billings, receipts and operational data to ensure accuracy.
  • Prepare and transmit required reports to day teams.
  • Collaborate with different departments to ensure smooth operations and quality customer experience.
  • Ensure the safety of the institution's clients, associates and assets during the night.
  • Apply Marriott International procedures, quality standards and values.

You will recognize yourself if

You enjoy working independently while contributing to collective success. Rigoury, organized and service oriented, you know how to manage priorities calmly and professionally and like to evolve in a multicultural environment.

You also have

  • First experience in hotel reception or similar function.
  • A good mastery of computer tools
  • Opera's knowledge is an asset.
  • Of interest to the administrative and financial aspects of the Front Office.
  • Excellent level of French and English.
  • Excellent relational skills and a sense of service developed.

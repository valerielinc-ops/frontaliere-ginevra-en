Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a (M/F) Tool Builder whose main mission will be to make available the manufacturing records, plans, models and tools necessary for the industrialization of new products, the reindustrialization of SAV products and the improvement of the serial products of the Ceramics sector.

Responsibilities

  • Design and make available manufacturing records, drawings, models and manufacturing and control tools.
  • Ensure technical consistency between product definition, manufacturing processes, tools and controls.
  • Ensure the manufacturing, maintenance and robustness of the proposed solutions.
  • Create, structure and maintain associated technical data in business systems.
  • To contribute to standardization, improvement of working methods and capitalisation of business know-how.
  • Facilitate communication and convergence between the various internal and external partners.

Profile

  • Diploma of engineer in mechanical design, mechanical engineering, industrialization or methods, with an aptitude for CAD, manufacturing and means of production.
  • Control of ISO rating
  • General mechanical knowledge.
  • Mastery of CAD software (CREO, an asset).
  • Team spirit, listening, sociable and friendly.
  • Demonstrated organizational skills, communication capacity and autonomy.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis, rigorous, technical curiosity.
  • Proposal Force

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

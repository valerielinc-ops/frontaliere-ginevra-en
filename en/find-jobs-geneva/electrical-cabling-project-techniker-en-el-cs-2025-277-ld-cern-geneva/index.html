Full description

Introduction Join the Cabling Section as an electrical technician, and play a key role in expanding, strengthening, and maintaining CERN’s control and power cable installations. Your work will support essential systems within the CERN accelerators, such as alarm systems, process control systems, beam instrumentation, and magnet powering systems. Functions

  • Analyse cable installation requests and liaising with requesters to obtain any missing information.
  • Conduct the necessary studies including the definition of the cable types, the connectors assembly, the wire conventions, the routing and the mechanical supports, in collaboration with users and considering the constraints of the work environment.
  • Create bills of quantities and budgets.
  • Organise cabling and de-cabling campaigns with external and internal coordination stakeholders (i.e contractors, transport and handling service, coordination team).
  • Ensure the preparation of detailed work documentation (e.g., drawings, cable lists, connector assembly procedures, test procedures).
  • Carry out risk assessments related to the execution of cabling work, identifying potential hazards and defining mitigation measures to ensure safety and compliance with CERN standards.
  • Manage the logistics required for cabling activities, such as material delivery, scaffolding setup, and site access.
  • Organise and manage worksite meetings.
  • Monitor and report the progress, the safety and the quality of the works.
  • Perform inspection, testing and acceptance of completed work. Technical certificate or general secondary education or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Industrial Electrical Installation or a related field. Experience:
  • Demonstrated experience in planning, preparing, and overseeing industrial electrical cabling activities, including the implementation of lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures.
  • Strong experience with control and power cables, including their connectors.
  • Experience in verifying the technical quality and compliance of cabling installations with applicable standards and specifications.
  • Experience in technical supervision of contractors. Technical competencies:
  • Design and manufacturing of high-voltage cables and connectors.
  • Design, construction, commissioning, and maintenance of electrical cable systems.
  • Design and manufacturing of low and high-voltage cables and connectors. Behavioural competencies:
  • Achieving Results: driving work / projects along and seeing them through to their conclusion.
  • Communicating Effectively: listening actively to others and letting others speak.
  • Managing Self: taking initiative beyond regular tasks and making things happen.
  • Solving Problems: anticipating obstacles; assessing alternative solutions in order to have a fall-back plan.
  • Working in Teams: building and maintaining constructive and effective work relationships. Language skills: Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the other language.

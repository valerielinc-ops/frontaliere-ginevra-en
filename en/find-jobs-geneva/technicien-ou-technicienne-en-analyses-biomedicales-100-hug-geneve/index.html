Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. Composed of nine medical services, its mission is to provide diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide patient care and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The primary mission of the laboratory medical service is to take charge of liquid samples of all kinds, such as blood, urine, cerebrospinal fluid, and wound smears, and to submit them to numerous and varied analyses that offer quality results as soon as possible. The results guide medical decisions and therapeutic choices. They are obtained using the latest robotic, semi-robotized or manual technologies and the most efficient methods to date. The service also has research and teaching missions.

Description of post

Within this framework the tasks performed will be to receive the biological samples, to record the requests for examinations, to use the automata Sysmex UC-3500 and UF-5000 and to technically and biologically validate the results of counting and cell distribution under the microscope of the cerebrospinal fluids, the liquids of the serous, joint liquids and broncho-alveolar washes. You also realize and validate urinary sediments (cells, crystals and others) and special stains. You participate in the courses and meetings of the service.

Qualifications

You have a diploma in Biomedical Analysis Technician (BAT) or an equivalent qualification and a very good knowledge of haematology. You know how to use the usual computer tools. Professional experience in the fields of hematology (cytology, automata) and urinary analysis would be an asset. Your rigour, your sense of responsibility and priorities allow you to perform the tasks entrusted to you in an optimal and efficient way. You are motivated or motivated and have a team spirit You are resistant or resistant to stress, autonomous, precise or precise, rigorous or rigorous, reliable, and have easy contact with patients, especially children.

Full description

The La Tour Hospital's intensive care department is responsible for the care of patients whose life expectancy can be expected following a medical, traumatic or surgical condition. A specialized team ensures admission and continuous care 24 hours a day, as well as emergency and medical services. Our service includes all the infrastructure necessary to establish a diagnosis for first aid and surveillance, regardless of the severity of the case. It also has a resuscitation room, a plaster room and various treatment and consultation rooms.

We recruit one

Caregiver in Intensive Care CDMax 9 months (ref: 2078)

In a state-of-the-art institution you have a variety of positions on a multidisciplinary team including nurses, health care workers, doctors. You will need to accommodate patients with acute overall care.

As such you are in charge of;

  • Welcoming the patient and his family by making them involved in care
  • Collect administrative data
  • Take care of the patient as a whole in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team and meet its basic needs (hygiene, pain, food, comfort, ...)
  • Ensure the supply of equipment, verify their operation and ensure the management of maintenance, apply cleaning and disinfection procedures
  • Participate in the proper functioning of the service, make transmissions on the computerized care file
  • Updating of professional practices to service or institutional care procedures

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Diploma of Caregiver
  • Self-governing, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and sense of priority are essential qualities
  • Experience in acute care min 2 years (ideally in resuscitation, intensive care, emergency or continuous care. At least in medical service)
  • Technical skill
  • Attendance in nursing care for dependent patients
  • Clinical sense
  • Dynamics
  • Flexibility

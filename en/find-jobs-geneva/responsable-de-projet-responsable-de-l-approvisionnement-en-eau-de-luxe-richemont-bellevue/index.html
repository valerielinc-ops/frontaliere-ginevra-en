Full description

Reference code: JR133449

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

The mission of the Responsible Sourcing Hard Luxury Project Manager is to support the expert in the suppliers & raw materials responsible sourcing performance monitoring and continuous improvement.

You will participate in the steady development of the diamonds and coloured gemstones risk mapping. You will also follow-up on the continuous evaluation of suppliers and subcontractors along the supply chain and formalize all due diligence related information. In a fast evolving ESG regulations context, this role will also contribute to the definition of new quantitative indicators, building a resilient Responsible Sourcing management system.

The scope of the mission will encompass all Richemont Maisons sourcing and/or producing finished products with diamonds and coloured gemstones.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Maintain an accurate, reliable diamonds and coloured gemstones supply chain database and KPI’s •

Maintain and continuously improve the supply chain database including all due diligence related information.

Prepare and follow-up on a regular basis the diamonds and coloured gemstones Responsible Sourcing key performance indicators - KPI’s

Participate in the definition of new Responsible Sourcing KPI’s

Engage in the different data digitalisation initiatives of the department

Ensure data security and confidentiality

Coordinate supplier naturality and traceability audits and follow-ups

In collaboration with the expert and the Maisons, prepare the audit campaigns

Follow-up with Maisons and ensure audit plan execution

Provide and formalize regular status update and escalate if needed

Participate in the animation of Group Diamonds & Coloured gemstones community

Support the Head of Responsible Sourcing Hard Luxury in the preparation of the different Committees and internal and external working groups

Preparation of minute meetings

Other transversal related tasks

Contribute to the Group ESG reporting

Collaborate with the team responsible for emission factors related topics

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent years of experience, preferably in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Compliance, or Sustainability related discipline.

Minimum 2-4 years’ experience in Procurement or within the Sustainability area with a strong interest in Responsible Sourcing topics

An experience in the field of the precious materials is a plus

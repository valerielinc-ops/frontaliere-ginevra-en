Full description

Mission

Attached to our Research & Development division, you will be responsible for evaluating the characteristics and optimizing a new precious alloy for watchmaking.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct a bibliographic review to identify existing knowledge and establish a state of art.
  • Prepare samples by fusion and then apply different thermomechanical treatments.
  • Study the impact of chemical composition and transformation parameters on the microstructural, mechanical and aesthetic properties of alloying.
  • Assess compatibility of alloying with watchmaking finishing processes.
  • Present and synthesize results as technical reports and presentations.

Profile sought

  • Currently in Master's degree in Materials Science and Engineering.
  • Knowledge of materials and metallurgy.
  • Skills in metallurgical characterization and surfaces.
  • Good project management skills.
  • Autonomy, power of proposition.
  • Analytical mind.
  • Relationship relationship.
  • Master's project lasting 6 months.

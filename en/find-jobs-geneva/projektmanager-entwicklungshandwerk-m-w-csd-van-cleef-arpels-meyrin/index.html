Full description

Reference code: JR134268

Who are we?

A High Jewelry Home with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allows our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

You justify a proven experience in managing complex development projects, ideally in the jewellery, watchmaking or Crafts sector. You are recognized for your rigorous analysis and structuring of projects, costs and timelines. Excellent communication and teacher, you know how to work with all stakeholders. Responsive and committed, you show initiative and a high sense of responsibility on a daily basis.

What do we want from you?

Attached to the development project manager, you orchestrate the development of exceptional objects integrating Crafts of Art, from the initial brief to the production. You ensure respect for technical, aesthetic, financial and calendar constraints while embodying the House's requirements for excellence.

As part of your missions

  • You analyse customer requests and marketing briefs in advance in order to identify real needs and offer tailored solutions.
  • You build and pilot exceptional objects integrating the complexity of the Crafts of Art, from the planning of the key steps to the production, according to the launch objectives and in the control of the budget, the burden and the deadlines.
  • You initiate, accompany and pilot technical and aesthetic studies with internal and external partners, and then validate the proposed constructions according to the rules and standards of the House.
  • In close connection with Planning and the Head of Workshop, you pilot the internal tests and evaluate the stocks and stocks related to the prototypes and the first series.
  • You guide the search for solutions, implement the necessary action plans and ensure the production after validation of the prototype.
  • You make up the price records, then solicit and negotiate with the prototype suppliers the price offers and deadlines.
  • You create and update technical data—articles, ranges and nomenclatures—and ensure strict compliance with the quality criteria defined with the Creation, Marketing and Development of Watches, under the leadership of the Quality Manager.
  • You negotiate the best compromises in the face of the constraints of the project and embody the values of the House in all your interactions.
  • You federate and coordinate the various members of the project teams internally as well as external partners. You facilitate collaboration and effective communication between all key players.

More than a job... we recruit for a career

Integrating Van Cleef & Arpels is first and foremost a team where the development of employees is at the heart of our ambition.

From your integration, you will be immersed in the world of the House thanks to trainings on our history, know-how and culture.

The recruitment process

Apply directly online.

If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for an interview.

You meet with the Development Project Manager, Crafts of Art, then the Development Manager, and the Human Resources Manager.

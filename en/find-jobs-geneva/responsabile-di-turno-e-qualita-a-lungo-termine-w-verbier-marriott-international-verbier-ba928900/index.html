Mission

As a Cook Apprentice, your main tasks will be

  • To participate in the preparation and production of the dishes in accordance with the recipes and quality standards of the hotel.
  • To learn and master the different culinary techniques taught in the course of your training.
  • To prepare the ingredients and ensure the necessary setting up at the various food outlets.
  • To train the cooks in the daily production and at special events.
  • To ensure compliance with hygiene, food safety and HACCP standards.
  • To help clean and maintain equipment, equipment and workspaces.
  • To help manage goods and store deliveries.
  • To develop your versatility by discovering the various stations and specialties of the kitchen.
  • To work with the whole brigade to ensure a smooth and quality service.
  • To show curiosity, commitment and a real willingness to learn to build the foundations of your future job.

Profile sought

You like to work as a team, take on challenges, take initiatives and have a sense of service. You are curious, passionate and want to evolve in an environment where the requirement is accompanied by benevolence.

You also have

  • Of a strong interest in the cooking and gastronomy professions.
  • Of a sincere motivation to learn and develop your professional skills.
  • Of a team spirit and a positive attitude to daily life.
  • Of a will to invest you fully in your training and to build your professional future.

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

We are looking for a Pay and Social Insurance Supervisor to manage all activities related to personnel administration, payroll, social insurance, time management and HR tool support. Your expertise will be essential to ensure compliance, data quality and unimpeachable proximity service, while actively contributing to the continuous optimization of our processes.

Support for employees and managers

  • Provide first-level support on HR tools (Workday, SAP HR, etc.) and meet the demands of employees and managers (pay, social insurance, taxation, working time, leave)
  • Organize and conduct information sessions, HR permanences and contribute to communication and training activities

Pay Management

  • Collect, control and integrate payroll data (fixes and variables)
  • Prepare, validate and process salaries in a timely and procedural manner
  • Manage elements related to employee movements (inputs, outputs, transfers)
  • Produce and collect annual documents (salary certificates, tax returns at source, etc.)
  • Ensuring the accuracy, compliance and confidentiality of payroll for entities under its responsibility

Data quality, reporting and monitoring

  • Ensure reliability of HR data
  • Conduct consistency checks and accounting reconciliations by cost centre where necessary
  • Participate in the production of HR reporting and propose improvements to steering tools

HR Administration

  • Managing Work Permit Applications and Renewals
  • Administrative management of social insurance
  • Managing Time Management Activities
  • Control the quality of the data transmitted between Workday and SAP HR
  • Organize the administrative reception of new employees and present the main HR elements (social insurance, taxation, pay, etc.)
  • Prepare employment certificates, salary certificates, unemployment certificates and any other administrative documents

Technical reference and continuous improvement

  • Identify and implement opportunities to improve HR TEC processes and tools
  • Actively participate in solution deployment projects, standardization and optimization of practices
  • Acting as a functional and technical reference for the team, documenting processes and sharing good practices
  • Contribute to testing and deployment of system developments and support users during production
  • To be a proposal force to improve service quality, operational efficiency and user experience

Piloting HR Tickets

  • Supervise ticket management at the Meyrin Hub, monitor and monitor service levels
  • Produce performance indicators and analyse trends
  • Identify recurring issues and work with HRIS to implement sustainable solutions to reduce ticket volume

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

A strong expertise: A graduate of a relevant higher education, you justify a confirmed experience of at least 8 years in Pay and HR Administration in Switzerland. Mastery of SAP and/or Workday, as well as experience in a multi-entity or international environment, are major assets.

Relationship and organizational qualities: With an excellent sense of service, you are very rigorous, organizational and autonomous. Your adaptability and proactivity allow you to evolve smoothly in a dynamic and constantly changing environment.

Language skills: You have perfect command of French and English, both oral and written.

Tool management: You are perfectly comfortable with IT tools, including SAP HR, Workday and reporting tools.

A plus: Experience in project management would be appreciated.

If you are passionate about HR operations and willing to contribute to the excellence of our services, we would be delighted to meet you!

Full description

High quality fresh chocolate - This is what the Swiss chocolate factory Läderach represents. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs more than 2,500 people from over 80 countries throughout the group, and the trend is on the rise. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glaris. Here, in the picturesque setting of the Alps, we produce our fresh chocolates and constantly develop new and innovative ideas within agile teams. The company's retail sector comprises more than 250 chocolate shops in more than 30 countries. This is where we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to rediscover fresh chocolate constantly. You want to enjoy it too? Then join us.

What's waiting for you

  • You advise and sell our wide range of products according to customers' needs, for example when choosing gifts
  • You ensure the processing of cash transactions and correct counts
  • You present the vast assortment of confectionery and chocolate, notably through the layout of the store
  • You implement high quality standards and respect the concept of hygiene and freshness
  • You show flexibility from Monday to Sunday, between 05:45 and 20:45, with an activity mainly on weekends and occasionally higher work rates according to the store's needs
  • You have a proven experience selling high-end chocolate or food products

What you bring

  • You have experience in retailing and handling food products
  • You are passionate about selling and making our customers happy
  • You give priority to quality and customer orientation, even in times of turbulence
  • You like taking responsibility, you're proactively thinking about the team and you're ready to learn
  • You attach great importance to open communication and have good knowledge in French and English, required. German is an additional asset
  • You can work flexibly on weekends, Saturdays, Sundays and sometimes public holidays.

What we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position full of responsibilities
  • The possibility of bringing new ideas
  • A culture of frank and respectful feedback
  • In-house and external continuing training opportunities
  • Opportunities for professional development
  • Generous discounts on our assortment
  • Chocolates in the workplace
  • Good accessibility to all sites

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Interested? So don't hesitate to apply at home. For any question, Michael Gandoin, Manager, is willing.

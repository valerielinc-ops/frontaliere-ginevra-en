Full description

The technical platform of our denoscopy centre has as its mission a high quality management in collaboration with the various services of the Hospital of La Tour. With a high-level technical platform, numerous diagnostic, follow-up and interventional examinations are carried out. In order to complete our service we are recruiting one: Medical Secretary in Endoscopy 100% - CDI (ref: 1958) Your mission: You perform all the administrative tasks of the referral department. As such you will be in charge of ;

  • Patient reception
  • Making appointments and organising the doctor's agenda
  • Organization of examinations and tests
  • Maintenance of Service Records and Databases
  • Managing incoming and outgoing mail
  • Archiving, classification
  • Reporting
  • Invoicing consultations in Tarmed and at private rate on Opale We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:
  • Excellent mastery of the medical secretariat.
  • Graduated as a medical secretary
  • Experience in Switzerland essential
  • Good computer skills
  • Perfect mastery of French and English (ideally).
  • Mastering MS Office tools, knowledge of Carefolio & Opale a real asset
  • Good understanding of the billing system and the Swiss outpatient and private healthcare system
  • Dynamics, autonomy and rapid and efficient organisational capacity
  • Discretion and versatility, good stress resistance
  • Very good contact with patients and sense of service
  • Excellent presentation
  • Available quickly We offer you: Type of contract: Contract with Indeterminate Duration Percentage: 100% Start date: as soon as possible

Your Purpose

As Senior Manager, Catering Newbuilding, you will shape how guests and crew dine on board MSC Cruises’ new and revitalised ships. Reporting to the Catering Newbuilding Director, you will lead Food & Beverage (F&B) projects from the initial concept and business case through design, construction and operational handover.

You will work with our shipboard Operations teams, the Owner’s representatives, shipyards, equipment manufacturers and franchise partners to turn each concept into a venue that reflects our brand and works at sea. Your decisions will shape both the guest experience and the crew’s ability to deliver it, from the layout of a restaurant to the equipment and provisioning flows that support a busy service.

Your Impact

  • Lead multiple F&B projects across newbuilds and major refits, managing scope, budgets, timelines and risks through to handover.
  • Partner with Brand Strategy, Innovation, Product and Operations to define each concept’s positioning, develop its business case and translate it into design and equipment specifications.
  • Oversee the design of guest and crew F&B spaces, including restaurants, bars, service counters, food stores, provisioning areas and crew messes. Coordinate architectural design reviews and review contractual drawings on behalf of the Owner.
  • Work with shipyards, architects and technical teams to resolve design issues and ensure layouts support service capacity, guest circulation and crew workflows.
  • Standardise catering equipment specifications across projects where appropriate and coordinate the selection, procurement and delivery of Owner-supplied equipment.
  • Ensure F&B layouts, equipment and operating flows meet applicable public health and food safety requirements.
  • Work with Hotel Operations to verify that venues, equipment and service flows are ready for operation at handover.
  • Use shipboard feedback and developments in the cruise and hospitality sectors to improve future designs, equipment choices and project delivery processes.

Your Journey so far

  • At least 10 years of experience in a management role, with a track record of delivering F&B projects for large cruise vessels.
  • Extensive experience planning and delivering cruise ship newbuilds and major refits, from concept development through to completion.
  • Strong technical knowledge of marine catering design, equipment specifications and the operational requirements of guest and crew spaces.
  • Advanced project management skills, including business case development, budgeting, risk assessment and reporting across several projects.
  • Experience using AutoCAD or comparable naval design software, proficiency in Microsoft Office and, ideally, familiarity with DWG drawing workflows.
  • The ability to bring operational, design and technical teams together, assess trade-offs and communicate decisions clearly to senior stakeholders and external partners.
  • Fluent English. Additional languages are an advantage.
  • Availability to travel at least 30% of the time.

Your Essentials

  • An EU passport or the right to work in Switzerland is required.

Your Purpose

As the Reporting, Process Excellence & Data Science Director, you will lead the design, development, and governance of FP&A analytics, reporting, and process excellence initiatives. You will ensure that financial and operational data are transformed into actionable insights that support accurate forecasting, strategic decision-making, and standardized, scalable processes across all brands.

Your Impact

Reporting & Dashboards

  • Design and maintain standardized reporting frameworks and KPI dashboards.
  • Automate reporting processes to improve efficiency, timeliness, and reduce manual effort.
  • Ensure reports are accurate, consistent, and tailored to executive, HQ, and local stakeholder needs.

Process Excellence & Governance

  • Define and enforce FP&A process standards across brands and local offices.
  • Implement process improvements to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and consistency.
  • Develop documentation, workflows, and KPI dictionaries to ensure alignment and reproducibility.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Partner with Finance Business Partners to interpret forecast outputs and provide actionable insights.
  • Collaborate with FP&A teams to align dashboards, reporting frameworks, and reporting cycles.
  • Guide, train, and upskill FP&A teams on analytics tools and best practices.

Leadership & Strategy

  • Lead a team of Data, Reporting, and Process Excellence Analysts.
  • Define the strategic roadmap for analytics, reporting, and FP&A process improvement.
  • Ensure alignment with corporate objectives and multi-brand operational requirements.

Data Science & Analytics

  • Develop predictive and prescriptive models for revenue, costs, occupancy, and other key business metrics.
  • Build scenario analyses and advanced forecasting tools to support strategic and operational decisions.
  • Collaborate with IT and Finance Transformation teams on data architecture, integration, and data quality control.

Your Journey so far

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field; MBA preferred.
  • 15+ years of experience in finance, analytics, reporting, or related functions, including leadership responsibilities.
  • Proven experience in FP&A, financial modeling, forecasting, and performance management.
  • Experience leading analytics, reporting, and process improvement initiatives.
  • Exposure to multi-brand and/or multi-country organizations is preferred.
  • Experience managing teams and driving strategic transformation projects.

Your Essentials

  • Strong expertise in data analytics, business intelligence, and forecasting.
  • Advanced knowledge of analytical and reporting tools such as Power BI, Python, R, SQL, and similar platforms.
  • Strong understanding of FP&A, financial modeling, budgeting, forecasting, and performance management.
  • Knowledge of process improvement methodologies, including Lean, Six Sigma, and standardization frameworks.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills across executive, HQ, and local teams.
  • Ability to translate complex data into actionable business insights and influence strategic decision-making.
  • Strong leadership, coaching, and team management capabilities.
  • Strategic mindset combined with strong operational execution.
  • Ability to work effectively in complex, international, and matrix organizations.
  • Strong focus on governance, standardization, and continuous improvement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.

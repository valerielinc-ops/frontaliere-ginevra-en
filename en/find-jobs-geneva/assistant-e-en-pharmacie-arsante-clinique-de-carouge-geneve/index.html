Full description

For our pharmacy at the Eaux-Vives station in the canton of Geneva, we are actively looking for an 80% pharmacy assistant. Your conditions:

  • Permanent contract
  • Start: as soon as possible
  • Activity rate: 80%
  • Right to holidays: 5 weeks per year Your missions:
  • Manage and streamline the stock, as well as prepare orders under the supervision of the pharmacist.
  • Advise our clients in a professional and personalized way, and prepare orders under the responsibility of the pharmacist.
  • Participate in pharmaceutical production for external customers.
  • Provide pharmaceutical assistance to health institutions.
  • Collaborate with the blistering center. Your skills:
  • CFC pharmacy assistant,
  • You enjoy human contacts and personalized customer advice,
  • You have a team spirit,
  • You are flexible and motivated,
  • You are organized and have a sense of priority,
  • You are discrete and conscientious,
  • You have experience in producing drugs in the laboratory,
  • You master the usual computer tools, Tactil knowledge would be an asset.

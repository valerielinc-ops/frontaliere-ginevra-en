Full description

Reference code: JR134650

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

You will promote analytics & reporting tools

  • Collaborate with Maisons to identify key performance indicators, translating business needs into actionable data insights
  • Develop, improve and maintain analytics and reporting tools within Excel and the Google Cloud Platform (GCP) environment
  • Support Maisons to implement analytics and reporting tools

You will contribute to ongoing projects

  • Support project manager to animate a Supply Chain project (e.g. preparation and animation of workshops)
  • Run ad-hoc analyses to bring data-base insights to business
  • Formalize outcomes of analyses/workshops to prepare key business meeting

You will support the team growth and structuring

  • Co-design and build methodological kits of our Supply Chain best practices
  • Build user guides for our in-house tools
  • Create communication support to promote initiatives internally

WHAT DO YOU NEED TO BE SUCCESSFUL WITH US?

  • Enrolled in a Master’s degree or equivalent in engineering
  • Strong interest and knowledge in Supply Chain
  • Solid analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Experience with dashboard/visualization tools (PowerBI or Looker)
  • SQL/Python skills are a plus
  • Strong organization, communication & interpersonal skills
  • Fluent in English and French

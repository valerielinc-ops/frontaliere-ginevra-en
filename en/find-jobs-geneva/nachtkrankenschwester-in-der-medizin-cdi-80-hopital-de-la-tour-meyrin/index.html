Full description

Your mission

In a state-of-the-art institution you hold a varied position on a multidisciplinary team of nurses, nursing assistants, doctors.

You will need to ensure that patients with multi-purpose pathologies are received as part of a comprehensive acute phase of care.

As such you are in charge of;

  • To inform the patient and to provide him with information on administrative organisation and care
  • Collect and transmit medical and administrative data and complete the computerized care file
  • Ensure and organize care planning according to priorities and urgency
  • Perform care on medical prescription and in accordance with unit and institutional procedures
  • Maintaining the Informatized Patient Record
  • Continuously assess the patient's clinical condition and its development in the acute situation
  • Identify situations of fragility, loss of autonomy and complexity requiring an interdisciplinary approach;
  • To assist in the management of elderly and polymorphic patients, as well as in the support of patients and their relatives in situations of Holas;
  • Collaborate with the various stakeholders to ensure continuity and coordination of care;
  • Ensure inter-service coordination for patient movement management
  • Coordinate inputs and outputs: internal mutations, external transfers of patients in collaboration with the health framework.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Diploma of Nurse(-E)
  • In possession of the Red Cross recognition if foreign diploma
  • Experience in internal medicine, geriatrics, palliative care or a complex area of care, an asset;
  • Has a strong interest in the management of elderly, polymorphic and/or hal patients;
  • Sensitive to the patient's holistic and interdisciplinary approach;
  • Meaning of contact and service in a private establishment
  • Self-reliance, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and priority-setting are essential qualities
  • Positive attitude within a team and are able to generate a climate of trust
  • Capable of contributing to building trust with patients, their loved ones and different professionals.

An evolving environment

Joining our team is integrating a dynamic internal medicine service, at the heart of a developing environment, offering the opportunity to contribute to the evolution of new care channels. We value employees wishing to develop their skills and invest in projects related to geriatrics, palliative care and the care of patients with complex situations.

Training or experience in these areas is an asset, but the motivation and interest in these specialties are also particularly appreciated.

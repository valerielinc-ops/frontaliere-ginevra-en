Full description

Your missions Linked directly to the ISO Deputy Director, a strategic reference, you are the interface between business needs and technological capabilities. In close collaboration with the product team Core Business of the Information and Digital Systems Directorate (DirSin), you ensure that information systems meet the operational needs of the function, whether expressed through projects or requests for evolution.

As part of the mandate entrusted to you by the function, you have the necessary autonomy to arbitrate and prioritize the needs and functional developments of your perimeter, ensuring consistency with the strategic orientations of ISO.

Your main responsibilities are

  1. Maximize the value of the product:
  • Collect, analyse, formalize and arbitrate business needs and initiatives by clarifying their objectives, expected results, value and impacts in case of non-achievement;
  • Challenge and prioritize perimeter needs according to their business value and the priorities of the function;
  • Maintain and prioritize the backlog of functional developments according to their business value, in coordination with the product manager;
  • Ensuring the relevance and added value of the developments envisaged;
  • Follow indicators of satisfaction, use and efficiency of solutions and integrate legal and regulatory requirements.
  1. Ensuring Strategic Alignment:
  • Translating the function's strategic directions into a product roadmap and ensuring consistency with business priorities;
  • Ensure the proper management of the priorities for the implementation of technological applications and solutions in the area concerned;
  • Bringing vision and business priorities to reviews of the roadmap led by the product manager;
  • Participate in governance bodies, prioritization committees and portfolio reviews.
  1. Contribute and participate in continuous improvement:
  • Define acceptance criteria, mobilise relevant users and ensure business validation of delivered solutions;
  • Measure results against expected objectives and contribute to reviews and retrospectives of continuous improvement.
  1. Facilitate communication and collaboration:
  • Ensure smooth communication between users, business stakeholders and the core product team;
  • Organize and coordinate testing and validation phases;
  • Contribute to accompaniment to the change in digital solutions.

Your profile

  • Bachelor's degree, university degree or training deemed equivalent in information systems, project management or a relevant field of work;
  • Additional certification or training in project management or agile methods (Hermes, PMP, Scrum, Agile) is an asset;
  • Experience confirmed by at least 3 years in identifying, analysing and formalizing business needs and prioritizing functional developments within an information system;
  • Experience in preparing and organising validation tests;
  • Practice agile methods such as Scrum or Kanban;
  • Experience in a public, para-public or institutional organization is an advantage;
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capability;
  • Ability to negotiate, influence and federate without direct hierarchical link;
  • Strong communication skills with trade and technical interlocutors;
  • Good understanding of the governance issues of information systems;
  • Knowledge of data protection and information security regulations (LPD, GDPR, LIPAD);
  • Knowledge of the context and environment of the General Hospital is an asset;
  • French: excellent oral and written mastery;
  • English: Good professional knowledge.

We offer you

  • An open and respectful corporate culture;
  • Continuous skills development through attractive training policy;
  • Opportunities for career development and internal mobility;
  • Many cultural and sporting activities to live together within the institution.

Full description

Occupational therapy aims to maintain, acquire or recover an optimal autonomy in daily, professional and leisure life, with the greatest possible safety. It acts according to the potential and constraints of the person's environment. Based in temporary reception units or in home support units located in the canton, the occupational therapist provides services independently, respecting the customer's lifestyle, beliefs, values and culture. She works in interdisciplinarity and interprofessionality, respecting the mission and objectives of the institution. Your missions and responsibilities

  • To provide, in collaboration and synergy with the multidisciplinary team, on medical prescription, occupational therapy benefits to people limited in their ability to act by illness, accident or age-related problems.
  • Promote the development and/or maintenance of independence, patient autonomy in the activities of daily life, through rehabilitation and rehabilitation measures
  • Coordinate the group and community activities of the unit.

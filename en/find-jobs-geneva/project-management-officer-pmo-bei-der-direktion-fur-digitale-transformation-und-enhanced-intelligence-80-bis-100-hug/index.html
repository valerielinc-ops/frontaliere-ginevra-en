Full description

In a development context, the DTN-IA is looking for a Project Management Officer (PMO), member of its management committee, and adjunta or adjunto of the Management of the DTN-IA

  • In this strategic position, you play a central role in the governance and steering of the digital project portfolio
  • In the elaboration and follow-up of the investment budget in the framework of the five-year investment plans
  • In the coordination of the prioritization of projects in relation to the digital product guidelines in collaboration with the bodies of governance Duties
  • Develop a consolidated vision of the project portfolio to support the management of institutional commitments
  • Optimize the allocation of resources and facilitate strategic arbitrations
  • Bring the requests into coherence with the institutional priorities, the available resources and the real ability of the teams to implement
  • Guarantee a portfolio that is both sustainable and realistic
  • Prepare, follow and consolidate the IT investment budget of the HUGs, under the responsibility of the administrative and financial manager of the management
  • Ensure alignment with the institutional processes and governance demands
  • Ensure the global piloting of a portfolio of approximately 150 projects per year
  • Ensure rigorous follow-up of planning, progress, budgets and risks
  • Accompany project leaders in their daily management
  • Develop and update control boards intended for the HUG Management Committee and the DTN-IA
  • Anticipate risks, identify them and implement adapted corrective measures
  • Ensure direct management of some projects based on the needs of the organization
  • Requirements
  • Being a member of the DTN-IA management committee
  • Being an adjunct or adjunto of the DTN-IA Management
  • Having a strategic vision and leadership ability
  • Having a good knowledge of project management methodologies
  • Having good communication and collaboration skills
  • Having a good knowledge of institutional processes and governance issues
  • What we offer
  • A strategic and leadership position within the DTN-IA
  • The opportunity to work in a context of development and innovation
  • The opportunity to develop one's skills and grow professionally
  • A dynamic and collaborative working environment Contact
  • For further information, contact the Administration and Finance Manager of the management.

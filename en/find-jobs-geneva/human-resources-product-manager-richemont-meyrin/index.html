CONTEXT

As part of Group Platforms department, Richemont is looking to further strengthen its dynamic Product Management team structured around key capabilities that support our Maisons' value chain strategy.

In this context, we are hiring a Human Resources (HR) Product Manager with two complementary responsibilities: first, overseeing the entire HR product portfolio, covering Operations, Talent, People Experience and Rewards, and managing the corresponding Product Owners; second, acting directly as Product Owner for the Rewards Product. Together, these responsibilities support the strategic oversight and continuous enhancement of integrated, high-performing HR solutions across Richemont entities.

The HR Product Manager will define, lead, and materialize the overarching product strategy for the HR pillar. This role ensures full alignment with Group HR business priorities, complying with business rules and processes defined by the Business Functional leads. The HR Product Manager plays a pivotal role in supporting and driving the Group HR strategy by leading the development of valuable products and fostering product transformation.

HOW YOU WILL MAKE AN IMPACT?

HR product portfolio oversight and Product Owner Management

· You will act as the strategic interface between the Group Technology team and the HR Function, ensuring deep understanding and alignment of strategic objectives for all HR Products. · You will build and maintain comprehensive roadmaps for the HR Product portfolio, ensuring strategic alignment with Group HR objectives and broader Richemont strategy. · You will implement consistent ways of working around all HR Products (e.g., ceremonies, demand management, fixed capacity models), fostering a mature and efficient product development culture. · You will proactively steer risks, issues, and related mitigation plans in close collaboration with the HR Function. · You will regularly communicate and articulate the strategic evolution and impact of the HR Products, demonstrating their contribution to the overarching business vision, particularly to senior stakeholders. · You will manage and coach a small team of Product Owners.

Product Ownership for the Rewards Product

· You will lead the transformation and strategic direction of the Rewards Product, in close alignment with Group HR, across Compensation, Benefits, Commission Management and International Mobility, while managing cross-Product and business dependencies. · You will partner with Group HR stakeholders to understand business needs, translate them into product initiatives, and prioritize demand according to strategic value and available capacity. · You will oversee and continuously refine the product backlog, balancing evolving business priorities, business value, ROI and resources. · You will partner with the technology team to translate the product vision into effective implementation, while ensuring seamless coordination across Product Areas. · You will champion change management initiatives, fostering adoption and dissemination of comprehensive product training, support tools, and documentation for the Rewards product. · You will drive continuous product improvement, leveraging user feedback, adoption, benefits realization and key product KPIs to maximize business impact and inform product priorities.

  • Excellence is a top priority for our teams. In this role you have the opportunity to play a pivotal role in major global strategic initiatives for the Richemont Group and its Maisons.

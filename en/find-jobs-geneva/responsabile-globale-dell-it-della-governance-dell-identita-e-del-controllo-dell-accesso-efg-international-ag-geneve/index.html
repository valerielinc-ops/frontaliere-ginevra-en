Job Description

We are looking for a Global Head of Identity Governance & Access Control to lead EFG’s global User Access Management (UAM) and Identity Governance function.

In this role, you will be responsible for defining and executing the bank’s strategy for identity lifecycle and access governance, ensuring secure, accurate and compliant access to systems and data across entities and business lines.

Reporting to the Global Head of IT Support, you will manage a global team and oversee the end‑to‑end access lifecycle, including access provisioning and deprovisioning, periodic access reviews and recertifications, and the design and maintenance of role‑based access models.

You will drive the implementation and continuous enhancement of the bank’s identity governance framework and tools, working closely with IT, Information Security, Architecture, Business and HR stakeholders.

You will act as the primary point of contact for internal and external audits on identity and access topics, ensuring robust controls, clear documentation and effective remediation of findings.

The role combines strategic leadership, operational excellence and strong stakeholder management in a highly regulated, control‑intensive environment.

For this IT / technology‑focused role, we keep the description of tools and platforms at a high level in the job ad. More detailed information on the technology stack and specific solutions can be shared with shortlisted candidates at a later stage of the recruitment process. No confidential or security‑sensitive details about EFG’s internal security architecture or processes are disclosed externally.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop the global User Access Management and Identity Governance function, providing strategic direction, coaching and day‑to‑day leadership to a team and additional indirect reports.
  • Oversee end‑to‑end identity and access lifecycle processes (joiners, movers, leavers), ensuring timely, accurate and compliant access provisioning and deprovisioning across systems and applications, while keeping external communications free of sensitive security configurations or architectures.
  • Design, maintain and continuously improve the bank’s role‑based access and business role models, including the management of deviations and segregation‑of‑duties considerations, in close collaboration with Business, IT, Information Security, Risk, Compliance and HR stakeholders.
  • Plan and run periodic access review and recertification campaigns, ensuring that access rights remain appropriate, well‑controlled and aligned with regulatory and internal policy requirements.
  • Lead the bank’s identity governance programme, defining the roadmap, onboarding applications to the central governance platform, and simplifying and standardising access‑related processes in partnership with IT, Security, Architecture, Business and HR.
  • Act as primary point of contact for internal and external auditors on identity and access topics, preparing and presenting evidence, reports and remediation plans, while ensuring that externally shared information remains high‑level and does not reveal sensitive security details.
  • Establish and monitor key performance and risk indicators for identity and access management, driving continuous improvement initiatives to enhance control effectiveness, operational efficiency and user experience.
  • Manage the relationship and service delivery with external providers supporting identity and access services, including performance oversight and budget responsibility for both run‑the‑bank and change‑the‑bank activities.
  • Represent the UAM/IGA function in steering committees, governance forums and senior management meetings, providing clear, business‑oriented updates on key initiatives, risks and issues related to identity and access.

Requirements and Qualifications

Education

  • Master’s degree (or equivalent) in Computer Science, Information Systems, Information Security, Engineering or a related field (minimum requirement).
  • Additional business or management education (for example an MBA) is an asset.
  • Relevant security or identity & access management certifications (for example in information security or audit) are a strong advantage.

- Project or programme management certifications are considered an advantage.

Professional Experience

  • At least 5 years of experience in Identity & Access Management / Identity Governance, including several years in a senior leadership role with responsibility for teams and complex environments.
  • Proven track record in implementing and operating identity governance solutions and role‑based access models in a large, regulated organisation.
  • Experience in banking or another highly regulated, control‑intensive industry (for example financial services, luxury, pharmaceuticals).

- Demonstrated experience managing complex, multi‑stakeholder programmes and interacting with internal and external auditors and senior management.

Technical and Functional Knowledge

  • Deep understanding of identity and access management concepts, including identity lifecycle, provisioning and deprovisioning, access recertification, role‑based access control and segregation of duties.
  • Strong knowledge of access control models and business role design, with the ability to define pragmatic, scalable solutions that balance control and usability.
  • Familiarity with information security standards and control frameworks and with regulatory expectations in the financial sector.
  • Good understanding of application architectures and integration patterns (on‑premise, cloud, SaaS) relevant to access management, without disclosing internal security architecture or specific configurations in external materials.

- Ability to translate technical identity and access topics into clear, business‑oriented language and governance decisions for senior stakeholders.

Languages and Personal Skills

  • Fluency in English and French (spoken and written) is required; additional languages are an advantage.
  • Strong analytical and problem‑solving skills, with a rigorous risk and control mindset and a focus on continuous improvement.
  • Mature leadership capabilities, with the ability to coach and develop a senior team and to influence stakeholders across Business, IT, Security and senior management.

- High level of integrity, accountability and professionalism, aligned with EFG’s values and culture.

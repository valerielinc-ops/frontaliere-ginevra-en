WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

From the beginning of August 2027, you will train for 3 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Manufacturing Capacity. During your apprenticeship, you will develop your skills in the machining and manufacturing of mechanical parts. You will learn how to make unit and serial parts using conventional and CNC machines, while developing your precision, autonomy and sense of quality.

Responsibilities

· Carry out machining operations on conventional and CNC machines · Manufacture unit and serial mechanical parts · Make the settings and start up machines · Read and interpret technical plans and documents · Control the quality and dimensions of the parts produced · Identify and analyse possible machining defects · Participate in continuous improvement of production processes · Collaborate with different trades and departments

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE · A good level of French in both oral and written skills to successfully complete vocational training · Good mathematical skills and interest in logical and technical reasoning · Interest, curiosity and motivation for mechanics, machining and new technologies · Manual dexterity, thoroughness and attention to detail · Autonomy, rigor and proactivity · Team spirit and relationship ease

Job Title: Manager, AI & Data (Data Management & Data Platforms focus) Business Line: Technology & Transformation Location: Geneva, Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80–100% Start date: Ongoing Experience Level: Experienced About the Role Help clients turn data into decisions and build the foundations to use it well. As a Manager in our AI & Data team, you will lead data management or cloud data platform work from strategy and architecture through implementation and adoption. You will work with clients and interdisciplinary teams to improve data quality, shape complex transformations and develop client relationships. You will also help junior colleagues grow while developing your own direction across clients, vendors and technologies. How you can make an impact

  • Lead clients in modernising their digital landscapes with cloud-based platforms and big data architectures, designing data management and engineering solutions that improve data quality and usability.
  • Develop data strategies aligned with clients’ regulatory, cost and revenue objectives, and shape complex data and analytics transformations through architectural, technical and organisational guidance.
  • Own projects from advisory and solution design through implementation and adoption at the client site, coordinating interdisciplinary teams and leading nearshore or offshore delivery where relevant.
  • Build trusted client relationships, identify opportunities to extend existing engagements and establish new relationships; shape go-to-market priorities with leadership and represent the practice at client events, conferences or through thought leadership.
  • Guide and develop junior colleagues, share your expertise and bring together cross-functional teams to deliver data management and engineering solutions. What you bring to succeed
  • At least six years of relevant experience in data governance, data management, data engineering or analytics, gained in consulting, technology or industry, including leadership of data and analytics projects and teams.
  • Strong technical and solution-delivery expertise in at least one of two pathways: data management, with several implementations involving master or reference data, data quality or catalogues; or cloud-based data platforms, with several data lakehouse implementations and experience designing and documenting platform architectures.
  • Hands-on experience taking solutions from vendor selection through implementation and operation, including coordination of nearshore or offshore teams; for the platform pathway, familiarity with common development methodologies and the ability to explain hybrid architecture options to senior stakeholders.
  • Ability to turn diverse business and technology needs into coherent solution designs, manage several projects and conflicting priorities, and communicate and present recommendations clearly in English.
  • Experience developing client relationships and contributing to the marketing and sale of consulting or project services. What sets you apart
  • Experience with relevant data management tools or cloud platforms, such as Collibra, Informatica, Azure, GCP, AWS, Snowflake or Databricks, depending on your specialist pathway.
  • Fluency in German and/or French in addition to English, helping you work with a wider range of clients and teams.
  • Experience contributing to market-facing thought leadership or representing your practice at conferences and customer events. Your team Join a diverse, multinational AI & Data team of more than 50 colleagues across Zurich, Geneva and Basel. The team works on how organisations structure, source, transform, store and use data, collaborating across disciplines on varied client projects. You can learn through training, mentoring and work with colleagues, clients and vendors across industries.

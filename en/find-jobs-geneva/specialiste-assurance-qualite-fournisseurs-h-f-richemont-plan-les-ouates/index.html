SPECIALIST INSURANCE QUALITY PROVIDERS (M/F)

Plan-les-Ouates

CSD contract

FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

Join one of the iconic houses of high watchmaking, where excellence is a constant requirement. Take part in our collective success and the creation of our future heritage.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

You are attracted to a versatile Quality Technician position and benefit from a successful experience in this area, this opportunity can meet your aspirations!

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

  • Leading quality performance with our partners: You will be the key link to ensure the deployment of the Quality Vacheron Constantin approach and ensure the dissemination of our requirements to suppliers.
  • Ensure conformity of purchased products: From the first prototype to serial life, you will ensure that our products comply.
  • Create and optimize acceptance control plans.
  • Provide support to production workshops during difficulties related to purchased components.
  • Pilot, monitor and validate action plans for supplier non-compliances or supplier progress plans. You will verify their relevance and implementation.
  • Conduct quality product and process audits with our suppliers.

QUALIFICATIONS FOR EXCELLING

Success in this role depends on

  • A typical Engineer training in Mechanical and/or Quality.
  • Your motivation for a rich role in a dynamic and developing Quality Assurance team.
  • Your autonomy in strategic decisions
  • Your ease with measurement means, problem solving methods, quality tools, project management, manufacturing processes and ideally the watchmaking field.
  • Your excellent ability to facilitate communication with internal teams and suppliers.
  • Your great ease with office software.
  • Your excellent mastery of French and good English skills.
  • Your experience as a quality technician in a watchmaking factory would be a real asset.

Vacheron Constantin celebrated 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.

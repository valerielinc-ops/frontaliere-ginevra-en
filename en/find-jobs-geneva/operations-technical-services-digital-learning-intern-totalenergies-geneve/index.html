Full description

Context & Environment: Are you looking to grow your career in the vibrant world of innovation in energy trading? Take your first steps into a fulfilling career and join our team for 6 months as an intern! Do you have a background related to Instructional Design, Education Technology or Digital Media? If yes, we would love to see your profile! Operations Technical Services ( OTS ), part of TotalEnergies Trading & Shipping is looking to hire an intern to support TotalEnergies’ ambitions and growth. By joining the team, the intern will support the NextGen Ops Learning initiative , aimed at transforming traditional operational training (currently in PPT/PDF format) into engaging microlearning video modules . This role is critical to reducing dependency on manual, expert-led training and creating a scalable, digital knowledge library for trading operators worldwide. Activities:

  • Content Conversion
  • Review existing PowerPoint decks, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and process notes to extract essential content.
  • Work hand-by-hand with training owners to define learning objectives and structure modules using microlearning principles.
  • Rewrite technical content into clear, operator-friendly language.
  • Develop storyboards and outlines for video modules.
  • eLearning Build
  • Create interactive learning modules using internal tools (e.g., VIBE, Synthesia).
  • Incorporate learning interactions such as click-to-reveal steps, scenario questions, and knowledge checks (Multiple-Choice Questions – MCQs, true/false, drag-and-drop).
  • Apply consistent templates for layout, navigation, and typography.
  • Visual Design & Animation
  • Redesign slides for clarity and visual hierarchy.
  • Produce simple animations and reusable visual assets (icons, diagrams).
  • Maintain a clean, professional design aligned with TOTSA standards.
  • Subject Matter Expert (SME) Review & Quality Assurance
  • Coordinate review cycles with SMEs.
  • Validate technical accuracy and compliance with internal standards. Perform QA checks for navigation, scoring, and accessibility. Objectives
  • Convert 80+ critical operational trainings into interactive, digital modules. Deliver high-quality, visually engaging microlearning content that accelerates onboarding and improves knowledge retention . Candidate Profile: Must-Have Skills
  • Ability to simplify content and communicate clearly.
  • Strong prompting skills.
  • Understanding of instructional format principles (objectives, practice, checks).
  • Attention to detail and quality mindset.
  • Comfortable working with SMEs and managing iterative feedback.
  • Basic motion graphics and visual design capability.
  • Fluent English Tools
  • PowerPoint (advanced level).
  • eLearning authoring tools (VIBE, Synthesia or similar).
  • Graphic design tools (Canva, Figma, Illustrator or equivalent). Education / Experience
  • Last year of Master degree in Instructional Design, Education Technology, Digital Media, UX, or similar.
  • Portfolio demonstrating PPT-to-eLearning conversion and visual design skills.
  • A tripartite agreement is mandatory Additional Information: TotalEnergies values diversity, promotes individual growth and offers equal opportunity careers.

Chemical production team leader 3x8 (m/f/d) - Your next job?

As Team Leader Chemical Production 3x8 (m/f/d), you are attached to our department Chemical Production and you report to our Chemical Production Supervisor. You will manage the day-to-day operations of the manufacturing team to ensure that the intermediate or finished products are delivered in accordance with established production specifications, while respecting safety, quality, cost and service level standards.

You're interested in this job? This could be the perfect opportunity if, as for us, your goal is to always meet the needs of customers and touch the emotions of the world around you.

Your Main Mission

Management of Chemical Production of Ingredients

  • You will be in charge of supervising the manufacture of ingredients in accordance with the manufacturing plan, following the operating procedures prepared by the production chemist in order to ensure a standard yield and quality;
  • You will assist the supervisor in the execution of administrative tasks;
  • You will intervene at the different stages of the process, control productivity and adjust material resources and collaborators if necessary;
  • You will ensure compliance with the manufacturing program, instructions and deadlines;
  • You will ensure that the installations and equipment are properly used and that the internal control regulations are implemented;
  • You will be the main point of contact for daily troubleshooting. You bring your support for the diagnosis of breakdowns;
  • You will be responsible for securing the installations before the troubleshooting and in charge of receiving the works;
  • You will act as support to the production chemist and supervisor to maintain cost competitiveness through technical or operational improvements.

Management

  • You will be in charge of supervising your team on a daily basis, communicating directives and information at daily meetings;
  • You will organize the distribution of work and staff according to the priorities and the various manufacturing stations;
  • You will train employees at their workstations and you will be in charge of raising them in skills with a view to continuous improvement;
  • You will monitor performance and evaluate your team with the support supervisor;
  • You will guarantee the respect of the rules of hygiene, safety and environment;
  • You will intervene and give guidance in relation to the company's policy on hygiene, security and environmental protection.

Your Profile

  • You benefit from a CFC of Technologist in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Production or from a title deemed equivalent;
  • You have 5 to 8 years experience in chemical production;
  • You are particularly well known for your mastery of chemical processes, trade-specific techniques and chemical installations;
  • You have perfect command of French. Proficiency in English would be an asset;
  • You have very good knowledge of computer tools such as Microsoft Office, SAP;
  • You have an excellent mindset of analysis and you have the strength to propose;
  • You are organized in nature with excellent priority management;
  • You have an excellent sense of communication and listening, you are naturally customer oriented;
  • You demonstrate a proactive attitude to changes and ongoing improvements to the site.

For safety and hygiene reasons, please note that wearing the beard is not allowed in the production functions.

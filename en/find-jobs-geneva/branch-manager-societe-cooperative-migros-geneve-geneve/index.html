Full description

Do you have experience in management or as a manager and know the Swiss market well? You want to join Migros Geneva, a company whose values and commitment you share. After a period of immersion, take the lead of one of our new stores that will soon open its doors. To the nearest customers, you inspire, develop and grow your team on a daily basis.

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, Sales Network, 1200 Geneva

Important remarks

The use of women aims for simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You drive your store with clarity and serenity: A clear vision, well-placed priorities and regular adjustments. Even when the pace accelerates, the course is held with solid performance.
  • You take a step back from the results: Indicators are closely followed, deviations quickly identified and then understood. Concrete actions are put in place with the team to improve results.
  • You care about the store experience: A welcoming, clear and pleasant space. The availability of products is guaranteed to make you want to come back.
  • You make your team grow on a daily basis: Accompaniment, encouragement and progression of each. Simple rituals install a collective energy, oriented towards the success and pride of the work well done.
  • You encourage initiatives: Ideas are listened to, trust is given and autonomy encouraged. The team dares to propose, test and change practices.
  • You set a solid and reassuring framework: Standards and safety are respected, Migros standards are embodied daily. The requirement combines simplicity for an efficient organization.

Required

  • 5 years of experience in retailing in Switzerland: with team management.
  • Completed studies (Uni/EFP, HES, ES): or significant experience in a management position within our cooperative.
  • Field Leader: Active in-store presence, ability to organize, decide and advance operations. Engage the team on a daily basis and set up a clear pace of work to achieve the objectives.
  • Control of numbers and direction of action: Monitoring of indicators, rapid identification of gaps and implementation of concrete actions. Ability to improve performance with simple and efficient levers.
  • Strong customer orientation: Field observation, adaptation of the offer and organization to ensure a smooth, practical and pleasant experience in store.
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

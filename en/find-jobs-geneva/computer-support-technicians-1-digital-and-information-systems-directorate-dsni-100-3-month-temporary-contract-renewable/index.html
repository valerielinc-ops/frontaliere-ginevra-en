Full description

Connected to the DSNI customer service you are responsible for ensuring the deployment and management of new computers for the entire institution, in collaboration with other service providers. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Deployment of new Smartphone models and recruitment management in our internal management tools (MDMs)
  • Transition of physical SIM cards to Esim
  • Support users in taking charge of their new hardware
  • Management of the restitution of old Qa smartphones and their reconfiguration, cleaning and labelling
  • Drafting of article for the knowledge base or documentation.
  • Information technology CFC or equivalent
  • Minimum 1 year experience in user support and/or computer testing.
  • Controlling Windows 11 operating system and Microsoft 365 office tools
  • Mobility throughout the Canton of Geneva and holder of the driving licence
  • Writing experience
  • Relationship skills and excellent sense of communication
  • Proactive, autonomous, dynamic and able to work as a team.

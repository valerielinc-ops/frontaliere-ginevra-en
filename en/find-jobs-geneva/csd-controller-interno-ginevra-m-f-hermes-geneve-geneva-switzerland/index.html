Full description

Background

Hermès has been present in Switzerland for more than 70 years and now operates a network of 8 stores in Switzerland. The Swiss subsidiary also manages an International Retail perimeter consisting of two shops in Central Europe, Poland and the Czech Republic. In order to support the growth of this network and to guarantee the highest standards of operational excellence, the Financial Directorate is looking for an Internal Control Retail Manager. Based in Geneva, this strong field and international position contributes to the security of operations, the protection of assets and the continuous improvement of processes throughout the network.

DETAILED MISSIONS

  • Pilot Internal Control Device Retail
  • Identify and assess the operational, financial and compliance risks of the shop network.
  • Define and deploy the annual internal control plan.
  • Conduct regular in-store reviews and follow associated action plans.
  • Control key processes: sales, receipts, inventories, inventories, returns, discounts and asset protection.
  • Analyze incidents, inventory discrepancies and fraud risks to strengthen prevention mechanisms.
  • Ensure proper application of Group procedures and local regulations.
  1. Supporting Projects and Transformation Retail
  • Integrate risk management issues into the opening, renovation or transformation of shops.
  • Participate in the evolution of Retail processes and tools (POS, stock, omnichannel, clienteling).
  • Assess the risks associated with new projects and define appropriate controls.
  • Ensure compliance of deployed devices with Group standards.
  1. Developing risk culture and internal control
  • Train and accompany boutique teams and managers.
  • Raise awareness of fraud, compliance, asset protection and inventory management issues.
  • Disseminating good practices and promoting continuous improvement.
  • Monitor incidents and share feedback within the network.

Profile

  • Bac +5 (Business School, University or equivalent).
  • Minimum 3 years experience in external audit, internal audit or internal control.
  • Experience in the popular Retail.
  • Good understanding of store operations and asset protection issues.
  • Excellent analytical, organizational and communication capabilities.
  • Autonomy, pragmatism and sense of the ground.
  • Mastery of Microsoft Office tools; knowledge of Cegid an asset
  • Mastery of French and English

Key points of the post

  • International Perimeter of 10 clean shops.
  • Strong field presence and proximity to Retail teams.
  • Transversal post without direct management.
  • Regular movements.
  • Key role in securing operations and maintaining standards of excellence.
  • Open mission to the Eole mobility programme

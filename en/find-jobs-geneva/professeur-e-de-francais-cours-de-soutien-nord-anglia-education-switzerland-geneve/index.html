Full description

MAIN OBJECTIVES OF THE POST

As a French teacher in our extracurricular department, you are responsible for teaching private lessons to our students after school, starting at 4 p.m. Your goal is to connect students' knowledge with their learning and develop ways to encourage and inspire students to deepen their knowledge and understanding. You follow the themes and skills with the class teacher of each student.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Provide individual review and support courses according to the needs of students and areas requiring reinforcement.
  • Track themes and skills with each student's classroom teacher.

SKILLS AND APTITUDES

You have a university degree in French teaching and a teacher qualification. A prior experience in international school is an asset as well as knowledge of the French Baccalaureate and Swiss Maturity. You are able to work closely with a team of dedicated colleagues.

ESSENTIAL INFORMATION

It is important that the incumbent of the position be always aware of child protection guidelines and that he/she observe a professional distance with students while developing effective and caring working relationships. You understand and accept the importance of diversity and integration and oppose any form of discrimination. Background surveys and reference checks for the past 10 years will be conducted during the selection process.

WHO ARE US

The Collège du Léman is one of Switzerland's most prestigious international boarding schools and externates, located on Lake Geneva, overlooking the Swiss Alps. We welcome children between the ages of 2 and 18 who are part of an international community of more than 120 different nationalities.

Our vision is to shape generations that value differences and give our students the power to change the world for the better.

We cultivate individual strengths through our outstanding educators and the wide variety of opportunities we offer in our program and activities.

The Collège du Léman is part of the Nord Anglia Education family, which has over 80 premium quality schools around the world.

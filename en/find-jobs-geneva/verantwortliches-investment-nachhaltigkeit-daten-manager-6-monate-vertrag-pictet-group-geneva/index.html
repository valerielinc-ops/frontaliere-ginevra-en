Full description

Your team

Pictet Wealth Management combines more than 200 years of Swiss banking heritage with global investment expertise. The partner-owned financial services group provides a comprehensive offering for wealthy individuals and families, including discretionary and advisory investment solutions and family office services.

We are seeking a diligent and insightful Responsible Investment Analyst to join our Research team. In this role, you will lead PWM’s proxy voting programme, including voting analysis, recommendation development, and coordination of voting decisions, while overseeing relationships with proxy voting and stewardship service providers. You will also manage the sustainability data ecosystem supporting investment and reporting activities and help integrate new sustainability indicators into investment tools and processes.

The role requires a strong understanding of responsible investment, particularly proxy voting, as well as knowledge of ESG data providers, sustainability frameworks, and reporting standards. You should be comfortable operating across investment, sustainability, and technology functions, with the ability to manage complex data-driven projects and operational processes. Strong analytical, communication and stakeholder management skills will be essential.

Your role

Stewardship and Active Ownership

  • Lead PWM's proxy voting programme, including voting analysis, recommendation development and coordination of voting decisions.
  • Oversee relationships with proxy voting and stewardship service providers.

ESG Data Management

  • Manage the sustainability data ecosystem supporting investment and reporting activities.
  • Support the integration of new sustainability indicators into investment tools and processes.

Your profile

  • Strong understanding of responsible investment, especially related to proxy voting.
  • Knowledge of ESG data providers, sustainability frameworks and reporting standards.
  • Ability to bridge investment, sustainability and technology functions.
  • Experience managing complex data-driven projects and operational processes.
  • Excellent analytical, communication and stakeholder management skills.

Starting date : 11 January 2027

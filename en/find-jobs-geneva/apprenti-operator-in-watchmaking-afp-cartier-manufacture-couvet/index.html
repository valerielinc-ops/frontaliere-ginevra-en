WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

From the beginning of August 2027, you will train for 2 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Professional Training for Operator in watchmaking. During your apprenticeship, you will develop your technical skills and discover different areas of watchmaking.

During this training, you will develop your skills by working on a variety of watch models, while acquiring essential know-how in terms of efficiency and productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Mastering the assembly of mechanical, automatic and quartz movements
  • Make the watches dressed: placing dials and needles, fitting and fastening the bracelet
  • Acquire technical basis for polishing
  • To learn about the fundamentals of micromechanics
  • Participate in various practical internships in our workshops and partner companies.

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

  • Sufficient knowledge of French in both oral and written form to enable the successful completion of vocational training
  • Mathematical skills and interest in logical and technical reasoning
  • Interest and curiosity and motivation for watchmaking products and precision mechanisms
  • Manual dexterity, autonomy and rigour
  • Faculty of concentration, patience and logical reasoning
  • Team spirit and relationship ease

YOUR EXPERIENCE WITH US

If you are motivated, we will be happy to review your complete application, which will be reviewed by our recruitment and training teams. If your profile gets our attention, you will be invited to a discovery day.

At the end of this first stage, and if your interest in watchmaking is confirmed, we will invite you to a day of recruitment focused on various practical and theoretical tests.

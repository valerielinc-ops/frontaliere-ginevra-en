Full description

Introduction

For our Commercial Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Terminator whose main mission will be to ensure the termination activities within the workshop on cases, bottoms, glasses, bracelets and clasps.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare parts before processing, including disassembly of components
  • Perform polishing, satining, deburring, smoothing and finishing operations according to technical instructions and defined ranges
  • Ensure the aesthetic quality, accuracy and conformity of the parts processed, in compliance with the fineness and finishing requirements specific to watchmaking
  • Check the surface state and detect any defects
  • Make the necessary alterations, rectifications or corrections to ensure perfect final rendering
  • Accurately comply with safety rules and quality standards in the workshop
  • Use manual tools, polishing machines and consumables suitable for different watch materials (steel, gold, silver, titanium and other alloys)
  • Treat steel, gold, silver, titanium, or other alloys
  • Visually monitor and touch the surface condition of the parts to detect any defects, scratches, marks or deformation.

Profile

  • SWC of polisher or equivalent, supplemented by a specific training in watchmaking termination
  • Minimum 5 years in watch polishing, with a proven mastery of finishing techniques (polishing, satining, deburring) and a solid knowledge of high quality requirements and surface conditions
  • Expert use of hand tools and finishing machines adapted to watch parts
  • Accurate handling of fine, fragile or small components
  • Fine detection of visual and tactile defects
  • Careful reading and application of technical guidelines, tolerances and quality procedures
  • Strict application of safety and cleanliness rules
  • Personal qualities: great thoroughness, rigor, patience, dexterity and a sense of detail, combined with a good ability to concentrate and calm. Autonomy, discretion and team spirit complete this profile.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

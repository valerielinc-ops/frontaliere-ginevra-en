Description of post

Within the Department of Medicine (CAF) Admission and Billing Centre team, you are in charge of admissions. You ensure the reception and admission of patients according to the procedures and rules in force. You inform any person who comes to the admissions and are the guarantor(s) of administrative identification. You manage releases related to non-compliant information. Your interpersonal skills and communication skills allow you to welcome all the patient with an excellent quality of service. In particular, you know how to act diplomatically and calmly in the face of a discontented or nervous person. As the first contact, you embody through your work the image of the institution. In addition, you ensure the coordination of transportation for patients who need it. In the same way, you offer a personalized service to the precarised patientel in collaboration with internal support partners such as social assistants or the complex case cell. Within the team, you will join in a continuity of service dynamic by taking over from your colleagues in case of absence and promoting the exchange of information within the team. You do not hesitate to improve your activities by seeking adequate help. More generally, you contribute to all activities aimed at improving the quality of services

Qualifications

You are in possession of an employee's or business employee's CFC or equivalent training. You have perfect command of the French language. You have knowledge of English or another language. You master HUG software (DPA, GRECO, DPI, etc.). You justify prior experience in an area of care and/or health. You have the ability to work as a multidisciplinary team. You are able to undertake and manage several tasks simultaneously, have a sense of organization, planning and priorities. You have tact, courtesy and a keen sense of human relations. You have strong stress resistance and discretion.

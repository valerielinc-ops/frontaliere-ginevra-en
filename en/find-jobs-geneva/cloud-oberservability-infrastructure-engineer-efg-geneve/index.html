Job Description

Join our Infrastructure Engineering team to design, implement and bring into operation a cloud-based observability platform for public cloud services and applications. You will also operate the existing on-premises observability environment and contribute to the engineering, automation and continuous improvement of the team’s infrastructure platforms.

Key Responsibilities

  • Define the cloud observability architecture, including integration patterns, reusable telemetry ingestion, secure application connectivity and monitoring standards.
  • Implement metrics collection for public cloud compute, networking, storage, managed platform services and data analytics platforms.
  • Onboard applications to the observability platform, collecting logs, service health metrics, performance indicators, and error and exception events.
  • Operate the existing on-premises observability environment.
  • Establish alert categories, severity levels, notification channels, ownership and escalation procedures so the appropriate teams know when and how to respond.
  • Build executive dashboards for service health, operational dashboards for troubleshooting and service-specific dashboards for deeper analysis.
  • Contribute to the engineering, automation and continuous improvement of Linux, container orchestration platforms, infrastructure automation solutions and application delivery controllers.
  • Collaborate with application and operational teams to troubleshoot issues and document observability solutions.

Requirements and Qualifications

  • Education: Minimum and preferred qualifications are to be confirmed.
  • Professional experience: Hands-on experience with enterprise observability platforms, public cloud platforms, cloud-native monitoring, Linux and infrastructure automation. The required number of years is to be confirmed.
  • Technical and functional knowledge: Experience with observability search and analytics, dashboards, agents, telemetry ingestion and alerting; working knowledge of networking, APIs and TLS; and strong troubleshooting, documentation and collaboration skills.
  • Preferred knowledge: Experience in one or more of container orchestration platforms, enterprise automation platforms, application delivery controllers, data analytics platform monitoring or Infrastructure as Code. Expertise across all platforms is not required.
  • Languages: Language requirements and proficiency levels are to be confirmed.

Description of post

As an engineer specializing in hydraulic heating, you strengthen the HVAC sector. The team, composed of 5 technicians and a group leader, specialises in the various technical areas of the building: production and distribution of overheated water, steam, sanitary hot water, heating and ice water. You thus evolve in an environment that is both demanding and innovative. In this role, you conduct pre-project studies by analysing feasibility, identifying needs, estimating costs and defining planning. You write detailed specifications and propose technical solutions that enable informed decision-making. You ensure a rigorous framing of the projects, thus guaranteeing precise validation of the objectives and controlled progress You also prepare the necessary bids for launching public tenders In partnership with employees in your sector and other sectors of the owner's area, you evaluate technical solutions by selecting the most suitable equipment and developing detailed projects You ensure active supervision by ensuring compliance with the laws, standards and regulations in force at each stage You are a proposal force for optimizations and improvements concerning your techniques You are a consultant or consultant in your field by participating in the analysis, validation and monitoring of the technical operation of the facilities and projects mainly in the AIS 1.2 and 6 phases. You are involved in the continuous improvement of the sector and service by participating in the development of guidelines, procedures and other standard documents allowing for the homogeneity of the services offered. You ensure a technological and regulatory watch in order to anticipate the necessary developments of the facilities in relation to medical requirements and safety standards You represent, within the framework of the major institutional projects, your sector and the techniques for which you are responsible In the various sessions, you discuss with your colleagues and give feedback to your hierarchy in order to optimize the management of your projects.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree in engineering or equivalent in thermal engineering. You have a solid experience (minimum 5 years) in managing complex technical facilities, ideally in critical environments (hospital, industry, public infrastructure). You have excellent control of the standards and regulations, as well as technical expertise related to the installations in your field. Comfortable in demanding environments, you know how to make decisions, prioritize and manage emergency situations with cold blood. You master GMAO tools and common office tools. An experience in industrial distribution networks (overheated water, steam, cold, etc.) is an asset. A good command of French is essential.

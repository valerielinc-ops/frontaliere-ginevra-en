Full description

Introduction For our Production division, we are looking for a Supply Chain Team Manager whose main mission will be to manage a production flow in terms of planning and supply, ensuring the overall objectives of the Supply Chain. Responsibilities Team management:

  • Give meaning: communicate strategic directions and set ambitious and realistic individual objectives.
  • Support: support the professional development of employees through coaching, feedback, and training.
  • Unite: establish a climate of trust, respect, and cohesion, promoting intra/inter-service cooperation.
  • Value: recognize successes and encourage initiative-taking.
  • Lead change: anticipate, explain, and support evolutions (tools, processes, organization). Operational management:
  • Supervise processes: manage the planning of internal and external flows until the validation of production and supply plans; control inventory and anticipate risks.
  • Manage performance and challenges: monitor reporting tools to ensure the achievement of objectives; analyze delivery discrepancies, co-build, and formalize corrective action plans with internal/external partners.
  • Ensure the introduction of new products and new processes in production flow planning.
  • Anticipate resources: adapt the organization and recruitment needs.
  • Continuously improve: adjust processes to increase efficiency in the face of new operational challenges. Profile
  • HES, EPF engineering degree or equivalent
  • Confirmed experience in Management and Supply Chain in the industry
  • Proven practice in change management
  • Natural leadership, listening skills, and empathy
  • Managerial courage and situational intelligence
  • Excellent communication, ability to motivate and unite teams Conclusion We offer a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working hours, and top-notch social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application file (CV, cover letter, certificates, and diplomas). FocusManager

Your mission

  • Perform and supervise aesthetic and dimensional controls.
  • Ensure the monitoring of parts, quality documents and non-compliance.
  • Upgrade control procedures and self-control plans.
  • Coordinate, support and evaluate the control team.
  • Participate in improving methods and means of control.

  • Work as a customer referent and quality suppliers.
  • Ensure compliance with ISO 9001 and internal quality system requirements

And now we're talking about you?

  • Good knowledge of ISO 9001 and non-compliance management.
  • First positive experience in managing a team.

  • Excellent level of French and good knowledge of technical English.
  • Rigor, organization, precision and ability to work transversally.

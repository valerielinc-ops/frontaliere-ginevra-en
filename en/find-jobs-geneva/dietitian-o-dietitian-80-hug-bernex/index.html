Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Care Branch embodies the authority for care. Its mission is to ensure the quality and safety of care within the institution. As such, the Directorate of Care has the right to look at all the activities of care (nurses, nurses and various health professionals). To fulfil its mission, it relies on three areas of expertise: quality, sustainability and digital innovation; clinical expertise and professional practices; management and transformation.

Description of post

You ensure the hospital and outpatient care of patients followed in rehabilitation and geriatrics at the Loex site, ensuring a careful, structured and tailored approach to individual needs. You also ensure a specific nutritional care for rehabilitation, with an interprofessional approach and shared care minutes. You contribute to the harmonization of care practices, ensuring compliance with the recommendations of the expert societies and actively participating in the continuous improvement of the quality of care. You work closely with the specialized multidisciplinary team and take part in the clinical and functional colloquiums, as well as the interprofessional colloquium on rehabilitation, in order to support a coordinated and coherent approach around the patient. You ensure, where necessary, the replacement of colleagues in the other units of the Loex hospital site, ensuring continuity and quality of services. Finally, you actively contribute to the training of students and health professionals.

Qualifications

You have an HES dietician or dietician training or an equivalent title (recognition Red Cross), and you have mastered the computer tools necessary to carry out your activity. You have a good command of English. You master indirect calorimetry measurements as well as bioimpedancemetric techniques for the assessment of body composition. You have already taken care of the geriatric patientel, in situations of rehabilitation and comfort care. You demonstrate strong skills to work as a team, to argue and negotiate in an interprofessional environment. You are comfortable in communication and have the ability to quickly integrate into a new team. Your reflexive attitude, your intellectual and professional curiosity as well as your autonomy in the management of time and organization complete your profile.

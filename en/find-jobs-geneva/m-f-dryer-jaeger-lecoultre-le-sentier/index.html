YOUR MISSION

Press and attach diamonds and other precious stones on jewellery, objects, watch components or glasses, in accordance with quality, delay and safety requirements.

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

  • On production:
  • Perform crimping operations on watch and jewellery components
  • Use manual crimping techniques: grain, traditional, baguette, closed, claw, invisible
  • Ensure conformity and quality of delivered parts
  • On the news:
  • Working on small series
  • Participate in the industrialization of new products
  • On the workshop:
  • Working as a team with the other crafts of the workshop
  • Be a force for proposals for improvement

THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS? YOUR SKILLS!

  • Experience in the field of indispensable crimping, ideally in a watchmaking environment, preferably between 3-5 years of practice,
  • A training in jewellery, is a plus.
  • You are passionate and you are demanding, thorough and accurate in your work.
  • You like working as a team and have good communication skills.
  • You are open to the participation of events for the presentation of the profession.

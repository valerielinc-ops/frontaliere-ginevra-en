Main responsibilities

  • Identify and assess safety risks in the environment of industrial control systems (ICS/OT, IoT, IT)
  • Develop and implement safety policies and procedures for industrial control systems
  • Design OT system architectures using "default security" and "zero trust" principles
  • Modernize current architectures to integrate cloud and artificial intelligence systems
  • Install and configure appropriate security measures to protect industrial control systems
  • Secure integration of equipment into IT infrastructure
  • Conduct technical audits and intrusion tests on ICS/OT systems and their IT environment
  • Be the preferred point of contact for cybersecurity advice and topics in the areas of: Technical Building Management (GTC), Industrial Control Systems (ICS), Automatic Storage Systems, Research & Development (R&D) equipment
  • To carry out technological monitoring of advances in safety ICS/OT, IoT, IT and good practice
  • Participate in the monitoring of industrial control systems to detect suspicious activities and respond to security incidents Profile

Full description

Introduction For our Information Systems division, we are looking for a Business Analyst – Basic Data. Your mission will be to bridge between business requirements and technical solutions to ensure the excellence of our reference data. You will be a key player in the design, governance and evolution of our data repository, in our SAP environment. The strategic data scope will be linked to Items/products & prices, business partners (customers/suppliers) and production (e.g. nomenclatures, ranges). Responsibilities As a functional reference for basic data, you will intervene in the following areas: Analysis & Design

  • Collect, analyse and challenge business team needs regarding and assess their impacts on end-to-end processes
  • Design and model governance processes (creation, modification, validation) in SAP MDG: workflows, management rules, roles and responsibilities
  • Guarantee the quality of the company's master data
  • Write functional specifications
  • Monitor developments and integrations (SAPs and non-SAPs) to ensure that they fully meet expressed needs Quality & Deployment
  • Organize, execute and document functional testing and recipe phases
  • Actively participate in data migration projects: analysis of the existing, definition of mapping and recovery rules, coordination of the "cutover" and post-start support ("hypercare")
  • Establish and analyse data quality indicators (completion, uniqueness, freshness) to drive continuous improvement Support & Watch:
  • Train and support end-users to ensure adoption of new processes and tools (guide writing, functional support)
  • Ensure technological and methodological monitoring of the best practices of Master Data Management and the evolution of SAP solutions Profile
  • Level of study: Engineer or Master (informatics, data or equivalent)
  • Minimum 3-5 years of experience on SAP topics, including significant experience on governance and quality of baseline data
  • Object Knowledge Article/Product, Business Partner Client, Supplier and, ideally, Production Data (PP)
  • Appreciated knowledge: SAP MDG, S/4HANA
  • Skills: workshop facilitation, formalization (process, management rules, specifications), multi-stakeholder coordination, quality-oriented approach and compliance
  • Qualitys: rigor, autonomy, sense of service, pragmatism, excellent relational
  • Languages: current French; professional English Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

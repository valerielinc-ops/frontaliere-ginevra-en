Full description

Who you are

As a member of the interior design department, you will create relevant, functional, aesthetic and commercial furnishing solutions that will help position the IKEA brand on the local market as the leader in home living.

  • You have a training in interior design/architecture, a passion for home furnishing, curiosity and an understanding of people's daily lives at home.
  • You have a creative mind, a strong commercial sense and a strong sense of aesthetics.
  • You actively seek to improve your working methods in a simple, efficient and economical way.
  • You have a practical approach to your tasks and are able to work in a constantly changing business environment.
  • You have a good knowledge of computer science and experience using appropriate software.
  • You have strong communication skills in French (mother tongue) and English.

Your responsibilities

  • Apply your furniture knowledge and experience of interior decoration to ensure that the rooms and other supports are fitted out that will exceed the expectations of those visiting the store.
  • Use the richness of the IKEA product range to reflect a wide variety of styles, life situations and price levels that meet many individual tastes and needs.
  • Contribute to the content of the department's action plan with initiatives that will develop the activity and support long-term profitability.
  • Work closely with your sales and logistics team and colleagues to ensure an attractive Swiss store every day.

About this industry

Where others see furnishings, we see clever solutions to improve daily life. With a lot of passion, imagination and brainstorming, we transform colours, textiles and furniture into inspiring atmospheres that inspire, attract and convince people visiting our IKEA stores that they can reproduce the same in their home.

Product Owner - AI-Powered • Geneva, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As Product Owner in our Artificial Intelligence Department, you own the backlog of a strategic legacy transformation program: complex functional modules, rebuilt one at a time over several years, with requirements shaped by a redesign of the bank's core processes together with senior stakeholders. You use the bank's internal AI ecosystem to analyze requirements, draft acceptance criteria and speed up every step of your work. You will join our newly established Artificial Intelligence Department and contribute to a strategic, multi-year legacy transformation program that modernizes core banking processes for our private banking business. You will be part of the AI team, leverage the bank's internal AI knowledge, tools and private-LLM ecosystem, and use AI to power your day-to-day work - while the focus of the role is on delivering the transformation, not on building AI products.

Your Responsibilities

  • Own and prioritize the module-by-module backlog, translating redesigned processes into build-ready requirements with business SMEs (compliance, relationship management, operations)
  • Define clear acceptance criteria per module and coordinate UAT with business users across our international locations
  • Drive the progressive cutover: each shipped module replaces its legacy function, so sequencing decisions follow business priority
  • Coordinate with third-party suppliers on integration contracts and acceptance
  • Use AI-powered tooling from the bank's internal AI ecosystem for requirements analysis, user-story generation, backlog refinement and stakeholder communication
  • Support data-migration rehearsals and user training in the later program phases, together with QA and UX/UI

Your Profile

  • University degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, or a related field
  • Minimum 5 years of experience as a Product Owner in complex software delivery, ideally in banking or another regulated industry
  • Deep understanding of client onboarding, KYC/AML or core-banking processes is a strong advantage
  • Proven ability to work with senior stakeholders, translate business needs into requirements, and hold sign-off gates
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools (LLM assistants, AI analysis and documentation tooling) in your daily workflow
  • Strong communication skills in English; other languages are an advantage

Our employees are our most valuable asset, we are proud of our unique culture that fosters entrepreneurship and is fueled by a high team spirit and the positive attitude of each individual.

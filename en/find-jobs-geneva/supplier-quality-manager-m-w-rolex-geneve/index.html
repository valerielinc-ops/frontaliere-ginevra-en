Full description

Introduction For our Production Division , we are looking for a Supplier Quality Officer (H/F), whose main mission will be to orchestrate the necessary support to develop the performance of our supplier partners. In addition, he/she will provide expertise in support of the supplier inspection team when resolving complex issues. Responsibilities

  • Conduct audits with our suppliers to support and support improved performance
  • Developing and piloting an audit plan by involving all stakeholders
  • Provide methodological support on quality tools and approaches available to our suppliers
  • Ensure smooth and effective communication with all contributors involved
  • Work closely with the supplier inspection team
  • Work with suppliers on root cause analysis and implementation of remedies for complex issues
  • Use statistics and analyse data to refine decision-making
  • Take part in cross-cutting projects aimed at continuous improvement
  • Develop and adjust, if necessary, quality tools to best meet user expectations Profile
  • Diploma of engineer HES/EPF, mainly quality or business management
  • Confirmed experience of at least 5 years in the field of Quality Assurance suppliers
  • Solid course as listener
  • Excellent understanding of how a company works
  • Demonstrated mastery of quality tools and methodologies (such as 8D, Ishikawa, AMDEC, QRQC, MSP) and audit techniques
  • Rigor, spirit of analysis and autonomy marked
  • Excellent communication skills, both oral and written
  • High adaptability, availability and team spirit
  • Leadership in group animation and pronounced customer service
  • Mobility allowing frequent travel over several days Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

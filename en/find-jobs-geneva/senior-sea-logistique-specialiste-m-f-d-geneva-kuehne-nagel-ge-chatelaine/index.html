Senior Sea Logistics Specialist (m/f/d) - Geneva — Kuehne+Nagel

Full description

As a Senior Sea Freight Forwarder at Kuehne+Nagel, you play a vital role in keeping global supply chains moving. From luxury goods and industrial machinery to toys and critical spare parts, every shipment you manage helps businesses succeed and people enjoy life's important moments. By optimizing transport solutions and delivering on our customers' promises, you create value far beyond logistics. At Kuehne+Nagel, every contribution makes a difference, often in ways we don't even realize.

‎

How you create impact

  • Manage customer relationships and act as the primary contact for sea freight and selected air freight shipments.
  • Coordinate the end-to-end shipment process, ensuring timely and accurate execution.
  • Prepare quotations, monitor shipments, and proactively resolve operational issues.
  • Collaborate closely with operations, sales, carriers, and customers to deliver service excellence.
  • Support and guide colleagues, acting as a key point of contact when the manager is away on customer visits or business development activities.
  • Contribute to business growth by identifying improvement opportunities and promoting a proactive, entrepreneurial mindset.

What we would like you to bring

  • Completed freight forwarding apprenticeship or equivalent logistics qualification.
  • Strong experience in international freight forwarding, particularly sea freight; air freight experience is an advantage.
  • Solid understanding of import/export, customs, and international transport processes.
  • Previous experience in a senior, coordinating, or informal leadership role.
  • Commercial awareness, ownership mentality, and an entrepreneurial approach to business development.
  • Fluent French and English; German is an asset.

What's in it for you

  • Attractive compensation package, including a 13th-month salary.
  • Modern office facilities with fitness, gaming, and kitchen areas.
  • Professional and personal development opportunities, including language courses.
  • Employee discounts across a wide range of partner brands.
  • Team events and employee referral bonus program.
  • Opportunity to take on a visible senior role with leadership responsibility and growth potential.

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.

Full description

Introduction

We are looking for a Polyvalent Injection Operator (H/F) whose responsibility will be to manufacture Ceramic Glasses and Discs on different production methods.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure the manufacture of ceramic glasses and discs on an injection press.
  • Ensure the packaging of ceramic discs and glasses for heat treatment operations.
  • Ensure the coloration of ceramic discs using a semi-automatic machine.
  • Perform serial changes, machine settings, and production tracking independently.
  • Pilot the production of several injection machines.
  • Conditioning of ceramic watchpieces, including recessing and de-encasting.
  • Loading and unloading ovens for heat treatment.
  • Semi-automatic machine production for the colouring of parts.

Profile

  • Level or CFC injection.
  • Mandatory significant experience in injection.
  • Engaged, with a strong team spirit.
  • Dynamic.
  • Autonomous and powerful proposal.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).

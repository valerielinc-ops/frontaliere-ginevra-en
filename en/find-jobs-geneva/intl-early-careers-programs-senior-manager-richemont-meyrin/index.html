  • HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT Are you driven by the curiosity to design, implement, and scale cutting-edge talent acquisition strategies and engagement models that attract and develop the best young-career talent to meet long-term organizational objectives?
  • The Early Careers Senior Manager plays a pivotal role in defining, leading, and executing Cartier’s Early Careers Strategy and scale programs that attract, train, and retain entry-level talent, interns, and recent graduates encompassing apprenticeships, internships, graduate rotation programs and VIEs.
  • The role focuses on building a robust and diverse talent pool to address future business needs across key functions while attracting nurture and providing exceptional experiences for Early Careers professionals, ensuring these programs effectively support Cartier's growth trajectory Acting as a central point of contact regarding the Early Careers strategy and being involved in Talent Acquisition initiatives, you will work closely with diverse team members and stakeholders across regional and market entities, Manufacturing and Central, contributing to impactful initiatives.
  • This role primarily focuses on: Drive Early Careers’ Global Strategy Define the vision and objectives for early careers, aligned to long-term talent and business needs.
  • Anticipate future business needs to identify and develop relevant early careers programs that build an effective pipeline of key talent across critical functions.
  • Ensure strategy reflects local market realities, regulations, and cultural differences while keeping global consistency.
  • Program Management Develop structured high impact early careers programs and implement robust recruitment and selection criteria to ensure the onboarding, placement, and professional development of interns, graduates, and apprentices.
  • Design experiences that balance on-the-job learning, mentoring, and structured development.
  • Maintain quality and consistency of delivery while allowing flexibility for local adaptation.
  • Talent acquisition and Pipeline building Develop and implement innovative sourcing strategies across online, offline, and social channels specifically tailored for early careers talent.

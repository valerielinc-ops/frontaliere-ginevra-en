This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You are open-minded, ambitious and using a forward-looking and leading-edge approach to the real estate sector is important to you? At Livit, you meet the expectations of owners and tenants with exemplary customer orientation, contributing to a lasting relationship of trust with customers and a positive image of the business. Ready to meet the challenges of today and tomorrow and move things around in the real estate world? You are in the right place at Livit.

We are delighted with your personal contribution.

Aufgaben

  • Active and comprehensive management of a commercial building portfolio
  • Monthly payments to owners
  • Personal and telephone supervision of tenants
  • Negotiations with tenants and establishment of commercial leases
  • Make changes to leases and adjust rents and indexed rents
  • Monitoring of current rent policy and advising the owner on market rents
  • Representation of the owner before the authorities (conciliation, expulsion, etc.)
  • Maintain the building in accordance with the management contract
  • Periodic inspection of land with corresponding status reports
  • Status of entry and exit points when developing and changing tenants
  • Control and visa of invoices payable, accounts of stewardship and incidental costs

Anforderungen

  • Business learning with CFC or equivalent training
  • Federal building manager's patent (obtained or in progress) or continuing training in real estate
  • Several years of professional experience in real estate management in the commercial sector
  • Good knowledge of the law (Leasing Law, CO, CC, LP)
  • Mastering the French language in oral and written language with good editorial qualities, English an asset
  • Digital connectivity and ability to participate actively in change
  • Strong customer orientation and relationship management on a partnership basis
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action with a good mind

Wir bieten

  • Internal offer of specialized courses and management courses in our Academy of the University of Paris
  • Financial support and/or working time arrangements for external specialised training and access to e-learning platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career plan and opportunities for change
  • Home- / Mobile Office
  • Bonus based on the company's performance and personal performance
  • 5 weeks of vacation at least and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional days of vacation (Ferienflex)
  • Over-average pension benefits: Daily and accident insurance premiums are fully covered by the Pension.
  • 14 days of paternity leave

We love real estate and we are enthusiastic about maintaining the value of our customers' buildings.

If you work with Livit, you are part of the Livit family. We appreciate you for what you are and what you are doing. Indeed, your skills and experience are certainly crucial, but the person you are also counting on. You are evolving in our pleasant work environment, in which you can thrive. This is due on the one hand to the diversity of our job profiles and the possibilities of evolution within our institution, and on the other hand to its incomparable corporate culture, which is still unrivaled in the real estate world.

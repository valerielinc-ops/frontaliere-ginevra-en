Mechanical Technician for CMS (EP-CMS-IC-2026-226-GRAE) — CERN

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are recruiting two early-career graduates for Mechanical Technician positions within the CMS experiment. Both positions involve hands-on mechanical work and close collaboration with technicians, engineers, and physicists, but each will have a different main area of responsibility. One position will join the Electromagnetic Calorimeter (ECAL) team, supporting the refurbishment of the barrel electromagnetic calorimeter and covering a large panel of activities and responsibilities, from mechanical work and the handling and assembly of detector parts to logistic activities. The second position will join the CMS Technical Coordination crew, supporting mechanical activities for the CMS detector and its infrastructure, including maintenance, upgrades, tooling, and heavy-lifting operations.

Your responsibilities

Key Tasks ECAL position

  • Participate in the extraction and insertion of the ECAL supermodules, including the preparation, installation, and removal of the required tooling.
  • Carry out mechanical operations during the refurbishment of the supermodules, including the replacement of cooling fittings.
  • Support the installation of ECAL services and electronics and assist with related logistics.

Key tasks Technical Coordination position

  • Participate in mechanical work for the upgrade, maintenance, and repair of the CMS detector and its associated infrastructure.
  • Manufacture mechanical parts and contribute to on-site assembly, installation, alignment, and repair activities.
  • Support the development of tooling and the preparation or modification of technical drawings using drafting software such as CATIA.
  • Participate in heavy-lifting and movement operations using equipment such as strand jacks, air pads, and hydraulic jacks, and support regular checks and maintenance of this equipment.

Your profile

  • Initial hands-on experience in the construction and assembly of mechanical components and structures.
  • Experience in mechanical equipment or detector assembly, including cooling connections, pressure testing, or leak detection.
  • Experience using machine tools and hand tools for mechanical manufacturing activities.
  • Experience with mechanical systems and the handling of large, heavy, or fragile equipment.
  • Experience with drafting software, such as CATIA.

Skills

  • Knowledge of manufacturing techniques for mechanical parts.
  • Experience in the assembly and integration of mechanical structures, equipment, and systems.
  • Knowledge of the operation and maintenance of cooling-water systems.
  • Experience in the safe operation of mechanical handling systems and the application of safety procedures related to heavy machinery and mechanical operations.
  • Problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Clear communication skills and the ability to follow technical instructions accurately.
  • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the other language.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • By the application deadline, you have a maximum of two years of professional experience since graduation in Mechanics or related (or a related field) and your highest educational qualification is a general secondary education diploma .
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
  • Applicants with a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD degree are not eligible.

Job closing date: 12.10.2026 at 23:59 CEST. Contract duration: 24 months, with a possible extension up to 36 months maximum. Working hours: 40 hours per week Job flexibility: Fully Onsite Target start date: 01-December-2026

This position involves

  • Work in Radiation Areas.
  • Interventions in underground installations.
  • Residence in the immediate vicinity of the Organization's installations.
  • A valid driving licence.
  • Work during nights, Sundays and official holidays, when required by the needs of the Organization.
  • For the ECAL position: Exposure to risk of falling from heights during scaffolding assembly and dismantling operations.
  • For the ECAL position: Exposure to electromagnetic fields under certain conditions.

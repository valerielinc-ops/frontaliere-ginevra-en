Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: February 2027 You bring ambition, we provide opportunities. In Deloitte Audit & Assurance we protect the public interest and build trust in business by delivering leading assurance and living a culture that empowers people to thrive. As an Intern in our Assurance practice, you will gain hands-on experience working with leading companies across industries. You will support our teams in delivering high-quality services, while being exposed to advisory engagements to assist client with their reporting requirements and internal control framework. You work independently while benefiting from the expert advice of experienced colleagues. Your active contribution is pivotal, as you engage in shaping the future of our offerings and nurture your own professional growth. How you can make an impact

  • Gain insight into how organizations operate and the economic and industry challenges they are facing
  • Develop an understanding of accounting, auditing, internal control, reporting and regulatory frameworks
  • Support assurance projects delivering on their offerings, including for example, GAAP conversions, preparation of financial statements, monthly closing, management reporting and internal controls documentation
  • Work collaboratively with teams from other service lines to deliver insights and recommendations to clients
  • Develop people early on in your career and build a broad professional network
  • Bring your fresh ideas and pioneering thinking to the forefront early in your career at Deloitte
  • Go a clear, long-term career path and receive first-class training for your professional development, including support to qualify as Swiss certified public accountant (Swiss CPA) ( https://www.expertsuisse.ch/ausbildung ) What you bring to succeed
  • You are a curious, well-organized and empathetic problem solver with the ability to explain complex technical issues to a business audience in a relatable and understandable way
  • You are confident using digital tools and quick to adopt new technologies that help you work effectively
  • You enjoy interdisciplinary consulting teamwork
  • You have excellent university degree (Bachelor’s, Master's) Your team Experience our friendly, collaborative and international environment with plenty of room for you to connect to other people, grow and just be yourself.

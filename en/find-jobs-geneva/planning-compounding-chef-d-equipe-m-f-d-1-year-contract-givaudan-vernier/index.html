As a Planning Compounding Team Lead (m/f/d), you will be responsible for leading the raw material and production planning functions for compounding activities on site, optimizing end-to-end cycle times, inventory, site productivity and in compliance with EHS requirements, in order to supply customers on time.

Reporting to the Planning Manager in Vernier, your main responsibilities will be the following:

  • Strategy design and execution
  • Oversee the design and execution of tactical and operational plans at site level to meet local Operations objectives;
  • Agree planning standards and levels with production mid to long-term and enforce schedule adherence;
  • Lead a team of Raw Material or Compound Production planners and ensure efficient execution of the day-to-day Planning operations on site.
  • Subject matter expertise
  • Maintain an optimal level of inventory and write-offs that meets supply chain service level and cost objectives;
  • Control that response to Customer Care is made on time;
  • Oversee the development of short term material supply and capacity plans;
  • Analyze planning performance and improve the KPI's by using best practices;
  • Monitor, track and define actions to improve or maintain site planning performance indicators;
  • Act as escalation point for resolution of problems, complaints and order prioritization issues;
  • Monitor workload and calendars to maintain the balance within the team;
  • Work closely with all relevant stakeholders in the division and in the GBS, namely Replenishment and R&F teams, Supply Planning team, Logistics hub.
  • People management
  • Lead, manage and develop the Planning Compounding teams, also developing an effective local talent pipeline and solid succession planning.

You?

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Supply Chain Management, Operations Management or related discipline;

  • Fluent in English and French;

  • Strong affinity to systems and analytical tools (Desired SAP, BI, BO);

  • Min. 3 years of experience in people management;

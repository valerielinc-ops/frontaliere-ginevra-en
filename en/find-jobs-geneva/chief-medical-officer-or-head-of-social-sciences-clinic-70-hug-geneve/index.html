Full description

In the context of the DMCPR promoting access to care for all, including precarious populations (documentless, migrants, prisoners, etc.), your mission is to coordinate and support aspects of intercultural communication. You accompany and support teams facing situations of intercultural communication and complex care. You identify language needs, implement appropriate strategies (professional and professional interpreters, translation tools, etc.) and ensure institutional coordination with interpretation service providers. You also need to develop practical projects and tools to improve access to care. As such, you are conducting advisory, analytical and development projects and contributing to the development of the skills of professionals and professionals of the HUG on issues related to linguistic and cultural diversity. You are also led or led to conduct qualitative research (ethnography, observations, interviews) on social inequalities in health and to disseminate the results through publications, conferences, symposia, etc. In the field of training, you participate in the initial and continuing training of health professionals and professionals and in university education, in particular by developing innovative teaching tools. Finally, you independently guarantee quality medical services, both clinically and administratively and organizationally.

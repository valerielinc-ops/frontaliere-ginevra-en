Full description

More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, competence and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We're looking for our branch Environment in Grand-Lancy (GE) two trainees (m/f/d): one person for the Environment domain and one person for the Geology domain. Both courses are offered at 100%, for a duration of 6 months each. Your tasks

  • Participate in ongoing projects and office life, according to your orientation and mandates: Environment — Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), noise and air quality studies, urban climate and heat island analyses, waste management and resource recovery
  • Geology — geological and hydrogeological studies, pollution diagnosis, gravitational studies, sampling of polluted waters and soils and analysis of data from the subsoil and groundwater
  • Contribute to the collection of data, field surveys and measurements, and then to the processing and interpretation of results: environmental data, environmental data for the Environment course, geological data, piezometric samples and surveys for the Geology course, according to ongoing studies
  • Contribute to the preparation of data, calculations and analysis of technical modelling results, depending on your direction and mandate: e.g. Acoustic modelling, hydraulic modelling, topographic remodeling, etc. for the Environment stage; underground flows and interactions between rivers and aquifers for the Geology stage
  • Use GIS tools to analyse data and produce thematic plans and maps in the areas studied; contribute to the comparison of variants and the drafting of reports in order to transform the results into support for decision-making

Your profile

  • You are in the course of EPF training or equivalent training: in environmental science and techniques for the Environment internship, or in geology for the Geology internship. These courses are for those still in training; graduates cannot be retained
  • Good knowledge of GIS tools, acquired on ArcGIS or QGIS, and motivation to deepen their use in the analysis of environmental or geological data
  • To benefit from a driving licence
  • A curious and dynamic personality, with a sense of initiative and a taste for teamwork; rigor in data processing and motivation to gradually develop your autonomy over the tasks entrusted

In your application, we would like to thank you for specifying the desired internship — Environment or Geology — as well as your availability period and the topics you would like to explore. Offers of employment agencies are not taken into account in this recruitment. Consequently, CSD will not enter into any negotiation for the payment of any claim.

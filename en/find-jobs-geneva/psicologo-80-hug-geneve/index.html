Description of post

As part of your duties, you offer psychological follow-up as well as psychotherapy, both individual and group, and ensure that the difficulties encountered by patients are addressed. You develop appropriate care plans and interventions for therapeutic and preventive purposes, thus contributing to comprehensive and proactive care. Your activity is carried out within an interdisciplinary framework that promotes close collaboration with psychiatrists, nurses and other health professionals and professionals at the Centre ambulatoire de psychiatrie et de psychomérapie intégré. As such, you take an active part in clinical seminars, as well as in reflections and exchanges on care situations. More generally, you are evolving in an environment that values collaboration, clinical expertise and knowledge sharing, serving demanding and humane hospital care.

Qualifications

Holder of a Master's degree in Psychology (or equivalent), you are in receipt of a federal diploma as a psychotherapist or are engaged or engaged in advanced training to obtain it. You justify an experience of at least 3 to 4 years in psychotherapy, allowing you to develop strong clinical skills and a rigorous and structured practice. At ease in multidisciplinary team work, you actively contribute to the collective dynamic while demonstrating autonomy, reliability and a strong sense of responsibility in the performance of your function. You also have experience with an adult population with mental disorders. Interested or interested in research and the evolution of practices, you sign up for a continuous process of developing and updating your skills.

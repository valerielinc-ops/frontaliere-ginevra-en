Full description

Your team

Pictet Alternative Advisors (PAA), part of the Pictet Group, is a leading alternative investment specialist with expertise in hedge funds, private equity and real estate. The Geneva- and London-based team constructs, manages and advises on portfolios of alternative investment solutions for institutional and private clients. You will be joining a dynamic and growing private investment team. The whole team manages a portfolio of about 90 top-tier GPs with AUM of USD 32bn. In view of these developments, we are looking to hire a talented Private Equity Investment Specialist who will report to the Co-Heads of Investor Relations and will be based in Geneva with strong international exposure. The role will focus on marketing and distributing Private Equity investment solutions, as well as client servicing.

Your role

As part of a team of 3 senior professionals, your role will be:

  • Assisting with fundraising for the Multi Manager Private Equity (MM PE) business (funds and mandates) and developing the activity by:

o Presenting the MM PE offering to existing clients and prospects. o Organising the launch, promotion and fundraising of new funds. o Preparing tailor-made investment proposals. o Responding to institutional RFPs. o Preparing a range of marketing documents.

  • Providing external and internal clients (Client Relationship Managers) with relevant information, reporting and advice, while contributing to investment decisions.
  • Involving in ongoing IT projects as a key user.
  • Preparing reports and ad hoc updates for existing investors.
  • Running cashflow simulations and portfolio modelling.
  • Monitoring competitors.

Your profile

  • University degree in Finance, Economics or related field from a leading institution.
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role, acquired in a top tier Bank or asset manager.
  • Proficient user of new technologies and keen to explore AI or other tools available to increase your productivity and the servicing to our clients.
  • Excellent command of either English or French is required. A proficiency in any additional language is highly valued (such as Arabic, Russian or Mandarin).
  • Willingness and flexibility to travel frequently for business.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland or willing to relocate.

As the ideal candidate, you will be conscientious and open-minded, bringing a strong sense of motivation and exceptional interpersonal skills to your interactions with your stakeholders. Your priority will be to understand and fulfil their needs effectively. You will thrive both in independent work and as a collaborative member of a professional team, where your ability to synthesize ideas and your keen sense of discretion and ethics will be highly appreciated.

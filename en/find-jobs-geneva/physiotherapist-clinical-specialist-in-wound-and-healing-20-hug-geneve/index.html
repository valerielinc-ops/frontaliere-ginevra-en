Full description

The clinical specialists attached to or attached to the clinical expertise and professional practices of the Directorate of Care constitute a team of professionals expert in various areas of care and contribute through their actions to improve the practices and quality of care provided to patients within the institution. They are grouped by clinical specialty or program such as the wounding and healing program. At the University Hospitals in Geneva, the wound and healing program is attached to the clinician and professional practice expertise cluster of the Department of Care. The three objectives of the program are to reduce the prevalence of scars within the institution, to develop the expertise of the health care teams in the management of wounds and scars, and to promote the development, dissemination and implementation of evidence-based clinical recommendations, protocols and practices. To date, the team has been composed of a responsible physician, a researcher, two nurses and clinical specialists, and two nurses and specialists. Its members are involved as consultants and clinical experts with collaborators and patients. The team members ensure the implementation of the recommendations of good practices and accompany the collaborators in the development of their knowledge and skills in the field of wounds. The wound and healing program, in partnership with the Health Care Directorate, is looking for a physiotherapist who can, perform a function of expert or clinical leader in the area of wounds and healing to strengthen his team, directly in contact with clinical activity. You are cross-cutting across the various departments and across the entire HUG services, with patients and patients, adults and children with skin lesions such as ulcers from the lower limbs of multi-factorial etiology, pressure ulcers, post-operative or traumatic wounds in complex situations, as well as with care professionals, to ensure the patient is given optimal care. You develop treatment protocols and work on projects to improve clinical practices. You introduce up-to-date practices based on successful outcomes in care and therapy in collaboration with management cadres. You accompany the patient and support the care teams in the path of the person suffering from the wound, by clinical routes. With or with a real interest in teaching and coaching, you design and deliver training for different professionals and health professionals in the field of wounds, healing and compression. By your curiosity and updating your knowledge, you are involved in the development of knowledge in this field.

Place

Geneva, Personenverkehr( Geneva ) (C290 2G00), CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Some 14,000 colleagues in passenger traffic ensure that the more than 1.3 million CFF customers we carry each day have easy access to public transport and travel from point A to point B safely and punctually. Manoeuvring personnel, as a central occupational group, make an essential contribution in this regard. An innovative work environment awaits you, in which you will move the SBBs with your colleagues, as well as a varied and paid training. In addition, you have various opportunities for in-house continuing training, such as the training of manoeuvring engineer category A40. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work are also paid at home - for example, 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night missions, you also benefit from time supplements of between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. What you can move.

  • As a manoeuvring specialist, you help our customers travel reliably and on time by moving, training and decomposing passenger trains in accordance with the train training plan.
  • You direct individual maneuver movements and reposition train compositions.
  • Your responsibility for train preparation includes train preheating and air conditioning as well as brake testing.
  • You report irregularities, specific incidents or deviations to the appropriate department.
  • Thanks to your safety awareness, it goes without saying that you work according to the requirements.

What you bring.

  • For this demanding work in the team 24 hours a day, you are physically and mentally resistant and you like to work outside at all times.
  • You have completed basic training and you ideally have a professional apprenticeship. Professional experience in the railway sector or in public transport is a plus.
  • In your daily work, you have no problem using modern work tools.
  • In French, you are comfortable in both oral and written (minimum B2 level).

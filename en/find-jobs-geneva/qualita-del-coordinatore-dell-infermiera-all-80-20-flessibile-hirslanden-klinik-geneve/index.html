Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique La Colline Permanent

Reference Number: 75863

As Switzerland's largest medical network, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency services. First-class medical quality and individual well-being are at the centre of our concerns.

MAIN MISSIONS

ISO 9001:2026 Certification Pilot: Coordinate the implementation, maintenance and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System (QMS) according to the requirements of the standard.

Risk and Incident Management: Administer the Incident Reporting System (ICRS), conduct root cause analyses (non-compliances) and follow corrective action plans with medical teams.

Follow-up of patient experience: Pilot satisfaction surveys and quality indicators (e.g. ANQ measures) to transform patient returns into concrete improvements.

Digitalization and Document Management: Develop, deploy and optimize electronic tools dedicated to quality (protocol management, incident monitoring and dashboards).

Field support and projects: Raising awareness of good care safety practices and providing methodological support for cross-cutting projects in clinics.

YOUR PROFILE

You have a diploma in general care.

You have a graduate degree or a CAS/DAS in quality management, risk management or health management.

You benefit from an experience of at least 2 years in hospital project management.

You have excellent computer and office knowledge.

You appreciate teamwork, you are persevering and autonomous.

You have very good relational and editorial skills.

You are native French, with very good knowledge in English (spoken and written). German an asset.

WE OFFER YOU

In addition to an attractive workstation, you will integrate a dynamic team that is looking forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contracts. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

YOUR APPLICATION

The Human Resources Directorate is pleased to receive your full application (CV, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates).

Please note that for data protection reasons, we cannot take into account paper files (which will not be returned) or applications sent by e-mail. Therefore, please make your request exclusively online via our recruitment site.

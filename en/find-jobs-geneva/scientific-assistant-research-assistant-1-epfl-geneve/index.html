Full description

Mission

The Hummel Lab at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) is seeking a highly motivated Scientific Assistant / Research Assistant to support a project in the field of transcranial temporal interference stimulation (tTIS).

The successful candidate will contribute to the neuroscientific, technological, and organizational activities of a multidisciplinary project focused on non-invasive deep brain stimulation using tTIS. The project encompasses computational modelling, hardware development, validation studies, and translational neuroscience research.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Provide scientific, technological, and technical support for the implementation of the project.
  • Perform tTIS simulations using realistic head models.
  • Assist with the preparation, execution, and documentation of experimental studies.
  • Contribute to data collection, management, quality control, and preliminary analysis.
  • Support the coordination of research activities across multidisciplinary teams, facilitate communication between internal and external collaborators, and contribute to the timely progression of the project.
  • Assist in preparing ethics and regulatory documentation, study protocols, and project reports.
  • Contribute to literature reviews and scientific documentation.
  • Organize meetings, maintain project records, and support project management activities.
  • Support the recruitment and coordination of study participants, where applicable.

Profile

  • Master's degree (or equivalent) in neuroscience, neuroengineering, computation, or a closely related field, such as EPFL's Neuro-X Master's programme. Excellent academic results and relevant coursework in translational neuroengineering, advanced neuromodulation, neural interfaces, and computational neuroscience are highly desirable.
  • Required qualifications and experience:
  • Experience in non-invasive brain stimulation using tTIS.
  • Experience in personalized electric-field modelling of tTIS using realistic human head models.
  • Experience in personalized brain-temperature modelling for non-invasive brain stimulation using realistic human head models.
  • Experience in preparing ethics applications and protocols for tTIS studies.
  • Excellent programming skills, particularly in Python.
  • Experience in research projects and scientific data management.
  • Excellent organizational and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Experience in industry, particularly in a start-up environment, is an asset.

We offer

  • The opportunity to contribute to an ambitious, internationally visible research project at one of Europe's leading science and technology institutions.
  • A stimulating and collaborative multidisciplinary research environment.
  • Close interaction with researchers, engineers, clinicians, and external partners.
  • Exposure to cutting-edge developments in neuromodulation and neurotechnology.
  • Opportunities for professional development and the acquisition of highly valuable research skills.
  • Competitive employment conditions in accordance with EPFL regulations.
  • A dynamic workplace at EPFL's Geneva campus, located at Campus Biotech.

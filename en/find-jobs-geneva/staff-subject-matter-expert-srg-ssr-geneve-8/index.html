Full description

As of 1 April 2026, the newly created SRG staff will be responsible for the uniform management of all SRG staff tasks in cooperation with regional management and national departments. This includes the management processes of SRG, corporate development, optimization of processes, legal issues and partner management SRG.

This is your contribution.

As a member of the law firm (Team Central Services), you are committed to the following topics:

  • Establishment and strategic coordination of the management of authorities, including support for important regulatory dossiers (e.g. new concession of SRG)
  • Advising and supporting the staff responsible of the management members on content, conceptual, coordinative and formal issues (including deputy function in absence)
  • Participation in the planning and organisation of management meetings, including examination of documents
  • Creating challenging concepts and mandates for transversal topics and projects
  • Managing projects and performing ad hoc tasks on behalf of the staff
  • Active participation through forward thinking, early recognition of the need for action and proactive support of staff work

They inspire us.

  • Higher education (e.g. Master) is an advantage. You are also welcome with a comparable qualification, which you have acquired through in-depth, multi-year professional experience in a staff-like or strategic function.
  • Very fast comprehension and conceptual strength with a high degree of self-organization and willingness to provide services when completing the tasks
  • Structured, timely and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong ability to efficiently and purposefully implement tasks even in complex situations (“hands-on” mentality)
  • Very good oral and written language skills in French or German, good knowledge of the other language, Italian skills beneficial
  • Experience in a rod-like function desired
  • Expertise in dealing with authorities and regulatory-political dossiers is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • Interesting key role in the staff of SRG and close to the Group management
  • Collaboration with the various business units of SRG and insight into important strategic issues
  • Versatile participation and design possibilities in a new team with highly experienced experts
  • Hybrid working model with flexible working hours and the possibility for home office

We thank you in advance to submit your application exclusively via the SRG job portal. The application deadline is 18. November 2026.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Senior Sales & Account Manager Banking Romandie (80-100%)

Geneva, Zurich | Up to 40% home office | Reference 8257

Would you like to play an active role in expanding our customer relationships in Romandie? Do you have a strong network in the Swiss banking market and navigate confidently at the interface between customers, business and technology? As Senior Sales & Account Manager Banking Romandie, you will further develop existing customer relationships, tap into new business opportunities and successfully position our solutions in the areas of Cards, ATM, TWINT, Fraud & Dispute and Banking Services in the market. With your feel for customer needs and entrepreneurial mindset, you will make a significant contribution to the growth of our business in French-speaking Switzerland.

These are your responsibilities

  • Managing and further developing a portfolio of banks and financial institutions in Romandie
  • Building long-term customer relationships and identifying and acquiring new business opportunities
  • Developing and implementing customer and growth strategies, as well as actively shaping our market approach in Romandie
  • Positioning and marketing our solutions in the areas of Cards & Issuing Services, ATM Services, TWINT Services, Fraud & Dispute Services as well as Banking & Processing Services
  • Leading sales, proposal and contract processes through to successful completion and advising banks on strategic, operational and technical matters

What you bring

  • several years of experience in sales, business development or account management in the financial services environment, with in-depth knowledge of the Swiss banking market
  • a strong network at banks and financial institutions, preferably in Romandie, as well as a proven track record in generating new business and developing customer relationships
  • Understanding of modern banking, payments and platform solutions, as well as the ability to convey technical and business topics appropriately to the target audience
  • strategic, entrepreneurial and self-reliant personality with strong execution skills, customer focus, negotiating skills and a confident demeanour up to senior management level
  • French at native-speaker level as well as fluent German and English; strong customer presence in Romandy and regular collaboration with the teams in Zurich (work location Zurich or Geneva)

Role overview

As part of the strengthening of our SAP Finance team, we are looking for a functional consultant SAP FI/CO to intervene on a scope combining application support, scalable TMA and participation in SAP projects.

You will support our clients in the stabilization and evolution of their financial processes under SAP, by intervening on both incident resolution, functional improvements and project implementation.

You are directly involved with Finance business teams to ensure continuity of service, process reliability and the evolution of SAP solution.

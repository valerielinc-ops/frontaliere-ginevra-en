Full description

The Mobile Social Emergencies Unit (UMUS), composed of nurses and social workers, intervenes in situations mainly related to violence and insecurity in coordination with the Geneva social and health network. The team works in pairs and operates from 17:00 to 08:00 on weekdays and 24 hours on weekends and public holidays.

  • Evaluate the nature of the request from a professional in the Geneva social and health network and respond as soon as possible or direct the situation towards a more suitable service
  • To intervene and carry out jointly with the nurse, a socio-sanitary evaluation by providing in this analysis its social expertise and developing short-term intervention projects, while guaranteeing the legal and legal limits in the interventions
  • Propose to the customer and his or her entourage ways to reduce the intensity of the crisis or emergency felt; offer him a listening and support, aimed at restoring immediately his balance and that of his entourage
  • To carry out the written assessment of each intervention, giving an accurate account, the assessment, the benefits offered and the orientation chosen
  • Evaluate the circuit and the relevance of the management of each intervention
  • Participate in colloquia and team supervision, outside intervention times
  • Actively intervene in sessions with partners.
  • Bachelor in social work or equivalent
  • Ability to manage crisis situations
  • Affluence in mediation and ability to position itself in the face of various interlocutors
  • Very good knowledge of the Geneva social and health network
  • Experience in the areas of exclusion, precariousness, care of minors or mental health would be an asset.
  • Deadline for application: 11.12.2026
  • Entry into office : 01.04.2027
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.

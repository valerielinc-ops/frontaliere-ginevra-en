Your Purpose

The role exists to support the Head of Diversity, Inclusion & Wellbeing in delivering global initiatives that strengthen the company’s inclusive culture and promote employee wellbeing. By contributing to cross-functional projects, facilitating yearly survey implementation & analysis and organizing the wellbeing activities at the Geneva office, the role ensures that D&I and wellbeing commitments are effectively implemented.

Your Impact

  • Support the D&I certification process by facilitating inputs from Procurement, Marketing, Product, Communication, and HR departments, to ensure the company meets compliance standards.
  • Support the annual global employee survey and manage results analysis, working with the Engagement team, analyzing the data, following up on the identified improvement action plans and supporting their implementation.
  • Engage with external D&I associations both globally and in Switzerland to foster partnerships.
  • Contribute to the global learning & development activities liaising with the L&D department to ensure full learning coverage.
  • Organize wellbeing initiatives at the Geneva headquarters and liaise with HR teams both from the ashore global offices and from the ships, to promote selected wellbeing activities geared to specific needs and follow up on employees satisfaction.

Your Journey so far

  • Shipboard experience a clear advantage.
  • Education in Human Resources, Psychology, Social Sciences or a related field.

Your Essentials

  • Passion for Diversity, Inclusion & Wellbeing.
  • Cpabilities of working independently.
  • Data analysis and reporting abilities.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Languages: fluent English, and any other language among Italian, Spanish or French would be a plus.

Our commitment

We are committed to building a future that values diverse perspectives, embraces the world beyond borders, and fosters an inclusive environment where every individual feels valued, respected and empowered to be their authentic selves. Our commitment extends to taking meaningful, measurable actions that have a long-term positive impact on our guests, our employees and our planet.

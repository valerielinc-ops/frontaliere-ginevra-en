Full description

  • Support beneficiaries in the implementation of carpentry projects of varying complexity (Examples: manufacture and installation of kitchens and tables for schools; manufacture of handicrafts; development of new projects, from design to manufacture), and guarantee the quality of the work delivered to customers.
  • Take responsibility for the socio-professional follow-up of beneficiaries: setting socio-professional objectives, assessing the development of skills, developing the appropriate teaching tools...
  • Contribute to customer search actions and manage client mandate support
  • Participate in organizational improvements of the workshop in a multidisciplinary team.
  • Completing the administrative tasks inherent in the position: tracking of turnover targets, creation of price offers, supplier orders, etc.
  • A carpenter CFC, and a professional experience of at least 10 years in the field
  • A proven experience in site management
  • Ideally an experience in accompaniment or training of persons with disabilities or difficulties in social integration.
  • Ideally a training of socio-professional master or deemed equivalent
  • A developed relational sense, a pronounced taste for teamwork, and ease in communication
  • Good editorial capacity.
  • Mastery of common office automation tools.
  • The sense of organization, rigour, sense of responsibility and an open mind.
  • Mastery of the French language.

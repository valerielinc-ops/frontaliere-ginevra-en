STORE PLANNING PROJECT MANAGER

CDI

Plan-les-Ouates

FOR THOSE WHO SEEK EXCELLENCE

Join one of the most respected Maisons in fine watchmaking where excellence is both a given and a never-ending quest. Play your part in driving our collective success.

YOUR ROLE

We are seeking a Store Planning Project Manager to join our dynamic Store Design & Visual Merchandising team and lead the delivery of retail design projects (shop-in-shops and POS) across our global network, ensuring the highest standards of architectural excellence, brand consistency and project execution, from initial concept to final installation.

PLAYING YOUR PART

PROJECT PLANNING & EXECUTION

  • You will lead store design projects, from initial layout conception to final installation
  • You will coordinate contractors, millworkers and site teams to supervise construction progress
  • You will manage site handovers, snagging lists and defect resolution to guarantee high-end retail execution

ARCHITECTURAL GUIDELINES & MATERIAL SOURCING

  • You will contribute to the development and maintenance of brand architectural guidelines
  • You will review local execution plans, ensuring compliance with brand aesthetic identity and technical standards
  • You will research, select and procure materials, finishes, lighting and fixtures, in line with budget, technical durability and corporate sustainability guidelines

LOGISTICS, BUDGET & STAKEHOLDER COORDINATION

  • You will coordinate domestic and international shipments, and support custom clearance and import/export documentation
  • You will monitor project timelines, milestones and budgets, including contractor quotations, invoices and cost variances
  • You will act as the main operational contact between HQ, regional teams and external partners

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU SUCCESSFUL?

Success in this role will require

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Project Management, Civil Engineering, Architecture or similar
  • A proven experience in retail architecture, store planning or construction project management
  • Deep understanding of construction methods, architectural detailing and MEP basics
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills, combined with strong project management skills
  • Organization, attention to detail and problem-solving abilities, as well as teamwork
  • Proficiency in AutoCad, advanced Excel skills and familiarity with Photshop/InDesign
  • Fluency in English and French

Vacheron Constantin celebrates more than 270 years as the world’s oldest watchmaker in continuous production since 1755. Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, we operate a global retail network of nearly 200 points of sale across 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional global community of watchmakers, artisans and dedicated professionals share a common devotion to our exceptional heritage and a passion to trailblaze an even brighter future.

Full description

Non-Sharpen Cardiology includes cardiac imaging, functional explorations, and follow-up of patients with the pace torsh disease. It also contributes to the management of patients in the context of cardiac rehabilitation.

Want to evolve in a demanding, modern and multidisciplinary medical environment, within a committed and dynamic team? We offer you the opportunity to join our non-Windows cardiology team as:

Medical Secretary in Cardiology non-Strong CDMax 10 months to 80% (ref: 2002)

A true interface between patients, physicians and different stakeholders, you ensure daily administrative management and contribute to the proper functioning of the service.

In this capacity, you will be led to

  • To receive, inform and guide patients with professionalism and kindness;
  • Make appointments and manage medical schedules;
  • Organize the cardiological examinations and the various tests;
  • Redeem and format medical reports;
  • Billing for consultations according to Tarmed and at private rate on Opale;
  • Provides various administrative tasks in collaboration with doctors and managers;
  • Actively participate in the quality and fluidity of the administrative care of patients.

Your profile

You are an organized, rigorous and autonomous person, with an excellent sense of contact and service.

You have ideally

  • Diploma in medical secretariat or administrative;
  • Excellent mastery of IT tools and MS Office;
  • Strong organizational and priority management capacities;
  • Excellent relational and communication skills;
  • A versatile, responsible and proactive personality;
  • Excellent presentation and a great sense of discretion;
  • An ability to work effectively in a dynamic and multidisciplinary environment.

Knowledge of institutional software such as Opale, Carefolio, Cardio Report or Xplore is a real asset.

Why join us?

Integrate a reference institution and evolve into a team specializing in non-Shart cardiology, in a stimulating environment where collaboration, quality of care and professionalism are at the heart of priorities.

You will have the opportunity to put your skills at the service of a varied medical activity, to contact experienced professionals and in a framework that promotes multidisciplinary collaboration.

Want to take on this new challenge and contribute to the smooth functioning of a specialized cardiology service? We look forward to welcoming you to our team!

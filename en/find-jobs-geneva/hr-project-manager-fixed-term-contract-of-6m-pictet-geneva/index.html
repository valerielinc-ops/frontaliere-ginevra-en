Main responsibilities

  • Your key responsibilities will include:
  • Leading complex, global HR projects from initiation to completion, ensuring alignment with organisational goals, timelines and quality standards.
  • Collaborating with HR leadership and other departments/teams to clarify project needs and ensure strong stakeholder engagement and buy-in.
  • Monitoring project progress, preparing reports and presenting findings to senior management, providing insights and recommendations.
  • Developing and embedding best practices and methodologies in project management, change management and employee experience, ensuring HR delivers high quality solutions for employees and managers.
  • Designing and executing change management plans to ensure successful adoption of new processes, tools and ways of working.
  • Developing clear, targeted communication materials and change narratives tailored to different audiences.
  • Coordinating and delivering training, workshops, and user support to ensure readiness and sustainability of the change.
  • Acting as the driver of innovation within HR by calling on your internal and external networks.

Key requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field.
  • Four to five years’ experience in a consulting firm or in project & change management roles within a global, multicultural environment.
  • Proven experience leading complex cross functional global initiatives and managing multiple priorities.
  • Experience supporting the implementation and change management of SAP SuccessFactors (e.g.
  • EC, Recruiting, Onboarding) is a strong plus.
  • Strong problem solving skills, with the ability to find simple, pragmatic solutions to complex problems.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex topics through a clear and compelling narrative.
  • Entrepreneurial flair and ease in navigating ambiguity, while balancing innovation with risk awareness.
  • Keen interest in new technologies, digital tools, artificial intelligence and machine learning.
  • Excellent spoken and written English and French.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland.

Full description

We are looking for a Senior Technical Business Analyst SAP S/4HANA with strong functional and techno-functional expertise in Finance & Management Control (FI/CO), Product Management (MM), Sales & Distribution (SD) and the SAP Fiori ecosystem. Key responsibilities

  • Collect and analyse the business needs of internal stakeholders and propose solutions as close as possible to the standard features of SAP S/4HANA.
  • Advise users on SAP S/4HANA good practices.
  • Contribute to the definition of solutions that meet business needs while meeting SAP standards.
  • Participate in SAP S/4HANA evolution and transformation projects.
  • Set and customize FI modules (GL, AP, AR and AA), CO, MM and SD.
  • Support UAT campaigns and contribute to release management activities.
  • Ensure the quality, durability, scalability and sustainability of the solutions, respecting the product standards and the business needs defined by the customer.
  • Maintain functional and technical documentation.
  • Provide application support, diagnose problems and resolve functional incidents.
  • University degree, EPFL, HEC, HEG or equivalent qualification.
  • Experience confirmed as Senior Technical Business Analyst SAP S/4HANA with expertise in FI/CO, MM and SD.
  • Good knowledge of SAP Fiori applications and architecture.
  • An experience with Esker, SAP Concur and/or in the banking sector is an asset.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to understand and respond to business issues.
  • Excellent communication and relational qualities.
  • English: current, both in writing and oral.
  • French: current.
  • Swiss Residence
